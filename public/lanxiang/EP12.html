Let me tell you about episode twelve, because this is the episode where the house gives a girl one day of ordinary happiness and then takes the master's standing in exchange — and the two events, though they happen to different people, turn out to be the same story. Lin Jinqi, still turning over what Lanxiang did with the flower, summons her and asks, plainly, what she wants for it. Most maids would name a trifle or a coin; Lanxiang names a day. She asks for a single holiday to go home and see her foster parents, and for a little of the army's salve, because her mother's hands split open every winter and the mutton fat no longer holds. The master, who has been looking for a way to reward her without making her dangerous to the others, grants both — a day, and the salve — and in that small granting a girl who has spent the whole season as someone else's instrument gets, for one afternoon, to be a daughter again.

让我跟你讲讲第十二集，因为这一集，是府里给一个姑娘一日寻常的欢喜、转而拿主子的体面作交换的一集——而这两桩，虽落在不同的人身上，末了是同一桩事。林锦岐还转着兰香对那花做的事，召她来，平平问她要什么。多数丫鬟会讨小物或碎银；兰香讨一日。她求一日假回家看养父母，并讨一点军里的膏，因她娘的手每冬绽裂、羊脂再也止不住。主子正愁怎么赏她而不致她惹旁人忌，便都许了——一日，与膏——而在那小小的许诺里，一个整季做别人器的姑娘，有一下午，重新做了回女儿。

Du Cuique, meanwhile, plays the longer game with a gentler hand. She comes to Xiaolian's cot with a jar of ointment, all concern, and dabs at the girl's imagined aches while she talks — and what she talks about, with a smile that asks nothing, is the flower business, and whether Xiaolian heard who first knew the cuique's meaning. Xiaolian, unguarded and flattered to be consulted by the favored new comer, lets slip that it was Lanxiang who saw it, in the books. Du Cuique's face does not change. She praises Xiaolian's honesty, smooths her hair, and leaves. But she has what she wanted: confirmation that the maid who blocked her is the same maid the master now rewards, and a thread she can pull later. A calculating girl does not quarrel with the hand that feeds her information. She simply remembers whose bowl to fill.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

杜翠雀那边，以更柔的手，下更长的棋。她拿一罐膏到小莲铺边，满口关切，抹着那姑娘想象中的酸疼，一边聊——笑里什么也不问——聊那花的事，聊小莲可听见谁先懂了翠雀的意思。小莲不设防、又被这得宠的新人问而受用，漏了口：是兰香在书里看出的。杜翠雀脸色不变。她赞小莲老实，抚她发，离去。可她得了要的：证实了挡她的人、与如今主子赏的人，是同一个丫鬟，和一缕她日后可拽的线。精于算计的姑娘，不与喂她信息的手争。她只记着该给谁添碗。

At home, Lanxiang is simply a child again. Her parents, knowing she has the day, beg the neighbors to keep their voices down so she can sleep past dawn for the first time in a year, and the whole lane obliges, because everyone in that corner knows the Lanxiang who left is the Lanxiang who grew up here. She wakes late, eats at her mother's table as if no time had passed, and then does the one thing that is hers to do: she takes the salve and, with her own hands, works it into the cracked skin of her mother's hands, the way she used to before the household took her. When she brings out the ten taels of gold the Senior Lady gave her, her father's eyes widen — he has seen gold in shops, never in his own house. The family, he says, has already saved enough for the three of them to buy back their contracts. They could be free.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

在家里，兰香只是个孩子。父母知她有这日，求邻居压低声让她头回睡过黎明，整条巷都依了，因为这角的人都知道：走时的兰香，是在这儿长大的兰香。她睡到迟，在娘桌前吃像时光没动过，然后做那件只归她做的事：她拿起膏，亲手揉进娘裂着的手皮，像府里带走她之前那样。当她取出林母给的十两金，她爹眼睁大了——他在铺子里见过金，没在自己家见过。他说，家里已攒够他们三口赎身的银。他们本可自由了。

But Lanxiang shakes her head, and in that shaking is the coldest political judgment the episode will show. She tells her parents, quietly, that the Lin estate is not short of money — a household that can spare ten taels of gold to a maid is a household that will not miss the price of three contracts. The danger is not the cost. The danger is the moment. To ask for freedom now, while the master's standing is what it is, is to beg a favor from a man who may soon need favors himself, and a contract granted out of weakness is a contract that can be taken back. She tells them to wait — to hold the silver, keep their heads down, and let the house's weather pass. Her parents, who do not fully follow the court logic, follow the daughter who has kept them alive this long. It is the first time she has used her new closeness to power to protect them not by pleading, but by refusing.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

可兰香摇头，而那摇头里，是全集最冷的政治判断。她静静告诉父母：林府不缺钱——肯舍十两金给一个丫鬟的府，不会在意三张身契的价。险不在价。险在时机。此刻去求自由，是从一个体面正如何的主子手里讨恩，而弱时给的契，是能收回的契。她叫他们等——捏着银、低头、让府里的天气过去。父母不懂那套宅中理，却信这个护他们活到如今的女儿。这是她头回用新近靠近的权，不是去求、而是去拒，来护他们。

The weather breaks the same week. In the garrison, the winter coats that never arrived prove to have been filled with rotten cotton and river reed instead of wool, and men trained through the frost have died of it — not in battle, but of cold, in their own barracks. A soldier named Sun Yong, whose brother is among the dead, blocks Lin Jinqi's gate and names him a thief: the commander who took the silver and sent the rags. The charge is a lie, but a useful one, because it lands on a man already stripped of the Zhao family's protection, and the ministry, unwilling to look closely, suspends Lin Jinqi pending inquiry. And the Xie family, whose son was to marry the second daughter, withdraws the match the next morning on the neatest of pretexts — incompatible horoscopes — which everyone in the city reads as what it is: a household abandoning a falling star before it hits the ground.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

天气就在同周崩了。卫所里，那从未到的冬衣，证出填的是烂棉与芦絮、而非羊毛；在霜里操练的兵，不是战死，是冻死在自己营里。一个叫孙勇的兵，兄弟在死者中，堵了林锦岐的门，指他为贼：吞了银、发了破袄的统兵。这告是谎，却是好用的谎，因它落在已失赵家庇护的人身上；衙门不愿细看，把林锦岐停职待查。而本要娶二小姐的谢家，次日一早以最干净的理由——八字不合——退了亲，满城都读成它本来的样子：一家在坠落的星砸地前，弃了它。

Inside the Lin residence the fallout is almost comic in its familiarity. The Second Aunt receives the news of the broken match and, instead of grieving, is quietly relieved — she had never liked the Xie family's pride, and now she can disdain their weather-cocking aloud. The father, Lin Jinqi's own sire, storms in to accuse his son, and the Second Uncle, never one to miss a chance to sting, adds a line or two of poison — whereupon the father, who has spent a lifetime being obeyed, chases the Second Uncle around the court with a slipper while the mother and Second Aunt shield the porcelain. It is the great house reduced, for one absurd minute, to a household like any other, because disgrace, it turns out, levels everyone to the same small room. Lanxiang, watching from the study door, files the scene away: a family's loyalty, she notes, is deepest exactly where the money is not at stake.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

林府之内，余波熟得近乎可笑。二婶听了退亲，不悲，反暗暗松了口气——她本不喜谢家的傲，如今能当面鄙他们趋炎。父亲，林锦岐的生父，闯来问儿子罪；二叔从不失刺人的机，添一两句毒——于是那位被服从了一辈子的父亲，拖鞋追着二叔绕院跑，而母亲与二婶护着瓷器。巨室在那荒唐的一刻，降成任一家——原来耻辱把所有人平到同一间小屋。兰香在书房门边看，记下了：一家人的忠，她注，恰在最不涉银处最深。

And then the mind Lin Jinqi has been filing away speaks. Jiugao and Nankè, his trusted men, return from hunting the merchant Lu Dalang and report they cannot find him — the man has simply vanished into the city's corners, and without the Lin name to open doors they have no way to follow. Lanxiang, hearing this, offers the thread the others missed. She points out what any girl who mends the household's clothes would see: that the rotten cotton and reed stripped from the coats do not disappear — they go home, in the offcuts, to the women who sew for a living, who use good cloth's scraps and bad padding to make shoes for their own children. Find the women sewing army cloth, she says, and you find the workshop. She leads Jiugao and Nankè to the south quarter of the city, and there Jiugao, disguised as a peddler, sees a child's shoe whose upper is cut from a coat's exact weave, stuffed with the very reed that killed the soldiers. The trail, cold a moment before, is suddenly warm.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

而后林锦岐归着的那颗脑，开口了。九皋与南客，他信得过的两个人，寻商人卢大郎回来，报说寻不见——那人没入城角，没了林家的名去开门，他们无从跟。兰香听了，递出他人漏的那缕线。她点出任何一个补府里衣服的姑娘都看得到的：从袄上剥下的烂棉与芦絮，不会凭空没——它们随边角料回了以缝纫为生的妇人手里，好人家的布头、坏里的衬，给自家孩子做鞋。寻到缝军布的女人，她说，便寻到作坊。她领九皋南客去城南，九皋扮货郎，看见一双童鞋，鞋面是袄上那确切的织纹，填的正是冻死兵的芦絮。一刻前还冷的线，忽地热了。

So we close episode twelve on a thread pulled taut. The master is suspended and the house diminished, but a maid who reads books and mends cloth has handed him the first real line to his name. Du Cuique, somewhere in the residence, has confirmed the girl she must undo. The parents, in their small home, hold their silver and their breath. And the soldier's dead brothers wait, in the barracks, for someone to prove the coats were murder and not weather. The episode asks the question the whole season keeps asking: who is the household, really — the name on the gate, or the girl who notices the shoe? For now, remember only this: in the Lin estate, a fall is never the end of the story. It is only the moment the quiet ones start to matter. Before that moment arrives, though, the season leaves the household in the strange peace that follows a storm — the master suspended but no longer accused, the parents holding their silver and their hope, the maid who found the shoe returned to her copying as if nothing had happened, and the two clever women of the house each certain, in her own way, that the next move is hers to make. A fall, in the Lin estate, is never the floor. It is only the stair the quiet ones climb while everyone else is still watching the dust.

于是我们在一根绷紧的线上收束第十二集。主子停了职、府小了，可一个读书又补衣的丫鬟，递给他名下第一条真线。杜翠雀在府中某处，认准了要除的那个姑娘。父母在小家里，捏着银、捏着气。而冻死兵的兄弟，在营里，等人为证那袄是谋杀、非天气。这一集问出全季不断在问的：这府第，究竟是谁——门上的姓，还是那个注意到鞋的姑娘？此刻你只记一句：在林府，一次倾覆从不是故事的终。它只是静处的人，开始要紧的一刻。
