# 兰香如故 — The Fragrance of Orchids Remains
## Episode 20 · Told Like A Story

The season, having spent many episodes breaking this household open, now tries to close it again, and episode twenty is where the Lin estate reaches for the ordinary life it remembers and finds the fit awkward, like a healed bone that sets a little crooked. It begins with the Old Master Lin giving his blessing to the match the third daughter offered herself for — her marriage to the Yongchang Marquis, the old general who stands as the family's greatest debt and its surest shield against the court's eye. The old man, who has always valued the household above the individual heart, sees in the match not a sacrifice but a settling of accounts: the family's honor mended by a daughter's willing hand, and the marquis bound to the Lin name by a knot stronger than any gratitude. The residence, still learning to breathe without a snake in its wall, turns its weary attention to the practical business of a wedding, and for one brief stretch the season's violence seems to have burned down to embers, leaving only the ordinary smoke of a household making plans for a happier day.

这季既把这座府拆开了许多集，今又试着合它；而第二十集，是林府伸手去抓它记得的寻常日子，却觉那合 awkward，如一根愈歪了的骨。它始于林老太爷应了三小姐自荐的那门亲——她嫁永昌侯，那林家最大的债、也是最稳的盾的老将。老人向来把家看得比个人之心重，看这门亲不是牺牲，是了账：家的名由女儿愿伸的手补起，而侯以比任何谢都牢的结，绑在林姓上。这府还在学着墙里无蛇地喘气，便把倦意下的心思，转到办喜的实务上；有一小段，这季的暴似已熄成余烬，只余一家为欢喜日子打算的寻常烟。

</details>

The second daughter, whose folly with Du Zheng began this latest trouble, is not permitted to simply walk back into the household's good graces as though nothing had happened. The old master, who brooks no disorder in his bloodline, places her under house confinement, kept to her rooms and watched by the household's women, the way a physician sets a fracture in a splint until the bone can be trusted to bear weight. It is neither cruelty nor forgiveness but the plain arithmetic of a great family: a daughter who mistook a traitor for a suitor must be held until the shame cools and the standing is sure. She sits within her walls and reckons, perhaps for the first time in her life, with the true cost of her own believing, while the sister who saved her from the old master's colder plan goes about the quiet, deliberate business of becoming a bride to a marquis, and the residence learns to live with one daughter caged and one daughter crowned by the same strange week.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

二小姐，这最近一桩麻烦由她的蠢而起，不被许就走回府里的好脸色，如无事发生。老太爷容不得血脉里乱，将她禁足——拘在房里、由府中妇人看过，如医者把裂处夹上夹板，直到敢托力。这不是狠，也不是恕，只是大宅的平账：一个把叛者当良人的女儿，须拘着，直到羞凉、门第稳。她坐在自己墙里，或许生平头回，算自己信人的真代价；而那把她从老太爷更冷之计里救出的妹，正忙着做侯的新娘那一桩静而笃的实务；这府学会与"一女被拘、一女被冠于同一奇周"共处。

</details>

Meanwhile, on the other side of the gate, Lin Jinqi turns his mind to the girl who is no longer his maid. He sends Jiugao, his most trusted hand, to help Lanxiang find a house of her own now that she is free, and Jiugao returns with a listing that seems almost too kind to be true — a small, tidy dwelling at a price so low it ought to raise a brow on anyone who has lived through a season of traps. But Lanxiang, who has spent the last many episodes reading lies in barrels and in smiling faces, sees the snare folded into the bargain at once. The cheap house sits among those who would prey on a woman living alone, or the deed hides a claim that would cost her the silver and the roof together; either way the generosity is a hook. She declines with a quiet, knowing smile, and the master, hearing of it later, is reminded all over again why he returned her contract rather than keeping her bound — a free Lanxiang is sharper than a bound one, and the household he set loose is the very one that saw through his own man's oversight.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

与此同时，门那侧，林锦岐把心思转到那不再是他丫鬟的姑娘。他遣最亲信的九皋，帮自由的兰香寻一处自己的屋；九皋带回一张好得近乎不真、却小的齐整房帖——价低得凡活过一季陷阱的人都该挑眉。但兰香，这许多集在桶里与笑里读谎，一眼看穿那便宜里的套。低价屋坐落在一群会欺独居女子的人中间，或那契里藏着会连银带顶一起吞了她的主张；无论哪种，那善都是钩。她淡淡一笑、了然地推了。主子后闻之，又一次被提醒：为何还她身契而非留她——自由的兰香比缚着的更利，而他放了的那户，正是能看穿他亲信疏忽的那户。

</details>

So Lanxiang's family does not take the cheap rooms but finds honest work instead, at the Lu family's calligraphy and painting shop — a quiet place of brushes, scrolls, and ink where the city's words and images are bought and sold by the foot and by the stroke. There, among the paper and the pigment, Lanxiang's own hand proves its worth in a way no contract ever measured: she who once painted to pass a message now paints to earn a living, and the Lu household, judging her by the quality of her work and not by her former collar, treats her as a craftsman worthy of the board. It is a small and real dignity, the kind that comes from being paid for what you can do rather than owned for what you are. The family that was once a master's furniture begins, faintly but truly, to stand on its own feet in the open city, and the freedom Lanxiang asked for starts to look less like a word on paper and more like a life she might actually live, among people who do not own her.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

于是兰香一家不租那便宜屋，却在陆家书画行寻着了正经活——一处笔、卷、墨的静地，城里的字与画在此按尺按笔买卖。在那纸与色间，兰香自己的手，证了它那契从未量的价：她曾为传信而画，今为谋生而画；陆家不以她旧领、只以她活的质地判她，待她如一个配坐席的匠。这是小却真的尊严，一种因你所能而被酬、非因你所是而被有的尊严。那曾作主子家具的一家，在敞亮的城里，隐隐却真地，立起了自己的脚；而兰香讨的自由，渐不像纸上的字，倒像一种她真能过的活——在一群不拥有她的人中间。

</details>

At the Lu shop Lanxiang meets a man who will come to matter far more than either of them yet understands: Han Liang, a young scholar who comes in for brushes and stays for conversation, and who takes to her the way a patient reader takes to a writer whose hand he has been waiting his whole life to meet. They speak of paintings and of poems, of the small quiet philosophies a person carries in the chest, and the talk is easy and unguarded in a way the sealed residence never permitted. The impression is good on both sides — he finds her clever and steady, she finds him gentle and earnest — and neither names what is forming between them, because naming is a thing great houses do with contracts and free people are allowed to delay. But the season, which has been watching every thread, files the scholar's name where it keeps the others who will yet be tested, and the city, unaware, lets two unlikely people grow a little fonder of one another by the day, as cities always do when they are not paying attention.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

在陆家，兰香遇见一个对两人都远比此刻所知之重的男人：韩梁，一个为笔而来、为谈而留的年轻秀才，对她生出如一个耐心的读者对那等候一生方遇的写手之手的亲近。他们谈画、谈诗、谈一个人胸中所怀的小小道理；那谈，自在而无防，是那封起的府里从不许的。两边印象都好——他觉她伶俐稳当，她觉他温厚恳切——谁也没点破其间正成的东西，因点破是大宅拿契去行的，而自由人准于延缓。但这季看着每根线，把那秀才的名，收进收着别的、待试之人的那一处；而城，不知觉，由着两个不相干的人，一日比一日更投契，如城总在不经意时那样。

</details>

Back within the courts of law, the long wokou collusion case at last reaches a bloodied close, tying off the very thread that began this whole unraveling seasons ago. It is decided, in the end, by two men — Dai Rong and Liang Yu — who take the weight of the treason onto their own necks and die in prison for it, their confessions knotting the case shut the way a noose knots a rope. Whether they were the true heads of the smuggling or merely the convenient necks is a question the season leaves deliberately hanging, but the court is content, the rolls are closed, and the trade that began this entire long trouble is, officially, finished. The Lin estate, hearing the news, marks another thread cut and exhales a little; the city, which had held its breath through a whole season of plots and poisons, turns back at last to the business of selling, marrying, and dying in the ordinary way, as if the dark year had merely been a bad dream best put behind it.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

回到官衙里，那缠长的倭寇通敌案，终见了带血的尾，系住了这整场散局许多集前由它而起的那根线。终是由两个人——戴荣与梁玉——把叛的份量扛上自己颈，死在狱中；他们的供，如结系住绳那样，把案系了尾。他们究竟是真头目、还是 merely 方便的颈，这季留作悬问；但官心满意，卷已合，而掀动这一整场长麻烦的私运，官面上，是了了。林府闻讯，又记一刀线断，略吐了气；那因一整季图谋与毒而屏过息的城，终转回那卖、娶、平常死的实务，如那暗年不过是一场最好抛在身后的噩梦。

</details>

The Crown Prince himself descends upon Jinling to carry the wokou matter to its final accounting, and his arrival changes the air of the city the way a thunderhead changes the light before a storm. The investigation that Lin Jinqi and the Yongchang Marquis set in motion now has the ear of the throne behind it, and every remaining thread, every name that was not yet named, is drawn toward the prince's hand like iron to a magnet. For the Lin family, who have stood close to the very heart of this treason, the prince's presence is both a relief and a danger: the case that nearly took their name is now the throne's own, but a throne that looks closely sees closely, and what it sees of the Lin household may not all be to its liking. The season, never quite done with this house, places a new and loftier eye upon it, and the freedom Lanxiang won and the marriage the third daughter offered both suddenly sit beneath a gaze that can unmake either with a single careless word.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

太子亲临金陵，要把倭案作最后的了结；他的到来，改了这城的气，如浓云在风暴前改了光。林锦岐与永昌侯启动的那查，如今有了御耳在后；每一条剩的线、每一个还没被点的名，都被引向太子的手，如铁向磁。对站在这一叛核心近旁的林家，太子的在，既是宽、也是险：那几乎夺了他们名的案，今是御自己的，但一个近看的御座，看得也近；它看林府，未必全合意。这季对这府从不甘休，又把一只新的、更高的眼，落在它身上；而兰香挣来的自由、三小姐献出的婚，忽都坐在了一道能凭一句漫不经心的话便拆了任一的目光之下。

</details>

Lin Jinqi, hearing that Lanxiang has found work and company at the shop, goes to call on her, and what he finds at the door gives him a pause he does not show. There she is, in easy conversation with Han Liang, the two of them bent over a single painting with the unselfconscious closeness of people who like each other's minds more than they have yet admitted. The master, who has watched Lanxiang through a season of poison and war and come to know her the way a man knows the one steady hand in a crisis, feels something he does not name, and his first move is the move of a person who governs by knowing: he sends word to have Han Liang's background investigated, to learn whose son the scholar is and what claims his family might hold. It is not yet suspicion and not yet jealousy, only the old habit of a steward of men — but the season, which knows the shape of what comes, notes plainly that the girl who was freed is now the girl another man would keep, and the master has begun, quietly and perhaps against his own will, to look.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

林锦岐闻兰香在店有了活、有了伴，去探她；在门口所见，令他有一瞬未露的顿。她在那里，与韩梁自在谈着，两人俯在一幅画上，带着一种互不设防的近——是那种互喜对方心思、却还未及承认之人的近。主子，这一季在毒与战里看着兰香、如一个男人识得危中那只稳手般识得她，觉出一样他未点名的东西；而他的头一步，是一个以知人而治的人的步：他传话去查韩梁的身家，要知这秀才是谁家子、他家或有的主张。这还非疑、非妒，只是个御人之人的旧习——但这季，识得来日之形，平白记下：那被放了走的姑娘，今是另一个男人想要留的姑娘，而主子，已悄然、或许违着己意，起了看的心。

</details>

So we close episode twenty the way a household closes a quiet day — by noting who has moved where, and who has begun, without quite meaning to, to watch the door. The third daughter's marriage was blessed and the second's feet were stayed; Lanxiang found honest work and an honest conversation; the wokou case ended on two prison deaths that satisfied the court; the Crown Prince came down to Jinling with the throne behind him; and Lin Jinqi, visiting the girl he had freed, saw her laughing with a scholar and set a quiet inquiry in motion. Episode twenty-one, when it comes, will carry Han Liang's widowed sister home and carry the Senior Lady's eye toward a very different bride for the master, and those two threads will pull hard at the girl who thought that freedom meant she was done being watched. But that, as ever, is next week's tell, and the lanterns of the Lin estate burn on, indifferent to what the season plans next.

于是我们如一户合一个静日那样收束第二十集——记谁移到了何处，谁又不由己地，起了看门的心。三小姐的婚得了应、二小姐的脚被留住；兰香得了正经活、也得了正经的谈；倭案结于两桩满足官心的狱死；太子带了御座下了金陵；而林锦岐，探那他放了走的姑娘，见她与一秀才笑谈，悄然布下了一道查。第二十一集，来的时候，会把韩梁的寡姊接回家，也会把林母的眼，引向主子另一门很不同的亲；这两线，会猛拽那以为自由即不必再被看的姑娘。但那，如常，是下周的讲法；而林府的灯，烧着，对这季的下一步毫不在意。
