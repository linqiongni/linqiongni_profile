# 兰香如故 — The Fragrance of Orchids Remains
## Episode 22 · Told Like A Story

Let me tell you about episode twenty-two, because this is the episode where the ducal bride the Senior Lady favored finally shows her hand, and the quiet match the mothers were brewing runs straight into the hard wall of a great house's will. It opens with Zheng Manyi visiting the Lin residence, and the Senior Lady Lin, who has already decided this girl shall be her son's wife, contrives with practiced ease to leave the two of them alone together. It is the old lady's particular art — the staging of a moment so that a thing may be seen to happen without ever being said to happen, the way a gardener arranges a path so the walker believes the turning was his own idea. Zheng Manyi, flattered and calculating both, plays her assigned part, and the residence, which has already hosted poisoners and princes within its walls, now hosts a different kind of danger entirely: a bride who means to be chosen, and who will not abide a former maid across the city standing in her light for one more day.

让我跟你讲讲第二十二集，因为这一集，是林母属意的那个国公之女，终亮出了她的手；而母亲们正酿的那门静亲，一头撞进了大宅意志的硬墙上。它始于郑蔓宜探访林府；林母既已定这姑娘作儿媳，便以熟极的从容，刻意留她与儿子独处。这是老夫人的独门艺——把一个时刻摆好，教一件事似发生、却从不被说破，如园丁铺一条路，让行路者以为那转弯是他自己的主意。郑蔓宜，又受用又算计，演她的份；而这府，既在墙内容过投毒人、也容过太子，今又容了一种全然不同的险：一个要被选中、且不容城那头一个旧丫鬟多挡她一日光的 bride。

</details>

Zheng Manyi, during that arranged solitude, notices the pot of orchids Lin Jinqi brought to Lanxiang, and she proves sharper than the household had given her credit for being. She senses at once that the small plant means something to the master — that it is not a mere gift but a message, a token of a feeling he has not spoken aloud, pressed into a maid's hands across the city like a letter with no address. The ducal daughter, who will not be second to a servant she considers beneath her, goes straight to the painting shop and buys the orchid from Lanxiang for a hundred taels of silver — a sum meant far less to own the flower than to own the meaning, to strip the maid of the one thing the master's hand had ever given her. And when she reaches her own rooms, she smashes the pot upon the floor, the way a rival crushes the proof that she was ever, even for an instant, rivaled at all.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

在那刻意安排的独处里，郑蔓宜注意到林锦岐带给兰香的那盆兰花草；而她比这府肯认的更尖。她一瞬便觉出那小株对主子别有意味——它不是 mere 礼，是信，是他未出口的一种情，按进城那头一个丫鬟手里，如一封无址的信。这国公之女，不甘落在一个她视作卑下的仆人之后，直奔书画行，以百两银向兰香买下那兰——那数，意在拥有那意味、多过拥有那花，意在剥掉丫鬟手主子唯一给过的物。回到自己房里，她把那盆摔在地上，如一个对手碾碎她曾被人争过、哪怕一瞬的证。

</details>

Having broken the orchid, Zheng Manyi sets about breaking the girl who owned it. She buys off an opera troupe — players who owe her family loyalty or her coin — and through them she engineers a trap with patient, practiced cruelty: she has Lanxiang drugged, slipped a sleeping draught so that the maid's wits are taken from her without a struggle or a scream. The plan is the kind a great house's daughter learns young, because she has always had others to do the dirty part while she keeps her own hands clean. The drug does its quiet work, Lanxiang's guard drops away, and the stage is set for the sort of scene that, once witnessed, cannot be unwitnessed — a scene meant to brand the maid with a shame no free woman could walk past, and to hand Zheng Manyi the Lin household without a single open fight.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

摔了兰，郑蔓宜便着手摔那姑娘。她买通一班戏子——那些欠她家忠或欠她钱的角儿——借他们布了一个耐而熟狠的套：她把兰香下了药，暗里递了睡汤，教丫鬟不挣扎、不喊便失了神智。这计是大宅之女少时学的那一种，因脏活向来有人替她做、她自保双手干净。药静静发了功，兰香的防尽去；台已搭好，等一场一旦被见、便不能当没见的戏——一场意在给丫鬟烙上任何自由女都迈不过的羞、并把林府不战而交到郑蔓宜手里的戏。

</details>

The trap, once sprung, is meant to produce a scandal with Han Liang — the very scholar whose matchmaker was sent only days before, and whose name has thus become a threat to the ducal bride's design. Zheng Manyi's people place the drugged Lanxiang and the unwitting Han Liang in a compromising tableau, the sort of picture that, in a household ruled by standing and reputation, would end a free woman's name and a scholar's prospects in a single breath. It is the oldest weapon of the great house: not the blade but the rumor, the careful arrangement of bodies that says what no words need say and denies the victim any defense. The ducal daughter waits beyond the door to be properly shocked, ready to reveal the scene and let the shame do the marrying for her — certain, in her pride, that the maid who read water and caught poisoners cannot possibly read her way out of a scandalous bed.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

那套一旦弹开，意在造一场与韩梁的丑闻——那秀才，媒人才遣出几日，其名于是成了国公之女算计里的刺。郑蔓宜的人把下了药的兰香、与毫不知情的韩梁，摆进一幅见不得人的局；这种画面，在门第与名誉为治的府里，一口息便断送一个自由女的名、与一个秀才的前程。这是大宅最旧的兵器：不是刃，是谣，是那不用言说、身子一摆便道尽、且夺了受害者一切辩白的摆布。国公之女在门外候着"惊见"，备好掀那场面，让羞替她去成这门亲——她以她的傲笃定，那读水、捉投毒人的丫鬟，绝读不出一张丑床的局。

</details>

But Lanxiang, even fogged by the draught, is quicker than the trap allows for. She wakes to the shape of the snare and, with the same quick invention that once dropped a hairpin to save a child, conjures an escape the season had not written for her: she poses as a ghost — a restless spirit returned, wild-eyed and wailing in the manner of the dead who will not rest — and the superstitious troupe, who bargained for a scandal and got a haunting instead, scatters in real fright. It is a stroke of pure nerve, the kind that turns the believer's own fear against him, and it wins her a door and a stretch of darkness to slip through. The ducal daughter's careful scene collapses into laughter and panic, and the maid who was meant to be ruined walks out of it wearing a ghost's face and a living girl's wits, having fooled the very men sent to disgrace her.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

但兰香，即便被汤熏得迷糊，也比那套料得的快。她醒来，识出那局的形；借那曾落簪救孩的同一机变，她造了一场这季没为她写的逃——她扮起鬼来：一个回了来的不安之灵，眼狂而号，如那些不肯安息的亡者；那迷信的戏班，本为丑闻讨价、却撞见闹鬼，惊散了。这是纯胆的一着，一种把信者自己的怕、反用来对付信者的着；它赢得一门、一片可溜的暗。国公之女精心摆的场，塌成笑与慌；那本要被毁的丫鬟，戴着鬼的脸、揣着活姑娘的清醒，走了出去，骗过了那群奉命辱她的人。

</details>

Yet the night is not done with her, and this season rarely grants a clean escape. No sooner has she slipped the troupe's grasp than the Duke's own men — the hard hands moving behind Zheng Manyi's pretty plots — seize her again, this time without the pretense of a scandal and without the opera lights to soften it. They take her somewhere the matchmaker's foot will never find, somewhere the free household she had begun to build cannot reach. The freedom she had only just begun to taste turns to a gag and a cart, and the season, which had seemed to offer her a choice between two men who wanted her, reveals the third force that wanted her merely silenced. The Lin household, oblivious across the city, sleeps while its former maid is carried into a dark it has not yet begun to imagine, and the orchid she once held is already smashed to dust in another woman's rooms.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

然这一夜还没放她过，而这季甚少给干净的逃。她才脱了戏班的掌，国公自家的手下——郑蔓宜那些好看计策后头的硬手——又抓住她，这一回，不借丑闻的幌、不借戏台的灯来缓它。他们把她带去媒人的脚永寻不到处、她才起手筑的那自由户够不到处。她才刚尝到的自由，化作了塞口与一车；这季，似要把她摆到两个想要她的男人之间选，却露出第三股只欲她噤声的力。林府在城那头懵然睡着，它旧日的丫鬟，被载进一片它还想象不到的暗里；而她曾握过的兰，已在另一女人房里碎作了尘。

</details>

Lin Jinqi, meanwhile, learns that Lanxiang is gone, and the master who governs by knowing moves at once, as he always does when something of his is taken. He traces the thread of her disappearance back to the opera troupe, and there, in the frightened talk of players who saw both a ghost and a kidnapping in one strange night, he picks up the scent of the Duke's hand moving beneath the bride's. Following that scent, he comes upon a man called Liu Shu — and in this meeting the season turns a page none of them expected. Liu Shu is no common wanderer but the deposed crown prince himself, in hiding from the court that hunts him; and Lin Jinqi learns that the throne is even now sweeping every alley of Jinling for the former prince. The master who came only to find a maid finds himself, in an instant, standing before the fallen heir to the empire, and two debts — to a girl and to a dynasty — tangle in his hands at once.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

与此同时，林锦岐闻兰香不见了；这以知人而治的主子，立刻动了，如每当他的物被夺时那样。他把她失踪的线，追回到那戏班；在那些一夜之间既见鬼、又见掳的戏子惊惶的话里，他嗅出国公的手，在 bride 之下动着。顺着那味，他撞见一个叫刘叔的人——而这一遇，这季翻了谁都没料的一页。刘叔不是寻常浪客，正是藏身的废太子，在躲那搜他的朝廷；而林锦岐得知，御座正经金陵每条巷，搜这位前太子。本只为寻一丫鬟来的主子，一瞬之间，立在那坠下的帝国继承人身前；两笔债——对一女、对一朝——在他手里倏然缠作一团。

</details>

It is worth marking, in this turning, how a single missing maid has drawn the master into the very center of the realm's hidden war. Lin Jinqi came for a girl who paints and reads, and he leaves with a secret that could unmake the Lin name or remake it beyond dreaming — the knowledge of where the deposed crown prince hides. The season, which has been tightening its noose around this household all year, suddenly places the fate of a dynasty into one man's quiet, capable hands, and the humble errand of finding a servant becomes the hinge upon which a throne may turn. A great house that thought itself merely beset by poisoners and jealous brides discovers, in a single evening, that it stands in the path of the empire itself, and that the girl it lost was the thread that led the empire to its door. The small and the vast, it turns out, are bound by the same thin string in this season, and to pull one end is to shake the other whether anyone intended it or not.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

值得在此一转处记一记：一个丫鬟的失踪，如何把主子引到了这国度隐战的正心。林锦岐为一个会画会读的姑娘而来，却带着一个能倾林名、或把它重筑得超乎梦想的秘离去——那废太子藏身处之知。这季一路把这府的套收紧了一整年，忽把一朝的命运，搁进了一个男人静而能的手里；而寻一个仆那卑微的差，成了御座或可由之转的铰。一座自以为不过被投毒人、被妒 bride 围攻的大宅，在一晚之间发觉，自己正立在那帝国本身的路上；而它失的那个姑娘，正是把帝国引到它门前的线。

</details>

So we close episode twenty-two the way a great house closes a gate — by discovering that the wall was never meant to keep the maid in, but to keep the prince out. Zheng Manyi staged a solitude and smashed an orchid; she bought a troupe and drugged a girl; she staged a bed and was answered by a ghost; she seized the maid and lost her to the Duke's men; and Lin Jinqi, tracing a disappearance, met the deposed crown prince the court is hunting through every street. Episode twenty-three, when it comes, will carry the master across the river to find the maid the Duke sold away, and will show what a man will do when the girl he watches and the prince he hides pull him in opposite directions at once. But that is next week's tell, and the lanterns of the Lin estate, unaware, burn on.

于是我们如大宅合一门那样收束第二十二集——发现那墙从不曾为把丫鬟关里、而为把太子挡外。郑蔓宜摆了独处、摔了兰；买通戏班、下了丫鬟的药；摆了床、却被鬼应答；掳了丫鬟、却让她落进国公手下；而林锦岐，追一桩失踪，遇着朝廷正逐街搜的废太子。第二十三集，来的时候，会把主子带过江，去寻那被国公卖走的丫鬟，也会教：当一个男人守的姑娘、与他藏的太子，把他往相反方向同拽，他会怎样。但那是下周的讲法；而林府的灯，不知觉，烧着。
