Episode 25 opens in the messy hour after the wedding attack. The man who threw the dart is run down fast, and he is no lone madman. It is Zheng Baichuan, head of the same family that smashed an orchid and bought a maid's ruin, and this time his target was the throne itself. The attempt fails, because Lin Jinqi was already moving, and the Yongchang Marquis's men closed the net. Zheng Baichuan is taken alive, bound and silent, the head of a house that reached too high and grabbed a blade instead. With the assassin in hand and the wounded prince stable, the march north begins. Lin Jinqi and the Yongchang Marquis flank the future emperor, and the road to the capital becomes a guard detail for the realm's next ruler. The man who would be rid of the throne now faces the throne's full weight instead. The season's long threat, the Zheng ambition, is caught by the throat at last.

第二十五集在婚宴刺杀后那团乱里开场。扔镖的人很快被拿下，不是什么孤身的疯子。是郑百川——就是摔兰、买丫鬟身败名裂那一家的话事人，这回他的靶子是龙椅本身。图谋没成，因为林锦岐早动了，永昌侯的人也把网收了。郑百川活捉，捆着、沉默，一个伸手太高、却攥了把刀的家的头。刺客落手、受伤的殿下稳住了，北归的队伍便启程。林锦岐与永昌侯一左一右护着未来的天子，回京的路成了给下任国君的护驾。这一季缠了许久的祸根——郑家的野心，终于被人掐住了喉咙。

The capital receives its prince as a king. The Crown Prince, patched and pale from the dart but very much alive, is carried through the gates and onto the throne, and the court that doubted him bows. He is the new emperor now, and the men who brought him home are the men he will remember. Lin Jinqi, who cleared the stage and shielded the body, stands among the few the new ruler trusts without question. The Yongchang Marquis, old and steady, takes his place beside the throne as the kind of pillar a new reign needs. For the Zheng family, the math is brutal and simple. They aimed at the crown and missed, and a missed throw at a king is not a mistake. It is treason, and treason has one price. The city can feel the weather turning, and everyone who played a side starts counting what their side will cost.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

京城迎回的储君，是做了王的。太子中了镖，裹着、脸色白，却实实活着，被人抬进宫门、抬上龙椅，方才还疑他的朝堂，齐齐跪下。如今他是新帝，而把他护回来的人，是他会记一辈子的人。林锦岐，清了台、护了身的那个，立在少数新主不加半点疑的人里。永昌侯，老而稳，立到龙椅旁，做新朝要的那种柱石。对郑家，账又狠又直：他们瞄着冠、偏没中，而对着君王落空的这一掷，不是失手，是谋逆，谋逆只有一个价。满城觉出天色在转，押了边的人，都开始算自己的边要付多少。

Away from the throne room, a quieter story is reaching its own turning point. Han Liang, the decent young man who helped pull Lanxiang out of Yangzhou and bring her home, has made up his mind. He loves her. He has loved the sharp, steady maid since before the trouble started, and now that she is safe, he means to do the right thing by her. He plans to ask her to marry him, to give a girl who has been bought and sold and nearly ruined a proper, chosen life, a door she walks through of her own will. He practices the words in his head as he walks to find her, because a proposal from a good man is the one kind of trap Lanxiang would be glad to fall into. He does not know that another man, with a louder voice and a tighter grip, is about to speak first.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

离了朝堂，另一桩更静的事，也到了自己的拐弯。韩梁——那个帮着把兰香从扬州拽出、领回家的厚道后生——拿定了主意。他爱她。从祸起前就爱这个利落稳当的丫鬟，如今她安稳了，他想堂堂正正待她。他打算向她提亲，给一个被买被卖、险些毁了的姑娘，一条她自己愿意迈进去的、正经选出来的活路。他一面走着去找她，一面在心里练词，因为好男人这一求，是兰香甘愿落进去的唯一一种阱。他不知，另一个嗓门更响、手攥更紧的男人，要抢先开口了。

That other man is Lin Jinqi. Fresh from saving an emperor, he is not a person the new court can say no to, and he knows it. Before Han Liang can open his mouth, Lin Jinqi goes to the new emperor and asks for a gift that only a throne can give. He petitions for an imperial marriage decree, one that bestows Lanxiang on him as his wife. It is a stunning move, and he makes it look like gratitude owed, like a loyal servant cashing in a saved life for a personal wish. The new emperor, still owing Lin Jinqi his own, grants it without a second thought. A decree is written, sealed, and sent. Just like that, the maid who was once a borrowed name becomes the emperor's chosen bride for his most trusted man. The letter of the law lands on the Lin estate before Han Liang's question ever leaves his lips.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

那另一个，是林锦岐。刚救过皇帝，新朝没几个人驳得动他，他自己也知道。韩梁还没张嘴，林锦岐先去了新帝面前，讨一份只有龙椅给得出的礼。他请一道赐婚的圣旨，把兰香许给自己为妻。这一着惊，他却做得像欠他的谢、像忠仆拿救回的命换一桩私愿。新帝还欠着林锦岐这条命，想也不想就准了。旨写、盖印、发了下去。就这么，那个曾借名活着的丫鬟，成了皇帝给他最信之人亲点的新娘。那张律令落到林府，比韩梁的话出口还早。

Why does Lin Jinqi do it. The show leaves the question hanging, and the answer is probably not one clean thing. Part of him loves Lanxiang, the way a man loves the person who has stood at his side through every ugly turn. Part of him knows the court is a hungrier, more dangerous place now that he saved the throne, and a maid with no protection is a target for everyone who wants at him. Marrying her by imperial decree is the one lock no one can pick. Whatever the mix, the deed is done, and Lin Jinqi does not apologize for it. He has spent the whole story protecting this girl in pieces. Now he protects her all at once, with a sheet of yellow silk and the weight of the realm behind his name. He would rather be the one judged harshly than watch her judged by everyone else.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

林锦岐为何这么做。剧把问悬着，答案多半不是一件干净事。一半他爱兰香，一个男人爱那个陪他走过每一道丑弯的人；一半他清楚，救了龙椅后，朝堂更饿更险，一个没靠山的丫鬟，是冲他来的所有人的靶子。用赐婚娶她，是道谁也撬不开的锁。不管几样掺着，事已定，林锦岐不为它道歉。整部戏他零碎护着这姑娘，如今用一方黄绸、用江山压在他名后的分量，一次性把她护全。

For Han Liang, the decree is a door slammed in his face. He arrives to find Lanxiang already promised, not by her heart but by an emperor's pen, to the man he has quietly measured himself against this whole time. There is no scene, because Han Liang is not built for scenes. He grieves in the quiet way good men do, by stepping back and meaning it. He tells Lanxiang, perhaps, that he is happy for her, and means most of it, and fails at the small part he cannot mean. The two of them share a look that says everything and nothing, the look of two people who would have been happy and were not given the chance. Lin Jinqi, watching from a distance, knows exactly what he took, and he does not soften it. He has seen what happens to the slow, and he has decided he will not be one of them. Some wins are won by being faster, not kinder.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

对韩梁，这道旨是迎面砸上的门。他赶到，见兰香已被许出——不是她的心，是皇帝的笔——许给这一路他暗暗拿来量自己的那个男人。没有闹，因为韩梁不是闹的性子。他安安静静地伤，好男人那种伤法：退后、当真。他或许对兰香说，他替她高兴，大半是真，小半那点装不来的，他认栽。俩人对望一眼，什么都说了、又什么都没说——是两个本可欢喜、却没被给机会的人。林锦岐远远看着，清楚自己夺了什么，也不软。有些赢，靠的是快，不是善。

The decree does not land softly on everyone. The Xu family, Lanxiang's adoptive house, reacts with a mixture of shock and outrage. In their eyes, she is still the girl they took in, and a maid being lifted to a general's wife by imperial order is not the future they pictured, or perhaps they simply do not like being told. They begin to talk of resisting the decree, of refusing to hand her over, of using whatever family pride they have left to stand against the order. It is the kind of talk that feels brave in a closed room and fatal the moment it reaches the wrong ear. The Xu household, wounded in its pride, edges toward a cliff it does not yet see, because defying a decree is not a disagreement. The law does not care who you are once the seal is already dry. It is a crime against the throne.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

这道旨，不是落在谁身上都软。许家——兰香的养家——又惊又怒。在他们眼里，她还是他们收留的那丫头，一个丫鬟被圣旨抬成将军夫人，不是他们想见的将来，或许干脆就是不喜欢被人安排。他们起念抗旨，不交人，拿剩的那点家傲去顶那道令。这种话，关起门说觉着勇，一出错的耳就是催命。许家伤着傲，正往一道它还没看见的崖边蹭——因为抗旨不是异议，是逆龙椅的罪。

Into that closed room walks Mr. Lu, the family's steady advisor, and he shuts the talk down before it becomes a sentence. He lays the truth on the table with no sugar. To defy an imperial marriage decree is to invite the extermination of the clan, the wiping out of every branch, the old and the young together, for the sin of refusing the throne. A royal decree is not a suggestion a family debates. Once the emperor's seal is on it, it is the law of the realm, and the law of the realm does not negotiate with a household's hurt feelings. Mr. Lu's words land like a bucket of cold water, because they are not a warning. They are a description of what will happen, step by step, if they open that door. The room goes quiet, the kind of quiet that follows the pulling back of a hand from a flame.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

进那道关门的，是陆先生，府里那个稳顾问，话没变成判词前先把它掐了。他把真相原样摆上桌，不加糖。抗一道赐婚圣旨，招的是灭门——一枝一枝全抹，老的少的，一起，为拒天子的罪。皇家赐婚不是一家商量着办的由头。皇帝的印一落，便是江山之法，而江山之法，不和一家受了伤的面子讨价。陆先生的话像一桶冷水，因为那不是警告，是一步步会发生的实写，只要他们开了那道门。屋里静了，是手从火苗上缩回后，那一种静。

So the Xu family backs down, and the decree stands, final and unmovable. Lanxiang will marry Lin Jinqi because an emperor said so, and no amount of family feeling can unwrite the seal. It is a strange kind of certainty. The girl who was once property, sold and traded and nearly broken, is now bound to a man by the highest authority in the land, and that authority, cruel as it sounds, is also the thing that makes her safe. Mr. Lu's intervention did not change her fate. It only kept her family from dying to protest it. The season has a way of trading one cage for another and calling it fortune, and Lanxiang, who has survived worse, receives this cage with her eyes open and her chin level. She has learned that survival is its own kind of winning, even when the win looks like a lock. She does not thank the emperor for it, but she does not weep either, because weeping was spent on harder things long ago.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

于是许家退了，旨立着，终局、挪不动。兰香要嫁林锦岐，因为皇帝这么说了，再多家情也抹不掉那印。这是一种古怪的定。那个曾被当货、被卖被易、险些碎了的姑娘，如今被天下最高的权绑给一个男人，而那权，听着狠，也恰是护她那道。陆先生拦的不是她的命，只是拦她家为抗议去死。这戏总爱拿一个笼换另一个笼，叫它福气；而兰香，熬过更糟的，睁着眼、下颌平着，接了这个笼。

So episode 25 is a story of thrones and decrees. Zheng Baichuan reached for a king and caught a death sentence instead, while the prince he stabbed rode home to become the emperor. Lin Jinqi traded a saved life for a wife, beating a good man to the words by the width of a decree. The Xu family raged and was talked down from the cliff by a calm advisor who simply told them the truth. Episode 26 will take us inside the Lin mansion on the day the marriage becomes real, and show what the old master does when his son brings home a bride who was born a maid. But that is next time's telling. For now, remember this. In a kingdom, the pen that makes a marriage can also unmake a family, and the people who love you are sometimes the ones who must watch the emperor choose for you.

于是第二十五集，是龙椅与圣旨的故事。郑百川伸手够王、却抓回一道死罪，而他刺的那个储君，骑马回京做了帝；林锦岐拿救回的命换回一个妻，比一个好男人抢先半道旨，到了嘴边的话；许家怒了，被一个冷静顾问从崖边劝回，那人只把实话告诉他们。第二十六集带我们进林府，到婚成那日，看老太爷见儿子领回一个本生来是丫鬟的媳妇，怎样发作。但那是下回的讲法。此刻你记一句：在一国里，写就一桩婚的笔，也能写灭一家；而爱你的人，有时正是得眼睁睁看皇帝替你挑的那拨。
