# 兰香如故 — The Fragrance of Orchids Remains
## Episode 27 · Told Like A Story

Let me tell you about the twenty-seventh episode, because this is the one where the decree becomes a door, and the girl who read it as a chain is walked through it in red and gold, with a slap waiting at the far end for the man who built it. The wedding morning finds Lanxiang in the small house she loves, bidding her parents a farewell she does not want to make. Xu Wanquan and Lady Xue have raised her as their own, and the daughter they send to the Lin estate today is not the bondservant the house remembers but a bride the emperor named — yet to them she is simply the girl they must let go, and the leaving sits heavy on all three. The season lingers on the threshold, because the door she walks through now was the one her true family was once refused at, and the girl who closes it behind her carries a name the Lin estate turned away years before she ever wore it.

The procession that carries her toward the marquis's gate meets, halfway down the city, another procession coming the other way — Han Liang's, and his is a procession of triumph rather than of bride-price. The scholar who would have asked for her hand has instead gone to the examinations and placed first among all candidates, and the streets that part for Lanxiang's red curtains part also for his rank, the two processions passing within a breath of each other. He does not stop; she does not step down; but the season marks the meeting as the hinge of the episode: the man who loved her freely, crowned by his own merit, and the woman bound to another by the throne's command, crossing in the same hour, each at the height of a fate the other could not share. The drums of his victory and the flutes of her wedding sound in the same air, and neither player hears the other's song.

抬她往侯门的那队，在城半道，迎面撞上另一队——韩梁的，而他的队，是喜庆而非聘礼。那个本要求她手的生，去了考场，中了所有考生里的第一；为兰香红帘让开的街，也为他的衔让开，两队列得近在一息之间。他不停；她不下轿；可这季把这次相遇记作这集的铰链：那个自由爱她、以己才加冠的男，与那个被御旨绑给另一人的女，同个时辰交错，各自在对方分不到的命的顶上。他捷的鼓与她婚的笛，响在同一片空气里，而两厢的乐师，都听不见对方的曲。

At the Lin estate gate the rite begins, and Lanxiang meets it the way she has met every bond the house put on her — with a stillness that is also a refusal. When the moment comes to bow in the mutual ceremony that makes her a wife before heaven and the ancestors, she will not bend; she stands in her bridal red and withholds the obeisance the hall expects, the one act left to her that no edict can force. The happy matron, hired to see the rite completed and paid to brook no delay, lays a hand to her back and presses her down by main force, so that the bow is made with a stranger's strength and not the bride's will. The season watches the courtyard fall silent at the sight — a marriage the throne commanded, sealed by a servant's hand upon the bride's spine, the empire's law and a maid's pride meeting in a single unwilling bend.

林府门前，礼起；兰香接它，如接这府加给她每道契——以一种也是拒的静。到了那要在天地祖宗前把她拜成妻的对拜之礼，她不肯弯；她立在她新娘红里，扣住满堂要的拜，这是剩给她的、无旨能强的最后一样。喜婆，雇来见礼成、拿了不肯误的酬，伸手按她背、以蛮力压她下去，于是那一拜，是凭一个外人的力、非凭新娘的意成的。这季看满院在这景象前静下——一道御座命的婚，由一个仆的手按在新娘脊上封了，天下的律与一个婢的傲，在一记不肯的弯里相碰。

The bridal chamber is where the withheld blow finally falls. When the veil is lifted from her face, Lanxiang does not smile or lower her eyes as a new wife should; she raises her hand and strikes Lin Jinqi across the cheek, hard and in full view of the chamber, a slap that rings louder than the small jokes the watchers had prepared. And as she strikes she speaks the words her mother spoke years ago, when the Lin estate refused the Shen family's match and broke a girl's first hope: "this is going too far." The season holds the line — the same phrase, carried from a mother's humiliation to a daughter's defiance, turns in Lanxiang's mouth from a complaint into a verdict. The marquis who forced the bond hears, in his bride's cold voice, the echo of the refusal his own house began, and the slap lands not on his face alone but on the whole proud line that started this.

新娘房，是那扣住的掌终于落下处。盖头从她脸掀起，兰香不笑、不低眼如新妇该的；她抬手，当着满房人的面，狠狠掴了林锦岐一记，响声盖过看客备下的那些小笑。而她动手时，吐出的是她母亲多年前的话——那当林府拒了沈家的亲、碎了一个姑娘头一盼时说的——"欺人太甚"。这季按住这句：同一句话，从母亲的辱，传到女儿的拒，在兰香口中由一句怨，变成了判。那强了契的侯，在他新娘冷声里，听见了自己家开的那个拒的回响；而那一掴，不止落他脸上，也落在这整条开了头的傲脉上。

Lin Jinqi does not raise his hand in return. He takes the blow and the words with a bitter smile, the kind a man wears when he knows the strike is earned and the giver has every right to aim it. The season shows him, for once, without the armor of command — a groom struck by his bride on their wedding night, bearing it because he asked for the marriage that made the strike possible. Senior Lady Lin, watching from the threshold, says what the house has not said aloud: that her son brought this forced wedding on himself, that the bond was his petition and his alone, and that the girl's fury is the fruit of a tree he planted. The old lady's admission is the rare moment the Lin estate speaks the truth about its own, and Lin Jinqi, still smiling through the mark on his cheek, does not contradict her. The household, which expected a tame bride, meets instead a wife who came to the chamber already wronged.

林锦岐没有回手。他把那一击与那话，以一抹苦笑受了——那种一个知道自己活该挨、而动手的人全有权瞄的人，才有的笑。这季这回显他，卸了指令的甲——一个在新婚夜被新娘掴的郎，受着它，因这婚是他自己讨的、才让这一击可能。林母，立在门槛上看，说了这府还没说出口的：她儿子这强婚是自找，这契是他一人请的，而姑娘的怒，是他自己种下的树结的果。老夫人的认，是林府头回对自己人讲实话的稀刻；林锦岐，颊上印还在，仍苦笑着，不驳她。那本预期一个驯媳的府，迎来的，却是个带着已受的冤来的妻。

The night settles, and the marriage bed becomes the last field of the bride's quiet war. Lanxiang will not share the master's couch; she withdraws to the warm chamber, the smaller heated room kept for the cold months, and there she means to pass the wedding night alone, a bride by decree and a stranger by choice. The Lin estate, which has never learned to read her, takes the withdrawal for sulking and leaves her to it, but the season knows better: the girl who survived poison and treason and a sealed house is not pouting, she is keeping the one boundary the emperor's command could not cross. The warm chamber holds her the way the small house once did, and for a few hours the bride of a marquis is simply a woman alone with her own unhappiness, the red of her gown the only thing the house managed to put upon her.

夜定了，婚床成了新娘静战的最后一处。兰香不肯共主榻；她退入暖阁——那为寒月备的小暖屋——要在那里独过新婚夜，一个凭旨的 bride、一个凭选的陌生人。那从不学会读她的林府，把这退当作使性，由她去；可这季知道得更清：那个从毒、逆、封宅里活下来的姑娘，不是在闹，是在守那道御旨跨不过的界。暖阁容她，如小屋曾容过；有几个时辰，一个侯夫人，只是个与自己的不快独处的妇，她身上红，是这府唯一成功披上她的东西。

Lin Jinqi will not leave her there. Past the hour when the household has turned in, he comes to the warm chamber against the advice of every servant who fears the lady's temper, and without a word he lifts Lanxiang and carries her back to the main bed — the marriage bed the rite meant for them both. She does not struggle loud enough to wake the court; he does not explain. He lays her down as gently as the blow she gave him allows, and then he withdraws, taking the warm chamber for himself, so that the bride sleeps in the place the law made hers and the groom in the place she abandoned. The season marks the strange tenderness of it: the man who forced the marriage will not force the night, and the girl who slapped him is given, by the very hand she struck, the larger bed and the smaller peace. The threshold that divided them at the rite is crossed now by his own feet, in silence.

林锦岐不肯留她那里。过了府中人皆安歇的时辰，他不顾每个怕夫人脾气的仆的劝，来到暖阁，一言不发，抱起兰香，把她送回主榻——那礼本为二人备的婚床。她没闹到惊动满堂；他也没解释。他放她下，尽量轻，以她给他的那击所许的轻；然后他退开，自取暖阁，于是新娘睡在律给她备的处，郎睡在她弃的处。这季记那奇的柔：那强了婚的人，不肯强这一夜；而掴了他的姑娘，被她掴过的那只手，给了大的床与小的安。那在礼上分了二人的门槛，此刻由他自己的脚，无声跨过。

So the episode turns, at its quiet center, on the phrase the bride flung — "this is going too far" — because those are not her words alone but her mother's, carried like an heirloom from the day the Lin estate sent the Shen family away. Years before Lanxiang wore the name Xu, the Shen house sought a match with this same family and was refused, and the mother who loved her then spoke those very words at the shutting of that door. The season, which keeps such debts, lets the daughter repay them now with an open hand: the house that once deemed the Shen blood unfit to marry in is the house that kidnapped the daughter into the bond, and the slap is the refusal returned, generations late. Lin Jinqi, who petitioned the throne to win what his family once denied, receives the old refusal upon his own cheek, and the circle the Lin estate started closes at last upon itself.

于是这集，在它静的中心，绕新娘掷出的那句转——"欺人太甚"——因那不单是她的话，是她母亲的，如一件传家物，从林府送沈家走那日带着。早在兰香顶"许"姓之前，沈家向这同姓求过亲、被拒，而那时爱她的母，在那门合上时，说的正是这句。这季，守着这样的债，让女儿如今以一只摊开的手还了：那曾以为沈血不配娶进的府，正是那把女儿诓进这契的府；而那一掴，是迟了几代的拒的回还。林锦岐，趋廷讨来他家曾拒的东西，在自己颊上，受了那旧的拒；而林府开的那个圈，终在自己身上合了。

So we close the twenty-seventh episode the way a wedding closes a door — by carrying the bride over the threshold she did not choose, and letting her slam the smaller one behind her. Lanxiang said her farewell and met Han Liang's triumph on the road; she withheld the bow until a stranger's hand forced it; she slapped the groom with her mother's old words and slept alone until he carried her to the bed and withdrew. The Lin estate got the marriage the emperor commanded and the wife it had not reckoned on, and Lin Jinqi got the bond he petitioned for and the blow that came with it. Episode twenty-eight will follow the new bride into the morning after, where the household expects a daughter-in-law and meets instead a woman who wears the marquis's name the way she wears the red — because the decree put it there, not because her heart did. For now, remember only this: the slap she dealt was her mother's words come home, and the door the Shen family was refused is the door she walked through to haunt it.
