# 兰香如故 — The Fragrance of Orchids Remains
## Episode 17 · Told Like a Story

Let me tell you about episode seventeen, because this is the episode where the sickness stops being a mystery and becomes a hand — a hand that held a ladle, and a hand that now holds a token. It opens not in the sickroom but in the bathhouse, where the third daughter, still awake past the lamp's hour, pulls Lanxiang aside and whispers what her own eyes caught: Du Cuique's maid, Lingdang, slipping into the bathing rooms not once but three nights running, when no business of a guest's girl should take her near the water at all. The third daughter says it plainly — the girl was not washing, she was watching the barrel, the way a cook watches a pot she means to salt, the way a thief watches a lock she means to pick. It is the thread Lanxiang needed, the loose end of last week's water, and she takes it without a word of thanks, because gratitude is a thing for peacetime and this is not peace, this is a house with a poisoner inside its walls.

让我跟你讲讲第十七集，因为这一集，病不再是谜，而成了一只手——一只执勺的手，和一只如今执令的手。它不开在病房，开在澡房：三小姐，过了灯时还醒着，把兰香拉到一边，低语自己眼见：杜翠雀的丫鬟铃铛，溜进澡房，不是一回，是连着三晚，而客来之女，本无半点事该近那水。三小姐说得平白——那丫鬟不是洗，是看那桶，如厨看她要下盐的锅，如贼看她要挑的锁。这正是兰香要的线，上周那水的松头，她接了，没道谢，因谢是太平时的礼，而这不是太平，这是墙里有个投毒人的府。

Lanxiang takes the thread to Gantang, and the two of them lay the sick against the water like pieces on a board. Yuchan fell ill — she drew from the barrel the night the rash began. The fourth daughter fell ill — same barrel, same night. Lady Zhou, the second master's concubine, who asked for the last of the hot water to wash an ache from her back, fell ill by morning. The ones who did not draw from that barrel — the Senior Lady, who bathes at dawn from a separate cistern, and Lanxiang herself, who had no part in it — stayed whole. The pattern is too clean for a curse. It is a poison, placed in one barrel, meant for a house already thin from the river and the trial, and Gantang, who keeps the accounts, says only that the barrel was drawn by a hand no one watched, and that a hand no one watches is a hand that means to be paid.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

兰香把线带去给甘棠，两人把病人对水，如摆子于盘。玉蝉病——病起那夜她从那桶汲。四小姐病——同桶同夜。二爷的妾周姨娘，讨了最后的热水去洗背上的酸，晨间也病。没从那桶汲的——林母，她黎明从另个池洗；和兰香自己，没沾半分——都无恙。这形太净，不似咒。是毒，下在一桶，意在已经因江与审而薄的府；而甘棠，管账的，只说：那桶是被一只无人看的手汲的，而一只无人看的手，是只要讨偿的手。

</details>

The hand that placed it moves to cover it. Before the second day is out, Lingdang is found floating in the old well, her face calm in a way the living are not, a silence bought with a life and a weight that settled the water to still. Lanxiang stands at the well's lip and reads the message in the surface: the poisoner does not deny the deed, she only cuts the tongue that might name her. It is, in its way, a confession — clumsy, bloody, and exactly what Lanxiang needed to turn a suspicion into a certainty she can carry to the door. She does not weep for the maid; in this house the dead are too often the proof, and she has learned, over sixteen episodes, to read the body as the traitor reads the room, and to let grief wait until the house is safe. Lanxiang notes that a poisoner who covers her trail with a body has already confessed, and files the confession the way she files everything that may yet bite, because a proof the house wants gone is the proof most worth keeping.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

下毒的手，去掩它。第二日未过，铃铛被发现浮在旧井，脸静得活人不会静，是用一条命买来的静、与一重压沉了水至止。兰香立井沿，从水面读出话：投毒人不否这桩，只割了那能指她名的舌。这，以它的方式，是供——笨、血淋淋，却正是兰香要把疑变成能带去门前的确所需。她不为那丫鬟落泪；在这府，死者常是证，而她过了十六集，学了以尸读如叛者读屋，且让悲等府安了再说。

</details>

She goes to Lin Jinqi's quarters, empty since he rode out, and finds the bath water drawn for him and left untouched — the master was taken from the residence before he could use it. She dips a cloth, breathes the sour edge no herb should have, and knows. She does not drink; she is not foolish, only certain, the way a reader is certain of a forgery by a smell no copy can carry. That night she writes two lines and gives them to Nankè, who slips the sealed house by the gate the watchers trust him at: one line to the master at the river road — the sickness is the Dai family's cousin, a poison in the water, hold the marquis from the well — and one line to the Senior Lady, that the barrel is the source and the maid who watched it is dead. The house, for the first time, has a name for its fear, and a name is the first cut of any cure.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

她去林锦岐的屋，自他出骑便空着，见为他汲的浴水原样未动——主子被从府里提走，不及用。她蘸布，嗅那任何药不该有的酸边，懂了。她不喝；她不蠢，只是确，如读者凭一股任何摹本带不来的气味认出赝。那夜她写两行，交南客，南客从守人信他的门溜出封府：一行给江路上的主子——病是戴家的亲，水里的毒，莫让侯近井；一行给林母，桶是源，看桶的丫鬟已死。这府，头回为它的怕，有了名，而名是任一药的头一刀。

</details>

The Senior Lady, reading the line, looks about her sealed residence and sees what the sickness has revealed: her daughters faint or foolish, her old stewards slowed by age, her trusted women either sick or useless at the hinge of crisis. Only one hand has moved sure through the days — the maid who read the water, blocked the poisoner, and carried the word out and back. So the old lady does the unthinkable for a great house: she lifts the steward's token from her belt — the carved wood that means a woman may speak for the Lin household — and presses it into Lanxiang's palm. Not as a gift. As a debt, and a charge, and the plain truth that the house now runs through a girl it once counted as furniture, the way a clock runs through a spring it never thought to name. The old lady, who has managed men her whole life, sees at last a woman she cannot manage, only trust, and the trust settles in her palm like the token she has not yet given but already means to.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

林母读那行，环视封起的府，见了病露出的：女儿们或昏或蠢，老管事被年岁拖慢，信得过的妇人，要么病、要么在关口无用。只有一只手，连日稳稳在动——那读水、挡投毒人、把话带出带回的丫鬟。于是老夫人做了大宅难为之事：她从带上解下管家令——那块雕木，意即一妇可代林府开口——按进兰香掌心。不是赠。是债、是责，也是一句平白：这府如今，过一只它曾当作家具的姑娘，如一座钟过一根它从未想命名的簧。

</details>

Lanxiang closes her hand on the token and walks to Yuchan's pallet, where the seasoned maid burns but listens. She tells Yuchan the plan, because the maid who has been the master's hand deserves to be the first to know the hand that answers, and because a secret carried alone is a heavier thing than a secret carried by two who have earned each other. Then she stands at the window and looks across the sealed court to Du Cuique's rooms, where the guest who would rule the house sits smiling, certain the poisoner's secret died with the well. Lanxiang does not smile back. She has spent the season protecting; this week she decides to answer, and the answer, she has learned from the woman who taught her, is best served warm, the way a traitor serves a poison she believes is a cure. The answer, she has decided, will not wait for the poisoner to slip; it will be served before the cup is even set down, warm as the traitor's own hand would set it.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

兰香握令，走到玉蝉榻边，这老练的丫鬟烧着，却听着。她把计告诉玉蝉，因曾为主子之手的丫鬟，该头个知那应答的手，也因一个独担的秘，重于两个挣得彼此的人同担的秘。而后她立窗前，望过封院，到杜翠雀的屋——那要掌府的客坐着笑，确信投毒人的秘随井死了。兰香不回笑。她这季在护；这周她定下应答，而答，她从教她的人那里学了，最好趁热奉上，如一个叛者奉她信是药的毒。

</details>

She sends Gantang to the bathing rooms at the hour no one watches, to draw from the poisoned barrel and set it to heat, not as a bath but as a brew. The token in her palm opens the door the old lady would never have opened for a maid; the kitchen gives the water because the steward's hand told them to, and the steward's hand is the only order the sealed house now obeys without question. They steep it as one steeps a bitter cure, pour it into the fine cup Du Cuique favors, and Gantang carries it to the guest's rooms with a face of plain concern — the second daughter is worse, she says, and the lady who knows herbs should taste the draught meant to draw the fire. It is the traitor's own logic, returned whole: a poison in the water, served as a cure, by the hand that read the water first.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

她遣甘棠，在无人看的时辰去澡房，从毒桶汲水、架起煮，不是浴，是煎。掌心的令，开了老夫人绝不会为丫鬟开的门；厨房给那水，因管家之手吩咐，而管家之手是这封府如今无疑唯从的令。他们如煎苦药般煎它，倾入杜翠雀爱的细杯，甘棠端去客屋，一脸平白的忧——二小姐更重了，她说，而识药的娘子该尝那意欲拔火的汤。这是叛者自己的理，全数奉还：水里的毒，作药奉上，由那只先读水的手。

</details>

Du Cuique drinks, because a woman who poisons believes herself safe from poison, and within the hour the fire she set walks into her own blood. The sealed house, which had feared a curse, watches its source named: the guest who offered to heal is the hand that sickened, and the maid who was furniture is the steward who caught her. The Senior Lady, token already given, needs no second proof; she sets watchers at Du Cuique's door as she once set them at the residence's, and the two clever girls who faced each other without a screen now face the truth the screen was meant to hide. Lanxiang does not gloat. She only thinks of Yuchan, burning, and of the well, and turns back to the sick, because a steward's first duty is the living, and the living are what the poison came to take. A steward's first duty, she tells Gantang later, is the living, and the living are exactly the ones the draught was brewed to steal from the house, the ones she means to keep.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

杜翠雀饮了，因一个投毒的人，信自己免于毒；不一时，她点的火，走入她自己的血。这封起的府，曾怕咒，如今见它的源被指名：那请来医人的客，是使病那只手；而曾作家具的丫鬟，是捉她的管家。林母，令已给，不需第二证；她如曾对府门那样，在杜翠雀门前设守；两聪明姑娘，无屏相对，如今对面那屏本要藏的真。兰香不骄。她只想着烧着的玉蝉、想着井，转身回病者身边，因管家的头责是活人，而活人，是那毒来取的。

</details>

So we close episode seventeen the way a poison closes a circle — by returning to the hand that poured it. The third daughter watched the bathhouse; Gantang read the accounts; Lingdang's silence named the poisoner; Lanxiang carried the word and turned it into a token and a token into a cure that bit. The house is still sealed, the sickness still walks, but the war inside the walls has turned, and the maid who once only read the lie now answers it with a cup. Episode eighteen, when it comes, will carry the treason from the hills into the open, and the brother the third daughter named will answer for his paper with his life. But that is next week's tell. For now, remember only this: in the Lin estate, the poisoner's own draught is the only confession the poisoned ever need, and a confession served warm is a confession that bites. The draught served warm did what no sword could — it named the poisoner in her own cup, and the house, for the first time, believed the maid who read the water, the maid it had counted as furniture until the sickness taught it otherwise.
