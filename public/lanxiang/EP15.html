Let me tell you about episode fifteen, because this is the episode where the youngest daughter learns, without a word of teaching, the one thing the whole season has been trying to show her: that a girl who decides for herself is a girl no gate can lock out. The Lin residence, still glowing from the river victory, settles into a rare calm, and in that calm the third daughter — the one who, episodes ago, clung to birth and rank while her second sister chose love — goes out to the hills behind the estate to gather the cooling herbs the household brews against the summer sickliness. She takes a maid and a basket and the particular confidence of a girl who has never once been refused, and she walks into a cave where the good moss grows, certain that the world will wait for her the way it always has.
🇨🇳 中文翻译

让我跟你讲讲第十五集，因为这一集，是最幼的女儿未受一字之教、却学了整季想教她的那一件事：一个为自己做主的姑娘，是再没有门能锁住的姑娘。林府还因江上的胜泛着光，落进难得的静；而在那静里，三小姐——那个几集前还攥着出身门第、而她二姐已选了爱的人——出府到后山采那府里防暑病的凉草。她带一个丫鬟、一只篮，和一种从未被拒过的姑娘特有的笃定，走进生好苔的洞，确信这世界会如常等她。

What waits in the cave is not moss but a man. Du Zheng — the plain soldier the second daughter has just chosen, and the brother of the girl who means to rule this house — has been using the hills to meet a contact, and the contact is not what the household believes. The third daughter stumbles on them mid-meeting, and in the half-light she sees enough: a folded paper passed hand to hand, a name spoken that should never be spoken in a loyal man's company, the easy intimacy of treason. She is a child of the inner court; she may cling to rank, but she knows a betrayal when she watches one form. She understands, with a clarity that costs her her breath, that the brother of the woman her sister now loves is the thread still loose in the master's cleared name — and that if she runs back silent, the house will be undone a second time by a man it fed.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

洞里等的不是苔，是一个人。杜正——二小姐刚选的那个平实兵，而要掌这府的姑娘的兄——一直用后山会一个联系人，而那联系人，不是府里以为的。三小姐撞见他们正会到一半，在半光里她看够了：一手递一手的折纸、一个绝不该在忠臣伴里说出的名、叛的熟稔。她是内宅的孩子；她或攥门第，却看得出背叛成形。她以费她呼吸的清明懂了：她姐如今爱的那个人的兄，是主子洗清的名里仍松着的那缕线——而她若悄然跑回，这府要被它养过的人，第二次毁。

And here the season's teaching lands. The third daughter does not faint, does not scream for a maid, does not wait for a brother to save her — the moves the old rules taught her. She remembers, though no one told her to, the girl she has been watching all season: Lanxiang, who read the flower, walked the den, sang the general back from the river. She remembers that a maid with no rank did all of it by deciding, once, to act. So the third daughter decides. She does not run. She steps into the light, names Du Zheng aloud by his crime, and tells the contact, flatly, that she is the Lin family's daughter and that the paper he holds is now a confession she will carry out of this cave. It is the first time in her life she has spoken as a person and not as a rank, and the cave, which expected a frightened child, gets instead a girl who has finally learned the lesson the season was trying to teach. She does not yet know what she will do with the knowledge, only that she now owns it, the way a child who finds a key owns the door it might open, whether or not she is ready to turn it.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

而这一季的课，落下了。三小姐不昏、不呼丫鬟、不等兄来救——那些旧规教的着。她记起，虽无人教，这一季她一直看的那个姑娘：兰香，读花、踏窝、把将唱回江。她记起一个无阶的丫鬟，凭一次"自己定下动手"，做尽了那些。于是三小姐定了。她不跑。她步入光，直呼杜正其罪，并平平告诉那联系人：她是林家女，而他手里的纸，如今是她要带出这洞的供。这是她生来头回以"人"而非"阶"开口；而那本以为是个怕孩的洞，得的，是一个终于学了这季想教的课的姑娘。

Du Zheng, cornered by the one person he thought least dangerous, does the telling thing: he does not deny it. He laughs, softly, and says only that the little miss has sharp eyes and a sharper tongue, and that she should run home and tell her brother before the brother learns it from someone less kind. It is a threat dressed as a warning, and the third daughter, who has just found her voice, hears the threat under the warmth. She leaves the cave with the paper — Du Zheng lets her, because a child with a story is less dangerous than a child who dies in a hill — but she leaves changed, understanding that the love her sister chose has a brother who would trade the house for his own end. She climbs down the hill not as the girl who went up, but as the first of the Lin daughters to look a traitor in the face and walk away with his secret.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

杜正，被他最不当回事的人逼到角，做了那露底的事：他不否认。他轻笑，只说小小姐眼利、舌更利，且趁兄从不如他温和的人那里听见前，跑回家告诉她兄。那是裹着警告的威胁，而刚寻回声的三小姐，听出暖下的威胁。她带纸下了洞——杜正放她，因一个带故事的孩，险过一个死在山里的孩——可她下了，变了，懂了：她姐选的爱，有个会拿这府换自己终的兄。她下山，不是上去的那个姑娘，而是林家女儿里头一个，直面叛者、带他的秘走开的人。

Back at the residence, the air is still calm, and no one yet knows what the third daughter carries. She goes first, not to her mother or her brother, but to Lanxiang — because the season has, without ceremony, made the maid the person the daughters trust with the truths that would break the house. She shows the paper, tells the cave, and asks the only question that matters: do I give this to my brother, and watch my sister's heart break, or do I hide it, and watch the house's throat stay open? Lanxiang, who has spent fifteen episodes learning that every truth has a cost, does not decide for her. She says only that the master reads people, and that a secret this size, once seen by him, becomes a weapon he will aim — and that the third daughter must choose which war she wants to start. The girl who learned courage from watching now learns the harder half: that deciding what to do with the truth is its own kind of bravery. Lanxiang, watching the daughter weigh the two wars, sees her own younger self in the girl's face — the self that chose, once, to read a flower and so stepped onto a road with no map — and she lets the likeness stand, saying nothing more, because some lessons are only ever learned by holding the weight yourself.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

回府，空气仍静，无人知三小姐揣着什么。她先去的，不是娘、不是兄，而是兰香——因这季不声不响，把丫鬟成了女儿们肯托"会碎府的真相"的人。她示纸、说洞，只问要紧的那句：我把这交兄、看姐心碎，还是我藏了、看府的喉仍开着？兰香，用十五集学了每桩真都有价，不为她定。她只说：主子读人，这样大的秘，一旦入他眼，便成他要瞄的器——而三小姐须选她要开哪场战。那从"看"学了勇的姑娘，如今学了更难的一半：决断拿真相怎么办，是另一种勇。

And Du Cuique, watching from the rooms she means to rule, sees the third daughter return changed and Lanxiang receive her — and the new girl understands, at last, the full shape of the enemy she faces. It is not the master. It is not the old ladies. It is a maid who reads, who walked, who sang, and who now sits, trusted, at the center of the daughters' confidences. Du Cuique has won every surface battle — the flower, the favor, the brother placed at the sister's side — but she has not touched the one person who keeps undoing her designs. She smiles, as she always does, and begins, quietly, to plan the move against Lanxiang herself. The season's two clever girls now face each other directly, and the house, calm for one episode, does not yet know the war has moved to its lowest and most dangerous floor.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

而杜翠雀，在她要掌的屋里看，见三小姐回来变了、兰香接了她——这新来的姑娘，终于懂了她面对的敌的全形。不是主子。不是老夫人。是一个读、踏、唱、如今坐着被女儿们托以秘、在中心的丫鬟。杜翠雀赢了每一场面上的战——花、宠、把兄安在姐侧——却没碰过那个总拆她局的人。她如常笑了，悄悄起手，筹划对兰香自己那一着。这季两个聪明的姑娘，如今正面相对；而府，静了一集，还不知战已移到它最低、最险的那层。

The strain first shows at the family table. That evening the Senior Lady Lin, recovered and eager to mend what the river victory began, bids her daughters and the favored Du Zheng to sup together — blind to the current running beneath the rice wine. The second daughter, still bright with the love she chose, laughs at the plain soldier's wit; the third daughter sits silent, her eyes on her bowl, the folded paper a weight against her thigh, knowing the man her sister adores is the traitor she named in a cave. No one at the board reads the room but Lanxiang, serving at the screen, who sees the youngest smile with her mouth and not her eyes, and files the evening as the hour the household first sat down to eat its own secret. And Du Cuique, seated where the Senior Lady placed her, watches the silence between the two sisters and smiles the smile of a player who has just seen her opponent's hand — for she too now knows that the house's peace rests on a paper one girl is afraid to show.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

那紧绷先显在家的桌上。那晚林母，好了、又急着补江上之胜开头的事，请女儿们与得宠的杜正同席——对米酒下流着的暗流毫无所觉。二小姐还亮着她选的爱，笑那平实兵的趣；三小姐静坐，眼在碗上，折纸压着大腿，知她姐爱着的那个，是她在洞里点名的叛者。席上无人读那屋，只有屏边当值的兰香，见最幼的以嘴笑、不以眼笑，把那晚记为这府头回坐下来吃自己的秘。而杜翠雀，坐在林母给她安置的处，看两姐妹间的静，笑了那类“看见对家手牌”的玩家的笑——因她也如今知道，这府的安，系在一张一个姑娘不敢示出的纸上。

So we close episode fifteen the way a season closes a chapter — not with a victory, but with a line drawn that everyone can feel and no one has yet crossed. The youngest daughter walked into a cave a child and came out a witness. The traitor's brother is named, and the secret waits in a girl's hand for the war she will choose. And the two clever women of the house — one a maid, one a guest — now turn to face each other, and the next move is theirs. Episode sixteen, when it comes, will be the season's reckoning; but that is next week's tell. For now, remember only this: in the Lin estate, courage is catching, and the girl who catches it first may be the one no one was watching. And the cave that expected a child got, instead, the house's future — walking out, unharmed, with the truth.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

于是我们如一季收一章那样收束第十五集——不是以胜，是以一条人人感到、却无人跨过的线。最幼的女儿，进洞是孩子、出洞是见证。叛者的兄被点名，秘在姑娘手里，等她要开的战。而府里两个聪明的妇人——一个丫鬟、一个客——如今转面相对，下一着是她们的。第十六集，来的时候，是这季的清算；但那是下周的讲法。此刻你只记一句：在林府，勇会传染，而先染上的，或许是没人正看着的那个。而那本以为是个孩的洞，得的，是府的未来——无伤走出，带着真。
