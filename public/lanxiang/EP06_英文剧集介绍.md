# 兰香如故 — The Fragrance of Orchids Remains
## Episode 6 · Told Like a Story

Let me tell you about episode six, because this is the episode where the girl who has been pretending to be nobody starts, very quietly, to become someone the house cannot help but notice — and where a marriage that was already only paper begins, at last, to tear at the edges. Up to now Lanxiang has survived by being invisible: a third-class maid who boiled water by the stove and asked for nothing. Episode six is where she is pulled, step by reluctant step, out into the light, whether she wants it or not.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

让我跟你讲讲第六集，因为这一集，是那个一直装作"无足轻重"的姑娘，悄悄开始变成"这宅子没法不注意到"的人的一集——也是一段本就名存实亡的婚姻，终于开始从边缘撕裂的一集。至此，兰香靠着"隐身"活了下来：一个在炉边烧水、别无企求的三等丫鬟。第六集，是她一步一迟疑，被拽出暗处、拽进光里的一集，由不得她愿不愿意。

</details>

It begins with Xiaolian. The girl they call Xiaolian — the one who has become Lanxiang's closest friend among the maids — corners her one evening after the lamps are lit, and says the thing she has been holding in all this time. She does not want to be the general's concubine. She does not want to be anyone's kept body in a back room, bought and set aside. She wants, plainly and almost foolishly, to marry — to be a wife, properly, to a man who is not her master and who does not own her name. Lanxiang listens. She has taught herself not to flinch at anything the house throws at her, but this flinches her anyway, because it is the one kind of hope the house has spent six years teaching her to bury.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

先从杜小莲说起。那个被称作小莲的姑娘——在众丫鬟里成了兰香最亲近姐妹的那一个——某天灯点起后把人拦下，说出了压在心底许久的话。她不愿做将军的侍妾。她不愿做谁房里被买来、被束之高阁的暖床之物。她想要，朴素得近乎傻气，好好嫁人——做正头夫妻，嫁一个并非她主子、也不曾买下她名姓的男人。兰香听着。她早已学会对宅子抛来的一切都不动声色，可这一回仍被戳中了，因为这恰恰是一种希冀，是这宅子用六年教她埋葬的东西。

</details>

For Xiaolian's longing lands squarely on the one wound Lanxiang has never shown anyone. She had a sister — little Jialian, the child who drank poison with a smile and a promise of peach-blossom cakes in the next life, the sister who walked to death so that Lanxiang could live. Jialian, too, had wanted a life and a choice, and was given neither; she was given dignity instead, and a silk noose. So when Xiaolian speaks of wanting to be a wife and not a thing, Lanxiang hears her dead sister speaking straight through her, and something in her goes soft and cold at the same moment. She says nothing of the ghost standing at her own shoulder. She only listens, and aches, and files the ache away the way she has learned to file everything.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

因为小莲的渴望，正落在兰香从不示人的那道伤口上。她曾有过一个妹妹——小嘉莲，那个含笑饮下毒药、许诺来世再吃桃花酥的孩子，那个为换兰香活命而走向死亡的妹妹。嘉莲，也曾想要一条命、一个选择，却两者皆无；她被给予的，是体面，与一截白绫。所以，当小莲说起想做妻子而非玩物时，兰香听见的，是她死去的妹妹借这姑娘之口在说话，而她心里，便在同一刻又软又冷。她对自己肩头立着的那个鬼影，一字不提。只是听着，疼着，又把这份疼，按她学会的那样，悄然收好。

</details>

Meanwhile, outside the walls, her borrowed family is working the only way they know how. Xu Wanquan and his wife — Lanxiang's foster parents, the ones who traded their own dead daughter's name to keep her alive — have scraped together the means to do one small, desperate thing. They bribe the woman who collects the night soil, who happens to be Xiaolian's mother and moves freely in and out of the inner court. Through her, they pass a bundle of winter clothes — warm padded garments, the kind that mean the difference between a maid's survival and a maid's death by cough. It is a tiny act of love, smuggled past a dozen locked doors, and when Lanxiang closes her hands around the padded coat she understands, again, exactly what it has cost them to keep her breathing.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

与此同时，墙外，她那借来的家人，正用他们唯一懂得的法子使劲。许万全夫妇——兰香的养父母，那对用亲生亡女之名换她活命的人——凑出了门路，去做一件微小而孤注一掷的事。他们买通了收夜香的那个妇人，恰是小莲的母亲，能自由进出内院。借着她，他们将一包冬衣——厚实的棉袄，对丫鬟而言是活命与咳血而亡之间的一道分界——悄悄递到兰香手里。那是一桩微小的疼爱，偷渡过一道道紧闭的门；而当兰香双手拢住那件棉袄，她又一次明白，为了留住她的呼吸，他们究竟搭上了什么。

</details>

And while the women trade small kindnesses, the men are following the money. Lin Jinqi — the master who has spent six years at the border and come home to a wife he no longer trusts — takes himself to the Rongbao Pavilion, the curio shop that belongs to his family, and starts asking the Da Zhanggui the kind of questions that make clerks sweat. The accounts do not add up. Certain sums flow where they should not, toward a shop called the Jinxiu Embroidery Workshop — and the Jinxiu Workshop, as it happens, came into the family as part of Zhao Xingtang's dowry. The general does not accuse. He only watches, and files the discrepancy away, the way a man who has survived one betrayal learns to watch for the next.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

而当女人们在交换微小的善意时，男人们正顺着银钱追下去。林锦岐——那个在外六年、归来后已不再信任妻子的主子——亲自去了荣宝阁，那间林家的古玩铺子，向大掌柜问起了一类让伙计冒汗的问题。账对不上。某些银两流到了不该流的地方，流向一间叫"锦绣坊"的铺子——而锦绣坊，恰是赵星棠的陪嫁。将军没有发作，只是看着，把那处对不上记下，像一个从一场背叛里活过来的人，学会去等着下一场。

</details>

Then Zhao Xingtang hands Lanxiang the first real thread. She is listening to a troupe of singers perform a play, and the plot turns on a woman who betrays her husband. The mere subject sends her into a rage; she flings a cup, knocks over a lamp, and scorches her beloved fox-fur shoulder wrap before anyone can snuff the flame. She turns on Lanxiang and orders the ruined fur mended, as if the girl's hands could undo a burned thing. Lanxiang takes the wrap, finds a corner to work in, and does as she is told, saying nothing of the fury she has just witnessed.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

接着，赵星棠把第一根真正的线，递到了兰香手里。她在听一班子伶人唱戏，戏文恰讲到一名妇人背弃丈夫。这由头便教她勃然大怒；她摔了杯子，打翻了灯，待众人回过神，心爱的狐皮披肩已燎了个洞。她转而吩咐兰香把那件残裘补好，仿佛这姑娘的手能抹去被烧的痕迹。兰香接过披肩，寻了角落坐下，照办不误，对刚才那场怒火，只字不提。

</details>

But mending the fur, she watches her mistress's hands and hears the edge under the anger — and she understands what the rage was truly about. A woman does not come apart over a sung story unless the story is her own; no one scorches her favorite thing for a stranger's sin. Somewhere a man exists whom Zhao Xingtang is desperate to keep hidden, a secret stitched into the lining of a marriage the whole house already knows is hollow. Lanxiang says nothing. She only files it away, exactly as her master does, and keeps her face as blank as the fur she is patching.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

可补着裘，她望着主母的手，听着那怒气底下的锋刃——便懂了那场怒火究竟为何而来。一个女人，不会因一段唱词就碎成那样，除非那戏文正是她自己；没人会为自己的陌生人的罪孽，燎了自己最心爱的东西。某处，存在着一个赵星棠誓要藏住的男人，一个缝进这段人人皆知早已空心的婚姻衬里的秘密。兰香不发一言，只像她主子那样把它收好，并把脸，掩得同她正补的那张裘一样空白。

</details>

Lin Jinqi moves next, and he uses Lanxiang without ever quite meaning to. He sets a trap for his wife's seal — stage-managing a moment where Zhao Xingtang must stamp a document, so he can glimpse the matched pair of seals she guards. In the confusion the stamp is knocked aside. Most would panic. Lanxiang, seeing the snare about to close on her mistress and knowing which side she is really on, slips the small seal into her sleeve, hides it, and later returns it — unmistakably, helpfully — to Zhao Xingtang. Zhao Xingtang reads the rescue for what it is and rewards her with a silver ring. It is a small, perfect move: Lanxiang has made herself useful to the very woman she is meant to watch, and earned her trust without once showing her hand.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

林锦岐接着动了手，而他用到了兰香，却未必存心。他为妻子的印章设了局——精心安排一个赵星棠必须盖章的时机，好叫他窥见那对她紧攥着的对章。混乱中，印鉴被碰落一旁。多数人要慌。兰香见陷阱将要落在这位主母头上，又深知自己究竟站在哪边，便将那枚小印掖入袖中、藏起，过后又分明"好心"地把它归还赵星棠。赵星棠看穿了这份搭救，赏了她一枚银戒指。这是一步小巧而完满的棋：兰香刚让自己对那位本该被她盯防的主母有了用处，又不动声色地，赢得了她的信任。

</details>

But Lanxiang is not finished. Before she returns the seal she does the one thing that will matter most: she presses its face against her arm and lifts the pattern onto her skin, a mark of ink and intent she can carry straight out the gate. She has seen enough — the books that will not balance, the Embroidery Workshop, the fear crouched beneath Zhao Xingtang's rage — to know the burned fur was only the surface. So when Zhao Xingtang, needing the wrap mended and the shop watched, announces a trip to the Jinxiu Workshop, Lanxiang steps forward without being asked and volunteers to go, as any dutiful maid would. She will walk into the lioness's den beside the lioness, and see what no one has permitted her to see.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

可兰香并未就此罢手。归还印鉴之前，她做了一件最要紧的事：将印面按在自己臂上，把那图样拓进皮肉，拓成一道她能径直带出府门的墨痕与心机。她见得够了——对不上的账、锦绣坊、赵星棠怒火底下蹲伏的惊恐——明白那件狐裘不过是个表象。于是，当赵星棠既要补裘、又要盯铺，宣布要去锦绣坊时，兰香不待吩咐便上前，自请随行，像一个本分的丫鬟那样。她要跟着母狮，走进母狮的巢穴，去看那无人肯让她看的东西。

</details>

That is episode six. Nobody dies in it, and the roof stays where it belongs. But a girl who was told, again and again, to be invisible chooses, deliberately, to look — at a friend's forbidden hope, at a dead sister's unlived life, at a mistress's hidden lover, at a seal's secret pattern. She has begun to gather the threads, one small ring and one inked arm at a time, and the house that wanted her small is about to learn that the smallest hands can hold the sharpest knots.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

那就是第六集。里面没有人死，房顶也好好待在原处。可一个被一遍遍勒令隐身的姑娘，刻意选择了去"看"——看一个朋友被禁的希冀，看一个妹妹没能活过的人生，看一位主母藏起的情夫，看一枚印章暗藏的图样。她已开始收拢那些线，一次一枚小银戒、一次一臂墨痕地收拢；而那个想要她渺小的宅子，很快就要明白，最渺小的手，也能攥住最锋利的结。

</details>
