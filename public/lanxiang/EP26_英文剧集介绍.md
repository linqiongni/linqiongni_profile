# 兰香如故 — The Fragrance of Orchids Remains
## Episode 26 · Told Like A Story

Let me tell you about the twenty-sixth episode, because this is the one where the decree that saved Lanxiang from her family's ruin becomes the very thing that costs her the life she might have chosen, and where the Lin estate learns that an emperor's gift delivered by force is still a wound to the one who receives it. Last week the new emperor set his seal and bound the girl to Lin Jinqi; this week the house receives that bond the only way it knows how — with a lash for the son who took it without asking and a silence for the girl who never wanted it. Han Liang walks away from a door that will not open for him, and Lanxiang walks toward one she cannot open at all. The season, which has been steering these two toward opposite rooms since they met over ink, now lets the door fall between them — and the marquis who won her by saving a throne must now survive his own father's wrath to keep her.

让我跟你讲讲第二十六集，因为这集里，那道把兰香从灭门里救出的旨，正是夺走她本可自选的一生的那道；而林府学着懂一件事：皇帝以强力送出的礼，对收受的人仍是伤。上周新帝落印，把姑娘许给林锦岐；这周这府以它唯一会的方式接这道契——鞭打那不问自取的儿子，沉默待那从未想取的姑娘。韩梁从一个不为他开的门口转身走开，兰香走向一扇她根本打不开的门。这季自他二人在笔墨间相识便把两人引向相反的屋，此刻任门落在之间——而凭救驾赢来她的侯爷，如今须挨过自己父亲的怒，才能留她。

</details>

For Han Liang the week ends in a quiet that is its own kind of grief. The scholar who rehearsed his asking, who carried his mother's blessing and a clerk's honest roof in his heart, reaches the Xu door only to find it already closed by a higher hand. There is no insult in it, no rejection he can answer — only a royal command that renders his gentle plan a memory, and the girl he would have married now spoken of as a marquis's bride. He does not storm the gate; he is not that man. He stands a moment in the street that knew his hope, then turns back toward the books that never lie to him, and the season lets him go with a small, unwilling respect. The proposal he meant to offer was the one true choice Lanxiang had been offered in episodes, and the throne, needing nothing of him, took even that. He leaves the tale poorer by a door he will not knock on again.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

对韩梁，这一周收在一份安静里，而安静自有它的悲。那个默过求亲的词、把母亲的允与书吏清白的屋檐揣在心的书生，到了许门口，只见门已被一只更高的手关上。里面没有羞辱，没有他能回应的拒绝——只有一道御令，把他的温软盘算变成回忆，而本要娶的姑娘，如今被称作侯夫人。他不去撞门；他不是那种人。他在熟知他盼望的街上立了一刻，便转回那从不骗他的书里；这季放他走，带着一丝不愿的敬意。他本要给出的那一求，是几集来兰香得的唯一真选择，而那并不欠他什么的御座，连这也要了去。他离了这故事，穷在一扇不再敲的门。

</details>

Lanxiang feels the loss differently — not as a door unopened but as a lock turned from outside. She is spared the Xu family's terror of clan death, and Mr. Lu's cold arithmetic has shown her the only path that kept them living, yet none of that softens the fact that her body and name are now the emperor's gift to a man she has spent the season fleeing. The girl who read every lie in the bathwater and the sealed house reads this truth plainly: the decree shields her clan and chains her in the same stroke. She does not weep where the Lin watchers can see; she folds the disappointment into the same stillness she used when the poisoner smiled. The season marks how far she has come — from a maid who feared a flogging to a bride who fears a marriage she cannot refuse, bearing both with the quiet that has kept her alive this long.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

兰香的失落不同——不是门没开，是锁从外头转了。她免了许家灭门的惊，陆先生冷冷的账也给她看了唯一保住一家活路的法；可这些都没软了那事实：她的身与名，如今是皇帝赠给一个她几季都在逃的男人的礼。那个读尽浴水与封宅里每一句谎的姑娘，把这真相读得明白：这道旨，护她一家与锁她，是同一下笔。她不在林家眼线看得见处落泪；她把失落折进那回毒妇笑时用的静里。这季记她走了多远——从怕挨鞭的丫鬟，到怕一场拒不得的婚的 bride，两样都凭这一路护她活命的静撑着。

</details>

Inside the Lin estate the old master does not share the new emperor's gratitude. He hears that his eldest grandson, the Jing'an Marquis, has taken a bondservant to wife by imperial command and reads it as disgrace, not deliverance — a grandson who once refused the proper matches now bound, by his own petition, to the girl the house held as property. The old man's rage is the old kind, the kind that reaches for the rod before the reason, and he calls for the family law to be laid upon Lin Jinqi then and there. The season lingers on the absurd picture: the realm's savior, flogged in his own hall for the very marriage the throne commanded, the decree that should have protected him becoming the charge against him in his grandfather's eyes. The household holds its breath, because the old master with a rod is a law older than any emperor's seal.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

林府里，老太爷不领新帝的感激。他听说长孙靖安侯，凭御旨娶了个奴婢为妻，读之作辱而非救——一个曾拒了好姻亲的孙儿，如今凭自己的请，绑给了府里当财物的姑娘。老人的怒是旧式的怒，那种先抄起棍、后讲理的怒；他唤人当场把家法落到林锦岐身上。这季在那一幅荒唐画上流连：天下救主，在自己厅里为那御座亲命的婚挨鞭，本该护他的旨，在祖父眼里成了罪状。满府屏息，因为持棍的老太爷，是比任何御印都老的律。

</details>

Lin Jinqi does not argue and does not flinch. He lets the family law fall across his back the way he let the emperor's business fall across his shoulders — as a weight he chose to carry the moment he petitioned for her name. The rod opens his skin; the blood comes, and still he stands, because to resist the old master would be to dishonor the very house he saved, and to dishonor the house would be to dishonor the girl for whom he took the blows. The season, which has shown him cold and certain, shows him now silent and unbreakable, a man paying in his own flesh for a wedding the bride never asked him to arrange. The Lin estate's watchers, who have seen him cut down traitors and steady a crown, see something stranger: their master whipped raw and bearing it without a sound, for the sake of a maid who will not even call him husband.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

林锦岐不辩、不退。他任家法落背，如他任皇帝的事务落肩——那是他请下她名字那刻，自己选来扛的分量。棍破了他的皮；血下来，他仍立着，因为抗老太爷便是辱他救下的府，辱府便是辱他为此挨打的姑娘。这季曾显他冷而确，此刻显他静而不可折——一个用自己一身肉，偿一场新娘从不曾求他张罗的婚。林府的眼线，见过他斩逆、稳冠，这回见着更奇的：他们的主子被打到见血，一声不响地扛着，为一个连"夫君"都不肯叫他的丫鬟。

</details>

The lash would have gone on had the third daughter not come home. She returns to the Lin estate now as the commander's wife — the Yongchang Marquis's bride, a title that outranks the old master's rod — and she walks into the hall where her eldest brother is being beaten and stops it with a single word edged like a blade. She tells the clan plainly what the season has been teaching all along: to strike the marquis for obeying an imperial decree is to strike at the decree itself, and to strike the decree is treason, the very crime that took Duke Zheng Baichuan's head. The old master's rage meets a higher law than his own, and the household, cowed not by kindness but by the cold logic of survival, falls silent. She shields her eldest brother the only way the season allows — not with love, but with the emperor's name wielded like the rod her grandfather put down.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

若非三小姐归家，那鞭还要接着落。她如今以都督夫人之名回林府——永昌侯的妻，一个压过老太爷棍的衔——走进长兄正挨打的厅，以一句如刃的话止住。她直白告诉族人这季一直教的：因从御旨而鞭侯爷，便是鞭那旨本身；鞭旨便是谋逆，正是取了郑百川头颅的罪。老太爷的怒，撞上一道比他自己的更高的律；满府不是被善、是被求生的冷逻辑镇住，哑了。她护长兄，只用这季允的法——不是以爱，是以皇帝的名，如祖父放下的那根棍般挥出去。

</details>

Far from the Lin hall, Duke Zheng Baichuan's house pays the rest of its debt. The men of the family, the old duke among them, are led out and beheaded for the regicide they attempted, and the treason that reached from the hills to the prince's bed is answered in full by the block. Zheng Manyi, the duke's daughter who spent the autumn trying to unmake a maid and who railed at the wedding that cost her father's cause, is given the choice the season reserves for the proud brought low: submit to the disgrace or end it. She will not be shamed before the court that already took her father. She seizes a blade from a guard's belt and turns it on herself, and the season closes her thread the way it began — in a fury that would rather break than bend. The girl who read the lie lives; the girl who dealt it does not.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

离林厅很远，郑百川的家清了余债。族中男丁，老公爵在内，因所谋的弑君被押出斩首；那从山里伸到储君榻前的逆，被铡刀全数作答。郑蔓宜，那耗尽秋日想毁一个丫鬟、又因那断了父业的婚而咆哮的公爵之女，得了这季留给被贬的傲人的选：受辱，或了断。她不肯在已取了她父的朝堂前受辱。她从侍卫腰间夺刀，转向自己；这季以开场的同一种怒收她的线——宁可碎，不肯弯。读谎的姑娘活着；行谎的姑娘，没有。

</details>

Back in the residence, Mr. Lu finds Lanxiang and gives her the one counsel the week allows. He reminds her that the decree which bound her is also the decree that shields her — the emperor's command that made her a marquis's bride is the same seal no Lin elder may whip or waive, the same wall no family law can climb. The girl who feared the marriage as a chain is shown, by the old friend of her blood family, how to wear it as armor: so long as she is the emperor's gift, the house cannot dispose of her as it once disposed of a bondservant. The season sets this quiet lesson against the old master's rod and the third daughter's blade — the same decree that earned Lin Jinqi his flogging now becomes Lanxiang's protection, a royal name she may stand behind when the house turns cold. She hears it the way she hears all things: weighing which lock it closes and which door it opens.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

回到府里，陆先生寻到兰香，给她这一周唯许的劝。他提醒她：绑她的旨，也是护她的旨——那把她许作侯夫人的御令，正是林家长辈打不得也免不了的同一枚印，正是家法爬不上的同一道墙。怕这婚如怕链的姑娘，被她血族的旧友点明：如何把它当作甲穿——只要她是皇帝的礼，这府便不能如昔日处置奴婢般处置她。这季把这条静的课，对账老太爷的棍与三小姐的刃——那道教林锦岐挨鞭的旨，此刻成了兰香的护，一枚她在府转冷时可立其后的御名。她听它，如听万事：掂它关的是哪把锁、开的是哪扇门。

</details>

So we close the twenty-sixth episode the way a household closes a wound — by leaving the scar where the rod fell, and the girl where the decree put her. Han Liang turns from a door that will not open; Lanxiang turns toward one she cannot open; the old master raises the law and the third daughter answers it with the emperor's name; Duke Zheng's house is beheaded to the last man and his daughter chooses the blade over the shame. And Lin Jinqi, whipped raw for the marriage he forced, bears it without a sound, while Mr. Lu hands the bride the one key the house cannot take — the seal that binds her is the seal that keeps her. Episode twenty-seven will carry them to the wedding itself, where the girl who was given will be asked to bow, and the bow, this time, will be witnessed by heaven. For now, remember only this: an emperor's gift delivered by force is still a wound, and the wound, once dressed in royal silk, is the only armor the girl is allowed to wear.
