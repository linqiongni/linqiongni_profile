# 兰香如故 — The Fragrance of Orchids Remains
## Episode 30 · Told Like A Story

Let me tell you about episode thirty, because this is the episode where the safety Lin Jinqi bought with fire is carried, still warm, into the one place it was meant to be kept from: the court. The Shen family name, buried by a wrongful conviction and by the marquis's own burned proof, is about to be dug up by the very hand that set it alight, because a secret kept between two is a comfort, but a family erased by the throne is a wound that will not close until the throne itself admits the cut. The season, which has walked Lanxiang from a maid's knee to the edge of her true name, now walks her the last steps into the light the court denied her, and does not soften the road, because exoneration is won, never given.  The fire that saved her was meant to hide her; the court will now be asked to see her plainly.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

让我跟你讲讲第三十集，因为这一集，林锦岐以火买来的那份安稳，还带着暖，被带进了那个本要避开它的地方：朝廷。沈家的名，葬在了一桩冤判之下、也葬在了侯爷自己烧掉的证里，如今即将被那双点了火的手，重新挖起——因两个人之间守着的秘，是一份慰；可一个被御座抹去的府，是一道不愈合的伤，除非御座自己认下那一刀。这季，把兰香自丫鬟的膝引到她真名的边缘，此刻引她走最后几步，走进朝廷不肯给她的光里，并不把路放软，因昭雪是争来的，不是给的。

</details>

The first instrument of that winning is a thing dead hands left behind. Long ago, in the chaos of the rebellion, Zhao Xingtang — Lin Jinqi's first wife, who fell aiding him on the road — pressed into Lanxiang's care a ledger of the Zhao family's hidden dealings, and within it the proof that the Shen family was not the traitor the court proclaimed but the victim of a plot laid close to power. Lin Jinqi read that ledger before Lanxiang, to spare him, burned it; and what his eyes took in that hour he has carried ever since, a dead woman's charge kept in a living man's memory. Now, at his wife's side, Zhao Xingtang's gift becomes the first stone laid against the wall that buried the Shen name, the secret witness speaking though the paper that held her is long ash.  It is the sort of proof that outlives the hand that left it behind.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

那争来的头一件器，是死者的手留下的物。许久前，在叛乱的乱里，赵星棠——林锦岐的元配，途中为助他而殁——把一本记着赵家暗务的账册，按进了兰香手里；而册中，正是沈家并非朝廷所宣的叛者、而是近权者所布之局的受害者的证。林锦岐在兰香为护他而焚册之前，读过那册；那一时他眼中所取的，自此一直带在身上——一个死者的托付，存在活人的记忆里。如今，在妻身侧，赵星棠的赠，成了垒向那道埋了沈家之名的墙的第一块石；那秘密的证人，虽托她的纸早成灰，仍说着话。

</details>

To that dead witness Lin Jinqi adds the living clues he gathered in silence: the bone record that was not Shen Jialan's, the Hanshan painting inscribed by the granddaughter's own hand, the pen name Shen Rugù, and the contradiction in Lady Xue's memory that first opened the door. Woven together, the ledger and the clues point to one man — a powerful minister who stood close to the throne and who, jealous of the great scholar's influence and fearful of the Shen family's upright counsel, engineered the false case that ruined them. The season, which has watched a hundred smaller traps close, watches this one open, and notes that the trap was always a person, and a person leaves a thread, if only a patient man will pull it.  Each piece alone a careful reader might doubt; set side by side they form a single story the court cannot unread, and a name it can no longer refuse.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

林锦岐给那死去的证人，添上他沉默里收来的活线索：那不是沈嘉兰的骨档、那长孙女亲笔题下的寒山图、那"沈如故"的笔名，以及最先开了门的、薛氏记忆里的那处矛盾。账册与线索织在一处，指向同一个人——一个立近御座、忌着大儒之势、又惧沈家刚正之谏的权臣，是他在背后捏了那桩毁了沈家的假案。这季，曾见上百更小的阱合上，此刻看这一只张开，并记下：那阱从来都是一个人，而一个人，总会留一根线——只要有个耐性的人肯拽。

</details>

They bring the case not as a plea but as a petition backed by proof no court can wave aside. Lin Jinqi, the Jing'an Marquis, places before the throne the ledger's substance and his own record, and names the minister who turned a scholar's house into a traitor's grave. The court, which had buried the Shen matter as settled, finds the grave stirring, and the ministers who benefited from the silence stir with it. The season watches the old machinery of a wrongful conviction creak backward — not easily, not quickly, but inevitably, the way a tide turned by a deeper current cannot help but retreat.  The throne has seen petitions drafted by the hopeful and dismissed by the busy; this one is different, for it carries the weight of names the court cannot wave aside, and a ledger whose columns name the guilty without flinching or fear, line by patient line.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

他们递上的，不是乞怜，而是一纸有证撑腰、任何法司都挥不开的呈。靖安侯林锦岐，把账册的要旨与他自己的存录，陈于御前，并点出那个把学士之府变成叛者之坟的权臣的名。曾把沈家之事当作已了而葬下的朝廷，发觉那坟在动；而那些因沉默得利的臣，也随之动。这季望着一桩冤判的旧机器，向后吱呀倒转——不轻、不快，却不可避免，如一股被更深暗流推着的潮，不能不退回。

</details>

The minister, confronted, reaches for the same tools that served him once: denial, the weight of his office, the threat of accusing the accuser. But the ledger names names his office cannot swallow, and Lin Jinqi's record answers each dodge with a fact the minister cannot unmake. Slowly, in the season's patient light, the great man is unmasked as the author of the Shen family's fall — the one who forged the charge, bought the witness, and sealed the grave while the true granddaughter walked the earth as a maid. The season, unsparing, lets the fall be public, because a wrong done in the open must be righted in the open, or it is not righted at all.  What the great office once protected, the ledger now lays bare, naming each dodge and the man who taught it, until denial has nowhere left to stand and the record speaks for itself.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

那权臣被质，伸手去抓曾助过他的同一套器：抵赖、官威、反咬原告的威胁。可账册点出的名，他的官威吞不下；林锦岐的存录，以那权臣抹不去的事实，答了他每一记闪躲。缓缓地，在这季耐心的光里，这大人物被揭作沈家倾覆的执笔人——捏了罪、买了证、封了坟，而真正的孙女，正以丫鬟之身在世上走。这季不饶，让这跌落当众发生，因明里铸的错，必须明里正，否则便不算正。

</details>

And so the wrongful case is reopened and, at last, reversed. The Shen family's name is cleared; the conviction that branded them traitors is struck from the record; the dead are honored, and the estate and standing taken from them are ordered restored. The court that once declared Shen Jialan dead and guilty now declares her absent and innocent, and the difference between those two sentences is the whole of a life the throne had tried to erase. The season marks the moment the grave the marquis burned proof beside becomes, at last, a true grave — one that holds the honored dead, not a false conviction.  The reversal is no act of mercy handed down from above but justice long delayed, and now, with proof in hand, made plain and simply undeniable to any who read it, the family's name returned by the court that wrongly took it.  The name, once taken, returns by the same hand that erred.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

于是这桩冤案重开，终被翻了过来。沈家的名，洗清了；把他们 branded 为叛的定罪，从档里削去；死者受恤，被夺的田产与门第，奉命发还。曾宣沈嘉兰"已死且有罪"的朝廷，如今宣她"不在而无辜"；这两句判词之间的差别，正是御座曾试着抹去的一整条命。这季记下这一刻——侯爷曾在旁烧证的那座坟，终于成了一座真坟：葬着受恤的死者，而非一桩假罪。

</details>

Then Lanxiang stands. Not as Xu Lanxiang the maid who rose to mistress, and not only as the Lin marquise, but as Shen Jialan — the granddaughter returned, the heir the court has just admitted was never guilty. She speaks her birth name in the place where it was once spoken only as a curse, and the season, which has carried her under borrowed names for a whole story, sets the true name down at last without disguise. To stand as Shen Jialan is not to discard the life she built; it is to gather all of it — the maid's wit, the mistress's steadiness, the mother's love — beneath the roof of the family the throne tried to take. She is, the season says, every name she was forced to wear, and one she was born to.  To speak it aloud is to claim the life the wrongful verdict tried to erase.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

而后兰香站了出来。不是作那个从丫鬟升到主母的许兰香，也不只是作林侯夫人，而是作沈嘉兰——归来的孙女，朝廷刚刚认下从未有罪的继承人。她在那曾只被当作咒来念的地方，说出了她的本名；而这季，一整故事载着她用借来的名走，此刻终于不带遮掩地把真名放了下来。以沈嘉兰之身站立，不是弃掉她建起的那一生，而是把全部——丫鬟的机、主母的稳、为母的爱——都收到御座曾要夺去的那个府的屋檐下。这季说：她是每一个她被迫戴过的名，也是她生来就有的那一个。

</details>

Back at the Lin estate the news lands like a second edict. The Senior Lady Lin, who once fainted at the thought of a maid for a daughter-in-law, now receives the truth that her son's wife is a great family's heir, and the old lady's pride, long bent against the match, finds at last a reason to straighten. The servants who weighed every word to a maid now weigh them to a lady of restored standing, and the season notes the small justice in the turning: the house that resented her low birth is awed by the high birth it never suspected. Xu Wanquan and the parents who raised her weep, not because she was lost but because the daughter they loved is, at last, also the daughter the law admits.  The fainting of before is ended; what remains is a pride rebuilt around a truth she never chose, and a house remade by it.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

回到林府，这消息如第二道旨落下。曾因"儿媳是个丫鬟"而晕过的林母，如今接下这真相：她儿子的妻，是一座大族的继承人；老夫人久压在这门亲事上的傲，终于寻着一个伸直的理由。那些对丫鬟一字一句掂量的下人，如今对一位门第复还的贵女掂量起来；而这季记下一桩小公道的转向：那嫌她出身低的府，被它从不疑有过的出身高的她，镇住了。许万全与养大她的父母落泪，不是因她曾失，而是因他们爱的女儿，终于，也是法所认下的那个女儿。

</details>

The season, looking back over the long road from the kitchen to the court, sets down its fullest mark. A girl taken from a scholar's house and lowered to a maid's knee became, by her own wit and by a marquis's stubborn love, the mistress of the house that owned her; and that mistress, by the same wit and the same love, became the heir the throne had erased. The fire at the grave did not destroy the truth; it freed it to be spoken where speaking could mend a family. And the season, which has been both the teller and the test of this tale, admits that some stories are carried not by fate but by people who refuse to stay where they were put. It has watched thrones rise and fall and cared for none of them; it cares for this, because this is the kind of story that is kept alive only by the stubborn, ungoverned hearts of the people inside it.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

这季，回望自厨房到朝堂的长路，落下它最满的一个记号。一个被从学士府夺出、按到丫鬟膝下的姑娘，凭自己的机、凭一位侯爷执拗的爱，成了曾拥有过她的那座府的主母；而那主母，凭同一份机、同一份爱，成了御座曾抹去的那个继承人。坟前的火，没有毁掉真；它把真放了出去，去到那能修补一个府的地方说。而这季，既是这故事的讲述者、也是它的试金石，承认：有些故事，不是命在载，而是那些不肯待在被安放之处的人，在载。

</details>

So we close episode thirty the way a struck bell closes a wrong: with the Shen name rung clear again, and a woman standing in it who was once told she had no name at all. The maid who read the water and the mistress who freed a sister and the granddaughter the court exonerated are, at last, one person, and the man who burned the proof now stands beside the proof made whole. Episode thirty-one, when it comes, will take this hard-won peace to its edge, where a loyal retainer's knees will freeze at a palace gate and a marquis will read, in that stillness, the true price of the throne. But that is next week's tell. For now, remember only this: in the Lin estate a family the court erased can be written back by a dead woman's ledger and a living man's patience, and the name a girl was born to is the one no fire can burn.

于是我们如一口被击的钟合上一桩错那样，收束第三十集：沈家的名，再次被敲清，而一个曾被说"根本无名"的女人，立在里头。那个读了水的丫鬟、放了妹妹的主母、被朝廷昭雪的孙女，终于，是一个人；而那个烧了证的男人，此刻立在已补全的证之侧。第三十一集，来的时候，会把这争来的安稳，推到它的边缘——那里，一个忠仆的膝将在宫门外冻僵，而一位侯爷将在那静里，读出御座真正的价。但那是下周的讲法。此刻你只记一句：在林府，一个被朝廷抹去的府，能被一本死者的账、一个活人的耐，写回来；而一个姑娘生来就有的名，是任何火都烧不掉的那一个。
