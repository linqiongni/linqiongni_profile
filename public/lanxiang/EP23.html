Let me tell you about episode twenty-three, because this is the episode where the master looks at a man called Liu Shu and sees, behind the common wandering face, the fallen heir to the throne — and the seeing changes everything that follows for the Lin household. Lin Jinqi, who has spent seasons reading liars in smiling faces and poisoners in wooden barrels, is not for a moment fooled by the rough coat of a wanderer. He reads the bearing, the way a man carries himself when he was born to be obeyed and never quite unlearns it, and he knows. The deposed crown prince stands before him, the very man the court is even now sweeping Jinling to find, and the master who came only to trace a missing maid now holds a secret that could unmake or remake the realm entire. The season, which has been tightening its noose around this family all year, suddenly places the fate of a dynasty into one man's quiet, capable hands.

让我跟你讲讲第二十三集，因为这一集，是主子看着一个叫刘叔的人，在张平常浪客脸后头，认出那坠下的储君——而这认，改了之后林府的一切。林锦岐，这季在笑里读谎、在木桶里读投毒人，不被那浪客的粗袍骗过半分。他读出那气度——一个生来被人从、却从未真正学得卸去的人，是怎样撑着自己走路的——他便知了。废太子立在他面前，正是朝廷正扫遍金陵要寻的那人；而本只为追一个失踪丫鬟来的主子，今握着一个能倾朝、也能立朝的秘。这季一路把套收紧在这家颈上一整年，忽把一朝的命运，搁进了一个男人静而能的手里。

Lin Jinqi does not hesitate long, because a man who governs by knowing also knows when to hide. He arranges a substitute corpse — a body that will be found and read by the searchers as the prince's own, a death staged with care so that the court's men may close their books and turn for home satisfied. It is a dangerous mercy, the kind that buys a fugitive heir a road to the sea and buys the Lin family a reason the throne will never come knocking. He sends the real Liu Shu — the former crown prince — away by water, out past the reach of the city's alleys and the throne's edicts, to take refuge beyond the shore where no warrant can follow. The master who governs by knowing has, this once, governed by hiding, and the fallen heir drifts into the dark with his life purchased by a stranger's corpse and a stranger's nerve, owing a debt he may never live to repay.

林锦岐没犹疑太久，因一个以知而治的人，也知何时该藏。他备了一具替身尸——一具会被寻着、被搜者读作太子之死的尸；一场细心 staged 的死，教朝廷的人合了簿、满意转身回家。这是险的仁，一种为逃亡的继承人买一条通海的路、也为林家买一个御座永不来敲门的由头的仁。他把真的刘叔——前太子——由水道送走，送出城的巷与御旨够不到处，去到岸那头、任何拘票都跟不上的地方避难。这以知而治的主子，此一回，以藏而治；那坠下的继承人，漂进暗里，命是一个陌生人的尸、与一份陌生人的胆买来的，欠着一笔他或许活不到还的债。

While the prince slips toward the sea, Lin Jinqi picks up the other thread that has been gnawing at him: who took Lanxiang. The trail leads, as such trails in this season do, to Zheng Baichuan — Duke Zheng, the father of the ducal bride who smashed the orchid and staged the bed — and the word that comes back is that the maid has been sent to Yangzhou, sold entirely away from Jinling and its protection. The court, meanwhile, is a knot of tension that will not loosen: the reigning Crown Prince, hunting his deposed rival, has himself become trapped in Jinling, caught in the same net of suspicion and shifting power the season has been weaving all along. Two princes, one fallen and one reigning, circle the city like storms that refuse to break, and the master, standing between them with both secrets in his pocket, takes the only road that leads to the girl he came for.

太子向海溜去时，林锦岐拾起另一根一直啃着他的线：谁掳了兰香。线引到郑百川——郑国公，那个摔了兰、摆了床的国公之女的父亲——而传回的话是：那丫鬟已被送往扬州，彻底卖出了金陵与其庇护。与此同时，朝堂是一团解不开的紧：在位太子，搜他的废敌，自己反被困在金陵，缠进这季一路在织的那张疑与权变的网里。两位太子，一坠一在，如不肯破的风暴绕着城；主子立于二者之间，两样秘都揣在袋里，取了唯一引向他来寻的那姑娘的路。

Lin Jinqi seizes the moment's cover with the precision of a man who has spent a season turning circumstance into advantage. He declares that he must travel to Yangzhou to seek a sea route for the Crown Prince's escape — a plausible errand that satisfies the court and frees him to move without question — and under that public pretext he goes privately to find Lanxiang. The journey is his own: a master who left a maid's contract in her own hand now crosses an entire province for her, telling no one his true aim, using the throne's business as the curtain behind which he searches. The season, which has always known what he would not say aloud, lets the lie stand undisturbed, because a man looking for a girl he will not name is a man the court has no reason to watch — and the long road to Yangzhou opens before him like a door the season meant all along to leave ajar for exactly this purpose.

林锦岐以把一个季的机缘化作己利的男人的精准，抓住这刻的蔽。他宣称须赴扬州，为太子寻一条出海之路——一桩说得通、又能安抚朝廷、放他无阻行动的差；便借着这公面上的由头，私下去找兰香。这程是他自己的：一个把丫鬟身契交还她自己手的主子，今为她跨过一整个省，真意不告一人，拿御事作帘，在帘后寻。这季，向来知他不肯说出口者，由那谎立着不动，因一个寻一个他不点名之女的人，是朝廷没理由盯的人——而通扬州的长路，如一道这季本要为此目的留虚掩的门，在他面前开了。

Far from Jinling, in the city of Yangzhou, Lanxiang has been sold into the Qingyan House — a courtesan establishment where girls are bought and taught to please, and where a former maid with a painting hand might, for a while at least, be kept alive by her usefulness. She does not break; she does what she has always done when cornered — she turns her skill to survival. With brush and ink she produces paintings fine enough to stay the buyers' hands and earn a wary, grudging space within the house, using art the way she once used reading — as a door that opens when others would close it. The Qingyan House believes it has bought a pretty ornament to display; it has, instead, taken in a girl who spent a full season outwitting poisoners and princes, and who now, in the heart of the painted cage, quietly maps every exit she can find.

远在金陵之外，扬州，兰香被卖进了轻烟楼——一处买女、教女娱人的烟花之地；一个有一双手会画的旧丫鬟，或能凭她的用场，暂且活下。她没垮；她做她被逼到角时一向做的——把她的技，转向活命。她以笔与墨，画出足以按住买客之手、在楼里挣得一角戒心而勉强容她的画；用艺，如她曾用识字——作一扇别人要关、它却开的门。轻烟楼以为买来个好看的摆设；它实则，收进了一个熬过一整季投毒人与太子的姑娘，而她今在画笼的正心，悄悄绘着她能寻着的每个出口。

Among the painted faces of the Qingyan House, Lanxiang encounters a face she knows: Lü Jier, the former Lin maid who left the household and fell into this very life, now a courtesan within the same gilded walls. Lü Jier, who might by rights have been a rival or a stranger, instead proves that gratitude can outlast resentment — she remembers the kindness Lanxiang showed her in the residence, and she repays it without hesitation. She helps the maid plan an escape, working the small levers of the house from the inside, and the two women, one free-born and one sold, find a common cause in the simple, stubborn wish to walk out the door alive. It is a rescue born of an old debt, the quiet kind this season has been accruing all along, and the Qingyan House does not yet know it is about to lose its prize to the very girl it thought it had caged for good.

在轻烟楼那些敷了面的脸里，兰香撞见一张她认得的脸：吕蕺儿，那个离了府、落到这境遇的前林家丫鬟，今是同墙里的一个妓。吕蕺儿，本可凭理是对手或陌路，却证出谢可久于怨——她记着兰香在府里给她的善，便毫不犹疑地报了。她助丫鬟筹划逃，从里头扳动楼里的小机关；两个女子，一个自由生、一个被卖，在一桩简而执的愿——活着走出那门——里，寻着了共的因。这是一笔旧债生的救，这季一直在静里积的那种；而轻烟楼还不知，它要失它的重宝给那个它以为永远关住的姑娘。

Lanxiang makes her break and slips into the Yangzhou night, but freedom, in this season, is a thing that does not hold for long. Scarcely has she cleared the wall than pursuers are at her heels — the house's hard men, sent to bring back the painted girl who slipped the leash. She runs, and the road that had seemed to open closes again behind her, the way it always does in a tale where a great house has decided a girl must not be free. And at the very edge of this closing net, Lin Jinqi arrives at the Qingyan House to look for her — a step too late to meet her at the door, a step soon enough to walk into the empty room where she was, to feel the cold of a cage that has just been sprung, and to know, with a certainty that chills him, that he has come the length of a province only to miss her by a single heartbeat.

兰香断了、溜进扬州的夜；但这季的自由，是留不住的物。她才出墙，追兵已到脚跟——楼里的硬手，奉命把那溜了绳的画女抓回。她跑，那似开了的路，又在身后合上，如所有"大宅决意姑娘不得自由"的故事里那样。而在这收拢的网的正边缘，林锦岐到了轻烟楼寻她——晚一步，未在门口迎着她；又早一步，走进了她方在的空房，觉出一只刚被弹开的笼的冷，并带着一种令他发寒的确定，知自己跨了一省而来，却只差一息，便与她错过。

It is worth pausing on the cruel symmetry of this moment, because the season has rarely been so tidy in its cruelty. Two searches cross in the dark of one Yangzhou night: Lanxiang fleeing the house she was sold into, and Lin Jinqi entering it to bring her out, the two of them passing like shadows on opposite sides of a single wall. The master holds the secret of an empire in one pocket and the image of a girl's painted fan in the other, and for one suspended instant the whole tangled year — the poison, the princes, the orchid, the bed — balances on the thin chance that they might turn and see each other. The season, of course, denies them that turn, and lets the net close one breath behind her fleeing heels, because a story this tangled is not yet ready to untie. Fate, in this telling, delights in the near miss, the almost-seen, the door that swings open a single heartbeat after the one who needed it has already passed through to the other side.

So we close episode twenty-three the way a chase closes a circle — with the hunter arriving at the empty den, the prey already gone to ground. Lin Jinqi saw the deposed prince in a wanderer's coat and sent him to the sea behind a corpse; he traced Lanxiang to Zheng Baichuan and to far Yangzhou; he crossed a province beneath a crown prince's errand to find her; and he reached the Qingyan House to find her gone, the pursuers close, the net tightening a breath behind. Episode twenty-four, when it comes, will catch the two of them on either side of a door — he searching, she fleeing — and will carry the whole tangled season to a wedding where a hidden crown prince listens through a wall. But that is next week's tell, and the lanterns of the Lin estate, unaware of the province between them, burn on.
