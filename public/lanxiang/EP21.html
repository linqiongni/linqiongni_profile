Let me tell you about episode twenty-one, because this is the episode where the quiet road Lanxiang thought she had earned turns out to lead right back into the thick of other people's plans for her, and where a household that prides itself on arranging marriages finds one arranging itself. It opens with Han Liang bringing his widowed sister home — a woman lately returned from a dead husband's house, carrying the particular weariness of one who has buried a man and then endured the family that came attached to him. The scholar, gentle and earnest as we have come to know him, means only to give his sister a roof and a rest, and to mend the small household the two of them will keep now that she is alone in the world. But the city is not yet done with her, and the short road to a safe roof proves, as such roads in this season do, to be lined with those who would block it for their own advantage.
🇨🇳 中文翻译

让我跟你讲讲第二十一集，因为这一集，是兰香以为自己挣来的那条静路，到头竟领回旁人替她铺好的算计深处；也是一座以排亲为傲的府，遇着一门自己排起来的亲。它始于韩梁把守寡的姐姐接回家——一个刚从亡夫家回来的女人，带着那种埋过人、又熬过随他而来的那家人的特有倦。这秀才，如我们渐识的温厚恳切，只想给姊一个檐、一口息，并补起如今她孤身、二人要守的小户。但城还没放她过；那通向安稳檐的短路，如这季所有的路，两旁立着要为己利挡它的人。

For the widowed sister is not left in peace. The family of her late husband — the in-laws who had counted her as theirs to command and to dispose of — come to press their claim, to harass a woman they still regard as their property, or as a debt left unpaid by her marriage. Lanxiang, who has learned a thing or two about walls and about the people who hide behind them, steps in to help without being asked twice. She uses what is at hand and what is in the law: a dose of tung oil to make a practical, unglamorous nuisance of the intruders, driving them out with discomfort rather than violence, and a plain recitation of the statutes that say a widow, once freed, is free — spoken with the calm authority of someone who has read the rooms of the powerful and knows that law, like poison, is a thing best served with a steady hand. The harassers, faced with oil and ordinance together, retreat, and the sister is given her evening and her dignity back.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

因那寡姊不得安生。亡夫的家人——那些把她算作可命之、可处之的姻亲——来逼他们的主张，来扰一个他们仍视作自己财产、或一笔婚中未偿之债的女子。兰香，已在墙与藏墙后的人身上学了几手，不待二请便上前相助。她用上手边与律中所有的：一剂桐油，把闯入者作成实务而不体面的厌物，以不适而非以暴把他们驱走；又平白诵出那"寡妇一旦自由便是自由"的条律——以一个读过权贵之屋、知律如毒、最宜以稳手奉下之人的静威说出。扰者面对油与法同至，退了；姊得了她的黄昏，也得了她的尊严。

Word of the rescue travels, as such things do in a city that listens at every wall, to the two mothers who have been quietly watching the shape of things. Xu's mother — Lanxiang's own adoptive mother, Xue — and Han's mother, seeing how deftly the girl defended the widowed sister and how gently Han Liang looked on while she did it, come to the same quiet conclusion: that Lanxiang and the scholar would make a well-matched pair. It is the kind of matching that great houses arrange by contract and free families arrange by inclination, and the two mothers, over a pot of tea, let the thought ripen without forcing it. The season, which has been steering daughters toward marriages not their own, now finds a match that wants no steering at all, and the finding sits uneasily beside the other, grander marriage the household has already set in motion for the master.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

这相救的话，如这类事在每面墙都支着耳的城里那样，传到了两位一直在看势的母亲耳里。许母——兰香自己的养母薛氏——与韩母，见姑娘护寡姊何其利落、韩梁看她何其温，落到同一个静结论：兰香与这秀才，甚是相配。这是大宅以契、自由之家以愿来成的那一种配；两位母亲，茶间，由那念头熟着，不催。这季一路把女儿引向非己的婚，今却遇着一门毫不需引的配——而这遇，与这府早已为那主子发动的另一门更隆之亲，并排得不大安稳。

Back inside the Lin walls, the second daughter — she who was confined for her folly with Du Zheng — has at last come to her senses, and not a moment too soon for the family's peace. Released from her confinement and made to sit with the full cost of her believing, she turns her face toward her mother and, with a frankness the season had not expected of her, urges the woman to let go of the grievance she has been carrying like a stone. It is a small redemption, the kind that costs pride but buys peace, and it signals that the foolish girl the old master confined has grown into someone the household can, perhaps, trust again. The residence, watching her change, softens a degree; the standing that was endangered by her error begins, slowly and from within, to mend itself rather than by the sacrifice of a sister who offered herself in her place.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

回到林府墙内，二小姐——那个因与杜正的蠢被拘的——终回了神，对这家的安，正及时。她出了禁足，被迫坐看自己信人的全代价，转脸向母亲，以这季没料她会有的坦，劝那女人放下一直如石般揣着的怨。这是小的救赎，一种费了骄、买了安的救赎；它示意：老太爷拘过的蠢姑娘，已长成这府或许能再信的人。府里看着她变，软了一分；那被她的错危及的门第，缓缓地、由内里，自己补起，而非借那代她而出的妹的牺牲。

But the Senior Lady Lin has her own marriage in mind, and it is not the one the mothers are quietly brewing over tea. She has fixed her eye on Zheng Manyi, the daughter of Duke Zheng — a match of standing and alliance, the very kind a great household reaches for when it means to bind itself to power and to weather the court's gaze. The old lady, who managed men and marriages her whole long life, sees in Zheng Manyi a bride who would strengthen the Lin name and please the sort of people who now, with the Crown Prince himself in Jinling, matter more than ever before. She begins, quietly and with purpose, to lay the groundwork for the master's next marriage, never mind that her son has shown no hunger for it, and never mind the girl he once could not take his eyes from, who now works across the city at a painting shop and sings with a scholar.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

但林母心里另有一门亲，不是母亲们正就茶悄酿的那门。她盯上了郑蔓宜，国公之女——一门讲门第、讲结援的亲，是大宅要绑自己于权势、以御御目时所伸手去抓的那一种。老夫人一辈子理男人与婚，看郑蔓宜是能壮林名、也能讨好如今太子亲在金陵、比 ever 更要紧的那种人的新娘。她悄然、且带着用意，为她儿子下一门亲铺起基；且不顾她儿子全无此饥，也不顾那他一度移不开眼、如今在城那头书画行做活、与一秀才唱和的姑娘。

Lin Jinqi, for his part, goes to visit Lanxiang and brings with him a pot of orchids — a small, telling gift, the kind a man carries when the words he will not say are the ones that matter most. He finds her, to his surprise and perhaps to his unease, singing in easy harmony with Han Liang, the two of them trading verses the way people do who like the sound of each other's voices more than they have admitted. He proposes, in that moment, to recommend Han Liang for some post or preferment, a gracious and distancing gesture that costs him little and says much — and is gently declined, the scholar content where he is and with whom he is. The master leaves with his orchid and his unspoken thing unsaid, and the singing he walked in on stays in his ear longer than he cares to admit, the way a tune does when it is not yours and yet you cannot quite put it down.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

林锦岐这边，去探兰香，随身带了一盆兰花草——一件小而有说的礼，是那种男人在他不肯说、却最要紧的话噎住时，会携的礼。他撞见她，出乎意、或许也出于不安，正与韩梁自在相和而歌，两人递着诗句，如互喜对方嗓音之人那样。他当下提议，要举荐韩梁某个职或进身——一个体面而拉开距离的姿，费他甚少、说却甚多——却被温拒，秀才安于所处、与所偕。主子携着他的兰、与他未出口的东西离去；他撞进的那歌，在他耳里留得比他肯认的久，如一支非你所有、你却放不下的调那样。

It is worth pausing here, in the hush between two matches, to mark what the orchid has come to mean in this tangled house. A pot of flowers should be a small thing, the sort of gift a person gives without a second thought, and yet in the Lin estate even a plant carries the weight of a feeling no one will name. The master brought it across the city to a girl who is no longer his, and the ducal bride who noticed it shattered it rather than bear the thought that his hand might reach past her. The season, which has been measuring this household in poisons and proxies, now measures it in a flowering weed, and the silence around that small green thing speaks louder than any of the contracts the old lady is drafting. A great house, it turns out, can be undone not by the blade but by the bouquet it refuses to acknowledge.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

值得在此停一停，在两门亲的静隙里，记一记那盆兰在这缠府里成了什么。一盆花本该是小事，是人想也不想便送的礼；可在这林府，连一株草也载着无人肯点的情之重。主子把它跨城带给一个不再属他的姑娘；而那注意到它的国公之女，摔了它，也不肯认他的手或越过她。这季，一路以毒与代身量这府，今以一株花野菜量它；而那小绿物四周的静，比老夫人正起着的所有契都响。一座大宅，到头，能倾它的不是刃，是它不肯认的那束花。

Then comes the move that turns the season's wheel and changes the stakes for everyone: Han Liang, encouraged by his mother and by the easy warmth between him and Lanxiang, asks his mother to send a matchmaker to Lanxiang's family to propose marriage. It is the proper, lawful, hopeful step — the exact opposite of the contracts and sacrifices that have governed every other match in this tale. A matchmaker's foot on the threshold means a free woman may choose a free man, and the household that was once Lanxiang's owner would, this time, be asked rather than told. The season, which has been arranging brides by debt and by decree, suddenly faces a marriage made by simple inclination, and the old powers of the house — the master, the mother, the great name — find themselves, for once, not the only hands upon the board, and perhaps not even the steadiest.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

接着来了转这季之轮、也改了所有人筹码的一步：韩梁，受母亲与和兰香之间的自在暖意鼓动，请母亲遣媒人往兰香家，去提亲。这是正当、合法、怀望的一步——与这故事里治着其余每门亲的契与牺牲，正相反。媒人一脚踏上槛，意即自由女可择自由男；而那曾为兰香之主的户，这一回，是被问、而非被令。这季一路以债、以旨排新娘，忽遇一门由简愿而成的亲；而府里的旧权——主子、母亲、那大名——头一回，发现自己不是盘上唯一的手，或许连最稳的都不是。

So we close episode twenty-one the way a story closes a trap — by springing two at once, and leaving the wire still humming. A widowed sister was defended with oil and law; two mothers saw a match worth making; a foolish daughter found her wits and urged peace upon her mother; the Senior Lady reached for a ducal bride; the master came with an orchid and left with a tune he could not name; and a scholar sent a matchmaker where a contract used to rule. Episode twenty-two, when it comes, will show what the ducal bride does when she learns an orchid means more than she thought, and how far a great house will go to unmake a marriage it did not arrange. But that is next week's tell, and the lanterns of the Lin estate, as ever, burn on. The season has taught this household that no thread is ever truly cut, only knotted and left waiting for the next hand to take it up, and the hand that reaches next may not be one any of them expect.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

于是我们如一个故事合陷阱那样收束第二十一集——一次弹开两个，且留那钢丝仍嗡着。寡姊以油与律得护；两母亲见一门值得成的配；蠢女儿回了神、向母亲劝了安；林母伸手去抓国公之女；主子携兰而来、带调而去，那调他点不得名；而一秀才，在契曾主治处，遣了媒人。第二十二集，来的时候，会教那国公之女，待她知一盆兰比她想的更重，会做什么；也会教一个大宅，为拆一门它没排的亲，会走多远。但那是下周的讲法；而林府的灯，如常，烧着。
