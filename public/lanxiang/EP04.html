Episode four is the quiet one — the kind of episode that looks like nothing happens, and is actually where everything is decided. Lanxiang is inside the Lin mansion now, a third-class maid with a borrowed name and a secret she cannot afford to show. And the question the episode asks is simple and merciless: now that you have gotten in, can you stay without being seen? It is a question this house will keep asking her, in a hundred small ways, for a very long time. Every corridor has ears; every silence is listened to; and the girl who wishes to disappear must first learn the architecture of being watched.
🇨🇳 中文翻译

第四集是安静的一集——那种看上去什么都没发生、实则一切都在此定局的集数。兰香如今进了林府，一个顶着借来名字、藏着不能示人秘密的三等丫鬟。而这一集抛出的问题，简单而冷酷：你既进了门，能不能不被看穿地，站住脚？这座宅子会以千百种细微的方式，把她这个问题，问上很长、很长一段时间。

Qingxiang, Zhao Xingtang's sharp-eyed confidante, is put in charge of settling the four new girls. She shows them their quarters and, under cover of ordinary kindness, sizes each of them up — which ones flinch, which ones watch, which ones calculate. Jialan keeps her head down and her face blank, and Qingxiang reads her as a timid, empty-headed little thing, the sort who will sweep floors and ask nothing. Exactly the mask Jialan wants her to see. Lü Jí'ér, by contrast, wears her ambition on her sleeve, bright-eyed and eager, and Qingxiang marks her at once as the one to watch — and, if needed, the one to use. Qingxiang reports her read of both girls back to Zhao Xingtang that very night, and the household's invisible map of who is dangerous is redrawn by a single, quiet conversation.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

赵星棠身边那个目光锐利的亲信青葙，奉命安顿四名新来的丫鬟。她领她们认了住处，又借着寻常的关切，把每一个人暗暗打量了个透——谁会退缩，谁在观察，谁在盘算。嘉兰低着头，面无表情，青葙便把她看成个怯懦、没心眼的傻丫头，是那种只会扫地、别无企求的主。这正是嘉兰要她看见的壳子。吕蕺儿则相反，野心写在脸上，眼亮心热，青葙一眼就把她标成了要盯紧的那个——也是，若有需要，要拿捏的那个。

Rank decides everything. As third-class maids, the four cannot serve the masters directly; they are passed down to a second-class girl named Bao Gai and set to boiling water by the stove — a post that can never be left unmanned, day or night. Jialan takes the drudgery without a word. That first night, the watch is meant to be shared, Lü Jí'ér and Jialan by turns. But Lü Jí'ér simply falls asleep and wakes at dawn, then, with no trace of shame, blames Jialan for not waking her. Jialan does not argue. She only says, mildly, that she too closed her eyes to rest a moment. One pushes the fault outward; the other swallows it. In a single sentence, the house learns two kinds of girl. Jialan files the lesson away under a colder one she already knows: that survival here is not about being good, but about being unremarkable.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

等级决定一切。身为三等丫鬟，四人不能近身侍奉主子，被拨给一个叫宝盖的二等丫鬟，派去炉边烧水——这是个离不得人的差事，昼夜都不能空着。嘉兰一声不响地受了这等苦役。头一夜，本该两人轮班守着，可吕蕺儿倒头便睡，一觉睡到天亮，再无半分愧色地，怪嘉兰没叫她。嘉兰不争。她只淡淡说，自己方才也闭眼歇了片刻。一个把错往外推，一个把事往肚里咽。一句话里，宅子认下了两种姑娘。

Autumn comes to the garden, and with it a small, telling scene among the chrysanthemums. Picking blooms, Jialan takes only the opened petals and leaves the roots undisturbed — a habit of someone who was taught to treasure things and to leave a path behind. Lü Jí'ér, wanting only the prettiest basket this instant, yanks the flowers up by the root. It is not idle business. One girl, raised high and brought low, still knows to tend what can grow again; the other, desperate to climb, grabs what she can see and trusts nothing to last. When Lü Jí'ér then covets the basket Jialan arranged so prettily and simply takes it, the price she demands for her silence is this: she will guard Jialan's deepest secret — that the quiet, 'stupid' girl can in fact read and write. It is a bargain struck over flowers, and Jialan agrees to it the way she agrees to everything now — calculating exactly what it costs her to be known, and deciding the cost is worth paying, for now.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

园子里秋意渐起，菊花丛间，有一场小却意味深长的戏。采花时，嘉兰只摘开好的花瓣，连根也不伤——那是被教过惜物、也教过给自己留后路的人的习惯。吕蕺儿只要眼前最体面的那一篮，便连根把花拔起。这不是闲笔。一个曾居高位又跌落的人，仍懂得养护能再生的东西；另一个拼命想往上爬的人，只抓得住眼前所见，绝不指望什么长长久久。当吕蕺儿看中嘉兰摆得齐整好看的那一篮、径自拿了去，她以此要挟换来的条件便是：替嘉兰守住她最深的秘密——这个安安静静、“蠢笨”的丫头，其实识字。

Then the episode hands Jialan its first real secret. A maid from the senior wife's rooms asks the two girls to deliver a Buddhist scripture to the young master, Lin Jinqi. Lü Jí'ér, hungry for any excuse to cross his path, snatches the errand without a thought. But the sky turns, rain comes, and on a shortcut she crashes into Qingxiang and drops the wrapped book — then, in her hurry, scoops up the wrong one and runs. She does not know it, but the book she is carrying is not a scripture at all. She is carrying someone's secret, and she cannot read the difference. By the time she reaches the gate, rain-soaked and pleased with herself, the real scripture lies forgotten on the path where she dropped it.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

接着，这一集把第一个真正的秘密，递到了嘉兰手里。大太太房里的丫鬟，托她们两个给大少爷林锦岐送一本佛经。吕蕺儿恨不得寻个由头接近他，想也不想便一把抢下了这差事。可天色骤变，落下雨来，她抄近路时撞上了青葙，怀里的书掉在地上——慌乱中，她捞起一本错的便跑。她不知道，自己手里捧着的，根本不是佛经。她捧着别人的秘密，却读不出那区别。

Jialan opens the book to check it — and freezes. Inside the scripture's covers, where prayers should be, is a ledger. Interest collected, silver overdue, names, sums, the careful arithmetic of money that should never have been lent under a holy name. Her mind flies back to the Da Zhanggui and his flattened books, and the shape of it clicks into place: this is Zhao Xingtang's private fortune — the usury, the lending, the quietly growing pile of silver behind the lady's smiling face. Jialan closes the book and tells Lü Jí'ér to rush it to the master at once, to let no one else's hands touch it. But Lü Jí'ér, giddy at the thought of standing before Lin Jinqi, only goes back to her room to primp and preen. The ledger sits in their room like a sleeping snake, and Jialan, who knows what it is, can do nothing but wait for the woman who owns it to come claiming it.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

嘉兰翻开书想看个究竟——随即僵住。佛经的封皮之内，本该是经文的所在，却是一本账。收过的利钱，拖欠的银两，人名，数目，是一笔本不该借在圣洁名下的银钱的精细算计。她的思绪一下子飞回大掌柜与那本被抹平的账册，整件事便咔哒一声，落了位：这是赵星棠的私房钱——是印子钱，是放贷，是那位主母含笑面容背后，悄悄涨起来的那堆银子。嘉兰合上书，催吕蕺儿立刻送呈主子，莫让第三只手碰过。可吕蕺儿满心想着能站到林锦岐跟前，只回房梳妆显摆去了。

What the two girls do not know is that Lin Jinqi has already suspected the Da Zhanggui of colluding with Zhao Xingtang. When the Da Zhanggui comes to the house to deliver the accounts, the young master corners him and, with a few cold sentences, makes plain that he sees the discrepancy and will be watching. It is a private warning, dealt before this episode's confusion ever begins — which is why, when the wrong book surfaces, Lin Jinqi is not surprised that his wife keeps a second set of books. He is only waiting for the right moment to use what he knows. The Da Zhanggui leaves white-faced, and the ledger that will one day surface is, for this moment, still buried where Zhao Xingtang believes it safe.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

两个姑娘不知道的是，林锦岐早就在疑心大掌柜与赵星棠暗中勾结。大掌柜来府中呈送账册时，少爷将他堵住，寥寥几句冷话，便点明自己看出了那一处对不上，且会盯着。这是一记私下里的警告，在本集的这场混乱开始之前便已落下——所以后来错书一出，林锦岐对妻子藏着第二本账，并不意外。他只是在等一个恰当的时机，好把所知用上。

Qingxiang, back in the senior wife's rooms, realizes at once that the wrong book was taken. Zhao Xingtang sweeps to the girls' quarters with her people, ready for the worst. She finds the scripture exactly where it was left, untouched, and reads the two of them in a single glance: they cannot read a word. The danger she feared dissolves — there is no one here who could have understood what they held, no one who could ever testify to what the book contains. So she says nothing. She simply takes the book and walks away, leaving Jialan and Lü Jí'ér none the wiser, and the secret safe exactly where Zhao Xingtang means it to stay. Jialan, watching the lady's retreating back, understands that she has been judged harmless and therefore spared — and resolves, more firmly than before, to keep that judgment intact.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

青葙回到大太太房中，立刻发觉拿错了书。赵星棠带着人，一路杀到丫鬟住处，已做好了最坏的准备。她看见佛经原样搁着，动也未动，一眼便把两人看了个透：她们一个字也不认得。她所惧的危险，就此消散——这里没人能读懂自己捧过什么，没人能指证那书里写着什么。于是她一言不发。只是取了书，转身便走，留下嘉兰与吕蕺儿懵然不知，而那桩秘密，仍安安稳稳地，停在赵星棠要它停的地方。

Lü Jí'ér, though, cannot leave well enough alone. Disregarding every rule of rank, she rushes to offer tea to Lin Jinqi herself, and in her flurry lets slip that the senior lady came to their room to take a scripture away — a detail no maid should have noticed, and fewer should have reported. The young master, amused or calculating, rewards her, and she returns to the quarters flushed with silver and importance, showing it off. Jialan, watching, feels the cold shape of the house close around them. She warns Lü Jí'ér, gently but firmly, not to be too clever, not to mistake a master's smile for safety, and to mind her tongue before it writes their deaths in ink they cannot read. Then she turns, and the two girls are left to wonder what, exactly, they have been spared — and what they have been made to carry without knowing its weight.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

可吕蕺儿，偏不肯安分。她不顾一切等级规矩，抢着亲自去给林锦岐献茶，慌乱之间，竟漏出“大奶奶曾到她们房里取走一本佛经”的话——这本不是哪个丫鬟该留意、更该少有丫鬟会去禀报的细枝。少爷是觉得有趣还是另有盘算，赏了她；她便揣着银子、满脸风光回到住处，四处显摆。嘉兰看在眼里，只觉那座宅子的冷意正悄悄合拢。她劝吕蕺儿，温和中带着坚决：莫要自作聪明，莫把主子的笑当成安稳，管好这张嘴，免得替他们写下一笔他们自己都读不懂的死罪。

That is episode four. It is the episode where you learn that in this house, the most dangerous thing you can do is understand what you are looking at — and the safest thing is to make everyone believe you don't. Jialan has read the ledger and pretended she cannot. She has let Lü Jí'ér's loose tongue pass without repeating it. The board is set, the secrets are buried, and the girl who sees everything has, once again, chosen to be unseen — because she knows that the one who is seen first is the one the house breaks first.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

那就是第四集。这一集教你明白：在这座宅子里，最危险的事，是看懂你眼前的东西；最安全的，是让所有人都以为你不懂。嘉兰读过那本账，却装作不识。她由着吕蕺儿那张漏风的嘴过去，自己一个字也没复述。棋盘摆定，秘密埋好，而那个看穿一切的姑娘，又一次，选了隐身——因为她知道，最先被看见的那一个，便是宅子最先折断的那一个。
