Let me tell you about episode three. Episode two left our girl Lanxiang — still secretly Shen Jialan, the granddaughter a whole family died to protect — three streets from the household that disowned hers, and it left Lin Jinqi, the man who was once promised to her, a battle-scarred general coming home to a house that had quietly learned to do without him. Episode three is the moment those two orbits finally, inevitably, begin to tilt toward each other. And it begins, fittingly, with a betrayal that isn't Jialan's at all — a slow, domestic unstitching that has been going on for six years in a house she has never set foot in, and which she is now about to be forced to enter.
🇨🇳 中文翻译

让我跟你讲讲第三集。第二集把我们的姑娘兰香——其实仍是沈嘉兰，那个全家拼死护下的孙女——搁在了抛弃她家的那一家三条街外；也把曾与她有婚约的林锦岐，一个满身战痕、回到早已悄悄学会没有他也能过的家里的将军，摆在了归途上。第三集，是这两个人的轨迹终于、不可避免地开始彼此倾斜的时刻。而它恰如其分地，始于一场与嘉兰无关的背叛——一场缓慢的、家常的拆解，在 she 从未踏足的一座宅子里进行了六年，而她如今，就要被强迫着走进去。

Lin Jinqi has been away six years, fighting the coastal raiders who plagued the southeastern shore. He comes home expecting his house to still be his — the same halls, the same servants, the same girl at his shoulder. Instead he finds that his wife, Zhao Xingtang, has spent those years quietly, thoroughly remaking it. The old household faces are simply gone: some turned out, some married off, some sold. And worst of all, his own personal maid, Yuchan — the girl who had been at his side since childhood, who knew which tea he liked and which boot pinched — has been married off to the idiot son of the Liu family's rice-shop manager, on Zhao Xingtang's order, while he was away at war. The message is unmistakable. The general has been away. In his absence, the lady of the house has taken everything that was his and made it hers. He has been emptied out of his own home.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

林锦岐离乡六年，在外抗击侵扰东南沿海的来犯之敌。他归来时，本以为这座宅子还是他的——一样的厅堂，一样的仆从，一样跟在他身后的姑娘。可他发现，妻子赵星棠在这六年里，已悄悄、彻底地将它重新捏了一遍。旧日的家仆面孔荡然无存：有的被赶走，有的被嫁人，有的被发卖。而最刺人的，是他身边最得用的丫鬟玉蝉——那个自小跟在他身边、知道他爱喝什么茶、哪双靴子磨脚的姑娘——已被赵星棠做主，许给了米行柳掌柜的傻儿子，就在他远在边关之时。意思再明白不过：将军在外，主母便趁他不在，把他身边的一切尽数收归己有。他，被从自己的家里掏空了。

So Lin Jinqi does the one thing a man in his position can do. He reaches back for his mother's old plan — a scheme the Senior Lady Lin had nursed for years, to plant her own people in the inner rooms — and he decides to bring in a fresh set of maids. Not bought girls from outside, but the household-born ones: the daughters the Lin family owns from birth, raised on its porridge and its discipline, now old enough to serve inside the inner rooms. It is not charity. It is a quiet countermove, a way to seed his own eyes and ears into the very spaces where his wife has already planted hers. And the decree goes out, written in his mother's hand and backed by his open authority: every household-born girl of fourteen or more, unwed, is to be brought into the main house and trained for inner service.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

于是林锦岐做了身处他这般境地的人唯一能做的事。他拾起母亲旧日的谋划——那是林大太太经营多年的盘算，要在内院安插自己的人——决意重新纳一批新丫鬟。不是外头买来的，而是家生子的姑娘：林家世代为奴、生来便是家生子的女儿，吃着林家的粥、受着林家的规矩长大，如今到了可以进内院当差的年纪。这并非善心。这是一步不动声色的反击，是要在妻子布下眼线之处，也安上自己的耳目。号令就此传出，由林大太太执笔、以他的明面权威为后盾：凡家生子年满十四、尚未婚配的，一律选入正府，受训内侍。

That decree falls like a stone into the Xu family's courtyard. Han Momo — the old stewardess who runs such errands for the Senior Lady — comes to collect, and the quiet life the Xus have built through six years of obscurity shatters at a single knock. Xu's wife, who has come to love the girl as her own daughter, is frantic. She sends word to the Da Zhanggui of the Rongbao Pavilion — the very man Jialan once outsmarted over a forged painting — begging him to plead for an exemption, to tell Han Momo the girl is sickly and unfit for service. He takes her silver and her hope and pockets both. He lies. He does nothing. And so, while the Lü family across the court celebrates — Lü Jí'ér's mother already dreaming of a rich match and a changed fortune for her ambitious daughter — Jialan is pulled out of the only home she has known in six years and marched toward the great house she has dreaded most.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

这道号令，像一块石头落进了许家的院子。管这等差事的韩嬷嬷奉林大太太之命来人挑选，许家六年来在籍籍无名中苦撑出的安稳日子，就在一声叩门下碎了。许母早已把姑娘当亲生女儿疼，这下慌了神。她托人去求荣宝阁大掌柜——正是当年被嘉兰以一幅假画将了一军的那一个——替嘉兰求个豁免，只说是姑娘身子弱，不堪当差。大掌柜收了银子，也收了她的指望，一并揣进了自己怀里。他骗了。什么也没办。于是，当院另一头的吕家正欢喜雀跃——吕蕺儿的母亲已在盘算攀一门好亲、改换女儿的门庭——嘉兰就被从六年来唯一的家里拽出，朝那座她最怕走近的宅子押去。

Her father snaps. Xu Wanquan, the gentle man who has never raised a hand to anyone, who counts a ten-tael windfall as a year's fortune, grabs a kitchen cleaver and goes after the Da Zhanggui, ready to die over his daughter. Jialan, walking ahead in Han Momo's grip, hears of it and is seized with a colder terror than any she feels for the house itself: the terror of bloodshed. A corpse in a courtyard would undo everything — would drag her family into the law, would expose the borrowed name, would end the fragile peace of six years in a single afternoon. So she turns to Han Momo on the road and pleads her own case, calm and quick: let me at least go back to the Pavilion first, she says, to settle my father's heart before he does something his own kin will mourn. The old woman, weighing the girl's composure against the trouble of a public scene, agrees.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

她的父亲崩溃了。从不曾对谁动过手的许万全，那个把十两横财当作一年福分的人，抓起一把厨刀，追着大掌柜而去，为女儿不惜拼命。走在前头、落在韩嬷嬷手中的嘉兰听得此事，被一种比怕那座宅子更冷的惊惧攫住——是怕流血。一条人命横在院子里，便什么都完了：会把她一家拖进官司，会戳穿那个借来的名字，会把六年里勉强维系的安稳，在一个下午里尽数葬送。于是她在路上转向韩嬷嬷，为自己陈情，又稳又快：容我至少先回一趟荣宝阁，安了父亲的心，免得他做出让至亲抱憾的事。老嬷嬷掂量着这姑娘的镇定，与一场公开闹事的麻烦，应了。

And in that small reprieve, Jialan does what she does best. At the Pavilion she finds her father already cornered by the Da Zhanggui's men, chest heaving, blade trembling. She pulls the steward aside and names his fear out loud, plainly, in front of Han Momo so he cannot wriggle free of it: he had skimmed twenty taels from the accounts and buried them in the ledgers, and he is terrified that a real audit — now that the young master is home and watching — will surface them. She tells him exactly what will follow if a fight is allowed to start: the authorities will come, and when they come they will open the books. The man, caught between a girl who sees too much and a stewardess who could ruin him with a word, goes pale and hands the money back. It is a quiet triumph. She has saved her father's life and recovered a theft, all before she ever sets foot in the Lin mansion.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

借着这点喘息，嘉兰做了她最擅长的事。在荣宝阁，她看见父亲已被大掌柜的手下围住，胸膛起伏，刀刃发抖。她把掌柜拽到一旁，当着韩嬷嬷的面，平平淡淡地点破他的心病：他私吞了二十两银子，埋在账里，生怕一场真查——如今少爷归府、正盯着账——会把它们翻出来。她把若由着打起来会怎样，原原本本说给他听：官家会来，官家一来，便要翻账。那掌柜夹在“看穿太多的丫头”与“能毁他的嬷嬷”之间，面如死灰，把钱退了回去。这是一场无声的小胜。她还未踏进林府一步，便救了父亲的命，又追回了一笔贪墨。

Back at the great house, the two wives circle each other like cats who have agreed, for now, not to show their claws. Zhao Xingtang lays a feast — ostensibly to welcome her husband home from the wars, really to deliver her father's command that he fall in line, that he accept the house as she has rebuilt it and stop stirring the servants. Lin Jinqi refuses it outright, in front of the household, with a courtesy so thin it is nearly an insult. The rebuff is public, and it lands. Six years of a marriage that was a contract between families, not a bond between people, cracks a little more in the hearing of the servants. And Zhao Xingtang, who has ruled this house unchallenged in his absence, feels for the first time the weight of a master who has come home and means to be one.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

回到大宅，两位主母像两只暂且约定不露爪牙的猫，暗自周旋。赵星棠摆下酒席——表面是为丈夫接风洗尘，实则是借父命要他乖乖就范，要他接受她重新捏过的这座宅子，别再搅动下人。林锦岐当着合府的面，一口回绝，礼数薄得近乎是羞辱。这记驳回是公开的，且落了实。一段本就是家族之间的契约、而非两个人之间的牵绊的婚姻，在仆从的耳目之下，又裂开了一道。而赵星棠——在他离家的岁月里独掌这座宅子、无人能撼——头一回感到，一个归来的、决意要做主的男主人，压下来的分量。

Then Han Momo arrives with the four girls the Senior Lady Lin has chosen — household-born, unwed, fourteen and up — and the moment of reckoning comes for Zhao Xingtang. Boxed in by her mother-in-law's authority and her husband's open backing, with the whole household watching to see who truly governs the inner rooms, she has no graceful way to refuse. She accepts them. She must. Among the four are Jialan, who has spent six years teaching herself to be nobody, and Lü Jí'ér, who has spent the same years teaching herself to be seen. The girl who borrowed a dead sister's name and the girl who dreams of climbing both walk through the same gate, neither knowing what the other is, neither knowing that one of them was once promised to the man who now owns them both.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

随后韩嬷嬷领着林大太太亲选的四名丫鬟到了——家生子、未嫁、年满十四——赵星棠的清算时刻也随之而来。她被婆母的威严与丈夫的明面支持夹在当中，满府的人都看着内院究竟谁说了算，她已无体面推拒的余地。她只能收下。四人之中，有嘉兰，那个花了六年学做“无足轻重”的姑娘；也有吕蕺儿，那个花了同样六年学做“被人看见”的姑娘。借了亡妹之名的那一个，和一心要往上爬的那一个，走过同一道门，谁也不知对方底细，谁也不知她们之中，有一个曾被许给如今主宰她们两人的那个男人。

Jialan crosses that threshold and feels, with a clarity that almost stops her breath, the full weight of the irony. This is the house that broke her family — that took a grandfather's loyalty and answered it with a death sentence, that took a bride's name and erased it. And now it employs her to boil its water and sweep its floors. The man who was once promised to her, the boy she would have married, is home from the wars and sleeping under this same roof, and he does not know her. He sees a household-born girl with downcast eyes. He does not see Shen Jialan. She makes sure of that. She keeps her face blank and her stride small, and she lets the gate close behind her like a held breath finally released.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

嘉兰跨过那道门槛，一种清晰得几乎令她窒息的讽刺感，重重压了下来。这座宅子，正是折辱她家的那一座——它受了一位祖父的忠诚，却以灭门相报；它夺了一个新娘的名，又将之名除。而如今，它雇她烧水扫地。那个曾与她有婚约的男人，那个她本要嫁的少年，已从边关归来的战事里脱身，睡在同一片屋檐下，却并不识她。他看见的，是个低眉顺眼的家生丫鬟。他看不见沈嘉兰。她要确保这一点。她把脸放得木然，把步子迈得细碎，任那道门在身后合拢，像一口终于吐出的闷气。

That is episode three. No roof has fallen yet. No blood has been spilled. Only a girl has stepped through a gate she spent six years dreading, and a husband and wife who can no longer pretend to be a family. The house that disowned her blood now pays her a servant's wage to tend its fires. And the man who was once her future, unknowing and unsuspecting, is about to meet her again — not as a bridegroom meets a bride, but as a master meets a maid. The board is finally set. The game, which Jialan had hoped to watch from far away, has come to find her.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

那就是第三集。还没有房顶塌下，也还没有血溅当场。只是一个姑娘，迈进了她六年里一直害怕的那道门；一对夫妻，再也无法佯装是一家人。那座抛弃了她血脉的宅子，如今付她一份奴婢的月钱，要她照看它的炉火。而那个曾是她未来的人，毫不知情、毫无防备，就要与她重逢——不是新郎见新娘，而是主子见奴婢。棋盘，终于摆定。那场嘉兰本想远远旁观的棋局，找上了她自己。
