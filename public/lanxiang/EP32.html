Episode 32 opens on a ugly truth inside the family. Lin Jinqi learns that his brother-in-law, Lu Jian, has been beating his eldest sister, Lin Xiulai, for a long time. The sister has hidden it, the way women in these houses often do, with long sleeves and quiet excuses. But Lin Jinqi sees the marks and hears the silence, and he does not let it pass. He goes to Lu Jian and teaches him a hard lesson with his own hands, the kind of lesson a man gives when words have failed and a sister is bleeding. The beatdown is not clean, but it is clear. Lu Jian is told, in pain, that the Lin family's daughter is not his to break. The lesson is rough, the kind a soldier gives, not a polite word, but it lands. Lu Jian learns that the Lin name still protects its own, even the daughter married out. Lin Jinqi does not boast about it.
