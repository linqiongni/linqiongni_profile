# 兰香如故 — The Fragrance of Orchids Remains
## Episode 18 · Told Like a Story

Let me tell you about episode eighteen, because this is the episode where the paper the third daughter carried out of the cave becomes a body on a field, and the house learns that the treason it feared was never only a smuggler's plot but a prince's ambition wearing a plain soldier's coat. Lin Jinqi returns from the river road with the Dai family's net in ruins, and for one breath the residence believes the danger past. Then he reads the third daughter's folded paper again — not the wokou names, but the one name she circled, the brother of the girl her sister loves — and he sees what the girl in the cave saw: Du Zheng was never a smuggler's contact. He was the prince's hand inside the army, placed there by a rescue that was never chance, and the padded-cotton case that nearly took the house was his first cut, not his last, a cut dressed as a kindness the whole residence thanked him for.

He replays the years the way a reader replays a book: Du Zheng, who leapt before a blade to save his commander, was praised for a courage that needed no second look — but a man who would die for the house is a man the house would trust with its throat, and that was the point. The brother placed his sister, Du Cuique, in the residence as the eye inside the women's quarters; the sister placed the poison in the water as the hand inside the sickroom. Two siblings, one plot, threaded through the very rooms the master thought he had cleared, the very barrels he thought he had watched. Lin Jinqi, who reads people, admits the one he missed — and the admission, in a man like him, is not shame but a whetstone. He will not miss the next cut, because there will not be one he does not see coming, and the house will owe its survival to a paper a child carried out of a hill.

他把那些年如读者重翻一书：杜正，曾扑在刃前救主，受赞的勇无须再看第二眼——可一个肯为府死的人，是府肯托以喉的人，而那正是点。那兄把妹杜翠雀安进府，作内宅的眼；妹把毒下进水，作病房的守。一兄一妹，一局，穿进主子以为已清的屋、他以为已看的桶。林锦岐，读人的人，认了那一个他漏的——而这认，在如他的人，不是耻，是砺石。他不会漏下一刀，因不会有他未预见的一刀，而这府的活，将欠一张孩带出山的纸。

The Han Prince's plot, read at last, is simple and vast: stir the border, loosen the capital, and take the throne while the loyal army's flank is held by a man who answers to him. Lin Jinqi cannot name the prince without proof the court would laugh at, so he builds the proof the way he builds a trap — a false march order, sealed and meant to pass through Du Zheng's hands, naming a rendezvous that exists only to be betrayed. He tells no one but the Yongchang Marquis, because the old general is the only other hand he trusts to turn a betrayal into a noose, the only man who has stood where he stands and lived to knot the cord. The house, still sealed and sick, knows nothing; the war has simply moved from the walls to the field, and the field is where Lin Jinqi means to end it, where a traitor's paper becomes a traitor's grave.

汉王之局，终读通，简而大：搅边、松京、趁忠军之侧握在一个应他的人手里，取位。林锦岐无名那王，因无庭会笑的信；于是他如结阱般结那信——一道假调令，封了，意在过杜正的手，点一处只为被叛而设的会。他只告永昌侯，因老将是他信得过的、唯一另一能把叛拧成索的手、唯一曾立他立处而活来结那绳的人。这府仍封着病着，一无所知；战只从墙移到了场，而场，是林锦岐意欲了结它的处，是叛者的纸成叛者坟的处。

</details>

The order does its work. Du Zheng, given the flank by the false march, opens it to the prince's men at the hour he thinks no eye watches — and walks into the eye Lin Jinqi built. He believes the flank he bought with a forged order is his to sell, never guessing the very seal he carried was the trap that carried him. The betrayal is clean: a flank gone, a column exposed, the master's own men riding into a mouth that was meant for them, a house's name about to be spoken by the very traitor it fed. For one terrible moment the residence's cleared name hangs on a word; then the Yongchang Marquis, warned and waiting on the ground the order falsely named, turns the prince's ambush inside out, and the men who should have died take the men who came to kill them. The field, which Du Zheng thought was his, becomes the place the house settles its account, and the account is paid in the coin he thought was his own.

那令成了事。杜正，凭假调得了侧，在自以为无眼看的时辰，把侧开给王的人——却走进林锦岐结的眼。这叛干净：侧失、列暴露、主子自己的人骑进一张本为他们设的口，一座府的名，将由一个它养的叛者说出。有可怖的一刻，这府洗清的名，悬于一语；而后永昌侯，被警、守在那令假点的地上，把王的伏翻了里朝外，该死的人，取了来杀他们的人。这杜正以为是他自己的场，成了府算账的处，而账，以他以为是他自己的钱付。

In the chaos of the turned ambush, the Yongchang Marquis looses a single arrow — not at the prince, whom the law still shields, but at the hand that held the flank, the brother who betrayed the men who trusted him. Du Zheng falls, and with him falls the inside thread of a rebellion the court did not yet name, the last clean proof the third daughter's paper will ever need. The second daughter, told at last that the man she chose below her rank was the traitor who nearly opened her brother's throat to the enemy, sits without a sound in the room where she once declared she had chosen love. She chose, the season reminds her; she was simply the last to learn what she chose, and the last to pay for not asking sooner whether the man was worth the rank she gave up. She chose below her standing, the season says, and learned too late that the man she chose had pledged that standing to a prince who would burn it. The second daughter, who once declared she had chosen love, now understands that what she chose was a traitor's protection, and the heart she thought she won was the bait on a hook she never saw. She sits in the silence the season leaves her, and begins, slowly, to unlearn the love that cost her brother his name and the house its peace for a season.

在被翻的伏的乱里，永昌侯只放一箭——不是向那律仍护的王，是向握侧的那只手，那把信他的人卖了兄。杜正倒下，和他同倒的，是那场庭尚未名的叛的内线，是三小姐的纸永远要的最后净证。二小姐，终被告知：她低于己阶所选的那人，是几乎把兄的喉开向敌的叛者，无声坐在她曾宣告"选了爱"的屋里。她选了，这季提醒她；她只是最迟知自己选了什么的人，也最迟为没早问那人不值她放弃的阶而付价的人。

</details>

The third daughter hears of the death not with triumph but with the quiet the season has been teaching her. She gave the name, and the name is dead, and the house she feared would be undone a second time by a man it fed is instead standing, because one girl decided to act and another decided to carry the word out. She does not tell her sister she was right to fear; she sits with her, because the courage the season gave the youngest daughter was never meant to cost the second her heart, only to keep the house from paying for one man's ambition with its name. The paper, folded so long against a thigh, is at last burned, and the smoke of it is the only funeral the traitor gets, the only rite the house spares a brother who broke it. The paper, folded so long against a thigh, is at last given to the fire, and the fire does not refuse it, because a confession in smoke asks nothing of the living.

Behind the sealed residence's walls, Du Cuique hears her brother is dead. The poison that felled her has not yet left her blood, but the news lands deeper — and here the season shows its unsparing eye, because her grief is real and her blame is twisted in the same breath. She does not mourn the traitor; she mourns the only kin who climbed with her, and she lays the death at Lanxiang's door, the maid who read the water and carried the word that cut the snake's head. The two clever girls who faced each other without a screen now stand on opposite sides of a brother's corpse, and the war between them, which the poison only slowed, turns cold and final, a war no token and no token's fire will end, only a road. The exile is sent past the passes with one look back that is no farewell but a promise the walls cannot keep — that a girl who nearly ruled this house will not forget the maid who read the water and won the room. Lanxiang feels that promise settle in her palm beside the token, and files it with all the season has given her to remember. The war is not ended, only removed to a road the season has not finished walking, and a road unfinished is a debt the season will, in its own hour, come to collect.

</details>

Lin Jinqi rides back from the field vindicated a second time, and the Han Prince's plot, set back but not ended, withdraws to lick its wounds and wait for a quieter hour. The residence, still sealed, hears the master return and understands only that the danger passed; it does not know the flank that opened, the arrow that closed it, or the girl whose paper began it. But Lanxiang knows, and the Senior Lady knows, and the token in Lanxiang's palm grows heavier with what it has already done — a poisoner named, a traitor answered, a house held. Episode nineteen, when it comes, will carry the poisoner from the sickroom to the road — and the maid who answered with a cup will ask, at last, for her own name back. But that is next week's tell. For now, remember only this: in the Lin estate, a traitor's paper ends in smoke, and the hand that carried it out walks free, carrying a token that grows heavier by the day.
