# 兰香如故 — The Fragrance of Orchids Remains
## Episode 18 · Told Like A Story

Let me tell you about episode eighteen, because this is the episode where every mask the season put on comes off at once, and the cave, the well, and the barrel all speak at last. It begins with Du Zheng, who has worn the face of a grateful subordinate long enough to be trusted near the master, and who now lets that face fall. He comes at Lin Jinqi with a blade he means to use, a desperate, final lunge born of a scheme that has already begun to unravel — for his sister sits poisoned in the residence, and a man with nothing left to lose is the most dangerous kind there is. The master, who has faced a wokou snake and a Dai ambush, is not easily taken; he turns the strike aside, and in the same breath the truth of the Du family stands bare. At the same moment, word runs through the house that Du Cuique, the guest who would rule the inner rooms, has been struck down by her own hand's poison, and lies burning where the steward's cup found her.

让我跟你讲讲第十八集，因为这一集，是这季戴上的所有面具一次落尽，而山洞、井、桶，终都开了口。它始于杜正——他戴了够久的感恩下属的脸，足以被信到主子近旁，如今把那脸卸下。他持刃扑向林锦岐，是孤注一掷的最后一刺，出自一个已开始散架的局——因他妹正中毒躺在府里，而一个没什么可失的人，是最险的那种。主子见过倭蛇、避过戴家伏，岂是易取的；他格开那一击，同一息间，杜家的真面目摊开。同时，话传遍府：那要掌内宅的客杜翠雀，已被自己手的毒放倒，正烧在那管家杯找到她的地方。

</details>

The third daughter, who has carried the cave's secret since the week it happened, chooses this moment to lay it open before the household. She steps forward and names what she saw — her second sister and Du Zheng locked in a shame that should never have been spoken, in a cave on the estate's wild edge, on a day the house thought the girl merely walking off the heat. The telling is plain and clear, with none of the mercy she once meant to grant, because the mercy has been spent and the poison has made a different kind of honesty necessary. The residence, gathered and listening, receives the name the way a wound receives air — painful, but clean. What was a whisper behind a hand becomes a fact in the light, and the second sister, who thought her sin buried in a cave, finds it dug up by the very sister she wronged, and the house rearranges itself around the truth it can no longer pretend not to know.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

三小姐，自那事发生那周便藏着洞里的秘，此刻选在众人前摊开。她上前，点出所见——二姐与杜正缠在见不得人的羞里，在府外野处的山洞，在那日全家以为姑娘只是去避热的日子。说得平白清楚，没了她曾想给的半分仁，因那仁已用尽，而毒逼出了另一种诚实。聚拢听着的全府，接下那名字，如伤口接进空气——痛，但清。原是掩在掌后的低语，成了光下的实；二姐本以为她的罪埋在了洞里，却被她亏负的那个妹亲手挖出，而这座府，绕着它再也装不知的真，重新排了座次。

</details>

With the cave named and the poison proven, Lanxiang steps into the center of the gathered room and speaks the whole of it aloud. She tells the household how Du Cuique came offering herbs for the sick child, how the bath water was the true source of the sickness, how the guest who would heal was the hand that sickened, and how the steward's cup returned the draught to its maker. And when she is done, Du Cuique — burning, cornered, and stripped of every screen — answers with the only honesty left to her: she says she meant to be rid of Lanxiang, that the maid who read too much was a wall she could not climb, and that the poisoning was meant, in the end, to clear the path. It is a confession no torture could have drawn, given freely in a moment of pride and ruin, and the room goes quiet at the sound of a villain naming her own villainy without a flinch.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

洞既点明、毒既证实，兰香步到聚着的人中央，把全幅说出口。她讲杜翠雀如何借医病孩之名近身，讲澡水才是病的真源，讲那要医人的客正使病那只手，讲管家的杯如何把汤奉还下毒的人。说完，杜翠雀——烧着、被逼到角、所有屏尽去——以只剩的诚实作答：她说她本要除了兰香，那读太多的丫鬟是她爬不过的墙，而下毒，到头来，是为清路。这是刑逼不出的供，在傲与毁的一刻坦然给出；满室在一恶人毫不眨眼地自报恶行之声里，静了下来。

</details>

Then the Senior Lady Lin, who has watched from the edge of the room with the stillness of a woman who has seen households rise and fall, comes forward and does what only she can. From her sleeve she draws the antidote — kept against just such a poison, or perhaps against any poison a great house breeds within its own walls — and administers it to those the draught has struck. The burning eases, the breath returns, and the ones the poison meant to take are, by the old lady's hand, given back to the living. It is a small, enormous mercy, delivered without flourish, the kind of mercy that a household's mother keeps in reserve the way a smith keeps water by the forge. The guests and the girls who watched the confession now watch the cure, and the balance of the room tilts from dread to relief, because the hand that gave the poison has been answered by the hand that carried the cure.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

接着，林母——她自屋角以见过宅起宅落的妇人的静，看着——上前，做唯有她能做的。她从袖中抽出解药——备着防这样的毒，或许也防大宅自墙里生出的任一毒——给被那汤击中的人灌下。烧退了，气回了，那毒要取走的，借老夫人的手，还给了活人。这是小而又大的仁，不加铺张地予下，如一户之母备着的仁，如铁匠在炉边备着的水。方才看着供词的客与姑娘们，此刻看着治；屋里的秤，从惧偏向了缓，因下毒的手，已被握着解药的手应答。

</details>

The Yongchang Marquis, who owes the Lin family his life twice over this season, takes his own account of Du Zheng. When the man who would cut the master is brought before him, the old general does not hesitate; he strikes Du Zheng down with a wound that leaves him broken but alive, because a clean death would be too kind for a traitor who aimed at two households at once. And so the Du siblings, the brother who stabbed and the sister who poisoned, are taken into custody together, the both of them bound for the prison that waits at the end of treason. The cave, the well, and the barrel have all spoken, and the speakers are silenced at last. The residence, which has lived for weeks with a snake in its wall, feels the wall go quiet, and the quiet itself is a kind of guest the house had nearly forgotten how to host.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

永昌侯，这季两度欠林家性命，亲自与杜正算账。当那要刺主子的人被带到他面前，老将不含糊；他重伤杜正，留他破而不死，因干净的一死，对一处瞄着两户的家叛，未免太仁。于是杜家兄妹——刺人的兄、下毒的妹——一同收押，都奔着叛末端那座监去。山洞、井、桶，都开了口，而开口的，终被噤声。这府，数周来墙里盘着蛇，此刻觉出墙静了；而那静本身，是一种这府几乎忘了如何相待的客。

</details>

With the poisoners caged and the traitor's blade spent, the Lin estate does the oldest thing a household does when the danger passes: it sets a table and calls it peace. A feast is laid in the Zhichun Residence, the same rooms that were a sickroom and a jail and a courtroom in the span of a fortnight, and now are only a dining hall with lanterns lit against the dark. The family gathers — those who were sick and those who tended them, the old lady who held the token and the girl who earned it, the master who rode out and came back whole. They eat, and the eating is its own kind of speech, the kind that needs no words because everyone at the board has earned the right to be there. For one evening the estate is only a family, and the war, inside and out, is somewhere else, and the lanterns do not ask where.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

投毒人入了笼、叛者的刃尽了，林府行了危过之后一户最旧的事：摆桌，称作太平。宴设在知春馆——半月间既是病房、是监、是公堂的同一处屋——如今只是点灯拒夜的饭厅。一家人聚拢——病过的与侍病的，握令的老夫人与挣来令的姑娘，出骑又整全回来的主子。他们吃，而吃 itself 是一种言语，一种不需词的言语，因席上每个人都挣得了在场的资格。有一晚，这府只是个家，而墙里墙外的战，在别处；灯不问去处。

</details>

Lanxiang, who has carried the weight of the season on a maid's narrow shoulders, drinks a little more than she ought and feels the edge of the week go soft. She is, for once, not reading a lie or blocking a poisoner but simply a girl at a feast, and the wine finds the tiredness she has been refusing to feel. Lin Jinqi watches her from across the board, and when she sets her cup down and rises to go, he does a thing the household will pretend not to see: he lifts her cup and drinks what she left, the way a man drinks after the one he watches, as if the trace of her on the rim were a thing worth keeping. Then he follows her, not beside her, not ahead, but behind, a step and a half back, silent down the lantern-lit path, the way a guard follows the charge he has decided, without saying so, is his to keep.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

兰香，这季把份量压在丫鬟窄肩上，多喝了些本不该喝的，觉出这一周的棱角软了。她这回，不是在读谎、不是挡投毒人，只是一个宴上的姑娘；酒找着了她一直拒认的乏。林锦岐自席那端看她，待她放杯起身要走，他做了一件全家会装没看见的事：他端起她的杯，饮了剩的，如一个跟着他守之人的人那样饮，仿佛杯沿上她的痕，是值得留的物。然后他跟她，不并排、不超前，而在后，退一步半，沿灯路无声；如一个卫，跟着那他不言自明、认定该自己守的托。

</details>

Before the lanterns are lit for the feasting, it is worth noting what the household does not say, because this season has always spoken more in its silences than in its sentences. The Du siblings are caged, the poison answered by an antidote, the traitor's blade spent against a wall — and yet no one in the Lin residence steps forward to name the girl who made all of it possible. Lanxiang moves through the aftermath as she has moved through the whole long trouble, quietly and without expectation, as if being thanked were a collar she has long since learned to slip. The old lady who pressed the token into her palm and the master who returned her contract both know what she is, and neither yet says it plainly to the room. The season, watching a household that praises with its eyes and almost never its mouth, files this unspoken gratitude where it keeps the rest, because in the Lin estate the things left unsaid are very often the ones that matter most, and the girl who saved the house asks for nothing but to be allowed to walk away from it.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

在宴灯未点起前，值得记一记这府没说出的话，因这季一向以静多于以言来说。杜家兄妹入了笼，毒以解药应答了，叛者的刃撞了墙——可林府里没一个人上前，点出那让这一切成了可能的姑娘。兰香在余波里走着，如她在整场长麻烦里那样，静而无所期，仿佛被谢是一道她早学会滑脱的领。那把令按进她掌心的老夫人、与还她身契的主子，都知她是什么，却都没对满室坦然说出。这季看着一座以眼赞、几乎从不以口赞的府，把这份未出口的谢，收进收着别处的那一处；因在林府，未说出的，往往最是要紧，而那救了府的姑娘，只求一件：准她离去。

</details>

So we close episode eighteen the way a household closes a wound — by naming it, cleaning it, and laying it to rest under a feast. The brother's blade fell on the master and was turned; the cave's secret was spoken in the light; the poisoner confessed her own hand; the old lady's antidote answered the cup; the marquis broke the traitor; and the Du siblings went to their cage. The Lin estate, for the first time in many episodes, sleeps without a snake in its wall. Episode nineteen, when it comes, will turn from the war within to the road without, and the maid who earned a token will ask for something smaller and harder than a token — her own name, back, and the right to walk out the gate she has spent the season defending. But that is next week's tell. For now, remember only this: in the Lin estate, a poison answered by an antidote is a peace, and a peace laid with lanterns is a feast, and the girl who earned both now walks home with a silent guard a step behind.
