# 兰香如故 — The Fragrance of Orchids Remains
## Episode 24 · Told Like A Story

Let me tell you about episode twenty-four, because this is the episode where peace proves to be only the prince's friends changing their coat, and where a wedding meant to celebrate the Jing'an Marquis becomes the last trap the Han Prince's faction builds around the blood of the realm. The mourning for the war has barely ended when the Yongchang Marquis, old general and survivor of the river road, announces his long-delayed wedding, and the capital, thirsty for a reason to laugh, gathers at his hall in Jinling as it once gathered for a victory. But Zheng Baichuan, the Duke Xian and the Han Prince's cleverest remnant, reads the wedding not as a feast but as a cage — for among the guests of honor will be the Crown Prince himself, and a prince of the blood in a general's hall is a throat the rebellion can still close. The season, watching the lanterns hung, marks the celebration as the most dangerous room of the tale.

让我跟你讲讲第二十四集，因为这一集，平只被证作王的党羽换了外衣；而一场本为庆靖安侯的婚，成了汉王一党围那国之血所搭的最后一阱。为战服的丧，未及尽褪，永昌侯——那老将、江路的活者——便宣了他耽搁已久的婚，而那渴于一个笑由的京，如它曾为胜聚那般，聚到他在金陵的厅。可郑百川，显国公、汉王最狡的余党，读那婚不是作宴，是作笼——因座上宾中，将有太子本人，而一个龙子在将的厅，是叛仍合得上的喉。这季，看着挂起的灯，把那庆记作全故事最险的屋。

Zheng Baichuan's plan is patient and cold: he will let the wine flow and the actors play, and at the height of the mirth he will seal the hall and name the Crown Prince a prisoner of the Yongchang Marquis, a charge of treason that turns the old general's loyalty into a noose and keeps the realm's heir in Jinling while the capital is taken. He has men placed in the household, and a signal agreed — a single arrow loosed at the staged play upon the platform, a token the guests will mistake for part of the show. The season notes the cruelty of it: a wedding is the one gathering where every enemy is invited and every guard is drunk, and the Duke Xian means to use the joy itself as the lock on the prince's cage, the way a spider uses the fly's own wing-beat to draw it close.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

郑百川的计，耐而冷：他要任酒流、任伶人演，而在欢之极时，封厅，指太子为永昌侯的囚，一桩把老将之忠拧成索、把国之储留在金陵、趁京被取的叛罪。他在府中有人，与一个约好的号——台上那出戏到高处时放一箭，一个宾客会误作戏中一段的记。这季注其酷：婚是唯一敌皆被邀、卫皆醉的聚，而显国公意欲以那欢本身，作那龙子之笼的锁，如蛛以蝇自己的振翅引它近。

</details>

But the Crown Prince is not the fool the Duke takes him for, and the boy who wears the dragon has learned, from the war that nearly took his house, to trust the Jing'an Marquis over the smiles of uncles. He comes to Lin Jinqi before the feast and agrees to a ruse the marquis proposes: a double, a young man named Liu Xiaochuan who has walked at his shoulder for years and learned his gait and his stillness, will wear the prince's robe upon the platform and draw the arrow's eye, while the true heir slips out with the marquis by the water gate, a boat already waiting on the silent canal. It is the old lesson worn new again — a danger named is a danger halved, and a prince who lets another wear his face is a prince who may live to wear his own. The boy agrees without a tremble, for he has seen what becomes of princes who trust the wrong smile, and he would rather lend his face than lose his head upon a platform built for his glory.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

可太子不是郑公以为的愚，而这穿龙的少年，从那几乎取了他家的战里学了：信靖安侯，胜过叔伯的笑。他在宴前去找林锦岐，应了这侯所提的一计：一个替身，一个名刘晓川、多年行在他肩侧、学了他步态与静的青年，要穿太子的袍立于台上、把箭之眼引去，而真储随这侯从水门出，一艘船已在静渠上候。这是旧课又穿了新：被指了名的险是减半的险，而一个肯让别个戴自己脸的龙子，是那个或能活着戴回自己脸的龙子。

</details>

So the play upon the platform reaches its climax, and the arrow the Duke loosened as a signal tears through the air and finds, not the Crown Prince, but Liu Xiaochuan, the double, who falls upon the boards with the prince's robe pooling red around him. The guests scream; the hall breaks into panic; and in that panic the true Crown Prince is already gone, rowing the dark canal with the Jing'an Marquis at the stern, the water closing over their wake before the Duke's men reach the empty platform. The season marks the moment with its unsparing eye: the arrow meant to begin a prison instead began an escape, and the boy who wore the dragon's face so the true dragon could flee is the season's quiet hero, dying in a robe that was never his to wear. No chronicle will sing his name, yet the realm he never ruled owes its morning to the breath he spent upon a borrowed throne, and the season lays a quiet flower on the grave of a boy who was a prince for an hour.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

于是台上的戏到了极处，郑公松开作号的箭，破空而去，中的不是太子，是刘晓川那替身——他倒在板上，太子的袍在他身周洇红。宾客惊叫；厅陷入乱；而那乱里，真太子已去，与靖安侯坐船尾划着暗渠，水在他们尾迹上合拢，早于郑的人到那空台。这季以不饶的眼记下了这一刻：那本要起一座囚的箭，反起了一次逃，而那穿龙面、好让真龙得脱的少年，是这季静的英雄，死在一件从不是他该穿的袍里。

</details>

In the chaos Zheng Manyi, the Duke's daughter who has loved Lin Jinqi without return since she first saw him, sees her chance and takes it. She has come to the wedding as a guest and a wound, and when the hall breaks she moves through the panic to Lanxiang's side and, with a small blade drawn from her sleeve, drives for the marquis's lady — not to kill the realm but to kill the woman who, in her fevered mind, stole the only man worth having. The blade finds cloth and a shallow line of blood before two bodies hit her: Han Liang, the loyal retainer who has watched the lady's back since the siege, and Furong Xiu, the opera woman whose troupe taught her to read a strike before it lands. They seize Zheng Manyi and bind her, and the season notes that the last danger of the episode is a lover's, not a rebel's, and the darker for being private.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

乱中，郑蔓宜——那自初见便无报地爱着林锦岐的郑公之女——见机便下了手。她以客、以一道伤来这婚，厅一破，她穿过乱到兰香身侧，袖中抽出小刃，向这侯的夫人刺去——不是为杀那国，是为杀那在她热昏的念里夺了她唯一值得之人的妇人。刃先中衣、划出一道浅血，才有两道身扑住她：韩梁，自围城便护着夫人背的忠仆，与芙蓉秀，那戏班的妇人，她的班教她在击落前读出一击。他们擒了郑蔓宜、捆了她，而这季注：这集最后的险，是情人的，非叛者的，且因私密而更暗。

</details>

Zheng Baichuan's trap, built upon a prince he no longer holds, collapses the moment the empty platform is searched and the true heir is found gone. The Duke, surrounded by the Yongchang Marquis's now-loyal men and the Jing'an guard that Lin Jinqi summoned by water, has no arrow left to loose and no hall left to seal. He surrenders — not with honor but with the cold calculation of a man who has read that the throne he served is finished and that a living Duke Xian is worth more to the new order than a dead traitor. The season, which has watched the Han Prince's weave unravel thread by thread, closes this last knot with a whimper rather than a roar, and the rebellion that began with a shut eye and a silent road ends with an old man laying down a bow he can no longer string. His bow rests upon the floor where his ambition once stood tall, and the hall that was meant to be a cage becomes, by the grey of morning, merely a room where a wedding was almost ruined and a realm was almost lost.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

郑百川那筑在他再握不住的龙子上的阱，在空台被搜、真储被发现已去的那一刻，崩了。这郑公，被永昌侯如今忠的卒、与林锦岐由水路召的靖安卫围住，再无一箭可放、无一厅可封。他降了——非以荣，是以一个读了他所侍的位已终、而一个活的显国公对新局贵过一死叛的男人的冷算。这季，看着汉王的织一缕缕散，以一声呜咽而非一吼，收了这最后的结，而那始于一只闭眼与一条静路的叛，以一老者放下一张他再也张不开的弓终了。

</details>

The old emperor, worn thin by the war and the plot that reached his own blood, dies in the night the Duke is taken, and the realm wakes to a new reign before the old one is cold in its tomb. The Crown Prince, returned by the water gate with the Jing'an Marquis at his side, ascends as the new emperor, and the gratitude of a throne that was nearly caged flows first to the marquis who carried him out. Lin Jinqi, who began this tale as a master reading a maid, ends the episode as the power behind a new crown, and the season marks how far the river has carried them both — from a poisoned residence to a saved realm, with a village woman's burning book somewhere at the heart of it all. The old man's tomb is sealed before the new one's name is dry upon the register, and the cycle the Han Prince tried to break turns, in the end, upon the mercy of a marquis who would not let a prince drown in a silent canal.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

那被战与那及了他自己血的局磨薄的老帝，在郑公被捉的那夜崩了，而那域在一旧朝尚温于墓中时，便醒了新朝。太子，由水门归、靖安侯立其侧，即新皇位，而那几乎被笼的位之感激，先流向那带他出的侯。林锦岐，这故事以读一丫鬟的主子起，这集以一新冠之后的力终，而这季记下了江把他们俩载了多远——从一座毒宅，到一座被救的域，而其间某处心，是一村妇焚的书。

</details>

And Lanxiang, now the Jing'an lady and no longer merely a reader of houses, takes the quiet hour after the new reign to do the one thing the season has been waiting for her to do: she goes alone to the shrine where the Shen family's tablet waits, and she speaks to the blood she carries beneath the borrowed name. She tells Shen Jialan's parents that the wrong done their house was forged by the Zhao family at a prince's order, that the proof burned in a cart upon a rebel road, and that she, their daughter returned in another's skin, has lived to see the throne changed and the forgers fallen. It is not yet justice — the name is still buried, the account still closed — but it is a promise spoken to the dead by the one who owes them everything, and the season lets the lady stand before the tablet as the girl Shen Jialan, if only for the length of a single held breath.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

而兰香，如今靖安夫人、不再只是读宅的人，趁新朝之后的静时，做了这季等她做的一件事：她独去那沈家牌位候着的庙，向那借名之下她背着的血说话。她告诉沈嘉兰的双亲：加给他们家的冤，是赵家奉王命伪造的，那证焚在一辆叛路上的车里，而她，换了皮归来的女儿，活到了见位易、伪造者倾。这还不是公义——名仍埋着，账仍合着——但这是一个欠他们一切的人，对死者说出的诺，而这季容这夫人，以沈嘉兰之女身，立在牌位前，哪怕只在一息屏住的长短里。

</details>

So we close episode twenty-four the way a new reign closes an old account — not with the sword but with the telling, and with a lady speaking her buried name to the dead she could not yet save. The wedding became a trap; the double took the arrow so the true prince could flee by water; Zheng Manyi's love turned to a blade and was bound by a retainer and an opera woman; the Duke surrendered; the old emperor died and the new one rose; and Lanxiang, alone at the Shen tablet, promised the parents she carries in her blood that their daughter has not forgotten them. Episode twenty-five, when it comes, will bring the mourning to an end and the marriage to the altar, and the two mothers who gave the lady her name and her rank will faint at the news of it. But that is next week's tell. For now, remember only this: in the Lin estate, a prince's escape is a double's death, and the lady who burns a book may yet speak the name the book was meant to clear.
