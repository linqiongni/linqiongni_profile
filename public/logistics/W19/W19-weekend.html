一、场景复述：3,000 平米德国仓，做 3PL 的两层合同

我方以德国 GmbH 身份，向本地工业地产基金租 3,000 平米仓库，对外做第三方海外仓。这票业务的法律骨架是两层：第一层租约（我方↔房东，解决「有没有地方」），第二层仓储协议（我方↔卖家客户，解决「货怎么管、怎么赔」）。两层必须对齐——尤其是转租权（租约允许我方分租，仓储协议才能合法收客户）和终止后货物处置（租约退场宽限期 ≥ 仓储协议客户搬离期）。本实战把这两层各产出一份可直接谈判/起草的文本。

记忆点：3PL 海外仓的命脉是「租约有转租权 + 仓储协议能合法收租」。两层不对齐，要么分租违约被房东解约，要么客户货在租约终止时被房东留置。谈判先从租约转租权破局。

条款 1 服务范围 Scope of Services（中文要点）

明确含：到仓收货清点、上架、常温存储、按指令拣货打包、贴标、退件收检与良/残判定。不含：尾程运费（代收代付）、特殊包材/组装/维修/销毁（另计费）。超范围服务列价目表。

Scope of Services. The Provider shall provide inbound receiving & counting, put-away, storage at ambient temperature, pick & pack upon Customer's instructions, labeling, and returns intake with good/defective segregation. Last-mile freight is pass-through only. Value-added services (custom packaging, kitting, repair, destruction) are charged per the then-current price schedule and require a separate work order.

条款 2 验收与异议期 Acceptance & Objection Period（中文要点）

货到仓 48 小时内（待核实）客户未提数量/外观异议，视为验收合格。隐蔽瑕疵在发现后合理期内提，但最迟不超入仓后 X 日（待核实）。逾期我方不担不符责任。

Acceptance & Objection Period. Goods are deemed accepted unless the Customer raises a written objection as to quantity or apparent condition within 48 hours of inbound receipt. Latent defects must be notified within a reasonable time after discovery, and in any event no later than [X] days after intake. After expiry, the Provider bears no liability for such discrepancies.

条款 3 责任限额与保价 Limitation of Liability & Valuation（中文要点）

基础限额：每次事故赔至 EUR [XX,XXX] 或货值 [X]%（待核实，须与保险对接）。高值货客户须申报价值并付保价费，超限按保价赔；未保价按基础限额。故意/重大过失不免责（德国民法对预先免除重大过失有限制，待核实）。

Limitation of Liability & Valuation. Except for willful misconduct or gross negligence (which cannot be excluded to the extent prohibited by mandatory law), the Provider's aggregate liability per occurrence is capped at EUR [XX,XXX] or [X]% of the goods' declared value, whichever is lower, and is aligned with the Provider's insurance coverage. High-value goods must be declared and insured at an additional valuation fee; undeclared goods are covered only up to the base limit.

条款 4 费用与滞纳金 Fees & Late Payment（中文要点）

仓储费按 [体积/库位/件] 口径计，设每月保底 EUR [XXX]（待核实）。逾期付款按日 [X]% 计滞纳金（利率须符合中德法律上限，待核实）。连续逾期超 [X] 日，我方有权停服并行使留置。

Fees & Late Payment. Storage fees are charged on a [per-volume / per-location / per-unit] basis with a monthly minimum of EUR [XXX]. Overdue amounts accrue late interest at [X]% per day (subject to applicable statutory caps). Data Processing (GDPR). The Customer acts as Controller and the Provider as Processor under GDPR Article 28. Lien (Zurückbehaltungsrecht). To secure outstanding fees, the Provider may exercise a statutory commercial lien over the Customer's goods under the German Commercial Code (HGB). The Provider shall give prior written notice and a grace period of [X] days; the lien extends only to goods attributable to the overdue amount, and after expiry the Provider may realize the goods by law.

任一方可提前 [X] 日通知终止（或依租约转不定期）。终止后客户须 [X] 日内搬离/导出库存；逾期我方收超期仓储费并有权处置。我方配合库存导出与数据可携，不得锁仓要挟。

Termination & Handover. Either party may terminate upon [X] days' written notice. Upon termination the Customer must remove or arrange shipment of its inventory and export all data within [X] days; thereafter the Provider may charge extended storage and dispose of unclaimed goods. Governing Law & Jurisdiction. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Germany, excluding the UN Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods (CISG) as inapplicable to services. The exclusive place of jurisdiction is the courts at the location of the warehouse (or, at the Provider's option, arbitration under the DIS Rules).

四、落到动作：两层合同对齐清单

  1. 租约转租权 ↔ 仓储协议收客户：先拿转租权，再签客户，顺序不能反。
  2. 租约终止宽限期 ↔ 仓储协议客户搬离期：租约宽限 ≥ 客户搬离期，留缓冲。
  3. 租约保证金 ↔ 仓储协议预存费：用客户预存费覆盖我方对房东保证金风险。
  4. 租约保险（财产/营业中断）↔ 仓储协议责任险：两层险种互补，不留空窗。
  5. 所有 BGB/HGB 条号、金额、时限、利率，落地前由德国执业律师按现行法核定并标注。
实战口诀：3PL 海外仓两层合同要咬合：租约给「转租权+退场宽限」，仓储协议才能合法收客户、平稳退场。租约谈判七项先破转租权，仓储协议八条把「赔多少」用限额+保价+留置封顶。所有德文条号与金额落地前一律待核实——框架我写、核定交律师。
