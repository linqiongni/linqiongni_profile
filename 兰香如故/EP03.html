Let me tell you about episode three. Episode two left our girl Lanxiang — still secretly Shen Jialan — three streets from the family that disowned hers, and it left Lin Jinqi, the man who was once promised to her, a war hero coming home to a cold house. Episode three is the moment those two orbits finally, inevitably, begin to tilt toward each other — and it starts with a betrayal that isn't Jialan's at all.
🇨🇳 中文翻译

让我跟你讲讲第三集。第二集把我们的姑娘兰香——其实仍是沈嘉兰——搁在了抛弃她家的那一家三条街外，也把曾与她有婚约的林锦岐，一个成了英雄、却回到冰冷家里的男人，摆在了归途上。第三集，是这两个人的轨迹终于、不可避免地开始彼此倾斜的时刻——而它始于一场与嘉兰无关的背叛。

Lin Jinqi has been away six years, fighting the coastal raiders. He comes home expecting his house to be his. Instead he finds that his wife, Zhao Xingtang, has spent those years quietly remaking it. His own trusted maid, Yuchan — the girl who used to be at his shoulder — has been married off to a simpleton, on Zhao Xingtang's order. The message is unmistakable: the general has been away, and in his absence the lady of the house has taken everything that was his and made it hers. He has been emptied out of his own home.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

林锦岐离乡六年，在外抗击海疆来犯之敌。他归来时，本以为这座宅子还是他的。可他发现，妻子赵星棠在这六年里，已悄悄将它重新捏了一遍。他身边最得用的丫鬟玉蝉——那个素来跟在他身后的姑娘——已被赵星棠做主，许给了一个傻子。意思再明白不过：将军在外，主母便趁他不在，把他身边的一切尽数收归己有。他，被从自己的家里掏空了。

So Lin Jinqi does the one thing a man in his position can do: he reaches for his mother's old plan and decides to bring in a fresh set of maids — household-born girls, the ones the Lin family owns from birth, now old enough to serve inside the inner rooms. It's not charity. It's a quiet countermove, a way to plant his own eyes and ears where his wife has planted hers. And the decree goes out: every household-born girl of fourteen or more, unwed, is to be brought into the main house.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

于是林锦岐做了身处他这般境地的人唯一能做的事：他拾起母亲旧日的谋划，决意重新纳一批新丫鬟——林家世代为奴、生来便是家生子的姑娘，如今到了可以进内院当差的年纪。这并非善心。这是一步不动声色的反击，是要在妻子布下眼线之处，也安上自己的耳目。号令就此传出：凡家生子年满十四、尚未婚配的，一律选入正府。

That decree falls like a stone into the Xu family's courtyard. Han Momo — the old stewardess who runs such errands — comes to collect. Xu's wife, desperate to keep the girl who has become her daughter, begs the head clerk of the Rongbao Pavilion to plead for an exemption. The Da Zhanggui, the same man Jialan once outsmarted over a painting, pretends to help. He lies. He takes the goodwill and does nothing, and Jialan is pulled out of the only home she has known in six years and taken toward the great house she most dreads.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

这道号令，像一块石头落进了许家的院子。管这等差事的韩嬷嬷来人挑选。许母舍不得这个已然当作亲生女儿的姑娘，去求荣宝阁大掌柜通融。大掌柜，正是当年被嘉兰以一幅画将了一军的那一个，佯装相助。他骗了。收了人情，却什么也没办，嘉兰就这样被从六年来唯一的家里拽出，朝那座她最怕走近的宅子走去。

Her father snaps. The gentle Xu Wanquan, who has never raised a hand to anyone, grabs a kitchen cleaver and goes after the Da Zhanggui, ready to die over his daughter. Jialan, walking ahead in the stewardess's grip, hears of it and is seized with terror — not of the house, but of bloodshed. A man dead in a courtyard would undo everything. So she turns to Han Momo on the road and pleads her own case: let me at least go back to the Pavilion first, she says, to put my father's heart at ease. The old woman, perhaps moved, perhaps practical, agrees.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

她的父亲崩溃了。从不曾对谁动过手的许万全，抓起一把厨刀，追着大掌柜而去，为女儿不惜拼命。走在前头、落在韩嬷嬷手中的嘉兰听得此事，惊惧攫住了她——怕的不是那座宅子，是流血。一条人命横在院子里，便什么都完了。于是她在路上转向韩嬷嬷，为自己陈情：容我至少先回一趟荣宝阁，安了父亲的心。老嬷嬷或是动容，或是觉着划算，应了。

And in that small reprieve, Jialan does what she does best. She corners the Da Zhanggui and names his fear out loud: he'd skimmed twenty taels from the accounts and buried them, terrified that a real audit would surface them. She says it plainly, in front of Han Momo, and the man — caught between a girl who sees too much and a stewardess who could ruin him — turns pale and hands the money back. It's a quiet triumph, the kind only Jialan seems to notice. She has just saved her father's life and recovered a theft, all before she ever sets foot in the Lin mansion.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

借着这点喘息，嘉兰做了她最擅长的事。她把大掌柜逼到墙角，当众点破他的心病：他私吞了二十两银子，埋在账里，生怕一场真查把它翻出来。她就当着韩嬷嬷的面，平平淡淡地说出口，那掌柜夹在“看穿太多的丫头”与“能毁了他的嬷嬷”之间，面如死灰，把钱退了回去。这是一场无声的小胜，只有嘉兰自己看在眼里。她还未踏进林府一步，便救了父亲的命，又追回了一笔贪墨。

Back at the great house, the two wives circle each other. Zhao Xingtang lays a feast — ostensibly to welcome her husband home, really to deliver her father's command that he fall in line. Lin Jinqi refuses it outright, in front of the household. The rebuff is public, and it lands. Then Han Momo arrives with the four girls Lin's mother has chosen, and Zhao Xingtang — boxed in by her mother-in-law's authority and her husband's open backing — has no choice but to accept them. Among the four are Jialan and Lü Jí'ér. The girl who borrowed a dead sister's name walks through the gate of the family that broke hers, and the board is finally set.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

回到大宅，两位主母暗自周旋。赵星棠摆下酒席——表面是为丈夫接风，实则是借父命要他乖乖就范。林锦岐当着合府的面，一口回绝。这记驳回是公开的，且落了实。随后韩嬷嬷领着林母亲选的四名丫鬟到了，赵星棠被婆母的威严与丈夫的明面支持夹在当中，无可奈何，只得收下。四人之中，有嘉兰，也有吕蕺儿。那个借了死去的妹妹之名的姑娘，就此走进折辱了她家的宅门，棋盘，终于摆定。

That's episode three. No roof, no blood, not yet. Just a girl stepping through a gate she spent six years dreading, and a husband and wife who can no longer pretend. The house that disowned her family now employs her to boil its water and sweep its floors — and the man who was once promised to her is, without knowing it, about to meet her again.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

那就是第三集。还没有房顶，也还没有血。只是一个姑娘，迈进了她六年里一直害怕的那道门；一对夫妻，再也无法佯装无事。那座抛弃了她家的宅子，如今雇她烧水扫地——而那个曾与她有婚约的男人，正要在毫不知情中，与她重逢。
