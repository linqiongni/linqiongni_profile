# 兰香如故 — The Fragrance of Orchids Remains
## Episode 19 · Told Like A Story

Episode 19 sweeps up the last of the broken pieces. The Dai family, the hand behind the smuggling and the wokou trouble that Lin Jinqi and the Yongchang Marquis have been fighting for episodes, is finally pulled down to the root. Every accomplice, every name in the account books, every man who moved forbidden cotton under a trusted sign, is found and dealt with. The court takes them, the marquis confirms them, and the snake that EP16 warned about loses its last coil. The household feels it before it is told. The letters stop coming, the watchers on the road stand down, and the old lady sleeps through a night without a knock. Lanxiang watches all this from the rooms she now runs, and she knows the quiet is real this time. The enemy outside the wall and the enemy inside the bath house were always one plot. With both cut, the Lin estate can finally draw a breath that is only air, not fear.

第十九集把碎了一地的残片扫净。戴家——那支藏在私运和倭寇乱子后头、林锦岐与永昌侯打了好几集的手——终于连根拔了。每个同党、账本上每个名、每个借信得过的招牌运禁棉的人，都查到、办了。官家收了，侯爷认了，第十六集警告过的那蛇，最后一圈也松了。府里没等告知就先觉出。信不来了，路上的眼撤了，老夫人一觉到天亮没敲门声。兰香在如今她管的屋里看着，知道这回静是真的。墙外的敌和澡房里的敌，本是一桩。两头都斩，林府总算能喘一口只是气的气，不是怕。

</details>

With the house calm, Lanxiang goes to Senior Lady Lin and asks for the one thing a bonded maid almost never asks. Her freedom. She does not beg. She lays out what she has done, plainly, the way she does everything. She found the poison in the water. She tied the proof. She held the inner house through the fever and the lie. She kept the children alive. That was work, she says, not luck, and a free person could have done no more. The old lady hears her, and there is pride in it, because the girl she trusted has become the girl who earned her way out. Lanxiang asks for her bond to be cut, for the contract that says she is property to be torn, so she can walk out the gate as a person and not a thing. It is a bold ask. In the Lin estate, it is also the right one. The old lady does not refuse.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

府里静了，兰香去见林母，问那桩奴婢几乎从不问的：自由。她不讨。她把自己做的平白摆出，像她做所有事那样。她在水中找出了毒，把证拴牢，撑着内宅过完那场热和谎，护住孩子。那是活计，她说，不是运，自由人也不过如此。老夫人听着，里头有骄傲，因为信过的姑娘成了自己挣出门的姑娘。兰香要剪了她的身契，把那张说她是物的纸撕了，好让她以人、不是以物出府。这问得大胆。在林府，这也是该问的。老夫人没推。

</details>

Lin Jinqi is the one who makes it clean. When Senior Lady Lin tells him, he agrees without a pause, almost as if he had been waiting for her to ask. He goes to the chest and takes out Lanxiang's bond, the paper that named her a bought servant, and he puts it in her hand and tells her to tear it. Not later, now. He adds a pouch of silver, settlement money, enough to rent a roof and eat through a lean season, because letting a girl walk out with nothing would be its own kind of cruelty. Lanxiang holds the torn paper and the silver and feels a weight she did not know she carried slide off her shoulders. For the first time she is Xu Lanxiang, a free woman, not a maid of the Zhichun Residence. Lin Jinqi watches her face and says little. He only tells her the gate is open, and she may come back or not, as she pleases.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

把事办干净的是林锦岐。林母告诉他，他一口应下，像早等她问。他到箱前取出兰香身契——那张写她是被买丫鬟的纸——塞她手里，叫她撕。不是以后，现在。他又添一袋银子，安家钱，够租个屋顶、熬过紧季，因为放姑娘空手走，是另一种狠。兰香攥着撕碎的纸和银，觉出肩上不知何时压的那分量滑走了。头回她是许兰香，自由身，不是知春馆的丫鬟。林锦岐看她脸，话少，只说门开着，回不回，随她。

</details>

To make the freedom real, they must walk it at the yamen, where the bond is struck from the books. So Lanxiang and Lin Jinqi set out together, a strange pair, the freed girl and the young master, through the morning streets. And on those streets they run into a sight neither expected. Zhao Xingtang, the woman who was Lin Jinqi's wife until the library broke them apart, is leaving to be married. The bridal chair passes, the red cloth up, the drums thin, and for a moment the two of them just watch it go. There is no scene. Lin Jinqi's face does not change, because that door closed long ago and the poison in his own house taught him what matters. Lanxiang looks at the chair and thinks of the girl who played the fox fur and lost the room, and she feels something like relief that her own road goes the other way. The chair turns the corner. The yamen is ahead.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

自由要落到实处，得去衙门销契。于是兰香和林锦岐一道出门——一对怪组合，自由的姑娘和年轻主子——走过早市街。就在这街上，两人撞见都没料到的景：赵星棠，那个藏书楼拆散前还是林锦岐妻子的女人，正出嫁。喜轿过，红布遮着，鼓声稀，俩人愣看它走。没闹。林锦岐脸不变，因为那扇门早关了，自家府里的毒教他懂了什么要紧。兰香看那轿，想起玩狐皮披肩、丢了屋的那个姑娘，竟有点松快——自己的路朝另一头走。轿拐了角。衙门在前头。

</details>

While Lanxiang's bond is being cut, the Du sister's fate is being set far from the feast. Du Cuique, the poisoner, is sentenced to exile in Qiongzhou, the far island where the court sends the living it does not wish to kill. It is a long, hard road, and most who go do not return. Lin Jinqi, who owes the Du family a brother's debt he never asked for, quietly makes the road less cruel. He sends word ahead, a little money, a kind turn from a guard who owes him. Not mercy for the crime, he would never call it that, but a man does not let a debt of the body go unpaid, even to an enemy. So Du Cuique rides south in chains, alive, sick, and forgotten by the house she meant to rule. Lanxiang hears of the arrangement and says nothing. She knows the difference between justice and vengeance, and Lin Jinqi, it seems, does too.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

兰香销契时，杜家姑娘的命在宴外定下。投毒的杜翠雀，判流放琼州——那远岛是官家放活人不杀的去处。路长而苦，去的大多回不来。林锦岐欠杜家一份兄弟情，从没讨过，却悄悄把路铺得不那么狠：递话在前，一点银，一个欠他情的护卫转个圜。不是赦罪，他绝不会这么叫，但人身的债，对头也认。于是杜翠雀戴链南去，活着、病着，被她想掌的府忘了。兰香听这安排，没作声。她懂公道和报复之分，林锦岐，看来也懂。

</details>

Back in the estate, the second daughter's trouble is only beginning to show. She was the one Du Zheng pulled into the cave, the one the third daughter shielded and the family hushed. But the silence let the lie root deep. The second daughter, flattered and used, fell for the guard's pretty words and thought herself in love with a man who meant to use a daughter of the house as a stair. She does not see it yet, or will not. She still speaks of Du Zheng as if he were a wronged man, not the blade at her brother's throat. The house watches her with a mix of pity and worry, because a daughter who cannot read a snake is a daughter who will step on one again. The old lady is gentle with her, but the gentleness has an edge now. The family has paid too much for one girl's blindness to pretend it is nothing.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

府里，二小姐的麻烦才刚露。她是被杜正拽进洞的那个，是三小姐护着、全家按下的那个。可那沉默让谎扎了深根。二小姐被哄、被用，信了那仆好听的话，以为爱上一个拿府里姑娘当踏脚石的人。她还看不清，或不肯看，仍把杜正当委屈的人说，不是抵兄长喉咙的刀。府里看她，怜里掺忧，因为认不出蛇的姑娘，会再踩上去。老夫人对她软，可这软如今带了刃。全家为这一个姑娘的瞎，付得太多，装没事不起来。

</details>

Old Master Lin, the head of the line, calls the family and lays the matter down without softness. He says the second daughter's name is now tied to a guard's, and in a great house that tie is a stain that reaches the marriage offers of every sister. He proposes a remedy, plain and hard. The family must mend the breach with the Yongchang Marquis, the man Du Zheng meant to kill, the man whose favor keeps the Lin name clean. The old master means to bind the house to the marquis by marriage, and the obvious bride is the daughter whose name was dragged through the cave. It is a cold calculation, but in his mind a kind one: marry into the marquis's protection, and the second daughter's shame becomes the family's shield. The room hears it and goes still, because everyone knows what it costs the girl who would be given, not asked.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

林家当家老太爷唤齐家人，毫不软地摊开。他说二小姐的名如今拴在个仆身上，大宅里这拴是污，会沾到每个姐妹的亲事。他提个硬方子：得修补和永昌侯的裂——杜正要杀的那个、凭他青眼林家名才清的那个。老太爷要以婚事把府绑到侯爷身上，现成的新娘就是被拖过洞的那个姑娘。算得冷，在他心里却是善：嫁进侯爷的护，二小姐的羞便成了府的盾。屋里听了静，因为谁都懂，这要那姑娘的代价——是被给，不是被问。

</details>

Before the old master's plan can land on the second daughter's head, the third daughter steps forward. She says she will go in her sister's place. Not because she was asked, because she was not, but because she cannot watch her sister be given to a man as a repair job for a wall. She tells the old master she is younger, unwed, and willing, and that a marriage to the Yongchang Marquis would bind the house just as well and spare the second daughter a life she did not choose. The room freezes. The second daughter looks up, shamed and saved at once. The old master studies the girl who once coughed in a cave to save a sister, and sees she would do it again in broad day. He does not answer at once. He only nods, slow, because the offer is brave and the math is the same. The third daughter has, without raising her voice, rewritten her own life.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

老太爷的方子还没落到二小姐头上，三小姐上前了。她说她替姐去。不是被问——没被问——是因为她看不得姐姐被当补墙的料给个男人。她跟老太爷说，她小、未嫁、情愿，嫁永昌侯一样能绑住府，还免了二姐一段没选的命。屋里冻住。二小姐抬头，又羞又救。老太爷打量这姑娘——曾在洞边咳一声救姐的——看出她大白天也肯再来一回。他没立刻答，只缓缓点头，因为这请勇敢，账却一样。三小姐没提高嗓，改写了自己的命。

</details>

So episode 19 closes the door on the poison and opens one on the price. The Dai net is cut to the root, and Lanxiang walks out of bondage with a torn paper and a pouch of silver, free for the first time. She and Lin Jinqi pass Zhao Xingtang's bridal chair and neither turns back. Du Cuique rides south in chains, alive by a debt Lin Jinqi would not let die. Inside, the second daughter's foolish heart nearly costs the family its name, and the old master moves to mend it with a marriage. Then the third daughter, the quiet one who started this whole reckoning by a cave, gives herself in her sister's place. Episode 20 will follow Lanxiang out the gate and into the city, where a cheap room hides a trap and a scholar at a book shop turns his head. But that is next time. For now, remember this. In the Lin estate, freedom is earned in a sick room, and a sister's life is bought with a sister's own.
