Let me tell you about episode nineteen, because this is the episode where the war finally ends and the house discovers that ending a war is its own kind of trouble, the kind that asks what to do with the people who won it and the people who were merely caught inside it. The Dai family's accomplices, every last name the investigation could reach, are brought to account — some to the magistrate's rod, some to the prison that has been filling all season, and the wokou thread that began this whole long unraveling is, for the moment, tied off. The estate breathes. The sickrooms empty, the seals on the gate come down, and the Lin household steps back into the city it had been cut off from. And yet the breathing is careful, the way a man who has been held underwater breathes — grateful, but not yet trusting the air, because the season has taught this house that peace, like poison, can be poured by a hand that smiles.

让我跟你讲讲第十九集，因为这一集，是战终于收尾、而这座府发觉收尾本身也是一种麻烦——一种要你处置赢家、也处置 merely 被卷进去的人的麻烦。戴家同党，查得到的每一个名字，都得了交代——有的吃官杖，有的入那整季在填的监；而掀起这一整场散局的倭寇线，此刻暂且系住了。府喘了气。病房空了，门上的封落了，林府踏回那座它曾被隔断的城。可那喘是小心翼翼的，如一个被按在水下的人喘气——感激，却还不信那气，因这季教会这府：太平，和毒一样，可以是笑着的手倒出来的。

Into this careful breathing walks Lanxiang, and she asks for the one thing the season has been bending her toward without her naming it. Having done the house a service it cannot deny — having read the water, caught the poisoner, carried the word that saved the marquis — she goes to the Senior Lady Lin and requests to be removed from the register of slaves. It is a small word and a large one: it means she would cease to be furniture, cease to be a thing the household owns, and become a free woman who could, if she wished, walk out the gate with no one's leave. She asks it plainly, without tears and without flattery, the way a steward asks for a wage she has earned. The old lady, who has seen the girl become the hand the house runs through, is not surprised that the ask has come, only that it comes so quietly, and from a mouth that has learned to ask for others before herself.

在这小心翼翼的喘气里，兰香走来，讨那这季一直把她往那处弯、她却未点名的东西。她既立了这府无法否认的功——读了水、捉了投毒人、带出救了侯的话——便去见林母，请脱了奴籍。这是个轻的词，也是个重的：意即她不再作家具、不再作府里拥有的物，而成了一个自由身，若愿，可无人准而出门。她平白地讨，不泪不谄，如管家讨她挣来的酬。老夫人见了姑娘成了府所凭的那只手，不惊这请会来，只惊它来得这样静，且出自一张先学着为别人讨、后为自己讨的口。

Lin Jinqi, when the request reaches him, agrees with a readiness that surprises no one who has watched the two of them this season. He does not haggle over a maid who has saved his name and his marquis; he simply returns the contract of her body — the paper that says she is owned — into her own hands, and he adds a sum of silver for her to set up a household of her own, the kind of parting gift a master gives a retainer he respects. There is no speech about gratitude, only the plain transaction of a man who knows a debt when he owes one. And so the two of them, the master and the maid who is ceasing to be a maid, walk out together toward the yamen, where the clerks will strike her name from the rolls and make the freedom real on paper, the way freedom in this world is always, in the end, a thing written down.

林锦岐接到这请，应得爽快，凡这季看过他二人者都不意外。他不为一个救了他名与侯的丫鬟讨价；只把那写她身属谁的身契，交还她自己手，又添一笔安家银，好教她立起自己的户——那是主子给敬重之随从的离别之物。无半句谢词，只有一个认得债、且知欠时的人，平白的交易。于是他二人，主子与那正不再是丫鬟的丫鬟，同走向衙门，那里书吏会把她的名从册上削去，教那自由在纸上成真，如这世上的自由，终归，总是一件写下的物。

On the road to the yamen, however, the season hands them a sight neither expected. They come upon Zhao Xingtang — Lin Jinqi's former wife, the woman who left this very household to make her own life — being married, or remarried, to another, with the small procession and the red that such days carry. It is a strange moment for the master to witness, the closing of a door he had long since walked through, and he does not linger or look twice; he only notes it, the way a man notes weather, and keeps walking. Lanxiang, beside him, says nothing of it either, but she files the picture the way she files everything — as one more piece of the world's turning, where the people who leave a house go on to wed beneath other roofs, and the house they left keeps its own counsel and its own road.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

然去衙门的路上，这季递来一幅两人都没料见的景。他们撞见赵星棠——林锦岐的前妻，那个离了这府去立自己生活的女人——正嫁与、或再嫁他人，带着那等日子所有的细小仪仗与红。对主子，这是奇特的片刻，一扇他早走过、如今阖上的门；他不留步、不回头，只如记天气般记下了，继续走。兰香在侧，也不置一词，却如收一切那样收下这画面——当作这世转的又一片：离了府的人，在别檐下成婚，而他们离的那府，守着自己的主张与自己的路。

While the contract is being struck and the silver counted, word comes of Du Cuique's fate, and it is not the death her poisoning might have earned. She is sentenced to exile in Qiongzhou, the distant island where the court sends those it wishes to forget rather than kill, and there the story might have ended in plain hardship. But Lin Jinqi, who has reasons of his own — old loyalty to her brother, perhaps, or a mercy he does not name — quietly arranges things on the road she will travel: a word here, a coin there, a small easing of the rough passage that exile means for a woman of the inner rooms. No one is told, and the arrangement changes nothing of the sentence, only the manner of it. It is the kind of unseen kindness this master deals in, the sort that leaves no mark on the record but perhaps a lighter step on a far southern road.

身契将销、银两在数之际，杜翠雀的结局传到，而那不是她下毒该得的死。她被判流放琼州——那远岛，是朝廷要忘而非杀之人所去；故事本可在平白的苦里收尾。但林锦岐，自有他的缘由——或许对她的兄旧谊，或许一种他不名的仁——在她将行的路上，悄悄打点：此处一句、彼处一钱，稍缓了流放之于内宅女子的那路颠簸。无人得知，而这打点不改判半分，只改其方式。这是这主子行的一种不见的仁，一种不在卷上留痕、却或许教那南方远路上步子轻些的仁。

The Du family's ruin, however, has left a quieter casualty inside the Lin walls: the second Miss Lin, who had allowed herself to be drawn into Du Zheng's deception, who believed his pretty words and his schemes and let her heart sit where it should not. With the brother caged and the sister exiled, the full shape of the trick falls on her at last, and she is left not with a lover but with a lesson the season taught cruelly. She is, in the household's eyes, a girl who was fooled, and the fooling is its own kind of stain in a family that prizes standing above most things. The residence watches her with the particular pity of those who have not been fooled, and the old master, who brooks little disorder in his bloodline, must decide what is to be done with a daughter who mistook a traitor for a suitor.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

然杜家的毁，在林府墙内留了个更静的伤者：二小姐，她曾任自己被杜正的骗术卷进去，信了他的好话与算计，把心放到了不该放处。兄入了笼、妹流了放，那骗的全形终落她身上，她剩的不是情人，是这季残酷教下的一课。在全家眼里，她是个被哄的姑娘，而在这把门第看得最重的家，被哄本身就是一种渍。府里以那些未被哄过之人特有的怜，看着她；而老太爷，容不得血脉里乱，须定夺：对一个把叛者当良人的女儿，该如何处。

It is the Old Master Lin who proposes the handling, and his word in this house is close to law. He sets a course that would bind the second daughter to the consequence of her own error — a marriage arranged to mend the standing the deception endangered, a way of folding the shame back into the order of things. The plan is practical, and cold, the kind of thing a man of his years reaches for when feelings have cost him enough. The household braces for it, because such arrangements are made without asking the bride, and the second daughter, who has already lost one future to a lie, now stands to lose the next to a remedy. The season, which has been turning daughters into pawns all along, lays another on the board and waits, with its usual cold patience, to see who will move it and what price the moving will draw from the family that thought the game already won.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

是林老太爷定了处置，而他在这府的话，近乎法。他定一条路，要把二小姐绑在她自己错的后果上——一桩为补那被骗危及的门第而安排的婚，一种把羞折回秩序的办法。这计务实、且冷，是年月够长的男人，在情已费他够多之后，伸手去抓的那种。全家为它绷了劲，因这等安排从不问新娘；二小姐既已因一个谎失了一重将来，如今要因一剂 remedy 失下一重。这季一路把女儿作棋子，又摆一子在盘上，且看谁去动它。

But before the old master's plan can settle, the third daughter steps forward, and she does the bravest and strangest thing the episode will hold. She offers herself in her sister's place — she will marry the Yongchang Marquis, the old general her family owes its name to, rather than let the second daughter be given to a remedy she did not choose. It is a sacrifice laid down without fanfare, born of the same protecting instinct that made her hide the cave, and it reshapes the room's air in an instant: the sister who was fooled is spared, the household's standing is mended by a willing bride rather than an unwilling one, and the third daughter, who began the season a watcher in the dark, ends it offering her own hand to bind the family whole. The old master, who thought to move a pawn, finds instead a daughter who moved herself.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

但老太爷的计未及定，三小姐便上前，做了这集最勇也最奇的事。她代姐而出——她愿嫁永昌侯，那林家欠其名的老将，而不让二姐被许给一剂她没选的 remedy。这是无声放下的牺牲，出于那教她藏起山洞的同一护念；它一瞬改了屋里的空气：被哄的姐免了，府的门第由一愿意的新娘、而非不愿的，补了起来；而三小姐，这季始于暗处一个看的人，终于伸出自己的手，把家绑回完整。老太爷本要动一子，却见一个自己动了自己的女儿。

So we close episode nineteen the way a house closes a book of accounts — by settling every name, and finding one of them volunteered rather than forced. The Dai accomplices answered for their part; Lanxiang asked for and was given her own name back; Zhao Xingtang wed beneath another roof as the master walked by; Du Cuique went to her island with a lighter road than she earned; and the second daughter's folly was met, not by the old master's cold plan, but by the third's willing hand. The Lin estate, for the first time, sees a daughter marry for the family by her own choice. Episode twenty, when it comes, will carry that marriage toward the altar and the maid who is free toward a small house of her own, and the road between them will prove shorter and stranger than either expects. But that is next week's tell.
