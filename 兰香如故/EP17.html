Episode 17 picks up where the sickness left everyone frightened. The third daughter finds Lanxiang alone by the well and tells her something that has been bothering her. She says Du Cuique's maid, Lingdang, has been acting strange. More than once, Lingdang was seen slipping in and out of the bath house at odd hours, when no one was bathing. The girl did not think much of it at first. A maid runs errands, she thought, that is her job. But the timing was wrong. Lingdang went in right before the water was drawn for the family, and she came out with empty hands and a nervous look. The third daughter is young, but she is not soft in the head. She tells Lanxiang this because Lanxiang is the one person in the house who actually listens, and because the rash on the fourth daughter's skin will not leave her alone. Lanxiang hears it and goes still. A maid near the bath water, a sickness that spreads through the bath water, the two facts land next to each other in her mind like two stones dropped in a pond.
🇨🇳 中文翻译

第十七集接着那场把人吓坏的病往下讲。三小姐在井边寻到兰香一个人，跟她说桩一直搁在心里的怪事。她说杜翠雀的丫鬟铃铛，近来举止不对。好几回，铃铛大不正常时辰在澡房进进出出，偏偏那人没在洗澡。姑娘起先没多想——丫鬟跑腿本就是差事。可时辰不对。铃铛总在给全府打水之前溜进去，出来时空着手，神色却慌。三小姐年岁小，脑子不糊涂。她告诉兰香，是因为满府就兰香肯听她说话，也因为四小姐身上那片疹子总在她眼前晃。兰香听了，整个人静下来。一个丫鬟挨着澡水，一场顺着澡水传开的病——两件事在她脑子里挨到一处，像池塘里两块石头。

Lanxiang does not waste time. She pulls Gantang aside, the steady maid who never panics, and lays the third daughter's words next to what they already know. The fourth daughter fell sick after a bath. Yuchan fell sick after a bath. Concubine Zhou, the second master's concubine, fell sick after a bath. The one thing they all shared was the same bucket of hot water. A real rash does not pick its victims by the water they use. So Lanxiang asks the plain question out loud. What if the sickness was never a sickness? What if someone put something in the water? Gantang goes pale, because the thought makes a kind of terrible sense. They go back through the days in their heads. Who drew the water. Who carried it. Who could have slipped something in without being seen. The more they talk, the more the bath house looks like the one place the whole trouble started. Neither girl says the name Du Cuique yet. They do not have to. The poison in the water points at the same corner of the house where the sweetest smile has been living.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

兰香不含糊。她把甘棠拉到一边——那是个从不动慌的稳丫鬟——把三小姐的话和已知的摆在一处。四小姐洗了澡就倒；玉蝉洗了澡就倒；二爷的周姨娘，用了同一桶热水，也烧在床上。三个人，一桶水，一只悄没声的毒手。真疹子不会挑着用哪桶水的人害。于是兰香把话挑明：万一这病根本不是病？万一是有人往水里下了东西？甘棠脸白了，因为这念头可怕地通。她们把日子往回捋：谁打的谁，谁提的谁，谁能神不知鬼不觉往里搁东西。越说，澡房越像整桩祸的起点。两人都没点杜翠雀的名。不必点。水里那毒，指着府里同一处——那张最甜的笑住的地方。

By now the truth is ugly and plain. Yuchan is down with the fever, the fourth daughter is worse, and Concubine Zhou, who shares the second master's roof, took the same hot water and now lies burning in her own bed. Three people, one bucket, one quiet killer. Lanxiang knows she has to move before more fall. But the house is still sealed, the old lady is tired, and the real poisoner is smiling at the dinner table. Then word comes that breaks the last thread of doubt. Lingdang, Du Cuique's maid, is dead. Not sick, dead. Someone fed her poison to stop her mouth, because a dead maid tells no tales. Lanxiang hears it and feels cold, not surprised. The girl was the loose end, the one who walked in and out of the bath house, and now she is gone so she cannot point a finger. That is the proof. A real sickness does not kill the one servant who could explain it. This was murder dressed up as measles, and the mask just slipped.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

到这会儿，真相又丑又明。玉蝉躺着发热，四小姐更重，二爷的周姨娘用了同一桶热水，也在自己床上烧着。三个人，一桶水，一只闷声的杀手。兰香知道得赶在倒下更多人之前动手。可府还封着，老夫人乏了，真投毒的还在饭桌上笑。随后传来一句消息，把最后一点疑也断了：铃铛，杜翠雀的丫鬟，死了。不是病，是死。有人喂她毒封口，因为死丫鬟不会说。兰香听着，发冷，不惊。那姑娘是松了的线，是在澡房进进出出那个，如今没了，便指不了人。这就是证。真病不会弄死那个能说清的丫鬟。这是披着疹子皮的谋杀，面具刚滑下来。

Lanxiang needs hard proof, not just a good guess, so she does something bold. Lin Jinqi's own bath water was drawn that same day but never used. He had ridden out before he could bathe. She goes to his rooms after the house sleeps, lifts the cover, and breathes the steam. There is a bitter edge under the heat, a smell that does not belong in clean water. She dips a strip of cloth and notes the color it leaves. It is enough. She will not drink it, she is not a fool, but she has seen what she needed to see. Before dawn she writes a short note and gives it to Nankè, the man Lin Jinqi trusts, who knows how to pass word beyond the sealed gate. The note says one thing plainly. The sickness is poison. It rides in the bath water. Watch the bath house, and watch Du Cuique. Nankè vanishes into the dark. For the first time since the fever started, Lanxiang feels the ground under her feet again. She knows who, and now she only has to prove it where it counts.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

兰香要硬证，不能只靠猜，于是她干了件胆大的事。林锦岐那日也打了洗澡水，却没用——他出府骑走，没赶上泡。她趁府里睡了，去他屋里掀盖，闻那热气。热底下有一股苦边，干净水里不该有的味。她蘸布条，看那留下的色。够了。她不尝，不是傻，但要看的她看见了。天亮前她写张短条交给南客——林锦岐信得过的人，懂怎么隔着封门把话递出去。条上只一句实话：这病是毒，随澡水走，盯澡房，盯杜翠雀。南客没入夜色。打发热起，兰香头回觉得脚底下有了地。她知道是谁，如今只需在要紧处证出来。

Back inside, the old lady has run out of hands she can trust. The sickness has thinned the household, the real poisoner is still at large, and Senior Lady Lin is a mother first and a manager second. She watches Lanxiang move through the rooms, calm, quick, and already three steps ahead of the trouble. So she does something the great houses almost never do. She takes the heavy token of the house, the piece that means you run the inner rooms, and she presses it into Lanxiang's hand. From this hour, Lanxiang speaks for the inner house. The other maids step back, because they know what the token means, and because they have seen the quiet girl turn into the steady one. Lanxiang does not bow and weep. She simply nods and gets to work. The old lady has given her the keys to the kitchen, the bath house, and every door in between. And the first door Lanxiang means to open is the one Du Cuique thinks is locked tight.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

墙里，老夫人信得过的人手已经空了。病把府里抽稀，真投毒的还在外头晃，林母先是娘、再是当家的。她看着兰香在屋里走，稳、快，比祸早三步。于是她做了一桩大宅几乎不做的事：把那块内宅令牌——意思是你管里屋那块——按进兰香手心。从这一刻，内宅兰香说了算。别的丫鬟退后，因为都懂令牌的分量，也因为见过那静女成了稳的。兰香没跪没哭，只点头，上手。老夫人把厨房、澡房、每道门的钥匙都给了她。而兰香要开的第一道门，正是杜翠雀以为锁死那扇。

Now Lanxiang plays the move she has been saving. She keeps a little of the poisoned bath water, the proof no one can argue with. Then she tells Gantang to do something sharp. Brew a pot of good tea, she says, and use that water. Not enough to kill, just enough to speak. The tea goes to Du Cuique's rooms, sent as if it were a kind gift from the house, a small comfort for a guest. Du Cuique drinks without thinking, because she never imagined the poison would come back to her own cup. Within the hour she is sick, the same sick the others had, and the smile drops off her face. Lanxiang watches from the hall and feels no pity. This is the old rule of the inner house, the one Du Cuique started. You poison the water, you drink the water. You reach for someone's life, and your own hand feeds you. The girl who wanted to rule by fear just drank the lesson she brewed for everyone else.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

这会儿兰香亮出压着的那招。她留了点毒澡水——谁也驳不倒的证。然后她吩咐甘棠办件利落事：煮壶好茶，用那水。不致死，只够说话。茶送到杜翠雀屋里，装作府里的一点心意，给客人的小慰藉。杜翠雀想也没想就喝，因为她绝想不到毒会回到自己杯里。不消一个时辰，她也病了，和别人一样的病，脸上的笑掉了。兰香在廊下看，不怜。这是内宅的老规矩，杜翠雀自己开的：你下毒在水，你喝那水；你伸手要别人命，你自己的手喂你。那想靠吓掌家的姑娘，喝下了她为众人熬的教训。

With Du Cuique down and the proof in her own cup, the inner house turns quiet in a new way. The whispers stop, because now everyone can see which way the wind blows. Lanxiang does not gloat. She keeps the token tight and the doors watched, and she waits for the old lady to wake and see what has been done in her name. Du Cuique lies in her rooms, weak and watched, no longer the guest with the sweet words. The third daughter stays close to her grandmother, because she knows the worst is not over yet. Somewhere in the walls, the people who helped Du Cuique are still breathing, and a cornered snake bites. So the household holds its breath through the night, lamps lit, ears open. Lanxiang walks the halls herself, a girl with a token and a cloth of poisoned water, guarding a house that finally trusts her to. The fever is broken. The lying is not. Not yet.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

杜翠雀倒了、证在她自己杯里，内宅换了一种静。流言停了，因为人人看清风往哪边吹。兰香不显摆。她把令牌攥紧、门看牢，等老夫人醒来看清以她名头办的事。杜翠雀躺屋里，弱着、盯着，不再是那个甜话的客。三小姐守着祖母近，因为她知道最坏还没完。墙里某处，帮杜翠雀的人还喘着气，蛇被逼急会咬。于是府里屏息过夜，灯亮着、耳张着。兰香自己巡廊，一个拿令牌、拿布毒水的姑娘，守着终于信她的府。热退了。谎没退。还没。

Far from the lamps, Lin Jinqi is busy with a different kind of hunt. Word of the poisoned water reached him through Nankè, but so did darker news. The men who set the sickness in motion were the same men who meant to kill the Yongchang Marquis. Masked assassins were moving on the old general's road, sent to finish what the bath house started. Lin Jinqi did not wait for the court. He picked the spot, set his men in the trees, and let the assassins walk into the trap. When the blades came out, his were already there. The fight was short and ugly, and by the time the sun touched the road, the masked men were dead or taken, and the Yongchang Marquis was safe. Lin Jinqi stood over the bodies and felt the cold settle. The poison in the bath and the knives on the road were one plot, one hand. He just cut off the other arm of it. The house inside would not know until morning. The road outside was already clean.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

离灯火很远，林锦岐在猎另一桩。毒水的消息南客递到了，更暗的消息也到了：在澡房起事的人，正是要杀永昌侯那拨。蒙面刺客上了老侯爷的路，要接着澡房没完的事收尾。林锦岐没等官家。他选了地，把人埋在树里，放刺客踏进阱。刀亮时，他的刀已在。仗短而丑，等日头照上路，蒙面的死透或拿下，永昌侯无恙。林锦岐站在尸上，冷意落定。澡房里的毒和路上的刀，是一桩、一只手。他刚把另半条臂斩了。墙里的府，要天亮才知道。墙外的路，已经干净。

So episode 17 turns the fever inside out. A maid's odd steps near the bath house became the thread that pulled the whole lie apart. The third daughter spoke, Lanxiang listened, and Gantang helped tie the knot. Three sick women and one dead maid became the proof no smile could hide. Senior Lady Lin put the running of the house in Lanxiang's hands, and Lanxiang answered by sending the poison home to the hand that poured it. Outside the walls, Lin Jinqi dropped the other half of the plot into the dirt. The house is not safe yet, not by a long way, but the shape of the enemy is finally clear. It wears a guest's face and carries a cup of bad water. Episode 18 will bring the reckoning into the open, where the Du brother and sister stand revealed, and where a few quiet people finally get to say the names out loud. But that is next time's telling. For now, remember this. In the Lin estate, the deadliest thing in the room is often the one smiling at the table.
