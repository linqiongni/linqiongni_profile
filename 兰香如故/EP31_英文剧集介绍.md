# 兰香如故 — The Fragrance of Orchids Remains
## Episode 31 · Told Like A Story

Let me tell you about episode thirty-one, because this is the last chapter of the upper story, the one where a loyal man's knees freeze at a gate and a clever man finally reads the price of the throne he spent his life defending. The peace the Shen family's exoneration bought is barely a season old when the court, in its usual weather, turns cold again — not on Lanxiang this time, but on a man who asked it, in the only language a loyal servant has, to be better than it was. The season, which has carried us from a maid's knee to a great house's clearing, now carries us to a palace gate, where the last lesson of this half of the tale is waiting to be taught in the snow.  The peace they bought with a frozen gate is the costliest peace the season has shown, and the quietest grief.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

让我跟你讲讲第三十一集，因为这一集是上部最后一章——一个忠仆的膝在宫门外冻僵，而一个聪明人，终于读出了他用一生护卫的御座，究竟值几文。沈家昭雪换来的安稳，才将将过了一季，朝廷便如常起了寒：这一回不是落在兰香身上，而是落在一个用忠仆唯一会用的言语、求它好过它本性的男人身上。这季，把我们自丫鬟的膝一路载到一座大宅的清白，此刻把我们载到一座宫门，这半部故事最后一课，正候在雪里教。

</details>

The man is Han Liang, the retainer who once pulled Lanxiang from a river and never once asked what she had been, only what she needed. He goes to the palace gate in the deep of winter and kneels — not in greeting, but in remonstrance, the old gesture of a servant who would rather die on the threshold than live with a wrong he cannot mend from below. He carries a petition, and the petition asks the throne to right some injustice the court would rather forget; and because the court would rather forget it, the gate stays shut and the kneeling man stays, and the cold, which answers no petition, begins its slow work on his bones. The season watches a loyalty that the throne did not earn and will not repay, and does not look away from the snow.  The loyalty the throne did not earn sits heaviest on the man who gave it without being asked.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

这人是韩梁，那个曾把兰香从江里拉起、从不问她曾是什么、只问她要什么的门下。他于深冬走到宫门，跪下——不是行礼，是死谏，一个宁可死在门槛也不肯带着一处他从下头补不了的错活着的仆，那古老的姿态。他带着一纸呈，呈里求御座去正一桩朝廷宁可忘掉的枉；而因朝廷宁可忘，门便关着，跪着的人也留着，而那不答任何呈的寒，在他骨上慢慢做起它的工。这季望着一份御座既未挣得、也不会偿的忠，没有把眼从雪上移开。

</details>

He does not rise. Through the day and into the night he holds his post at the gate, and the cold, which has no politics, takes him the way it takes any man who will not move. By morning the palace doors open on a figure still on its knees, frozen where it knelt, a loyal retainer turned to a statue the court did not ask for and does not know what to do with. Han Liang, who saved a life at a river, has given his own at a gate, and the season, unsparing, records that the throne he died petitioning was, to the last, indifferent to the petition. A great house lost a faithful servant; the Empire barely notices it lost a faithful man.  The cold, which has no politics and no mercy, takes him the way it takes any man who will not move, and the gate keeps its silence.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

他没有起。从白日到长夜，他在门外守着他的位；而那无党无派的寒，如取任何一个不肯动的人那样取了他。到天明，宫门开处，是一具仍跪着的身，冻在他跪过的地方——一个忠仆，成了御座没讨要、也不知如何处置的像。韩梁，曾在江边救过一条命，如今在门边给了自己的命；而这季不饶，记下：他至死叩求的御座，到最后一刻，对他的叩求无动于衷。一座大宅失了一个忠仆；这帝国几乎没察觉它失了一个忠人。

</details>

When Lin Jinqi hears of it, something in him that has held since the river gives way. He rode out for this court, cut traitors for it, married beneath his rank for a woman it would have killed, and burned a proof to keep that woman breathing — and the court, in return, let a man who would have died for it simply die at its door. The marquis goes to the gate himself and stands where Han Liang knelt, and the silence there is louder than any edict he has ever carried. The season sees the crack run through a loyalty years in the making, because a man can serve a throne and still see, in a frozen kneeling form, what the throne is worth.  He rode out for this court, cut traitors for it, married beneath his rank for a woman it would have killed, and now the same court asks why he knelt.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

林锦岐闻讯，自江边起便撑着他的某样东西，溃了。他为这朝廷出过骑、斩过叛，为着一个它本要杀的女人降了阶娶了亲，又烧了一道证留那女人一口气——而朝廷回他的，是任一个本肯为它死的人，就那么死在它门前。侯爷亲自去到那门，立在韩梁跪过处；那里的静，比他携过的任何一道旨都响。这季看见一道筑了数年的忠，裂了缝，因一个人尽可为御座效力，仍能在那冻跪的形里，看清御座值几文。

</details>

And in that standing he sees through the throne at last. Not the idea of it — the banners, the law, the order he defended — but the thing beneath: a wheel that grinds the loyal and the false alike, that takes a Han Liang's knees and gives nothing back, that made him marry his wife by edict and would, in another season, have taken her by the same power. Lin Jinqi understands that to serve such a throne is to lend one's spine to a machine that does not know his name, and the understanding is not a rebellion but a waking. The season, which has watched him bind a maid, free a sister, and burn a proof, watches him now unbind himself from the one chain he had worn willingly: the chain of office. He understands at last that the loyalty he prized was a leash he mistook for a purpose, and the freeing of it feels less like rebellion than like a man setting down a load he had forgotten he was carrying.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

而在那站立里，他终于看透了御座。不是它的"意"——那旌旗、那法度、他护过的那秩序——而是其下之物：一只忠奸一例研磨的轮，取了韩梁的膝、不回半分，逼他以圣旨娶妻、又会在另一季以同一份力取走她。林锦岐懂得：侍这样的御座，是把自家的脊借给一台不识他姓名的机器；而这懂得，不是反，是醒。这季，曾见他缚丫鬟、放妹妹、烧证，此刻见他从那条他自愿戴着的链——官之链——上，解下自己。

</details>

So he resigns. He lays the marquisate and its duties back at the feet of the throne that could not save Han Liang, and walks away from the capital the way a man walks away from a fire that took someone he loved. His destination is Jinling, the southern city where the air is softer and the gates do not demand a life to open — the city, not by chance, where the Shen family's roots run, and where a granddaughter restored can walk without the court's shadow on her back. The season notes the symmetry of the road: the marquis who rose by the throne's favor now descends by his own choice, and the descent, for the first time in his life, is his own.  He lays the marquisate at the feet of the throne that could not save Han Liang, and walks from the capital as a man leaves a fire that took someone he loved.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

于是他辞了。他把侯爵与职事，交还到那个救不了韩梁的御座脚下，走出京城，如一个人走出一场夺了他所爱之人的火。他的去处是金陵，那座南边的城，气更软，门也不索一条命才开——而这座城，并非偶然，正是沈家根脉所系，是一个复了名的孙女可以背着朝廷的影行走的城。这季记下这路里的对称：一个凭御座之恩起的侯，如今凭自己的选落下；而这落下，是他一生头一回，是自己的。

</details>

Lanxiang goes with him, and this time the going is not a flight but a choosing. She who was carried from a burning field, who was married by an edict she could not refuse, who stood as Shen Jialan before the court that erased her, now walks out of the capital at her own pace, beside the man who burned the proof and beside the children who are the heirs of two houses. The Senior Lady Lin, the estate, the old servants — they are left behind, but not abandoned; the marquis leaves the house in good hands and takes only what he cannot leave: the wife who is also the granddaughter, and the quiet he has finally earned. The season watches a family become small in order to become free, and finds the trade a fair one.  What the capital demanded, Jinling returns without demanding, and the smallness proves lighter than the rank.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

兰香随他而去，而这一回的走，不是逃，是选。那个曾被从火里的场抱出、曾被一道推不掉的圣旨娶、曾在抹去她的朝廷前以沈嘉兰之身站出的她，如今以自己的步子走出京城，走在烧了证的男人身侧，走在两座府的继承人——孩子们——身侧。林母、府、老仆——都留在后头，却非被弃；侯爷把府交在妥手，只带走他丢不下的：那个同时也是孙女的妻，与他终于挣来的静。这季望着一个家庭为得自由而变小，觉这交易公平。

</details>

In Jinling, in a modest house with a courtyard and no gate that demands a life, they do the one thing the edict and the slap and the years postponed: they drink the bridal cup, the marriage cup they never properly shared. On the wedding night of episode twenty-six, the cup was set aside by a palm and a slap; now, with no court to bless it and no chain to bind it, they pour for each other and drink, husband and wife who bear fortune and misfortune together, exactly as he named them at the grave. The season, which has followed this pair through poison and war and a buried name, lets the small rite stand as the true marriage the edict could not make — a cup taken freely, by two hands, under no roof but their own.  It is the marriage the edict could never grant, given now by choice and not by command.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

在金陵，一座带院、没有门索命的朴屋里，他们做了那件被圣旨、被一掌、被年岁耽搁了的事：他们饮了合卺酒——那杯他们从没好好共饮的婚酒。二十六集大婚夜，那杯被一只手、一记掌撂在了一旁；如今，没有朝廷赐福、没有链来缚，他们互为对方斟、饮下，祸福同担的夫妻，正如他在坟前唤他们的那样。这季，自毒、自战、自一个被埋的名一路随这对人走来，让这小小的礼，立作那道旨做不成的真婚——一杯被两只手、在无人檐下、自由取起的酒。

</details>

The season, looking back over the whole of the upper story, sets down its final mark with a steady hand. A maid became a mistress, a mistress became an heir, an heir found a husband who chose to bury her danger rather than wield it, and that husband, having seen a loyal man freeze at a gate, chose a gate of his own to walk through. The fragrance of the orchid, the season says, remains not because the throne preserved it but because people carried it — through water, through fire, through a wrongful grave, and out the far side of a capital that would have kept them all. Some stories, the season admits, are kept alive less by the powerful than by the faithful. The orchid's fragrance was carried out of the capital not by edict or by army but by a husband and wife who simply refused to let the throne be the author of their ending, and that refusal, the season says, is the whole of the tale.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

这季，回望整部上部，以一只稳手落下它最后的记号。一个丫鬟成了主母，一个主母成了继承人，一个继承人寻到一个情愿葬下她的险、而非去用它辖她的丈夫；而那丈夫，见过一个忠人冻死门边，选了一道自己的门走了过去。这季说：兰之香不灭，不是因御座护住了它，而是因有人载着它——载过水、载过火、载过一座冤坟，载出了那座本要留他们全在里头的京城的远侧。有些故事，这季承认，少靠强者活，多靠信者活。

</details>

So we close the upper tale the way a cup is set down after it is drunk: gently, and with the warmth of it still in the hand. Han Liang lies frozen at a gate the court will not mourn, and Lin Jinqi and Lanxiang sit in Jinling with the cup that finished what the edict began, free at last of the throne that gave and took in the same breath. The orchid's fragrance remains, and the tale, though this half ends here, is not done — for a pirate chief waits on the waves, and a house made small will grow strange roots in the south. But that is the lower story's tell. For now, remember only this: in the Lin estate the truest marriage was the cup no edict could pour, and the freest gate was the one a marquis chose to walk through, away from the throne that froze the loyal and freed no one but itself.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

于是我们如饮尽后放下一杯那样，收束这上部：轻，且那暖还留在手里。韩梁冻毙在一道朝廷不肯哀的门外；林锦岐与兰香坐在金陵，捧着那杯了结了圣旨所起之事的酒，终于脱了那个同一息里予又取的御座。兰之香不灭；而这故事，虽半部止于此，却未完——因一位匪首候在浪上，而一座变小了的府，将在南边生出陌生的根。但那是下部要讲的了。此刻你只记一句：在林府，最真的婚，是那杯任何圣旨都斟不出的酒；最自由的门，是一位侯爷选了走过去的门——远离那个冻了忠者、却只放了自己身的御座。
