# 兰香如故 — The Fragrance of Orchids Remains
## Episode 32 · Told Like A Story

Episode 32 opens on a ugly truth inside the family. Lin Jinqi learns that his brother-in-law, Lu Jian, has been beating his eldest sister, Lin Xiulai, for a long time. The sister has hidden it, the way women in these houses often do, with long sleeves and quiet excuses. But Lin Jinqi sees the marks and hears the silence, and he does not let it pass. He goes to Lu Jian and teaches him a hard lesson with his own hands, the kind of lesson a man gives when words have failed and a sister is bleeding. The beatdown is not clean, but it is clear. Lu Jian is told, in pain, that the Lin family's daughter is not his to break. The lesson is rough, the kind a soldier gives, not a polite word, but it lands. Lu Jian learns that the Lin name still protects its own, even the daughter married out. Lin Jinqi does not boast about it. He simply makes sure the sister is seen, the bruises noted, and the message carried back to the Lu house that the next hand raised will be the last one Lu Jian ever lifts against her.

While the men handle the Lu matter at the edge, Lanxiang keeps busy at the Shen family's old residence in the capital. She runs the house day to day, but she also works a quiet second line, gathering clues about the old Shen case, the matter that broke her true family years ago. She asks the right servants, reads the right papers, and files what she finds in her head. No one in the Lin house knows the full shape of it yet, and she means to keep it that way until she has enough to use. The old home, once a wound, is now her base for digging into who took her blood down. She is careful with the papers, because some were meant to stay buried, and a wrong question in the capital can reach the wrong ear. The clues point at names she does not yet trust herself to name, so she holds them close and keeps digging. The house that once held her family's ruin is now the quiet room where she rebuilds the story of how it happened, one small fact at a time, alone with the door shut.

The gap between Lanxiang and Lin Jinqi has eased a little since the boat and the talk in the street. He no longer feels only like the man who carried her by force, and she no longer meets him with a wall of pure cold. But she is not open either. She keeps a guard up, because trust, once broken by a drugged cup, does not grow back in a week. She will work beside him, she will even stand with him, but she watches his hands and his words the way a person watches weather that has turned before. The peace between them is real but thin, and both of them know it could sheet over again. She will share a meal with him, she will speak without the old edge, but she does not give him the full map of her mind. The Shen case, the plans she runs, the friends she frees, those she keeps to herself until she is sure. A guarded heart is not a cold one, and he seems to understand that now, which is its own small surprise. They move like two people learning a new step, slow, and not yet sure of the rhythm they are making together.

Then the second sister comes to Lanxiang in a panic. She begs Lanxiang to save the eldest sister, who is still suffering under Lu Jian even after the lesson. The girl is frightened and out of ideas, and she trusts Lanxiang the way the younger ones have learned to. She does not run to the brothers, because a brother's fist is a short answer, and what the eldest sister needs is a way out, not just another beating traded back and forth. Lanxiang listens, and the old clever light comes into her eyes. She agrees, not with heat, but with the calm of a person who has already started to plan. She asks the girl what the eldest sister wants, not what the brothers want, because a rescue that ignores the person saved is no rescue at all. The second sister says her sister wants out, wants home, wants the beatings to stop, and that is enough. Lanxiang nods, and the light in her eye turns to a line of action. She does not promise a pretty result. She promises an end, and the younger sister believes her the way the whole house has learned to.

Lanxiang sets her trap. She sends men to give Lu Jian another beating, harder and more public this time, so the message lands where the Lu family cannot ignore it. At the same time she spreads a story through the city, that Lin Jinqi himself beat his brother-in-law to protect his sister, the proper family man defending his own. The tale travels fast, because it paints the Lins in a good light and makes Lu Jian look like the one in the wrong. By the time the Lu family hears it, the ground has already shifted, and they are the ashamed ones, not the Lins. The men she sends know their work and leave Lu Jian with a lesson he cannot mistake, while the rumor does the softer part, shaping how the city sees the two families. By morning the Lins look like the wronged side protecting their own, and the Lus look like the abusers everyone quietly pities the sister for marrying. Lanxiang does not cheer. She simply notes that the board has turned, and the next move belongs to the Lu family, who do not know they are already losing.

The Lu family, stung by the talk, comes to the Lin residence to demand an account. They want Lin Jinqi to answer for the beating and the rumor. But Lin Jinqi does not know the full story yet. He thinks the first lesson was the end of it, and he walks into the room expecting to brush the Lu clan off. Then the truth comes out, that Lu Jian has been abusing his wife all along, and that the new beating and the street story were Lanxiang's work, not his. For a breath he is caught between surprise and pride, because his quiet bride ran a play he did not see. Then the older feeling wins, the brother's rage at what was done to his sister, and the surprise turns to purpose. He does not scold Lanxiang for acting on her own. He listens, weighs the truth, and decides that whatever she set in motion, he will now finish in the open, as the head of the house should, standing where she paved the way.

When he learns what Lu Jian did to his sister, Lin Jinqi's face goes hard. He turns on the Lu father and son and scolds them in front of the house, no longer the man brushing them off but the master defending his own. He orders the Lu family out, father and son both, and tells them the marriage is finished in his eyes. The Lu clan came to demand an account and leaves with none, expelled and shamed. It is the moment the sisters have waited for, a brother who does not hide the abuse but names it and throws the door open on the men who caused it. The Lu father tries to argue, to say the sister exaggerates, but Lin Jinqi cuts him off with a voice that brooks no reply. The brothers of the Lin house step in behind him, a wall of kin the Lu clan cannot push through. Father and son are shown the gate, and the marriage that bound the families is declared done by the Lin side alone, with the sisters watching from the hall.

Then Lanxiang and Lin Jinqi move as one, inside and out. She has already set the next step, and he follows it now that he knows the truth. They take men and go straight to the Lu family's back courtyard, where the eldest sister is kept. The plan is bold. They mean to pull Lin Xiulai out of that house for good. Lin Jinqi leads the front, Lanxiang guides the back, and the two of them, who were a forced pair a few episodes ago, now run a rescue like a team that has done it before. The Lu courtyard does not expect a Lin raid, and the door gives. Inside, Lanxiang has already told the servants which way to go and which rooms to avoid, so the raid moves clean. Lin Xiulai is found in the back, small and worn but not broken, and the sight of her brothers at the door undoes something in her. She does not weep, she simply reaches for the hand offered, and the Lins close around her. The Lu courtyard, caught flat, has no move left, and the sister walks out of the house that hurt her, into the arms of her own blood.

To scare the Lu family off for good, Lanxiang uses a clever trick. She brings packs of fake blood and stages the scene so it looks like Lin Xiulai is terribly hurt, a victim beaten near to death. The Lu clan, already shamed and now faced with what looks like a dying sister, loses its nerve and backs away fast. The fake blood does the work of an army, because no family wants to be seen as the one who killed a wife. The fright is enough. The Lins walk out with Lin Xiulai, and the Lu house is left holding a horror it dare not question, for fear the truth is worse. Lanxiang has thought the trick through, the placement of the red, the story the Lu clan will tell themselves when they find it, and it works exactly as she meant. A family that might have fought back instead flees its own yard, afraid of the ghost it thinks it made. The Lins do not explain the blood, because an unexplained horror is the one no one dares to question, and they simply carry their sister clear while the Lu house sits in the mess of its own fright.

Back at the Lin residence, Lanxiang works with the imperial physician to report the injury as severe, so the story of a badly beaten sister stays solid and the Lu family cannot come back with a counter tale. The old doctor plays his part, the household closes ranks, and Lin Jinqi and his brothers stand behind the eldest sister's choice to end the marriage. The marriage is ended by divorce at last, and the sister who suffered is free of the man who hurt her. What started as a hidden beating ends with a family lining up behind its own, and a bride who ran the whole play from the shadows. No one in the house quite realizes how much of the rescue was her design, from the rumor to the blood to the physician's words, and that is exactly how she likes it. The sister is safe, the abuser is gone, and the Lin family believes it stood together, which it did, with Lanxiang as the quiet hand that set the pieces. She asks for no credit, because the win itself was the only thanks she wanted to see, and the sister's free breath is payment enough.

So episode 32 closes the abuse line and shows how far Lanxiang has come. She took a panicked sister's plea and turned it into a clean rescue, using rumor, a staged beating, and a blood trick the Lu family could not stand against. Lin Jinqi, who began the season forcing her, ends it standing with her, expelling the abuser and backing his sister's freedom. The old Shen case still waits in her notes, but for now the Lin house is whole, and the girl who was carried to the capital by drug and boat sits at its center, quiet, trusted, and very much in charge of the moves. The abuse that hid behind closed doors is now a story the whole city knows, and the man who dealt it has no roof left to return to. Lin Xiulai rests under her own family's roof, free to choose what comes next. And Lanxiang, who came to the capital as a dragged bride, ends the episode as the one the family turns to when something must be done right, with the Shen case still waiting in her notes for another day.
