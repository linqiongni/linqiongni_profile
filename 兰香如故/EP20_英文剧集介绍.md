# 兰香如故 — The Fragrance of Orchids Remains
## Episode 20 · Told Like A Story

Episode 20 follows Lanxiang out of the gate and into the city she has only known from the well side. First, inside the estate, the old master's word lands. He agrees to the third daughter's offer to marry the Yongchang Marquis, and the cave that started it all becomes a closed chapter. The second daughter, whose name the marriage now spares, is not set free. She is confined to her rooms, a quiet punishment for the heart that let a guard climb too far. She takes it without a fight, because she knows she earned it, and because her younger sister just traded a life to keep hers. The house breathes different now. One daughter goes toward a marquis, one stays behind behind a locked door, and the quiet that follows is not peace exactly, but the kind of stillness after a fever breaks. Lanxiang, watching from the outside edge of it, sees the cost of both and says a small prayer for the sister who chose the gate.

第二十集跟兰香出府，进了她只在井边听说过的城。先说墙里：老太爷的话落了。他应了三小姐嫁永昌侯的请，那起了一切的洞，成了合上的章。二小姐——这婚如今免了她的名——没被放，是被禁足屋里，为那颗让仆爬太高的心，轻轻罚。她不闹，认了，因为懂是她挣的，也因为妹刚拿自己命换了她的。府里气不同了。一个女儿朝侯爷去，一个留在锁起的门后，随之的静不算平安，是热退后的那种静。兰香在边上看，看尽两头的价，替选了门的妹妹悄悄念了句。

</details>

Lin Jinqi keeps his promise to see Lanxiang settled, and he does not leave it to chance. He sends Jiugao, his trusted man, to help her find a place in the city. Jiugao knows the streets and the landlords, and he comes back with a list of rooms, some dear, some cheap, all within a walk of honest work. Lanxiang looks them over with the eye of someone who has counted linens for a great house. She does not want a fine room. She wants a door that locks, a roof that does not leak, and neighbors who mind their own business. Jiugao is patient with her, because he has watched her hold a household through poison and lie, and he knows a free woman who can do that will pick a roof better than most. They walk the lanes, the big man and the small former maid, and the city opens around them like a book neither has had time to read.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

林锦岐守诺，要安顿兰香，不交给运气。他派信得过的九皋帮她在城里找房。九皋熟街熟房东，回来一列屋子，有贵的、有贱的，都在正经活计脚程内。兰香用那种替大宅数过布草的眼挑。她不要精舍，要一扇能锁的门、不漏的顶、各管各事的邻。九皋对她耐，因为他见过她撑着府过完毒和谎，知道这样的自由女挑屋顶比多数强。他俩走巷，大汉和小丫鬟，城在四周摊开像本都没空读的书。

</details>

One of the rooms Jiugao finds is cheap, almost too cheap, and the landlord is a little too eager to hand over the key. Lanxiang feels it before she sees it. The rent is low because the place sits next to a debtor who wants a body in the room to hide his own, or a lease written so the buyer forfeits the silver at the first missed coin. She reads the paper the way she read the bath house, line by line, and she finds the hook. A clause that says the deposit is lost if she leaves inside a year. A neighbor who is the landlord's man. A door that opens from the outside. None of it is a crime, all of it is a trap. She thanks the man, hands back the key, and walks out. Jiugao asks if she is sure, and she says she has spent a year learning how poison hides in sweet water. A cheap room with a smile is the same poison in a different cup.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

九皋找的屋里，有一间贱，贱得几乎过头，房东递钥匙急得有点过。兰香没看见就先觉出。租贱，因为这屋挨着个欠户，要屋里塞个人替他藏自己；或契写得买主一缺钱就罚没银。她像读澡房那样一行行读那纸，找着钩：一条说一年内走就吞押金；一个邻是房东的人；一道能从外开的门。没一样犯法，样样是阱。她谢了那人，退钥匙，走人。九皋问她可是拿准，她说她花了一年学毒怎么藏在甜水里。便宜房带张笑，是同一杯毒，换个杯。

</details>

Lanxiang finds the right work through Mr. Lu, a steady man who runs a painting and calligraphy shop in the busy part of the city. The Lu family shop takes her whole family in, her mother and father and herself, to do the plain honest labor a book shop needs: wrapping scrolls, sweeping, running errands, keeping the ink and paper in order. It is not the Zhichun Residence, and that is the point. Here no one owns her, no one watches her sleep, and the worst a day brings is a sore back and a rude customer. Her parents, who feared this day would never come, settle into the rhythm of it with a relief they do not say out loud. Lanxiang learns the feel of the shop the way she learned the feel of a household, by noticing what others walk past. Mr. Lu notices her noticing, and he gives her a little more to do, and then a little more.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

兰香在陆先生处谋到正经活——那是个稳当人，在城里热闹处开书画行。陆家铺子把她们一家都收了：娘、爹、她，干书店要的笨实活——裹卷、扫地、跑腿、把笔墨纸理顺。不是知春馆，而这正是点。这里没人占她，没人盯她睡，一天最坏不过腰酸、撞个刁客。她爹娘怕这天不来，悄悄安进这节奏，松了口不说的气。兰香像摸透一座府那样摸透铺子——靠别人走过不看的那些。陆先生看她看事，多派她点活，又多点。

</details>

At Mr. Lu's shop Lanxiang meets Han Liang, a young scholar who comes to read and to help with the accounts. He is polite, and he is sharp, and he treats a former maid like a person and not a servant, which she has not had from a man in a long while. They fall into an easy way of talking, about books and prices and the small nonsense of a shop day. Han Liang finds her quick, and she finds him kind, and neither makes a show of it. The other clerks see the two of them bent over a ledger and smile, because it is plain to watch. Lanxiang, who has spent a year reading poison in smiles, reads this one and finds it clean. For the first time since she left the estate, she laughs at something that is not relief. Mr. Lu watches the match of them and says nothing, but he sets them on the same tasks more often than the work requires.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

在陆家铺子，兰香结识韩梁——个常来读书、帮理账的年轻秀才。他礼，他利，把前丫鬟当人、不当仆，这是她许久没从男人处得的。两人聊得来，书、价、铺子一天的碎絮。韩梁觉她机，她觉他和，都不张扬。别的伙计看俩人趴一本账上，笑，因为明摆着。兰香花了一年从笑里读毒，这回读出的，是净。出府后头回，她为不是松快的事笑了。陆先生看俩人合，没说，却把同一桩活派给他们的次数，多过活本要的。

</details>

Far from the shop, the wokou case that started episodes ago finally names its dead. The collusion with the Japanese raiders, the smuggling the Dai family hid behind, is laid at the feet of Dai Rong and Liang Yu, two men made to carry the whole of it. They take the blame, whether bought or broken, and they die in prison before the season turns. The court closes the file, the marquis signs it, and the city is told the trouble is ended. Lanxiang hears the names and feels the old cold, because she knows two men in a cell are a small price for a net that reached the bath house. But the house is safe, and that is what matters to the people she left. The poisoners are named, the traitors are chained, and the long shadow of the Dai family, which EP16 first showed her, lies flat at last.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

离铺子远，好几集前的倭寇案终于点了死名。通倭的勾串、戴家藏后面的私运，都压到戴荣、梁玉脚下，两个被架上扛全案的。他们顶了罪——买的通、压的垮——在换季前死在牢里。官家合了卷，侯爷签了，城里说祸完了。兰香听那名，旧冷回来，因为她懂，牢里两条命，抵不了伸到澡房的网。可府安了，这对她留下的人要紧。投毒的被点名，叛的被锁，戴家那长影——第十六集头回让她见的——终于平躺。

</details>

Safe does not mean finished. The Crown Prince himself arrives in Jinling to keep the wokou matter open a while longer. A prince does not travel to count scrolls. He comes because the raiders touched the coast and the coast touches the throne, and because a case this big always has a tail no prison can hold. He sets up at the prefect's seat and begins to ask the questions the dead men could not answer. The city straightens its streets and holds its breath, the way cities do when power rides in. Lin Jinqi is called to stand before him and give account of the road fight and the marquis's rescue, and he goes without flourish. Lanxiang, at the shop, only knows a great person has come, and that her old master will be busier than ever. The wokou story is not over. It has only changed hands, from the blade to the court.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

安不等于完。太子亲抵金陵，要把倭案再敞一阵。太子不是来数卷的。他来，因为寇碰了岸、岸碰了龙椅，因为这等大案总有条牢关不住的尾。他在府衙落了脚，开始问死人答不出的那些。城把街捋直、屏了息，像权来时城常做的。林锦岐被传去面前，报路上那仗、救侯爷的事，去得不起眼。铺里的兰香只知来了贵人，旧主更忙了。倭案没完，只是换了手，从刀到了堂。

</details>

Lin Jinqi, busy or not, comes to see Lanxiang at the shop. He tells himself it is to check she is settled, and maybe it is, but when he finds her at the ledger with Han Liang, leaning close, laughing at some small thing, he goes quiet. The scholar is clever and clean, and the two of them fit the way a key fits a lock. Lin Jinqi watches longer than he should, then tells Jiugao to find out who Han Liang is, where he came from, what family, what debt, what he wants. Not because he doubts the boy, but because he has spent a year learning that a pretty face in the Lin story is never only a face. He does not speak to Lanxiang of it. He only asks after her roof, nods at Mr. Lu, and leaves. But the order to look into Han Liang goes out that same hour, quiet as a dropped pin, the way Lin Jinqi does everything that matters.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

林锦岐忙不忙，都来铺子看兰香。他跟自己说是看看安没安，也许真是，可一见她在账上和韩梁凑近、为点小事笑，他就静了。那秀才伶俐干净，俩人合得像钥匙配锁。林锦岐看得久了些，然后吩咐九皋查韩梁是谁、哪来、什么家、什么债、要什么。不是疑那后生，是他花了一年学：林家故事里好看的脸，从不只是脸。他没同兰香提。只问了屋顶，朝陆先生点点头，走。可查韩梁的话，当刻就出了，静得像落根簪——林锦岐办要紧事，向来这般。

</details>

So episode 20 sets the freed girl down in the city and watches the old world reach for her anyway. The third daughter goes to a marquis, the second stays behind a locked door, and Lanxiang walks out with Jiugao to a room that almost trapped her and a shop that took her in. At Mr. Lu's she meets Han Liang, a scholar with a clean smile, and the two of them fit in a way that does not escape the young master's eye. The wokou case buries Dai Rong and Liang Yu in prison, the Crown Prince rides in to keep asking, and Lin Jinqi leaves the shop with a quiet order to learn the scholar's name. Episode 21 will bring Han Liang's sister home, a fight at the door, and a matchmaker's first step. But that is next time. For now, remember this. A girl who reads poison in sweet water reads a clean smile just as fast, and the man who taught her to watch is watching still.
