Let me tell you about episode thirty-six, because this is the episode where the long arc of a buried name finally bends toward the light, and the woman who set it bending lives to see almost everything except the light itself. The years between Lin Jinqi's passing and Lanxiang's eightieth winter are not a story the season tells in storm — they are a story told in the slow weathering of a page, the steady scratching of a brush, the quiet accumulation of a reputation whispered from one tea house to the next. She ages the way a tree ages, openly, in the courtyard of Jinling, and the girl who once hid behind a maid's name and a poet's name now hides behind neither, because at eighty the only name left to her is the one the people gave back: the teller of the tale that cleared a house. The years between have been long, and mostly quiet, and they have bent the arc without her noticing.
🇨🇳 中文翻译

让我跟你讲讲第三十六集，因为这一集，一个埋名的长弧，终于向光弯去，而那把它弯向光的女人，活到几乎见尽一切，只没见着那光本身。林锦岐逝去、到兰香八十之冬之间那些年，不是这季以风暴讲的故事——是写在一页慢被风化、一笔稳稳的划、一处从一座茶楼低语到下一座的声名静积里的故事。她如树那样、公开地，在金陵的院里老去；而那曾藏在一个丫鬟名、一个诗人名后的姑娘，如今两者都不藏，因到了八十，留给她的唯一名字，是那人还回她的：那洗了一座府的故的讲述者。

She still writes. At eighty the hand is not what it was, and the characters sometimes wander, but the plays keep coming, because the story is not finished so long as one soul who knew the truth is still breathing to tell it. Under the name Shen Rugù she sets down the later chapters — not only the fall of the Shen family but the small mercies after it: the contracts burned, the bridal cup drunk, the grain delivered to a starving border by a man who loved her. The teahouses that once staged the first script now stage the whole cycle, and the boatmen and the fruit-sellers and the grandmothers with their granddaughters know the fallen daughter's song by heart, and sing it without knowing they are keeping a dead woman's name alive, the way a river keeps a stone smooth without knowing it polishes. The hand may wander, but the aim does not, and the aim was always to leave the truth where the people could find it without needing leave of any court.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

她仍在写。八十的手不如从前，字有时走了样，可戏一本本仍来，因只要还有一个知真、仍喘着气讲它的魂在，这故就没完。她以沈如故之名，记下后头的章——不只是沈家的倾，还有倾后的小慈：烧了的契、饮了的合卺酒、一个爱她的男人送到饿边去的粮。曾演头本戏的茶楼，如今演整一轮；船夫、卖果的、抱着孙女的祖母，把那落难女的歌熟记于心，唱着而不知自己正让一个死去的名活着，如河让石光润而不知自己在磨。

At eighty she is something the great house never imagined her to be — a public author, though she has never left the courtyard. Strangers who only know the name Shen Rugù send her small gifts: a carved puppet of the fallen daughter, a ballad-sheet with the lament set down, a letter from a grandmother three towns over who taught the song to seven grandchildren. Lanxiang receives these at the gate, and the old companions tease her gently for being famous, and she laughs the way an eighty-year-old laughs at a title that means nothing beside a warm hearth. The season, which once set her to hide her name in every room, now lets her name live in every tea house, and the irony is not lost on her, though she would never call it that. She has become a voice the great house never imagined, writing from a courtyard it once locked her inside.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

到了八十，她成了那大宅从不曾想她能成的——一个公开的作者，尽管她从未离过这院。只识得"沈如故"之名的陌生人，给她寄小礼：一具雕的落难女偶、一张写了哀歌的唱单、一封三镇外祖母的信——她把那歌教给了七个孙。兰香在门口收这些，旧人轻逗她出了名，而她笑，如一个八十岁的人笑那在暖炉旁毫无意义的头衔。这季曾派她在每间屋藏名，如今任她的名活在每座茶楼；这反差她不是没觉，只是她绝不会那样叫它。

Around her, in these late years, the old companions gather the way leaves gather at the foot of a tree. Xiaolian, who once wept at her side in the great house, is now grey and steady, and brings the broth; Yuchan, the seasoned maid who read a fever before anyone, keeps the house with the same quiet competence she once kept the Zhichun Residence; Gantang and Bao Gai, the two who shared the sealed days of the sickness, come with news of their own grandchildren, proof that the line they survived continues. And Xu Wanquan and Lady Xue, the couple who took a stranger's child and called her daughter, are still there — older even than she in the way that parents always are — and they sit by the brazier as they did the night she walked back to them free, because some homecomings, unlike some wars, never really end.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

这些年近末，旧人聚到她身边，如叶聚在树脚。曾在大宅里在她身旁泣的小莲，如今灰发而稳，提了汤来；曾比谁都先读出热的老练丫鬟玉蝉，以她曾管知春馆那般的静能守着这屋；同熬过封起来的病日的甘棠、宝盖，带着自家孙辈的消息来，证着他们活下来的那一线在续。而许万全与薛氏，那收陌生孩叫女儿的夫妇，也还在——以父母总比子女老的那种老——他们坐在火盆边，如她自自由走回他们那夜那样，因有些归，不似有些战，是永不会真终的。

In the long evenings of her eightieth year she sometimes reckons the shape of the life she was given and the life she took. She was a maid who became a steward, a steward who became a free woman, a free woman who became a wife, a wife who became a widow, and a widow who became, by the stubbornness of a pen, an author the empire could not silence. Each self was a room the season pushed her into, and each she furnished with what she had — a dropped hairpin, a burned contract, a red thread, a written line. She does not regret the rooms she was forced through; she only marvels, the way an old woman marvels at a garden grown from a single seed, that the buried girl Shen Jialan should end as the sung name Shen Rugù, remembered by people who never knew her face.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

在她八十之年的长夜，她有时算一算那被给的命、与那被她取的命的形状。她是一个成了管家的丫鬟、一个成了自由妇的管家、一个成了妻的自由妇、一个成了寡妇的妻、一个凭一支笔的拗、成了帝国噤不住的作者的寡妇。每一个"我"，都是这季推进去的一间屋，而每一间她都以手边之物陈设——一支落簪、一张烧了的契、一截红线、一行写下的字。她不悔那些被逼穿过的屋；她只讶，如老妇讶一座从一粒籽长出的园，那埋下的姑娘沈嘉兰，竟以被唱着的名沈如故作结，被从不识她面容的人记着。

Then the word arrives from the capital, carried by a merchant who does not understand the weight of what he carries: the Shen family, convicted these many decades past of a treason it never committed, has at last been exonerated by the court, its name struck clean, its dead honored, its confiscated honors returned to heirs the old case never knew existed. It is the ending the play scripts always pointed toward, the vindication the page foretold when the sword could not win it. But the word arrives a season too late for the one who earned it, because Lanxiang, who was born Shen Jialan and wrote herself back into memory as Shen Rugù, has already laid down the brush, and the red thread her husband tied rests now with her, and she did not live to hear the empire speak the word her stories had already sung. She had written the verdict years before the court could bring itself to read it, and the delay, though it cost her the hearing, could not cost her the right.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

于是话从京里到了，由一个不解所携之重的客商捎来：沈家，数十年前被判了从不曾犯的叛，终被朝昭雪，名被洗清、死者受荣、被抄的荣誉归了旧案从不识存在的嗣。这是那些戏本一向指向的终，是那剑赢不得时、页先言中的昭雪。可这话，对那挣来它的人，到晚了一季——因那本生为沈嘉兰、又把自己写回记忆作沈如故的兰香，已放了笔，而她夫系的那截红线，如今与她同息；她没活到听那帝国说出她故事早已唱出的字。

It would be a cruelty, if the season were cruel, that the woman who cleared her family's name should miss the clearing. But the season, having taken her husband and her youth, seems here to grant a stranger mercy: the court's word was never the real exoneration. The Shen name was washed clean long before the edict, in the mouths of the boatmen and the grandmothers, in the puppets and the lullabies, in the thousand small tellings that no decree can unsing. Lanxiang understood this better than any magistrate; she wrote not to move a court but to move a people, and a people, once moved, does not need a seal to know the truth. The fragrance of the orchid, passed breath to breath, had already reached the capital — it only took the court this long to notice the smell of justice it had been standing in. The court's word was never the only word that mattered, and the people's came later and lasted longer.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

若这季是残酷的，那洗清自家名的女人竟错过那清，便是残忍。可这季既夺了她的夫与她的青春，于此似予一种异样的恕：朝的话从不是真的赦。沈姓早在诏前就被洗清了——在船夫与祖母的口里、在偶与摇篮曲里、在千桩没有诏能不唱的讲述里。兰香比任何官都更懂这个；她写，不是为动一座朝，而是为动一方人；而人，一旦动了，不需印才知真。兰的香，息传息，早到了京——只是朝站在这正义的气里，过了这么久才闻出来。

So she is not, in the end, a woman denied her victory. She is a woman who won it elsewhere, and left the court to catch up. The old companions who loved her know this, and they do not mourn the missed decree so much as they honor the life that made it inevitable. Xiaolian and Yuchan and the others keep the pages she left, the way one keeps a widow's seam — as proof of a hand that was here. And the adoptive parents, Xu Wanquan and Lady Xue, who gave her a free name when the great house would have kept her a thing, sit by the brazier in the knowledge that the daughter they chose outlived every title the empire bestowed and was undone by none of them, and that her name, the buried one and the borrowed one and the written one, has at last settled, like silt, into the clear water of being simply remembered.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

于是她终不是个被夺了胜的女人。她是个在别处赢了、留朝去追的。爱她的旧人懂这个，他们不怎么哀那错过的一道诏，只敬那使它不免的命。小莲、玉蝉与众，守着她留的页，如人守一痕寡妇的缝——作一只曾在此的手的证。而那养父母许万全、薛氏，在大宅本要把她当个物时给了她一个自由的名，坐在火盆边，知他们选的女儿，活得过了帝国授的每一个爵、没被其中任何一个毁掉；而她的名——埋下的、借来的、写下的——终如淤沙，沉进了"只被记着"这清水的底。

So we close episode thirty-six the way an old woman closes her eyes — knowing the tale is told and the teller may now rest. The playwright has reached eighty and written the truth into the memory of a people; the old companions keep her pages and her hearth; the Shen family is cleared at last, though the one who cleared it did not stay to see the seal. Episode thirty-seven, when it comes, is not an episode at all but a coda — the fragrance of the orchid, passed on, long and unbroken. But that is next week's tell. For now, remember only this: she did not live to hear the court say her name was innocent, but she lived to hear the people sing it was true, and the second, as every teller of tales knows, is the only exoneration that needs no emperor to sign it. The old companions keep her page warm, and the tale she finished keeps her name alive.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

于是我们如一个老妇合眼那样收束第三十六集——知故已讲完、讲者如今可歇。这写戏的到了八十，把真写进了人的记忆；旧人守着她的页与她的炉；沈家终清了，尽管那清它的人没留下来见那印。第三十七集，来的时候，根本不是一集，而是一段尾声——兰的香，传下去，长而不断。但那是下周的讲法。此刻你只记一句：她没活到听朝说她名无罪，却活到听人说它真；而第二件，如每个说书人都知，是那唯一不需皇帝签署的赦。
