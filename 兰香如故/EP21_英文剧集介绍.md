# 兰香如故 — The Fragrance of Orchids Remains
## Episode 21 · Told Like A Story

Episode 21 opens at Mr. Lu's shop, where Han Liang does a hard and kind thing. He goes and brings his sister home. She is a widow, sent back from her late husband's house with little but her name, and in this world a widow with no son is a woman the in-laws feel free to press. Han Liang will not leave her there. He settles her in the rooms above the shop, next to Lanxiang's family, and the two households become one small roof of quiet people. Lanxiang's mother takes to the sister at once, because a woman who has survived a hard marriage and come home with nothing is a woman she understands. The sister is gentle and tired, and she keeps to herself at first, but the shop wraps her in its plain rhythm. For a while it seems the worst is behind them, a brother, a sister, and a friend who reads poison for a living, all under one roof.

第二十一集在陆家铺子开。韩梁干了桩难而善的事：他把姐姐接回家。她是个寡妇，从亡夫家被打发回来，除了名什么也没带；这世道，没儿的寡妇，婆家觉得尽可拿捏。韩梁不肯留她在那。他把她安在铺子楼上，挨着兰香家，两户并一成一小片静人的屋顶。兰香娘一见那姐就投缘，因为熬过狠婚、空身回家的女人，她懂。那姐温而乏，起初自守，可铺子用平实的节奏裹住她。有一阵像最坏已过：兄、姐、一个靠读毒吃饭的朋友，同在一顶下。

</details>

The peace does not last a week. The late husband's family comes for Han's sister, the way they always do. They say she owes them, that the trousseau was theirs, that a widow with no child has no right to leave, and they stand in the shop door with the law twisted to fit their want. The clerks freeze. Mr. Lu frowns but will not brawl in his own store. Han Liang goes red and reaches for words that will not come, because poor scholars do not win shoving matches with men who own the street. The sister shrinks behind the counter. It is the old story, a woman with no man to shield her, and the shield they send for is the one person in the room who has spent a year learning how the powerful lean on the weak. Lanxiang steps out from the back, calm, and the men in the door do not know they have already lost.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

静没撑过一周。亡夫家来要韩姐，老样子。他们说她欠的、妆奁是他们的、没儿的寡妇没权走，杵在铺门口，拿法律扭成要的样。伙计僵了。陆先生皱眉，不肯在自己店里动手。韩梁涨红，话堵在喉，因为穷秀才推不过占街的汉子。姐缩在柜后。老戏：女人没男人护，而他们唤的护，是屋里那个花一年学强者怎么压弱的人。兰香从后头走出，静，门口那伙不知道，他们已经输了。

</details>

Lanxiang does not raise her voice, and she does not raise a hand. She reaches for a jar of tung oil, the kind used to seal paper, and she spills a little across the threshold, then she names the law out loud, plain and slow, the parts that say a widow may not be taken by force and that a man who enters a closed shop unasked is a trespasser the magistrate will fine. The oil makes the door a mess they will not cross without proof they own it, and the words make the cost of crossing clear. She speaks like a maid who has read the house rules to a hundred guests, certain and unbothered, and the men hear in her the same flat calm that faced down a poisoner. They came to bully a widow. They find a wall that quotes the court. They mutter, they step back, and they go, and the sister behind the counter has not been touched.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

兰香不提高嗓，不扬手。她取来一罐桐油——封纸用的那种——在门槛泼了些，然后大声、平、慢，背出律法里那段：寡妇不得被强抢，不请自入封起的铺子是擅闯，县官要罚。油把门弄成滩他们没产权证不敢跨的脏，话把跨的代价说清。她像给百个客人念过家规的丫鬟，笃定、不烦，那伙人从她身上听出吓退投毒者那同一种平静。他们来欺负寡妇，撞上一面会引律法的墙。他们嘟囔，退，走，柜后那姐没被碰一根指头。

</details>

After the door clears, the two mothers do what mothers do. Lanxiang's mother and Han's mother sit by the well of the shop court and talk, and the talk turns, as such talks will, to the two young people under their roof. Lanxiang's mother has watched her daughter hold a great house through poison and come out free, and she sees in Han Liang a man who treats that daughter as an equal, not a servant. Han's mother has watched her son light up around the same girl, and she sees a daughter-in-law who could read the world for a scholar too soft for it. Neither says the word matchmaker yet. They only trade small smiles and say the boy is steady, the girl is quick, and the roof is lucky to hold them both. Lanxiang, passing with a stack of scrolls, hears and pretends not to, but her ears are warm. The mothers have, without asking, already said yes.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

门清了，两位娘做娘会做的。兰香娘和韩娘坐在铺院井边聊，话绕，总会绕到屋顶下两个年轻人。兰香娘看女儿撑过大宅的毒、自由出来，又看韩梁把那女儿当平人、不当仆；韩娘看儿子在同一姑娘身边亮起来，看得到一个能替太软的秀才读世界的儿媳。都没吐"媒人"二字，只互换小笑，说后生稳、姑娘机、这屋顶有福气同罩俩。兰香抱一摞卷走过，听见，装没听，耳却热。两位娘没问，已经应了。

</details>

Inside the estate the second daughter turns a corner of her own. The confinement that followed the cave has done what a scolding never could. Alone with her thoughts and the quiet, she sees clearly the guard she thought she loved was the blade at her brother's throat, and that her young sister is walking to a marquis to buy back the name she lost. The shame of it settles, and then it lifts, and she does the brave thing she has not done before. She goes to her mother and tells her to let go. Not of the family, of the hurt. She says the second daughter was a fool, the third daughter was a hero, and the mother should stop guarding one girl's pride and start blessing the other's road. The mother listens, surprised, then grateful, because a daughter who sees her own error is a daughter worth trusting again. The locked door, it turns out, opened something in the girl behind it.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

府里，二小姐也转了自己的弯。洞后那禁足，骂办不到的事办到了。独对着心思和静，她看清以为爱的那个仆，是抵兄长喉的刀；看清小妹正朝侯爷去，赎她丢的名。羞落定，又散，她做了从前没做的勇敢事：去见娘，叫她放下。不是放家，是放那口气。她说二小姐是蠢的，三小姐是英雄，娘该停护一个姑娘的脸面、开始祝福另一个的路。娘听着，先惊，后谢，因为能认自己错的女儿，才再值得信。那锁起的门，原来开了门后姑娘里头什么东西。

</details>

But the estate has its own marriage in mind, and it is not Han Liang's. Senior Lady Lin, watching the young master and the house he will one day lead, sets her eye on Zheng Manyi, the daughter of the Duke. It is a match of standing, not of the heart, the kind great houses make to keep their name high and their doors open to power. The old lady speaks of it to Lin Jinqi as a thing to be arranged, a duty dressed as a favor. She does not know, or does not say, that her son has been to a book shop and watched a former maid laugh over a ledger. Lin Jinqi hears the name Zheng Manyi and gives the answer a son gives, polite, noncommittal, the kind that agrees to nothing and refuses nothing. The Du poison is barely cold, and already the house is measuring him for a wife he has not chosen. The old lady's plan is sound. It is also, to him, a wall he did not ask to be built.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

可府里另有一门亲，不是韩梁的。林母看着年轻主子和他日后要撑的府，相中国公之女郑蔓宜。是门第的配，不是心的配，大宅拿来抬名、朝权开门的那种。老夫人对林锦岐说，当一件要办的事，披着好意的职分。她不知、或没说，儿子去过书店，看前丫鬟趴账上笑。林锦岐听"郑蔓宜"三字，给儿子那种答：礼、不置可否，不答不拒。杜家的毒才凉，府已给他量起没挑的妻。老夫人的方稳，对他，却是道没请人修的墙。

</details>

Lin Jinqi goes to see Lanxiang anyway, and he goes with orchids, a small pot, the kind a man brings when the words have not caught up to the feet. He finds her at the shop, and before he can speak he hears her singing, a plain tune, and Han Liang singing it back across the counter, the two of them in easy harmony, a song with no shame in it. Lin Jinqi stands with the orchids and feels the room tilt. He holds out the pot, says a few plain things, and then he does what the young master of a house does. He tells Lanxiang he could put Han Liang forward, recommend him for a post, lift the scholar a rung. It is a kind offer and a test in the same breath. Lanxiang refuses it, gently, because she will not have the boy she likes owe his road to a man's favor. The orchids sit between them, and the song hangs in the air, and Lin Jinqi understands the answer before she finishes it.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

林锦岐还是去看兰香，带一盆兰草——男人话赶不上脚时会带的小盆。他在铺里寻到她，没开口先听见她唱，一支平调，韩梁在柜那头接唱，俩人合得松快，歌里没半点羞。林锦岐端着兰草立着，觉屋斜了。他递出盆，说几句平话，然后办年轻主子会办的那桩：他说他可以举荐韩梁，给个差、抬秀才一级。是好意，也是同口气里的一试。兰香婉拒，因为她不肯让喜欢的后生拿路去欠人的情。兰草搁在俩人之间，歌悬在空气里，林锦岐没等她说完就懂了答。

</details>

Han Liang, for his part, does not wait on favors. That evening he goes to his mother and asks her to do the oldest thing a serious boy can. Send a matchmaker. To Lanxiang. He says it plain, that the girl at the shop is the one he wants, that he has watched her stand between a widow and a mob and come out clean, and that he would rather build a life with her than climb a post someone else opens. His mother smiles, because this is the son she raised, and she sends word to the go-between the next morning. The matchmaker's foot is not yet on the step, but the intent is set, and in a city where marriages are made by letters and locks, a mother's yes is half the door. Lanxiang does not know yet that the question is coming. She only knows the scholar looked at her different at supper, and that her mother hummed, and that the shop felt, for the first time, like a home with a knocking at the gate.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

韩梁这边，不等谁施恩。当晚他去见娘，请她办正经后生最老那桩：请媒人。向兰香。他说得明：铺里那姑娘就是他要的，他看她站在寡妇和一群人中间、干干净净出来，他宁可和她搭一辈子，不爬别人开的阶。他娘笑，因为这是她养的儿子，翌晨就给媒人递了话。媒人的脚还没踏上阶，意已经定了；在这婚事靠信和锁定的城，娘一句应，是半扇门。兰香还不知问要来。她只知晚饭时秀才看她不同，娘哼了调，铺子头回像个家门口有人敲的屋。

</details>

So episode 21 brings the freed girl to the edge of a new life and shows the old one still circling. Han Liang carries his widowed sister home, and Lanxiang turns a jar of oil and a line of law into a wall no bully will cross. The two mothers trade smiles over the match, the second daughter finds her sense behind a locked door, and Senior Lady Lin reaches for a Duke's daughter to wife her son. Lin Jinqi brings orchids, hears a song, offers to lift the scholar and is refused, and Han Liang answers by sending a matchmaker for the girl. Episode 22 will open that door, or close it, and the young master who taught her to watch will have to decide what he is watching for. But that is next time. For now, remember this. A girl who read poison in sweet water reads a clean love just as fast, and the man with the orchids is running out of reasons to stand at the gate.
