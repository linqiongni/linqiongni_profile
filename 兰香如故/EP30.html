Episode 30 lands the whole family in the capital at last. The boat brings them up the river and into the great city Lanxiang feared, the place where her own Shen blood fell apart years before. They move into the Shen family's old residence, the house her family used to own before ruin took it. Walking through those doors is strange for her, because the walls remember a life she lost, and now she lives in them again as a married stranger. The rooms are quieter than they were, the money is gone, but the bones of the place are the same. She does not say much. She just looks, and the old shadow she carried on the water settles onto solid ground with her. She walks the rooms alone that first night, touching a wall here and there, letting the place tell her what it was. The house does not answer, but it does not scare her either. She came as a forced bride, yet the ground under her is her own family's, and that changes how she will stand in this city, on her own blood's old floor.

第三十集把全府终于送进了京城。船顺着河把他们带进这座兰香怕的城——她沈家骨血多年前就是在这儿垮的。他们住进沈家旧宅，那座她家败落前本属于自己的屋子。踏进那些门，对她怪得很，因为墙记得她丢掉的活法，如今她以陌生媳妇的身份又住在里头。屋子比从前静，钱也没了，可骨架还是那样。她没多说，只看着，她在水上扛着的旧影，随她落到了实地上。

The first thing on Lanxiang's mind is not the house but the friend. She goes looking for Lü Jí'ér, the girl A Zhi told her was trapped in this city. It takes asking around, quiet asking, the kind a clever maid knows how to do without drawing the wrong eyes. She learns Lü Jí'ér is indeed here, kept close by Zhao Xingtang, the woman who holds her bond. The news tightens Lanxiang's jaw. She came to the capital against her will, but now that she is here, the reason is in front of her, a friend held like property. She does not waste a day. She starts to plan how to get Lü Jí'ér back, the way she always plans, quiet and a step ahead. She sends A Zhi to listen in the markets and questions the right servants with a smile, never letting on how much the answer matters. By the second day she has the shape of it, where Lü Jí'ér sleeps, who guards her, and what Zhao asks of her. The plan forms in her head the way a map forms in the dark, one line at a time, until she can see the road.

兰香心里头一件事不是这屋子，是那个朋友。她去寻吕蕺儿，就是阿枳说困在这城里的姑娘。她悄悄打听——聪明丫鬟都懂怎么问不引错眼的那种。她探明吕蕺儿确在这，被赵星棠看紧，那女人攥着她的身契。这消息让兰香咬了牙。她是被逼来京城的，可既来了，理由就在眼前：一个被当财物攥着的朋友。她不耽搁，开始盘算怎么把吕蕺儿要回来，还是她那套：静、早一步。

The hard part is the paper. Lü Jí'ér's bond, the document that says who owns her, sits in Zhao Xingtang's hands. Zhao is Lin Jinqi's wife and a woman of standing in the capital, not someone you walk up to and simply ask a favor from. To free Lü Jí'ér, Lanxiang must go through Zhao, and Zhao will not give her up for nothing. So the bond is the wall, and the only door through it is Zhao's own consent, bought at a price. Lanxiang understands the shape of the problem at once. This is not a fight of strength but a fight of words and wits, and those are the tools she trusts most. A bond is just ink on paper, but ink backed by a powerful family can lock a person for life. Lanxiang has seen that kind of lock before, and she knows the only key is the owner's hand. So she does not rage at the paper. She studies the owner, because to free Lü Jí'ér she must first read the woman who holds the string, and a woman is easier to move than a wall of silver.

难的是那张纸。吕蕺儿的身契，写着归谁所有的文书，攥在赵星棠手里。赵是林锦岐的妻，在京城是个有头脸的人，不是你走上去随口求个人情就成的。要放吕蕺儿，兰香得过赵这一关，而赵不会白放人。所以身契是墙，唯一的门是赵自己的应允，拿价换。兰香立刻看透了这局的形状。这不是力的仗，是话和心的仗，而这两样，是她最信的家什。

Lanxiang dresses plain and goes to Zhao Xingtang's door. She is shown into a room full of fine ladies, the kind of gathering where a servant girl is meant to keep her eyes down. But Lanxiang does not crumble. When the talk turns and someone presses her, she answers with calm, clear sense, holding her own among women who outrank her by birth and marriage. She does not shout, she does not beg, she simply meets their words with better words. The room, used to girls who fold, finds one who stands. Zhao Xingtang watches this with a cool eye, because a bride who can hold a room is a bride worth watching, and maybe worth fearing. Lanxiang answers a cut with a calm fact, and a sneer with a plain truth, until the room forgets she came in as a servant. Zhao says little during the exchange, but her eyes move between Lanxiang and the other ladies, weighing a new thing in her house. A bride who can hold her own among equals is a bride who may one day hold more than anyone expects.

兰香穿得素，去敲赵星棠的门。她被领进一间坐满贵妇的屋，那种本该叫丫鬟低眼的场合。可兰香没垮。话头转过来、有人逼她时，她回得镇定、清楚，在一屋子出身嫁资都压她一头的人里，稳稳立住。她不喊不求，只用更好的话接住人家的话。这屋习惯低头姑娘，偏撞上一个站着的。赵星棠冷眼瞧着，因为一个能撑住一屋的新媳妇，是值的瞧、或许也值的怕的。

Then Zhao names her price. She wants thirty thousand taels to hand over Lü Jí'ér's bond, a sum meant to be impossible, a wall built of silver. Most people would step back and give up. Lanxiang does not. Instead she turns the moment with a bit of business sense. She points out a tangle in Zhao's own family affairs, a dispute that could turn ugly and drag Zhao's name through the court, and she offers a clean way out. By showing Zhao how the ransom could pull her into a mess she does not want, Lanxiang makes the price look like a trap rather than a win. The ladies around them go quiet, because none of them saw the angle she just opened. Zhao thought the number would end the talk, a wall of silver no maid could climb. Instead it becomes the ground for Lanxiang's real move. She does not reach for coin she does not have. She reaches for the one thing Zhao cannot buy her way out of, a family scandal that a ransom would only pull closer. The price, turned, becomes the trap, and the room goes still as the trap shuts.

接着赵开了价。她要三万两才肯交吕蕺儿的身契，这数是存心叫人拿不出，是用银子砌的墙。多数人早退了。兰香没。她反倒用点生意经把这一刻翻过来。她点出赵自家一桩家务乱麻——一桩能闹大、把赵的名声拖进官的争，又给了条干净脱身法。她让赵看清：这赎金会把她拽进她不要的麻烦，价不像赢，倒像阱。周遭贵妇静了，因为这角度她们谁都没看见。

On top of the talk, Lanxiang proves herself another way. She is asked, or dares, to show her hand at the court ball game, the stick-and-ball sport the fine ladies play to pass the afternoon. Lanxiang lines up the shot the way she does everything, calm and exact, and she strikes it true in a single stroke. The ball drops where it should, and the room lets out a small sound of surprise. A servant girl who can out-play her betters at their own game is not a servant girl they can keep looking down on. The display is not just sport. It is a message, that this girl's worth is not written on the paper Zhao holds. The ladies who laughed a moment before now watch with a different face, because skill like that does not come from a low place. Lanxiang sets the stick down and meets Zhao's eye, calm as ever. The shot said what her words did not need to, that she is not the small thing the bond makes her look, and the room has seen it with its own eyes, not just heard it claimed.

除了嘴上，兰香另用一招自证。有人撺掇、她也敢，去显显那 court 球戏的手——贵妇们午后消遣的杆球。兰香像办别的那样，镇定、准，一杆就中。球落在该落处，屋里轻轻一声讶。一个能在她们自家戏上赢过主子的丫鬟，再不是能随便低看的丫鬟。这显不是玩，是话：这姑娘的价，不在赵手里那张纸上。

The mix of wit and skill does what shouting could not. Zhao Xingtang, pressed on the family mess and impressed despite herself by the ball shot, agrees to let the bonds go. Lanxiang walks out with the papers for both Lü Jí'ér and A Zhi, the two girls who came to the capital as property and leave it as free persons. The thirty thousand taels is never paid in full the way Zhao first demanded, because Lanxiang's move turned the trap back on the trapper. It is a clean win won by a girl who refused to be small in a room full of people born above her. The bonds are hers, and so are the two lives tied to them. Outside, Lü Jí'ér and A Zhi are told they are free, and for a breath neither believes it. Then the truth lands, and the two girls who came as things to be owned walk out as their own. Lanxiang does not make a speech. She simply hands over the papers and lets the freedom speak. The capital that took her family once has just given two girls back their names, and she counts that as the real win of the day.

话与艺合起来，办成了喊破嗓子办不成的。赵星棠，被家务乱麻逼着、又由衷服了那杆球，应了放契。兰香拿着吕蕺儿和阿枳两人的文书出来——两个进京是财物、出京是自由人的姑娘。那三万两没按赵起初的要法全付，因为兰香这招把阱反扣回了设阱的人。这是个干净胜，胜在一个不肯在满屋生来高她一头的人里矮下去的姑娘。契是她的了，拴在契上的两条命也是。

With Lü Jí'ér free, the girl makes a bold choice of her own. She does not want to go back to serve anyone. She wants to go to Quanzhou and set up a household headed by a woman, a home with no master, where she answers only to herself. It is a rare thing for a girl of her standing to try, and Lanxiang admires it. To help her on the road, Lanxiang takes her own jewelry to the pawn shop and trades it for cash. Then, so Lü Jí'ér will not arrive empty, she sews gold leaf, thin sheets of real gold, into the lining of her clothes. The gift is quiet but heavy, the kind only a true friend would think to give. She tells Lanxiang the plan on a quiet evening, and Lanxiang listens without trying to talk her out of it. A household headed by a woman is a hard road, but harder roads are sometimes the only honest ones. The gold leaf in the lining is meant to buy time and safety until Lü Jí'ér finds her feet far from any master's gate. It is the most practical love Lanxiang knows how to show, and she shows it without a word of pity.

吕蕺儿自由了，自己做了个胆大的选。她不想回去伺候谁了。她要去泉州，立一户女户——没有男主的家，只对自己交代。一个她这身份的姑娘敢试，少有，兰香佩服。为帮她上路，兰香把自己首饰送进当铺换了现钱。再者为叫吕蕺儿不是空手到，她把金叶子——薄薄的真金片——缝进她衣里。这礼静，却沉，是只有真朋友才想得到的给法。

What Lanxiang does not know is that Lin Jinqi is watching, and he quietly redeems the jewelry she pawned. He says nothing to her about it. He simply goes to the shop, pays the debt, and brings the pieces back where they belong, so that her gift to Lü Jí'ér costs her nothing in the end. It is his own quiet way of standing behind her without stealing the moment. The man who drugged her onto the boat is also the man who will not let her sacrifice what she owns for a friend. She finds out later, and the knowledge sits oddly in her chest, soft where she expected only hard. When she learns it, she does not thank him loud, because thanks between them is a tangled thing. But she stops seeing him as only the man who drugged the boat. He is also the one who quietly kept her whole, even when she was busy giving herself away for someone else. The gold he returned is small, yet it shifts the weight she carries when she thinks of him, and the shift surprises her more than the gift.

Lanxiang is not done earning. She takes a pen name, Shen Rugú, and begins to write play scripts for a troupe in the city. The stories she sets to paper are sharp and fresh, and the one called The Feathered Immortal catches fire with the crowds. In three months the play runs hot, and the money comes in steady, until she has earned five hundred taels, the sum she means to give back to Lin Jinqi for the cost of bringing her and the others this far. She works behind a false name so the Lin family's standing stays clean, but the pen is hers, and the win is hers too. The troupe keeps her scripts because the crowds laugh and weep in the right places, and the name Shen Rugú starts to mean something on the city's walls. Lin Jinqi hears the talk but says nothing, letting her have the stage without his shadow on it. Five hundred taels is a large sum for a girl who came with nothing, and she counts it twice before she goes to hand it back, proud that the coin is earned and not given.

So episode 30 shows Lanxiang turning a city she feared into a stage she can work. She found her friend, beat a price meant to be impossible, freed two girls with words and a single clean shot, and sent Lü Jí'ér toward a free life with gold sewn into her clothes. Lin Jinqi watched and gave back what she gave away. And with a pen name and a hit play, Lanxiang earned her own way and repaid the man who carried her here by force. The capital that broke her blood now pays her, and the girl who came as a prisoner starts to look like the one holding the pen. She has not forgiven the boat, and she may never, but she has built something in the city that broke her blood. The friend is free, the plays are hot, and the debt is paid. What stays between her and Lin Jinqi is still unfinished, yet for the first time in the capital she stands on her own ground, with ink on her fingers and gold she earned in her pocket, no longer a guest in her own family's house.
