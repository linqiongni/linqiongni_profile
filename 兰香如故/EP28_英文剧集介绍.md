# 兰香如故 — The Fragrance of Orchids Remains
## Episode 28 · Told Like a Story

Episode 28 opens the morning after the wedding, and the first job is to meet the elders. A new bride is supposed to dress fine and pay respect, but Lanxiang has other ideas. She puts on the plain, simple clothes her own parents prepared for her, the kind that shout she did not come here to pretend. No fancy jewelry, no bright silks from the Lin family. She wears what her family gave her, and she wears it on purpose. The other women in the house notice right away, because in a great family the clothes are the first thing everyone reads. By dressing this way she tells the room without a word that she is here under force, and she will not play the happy daughter they wanted. It is a small rebellion, but in that house a small one is still seen by everyone. The morning after a forced wedding is never a soft thing, and she means to start it on her own terms rather than theirs. A plain dress on a rich bride is a small sentence, but in that house everyone reads it clearly.

The rite calls for serving tea to the elders, and that is where Lanxiang goes quiet for real. She walks up, pours the tea, and hands the cup, but her face is flat and she does not say a word of the sweet lines a bride is meant to say. No smile, no soft words, no calling anyone grandmother or mother the way they expect. The hall goes still, because everyone knows the script and she is not following it. The old ladies wait for the pretty words, and they do not come. She just stands there with the cup, cold and done. It is the kind of silence that speaks louder than any slip of the tongue, and the family feels it like a draft through a closed door. The elders trade glances as the tea cools a little in her hand before anyone steps in to fill the gap. A bride who will not speak her lines leaves the whole hall to guess what she is thinking, and none of them guess anything kind.

礼数要奉茶给长辈，兰香到这儿是真沉了脸。她走上去、倒茶、递杯，可脸是平的，该说的那套新娘甜话，一句没有。不笑、不软言、不按他们指望的叫一声祖母娘。厅里静了，因为人人都知剧本，她偏不照演。老夫人们等那几句好听的话，没等来。她就端着杯站着，冷冷的，完了。这种沉默比说错话还响，这家人觉着它，像风从关着的门缝里钻进来。

Senior Lady Lin sees the trouble before it spreads, and so does Second Aunt. The two step in to smooth the moment over. They laugh a little, say the girl is just shy, say the clothes are a humble choice that shows good breeding, and turn the flat air into something the room can swallow. They cover for Lanxiang without shaming her, which is more than most mothers would do. Second Aunt in particular keeps the talk moving so no one can press the silent bride. It is a small kindness, and Lanxiang feels it. She does not soften, but she lets the two women carry the minute, because fighting them would only hurt the people who are trying to help her stand in this hard house. For one strange minute the two older women and the silent bride stand on the same side, and the house does not know what to make of it. Kindness from the very people who run the place is the last thing Lanxiang expected on her first morning.

Then comes the part that softens the old lady for real. Lanxiang and Gantang have prepared a fish soup, a plain dish made with care, and they offer it to the eldest grandmother of the house. It is not a grand gift, just honest food from a girl who will not bow but will still cook. The old lady hesitates. She could refuse and let the silence win, or she could take it and show the house that this strange new bride is not heartless. After a beat, she takes the bowl. She eats, and the room relaxes a little. That one sip of soup does more than all the pretty words Lanxiang left unsaid. The grandmother has accepted her, in her own quiet way, and the family reads it. A bowl of plain soup, taken in silence, becomes the first true peace between the new bride and the oldest woman in the room. The grandmother does not say she forgives the cold tea, but the sip she takes says she will make room for this girl after all.

Once the tea is done, Lin Jinqi brings up a bigger plan. He tells the elders he wants to take the whole family to the capital. Not just himself, but the old ladies, the sisters, the household, everyone. The capital is where the real power and the old family ties live, and he thinks it is time to move the center there. The proposal lands heavy, because leaving the home they know is no small thing. The elders look at each other, and the room turns from the morning's small drama to a large question. Where they live next will change every life in that house, and Lin Jinqi has just put the idea on the table without warning. Some of the younger sisters brighten at the idea of the city, while the older ones sit with the heavy thought of leaving the only home they have known. The capital is a far word, and Lin Jinqi has dropped it into the room like a stone into still water.

Lanxiang hears the plan and pushes back hard. She does not want to go. The reason is plain to her: she misses her own parents already, and the thought of leaving them farther behind hurts. On top of that, the capital holds a bad memory for her. It is where her own family, the Shen family, fell apart years ago, and the shadow of that old ruin still sits on the city in her mind. She will not say yes to walking back into the place that broke her blood. So she says no, quietly but firmly, and the house sees that the new bride has a line she will hold even against her husband. The capital is not just a city to her. It is the scene of a wound. She does not raise her voice, but her no is clear, and the capital plan meets its first real wall in the girl who was just married into the family. The city that broke the Shen name is the last place she will walk into willing, and she says so without shame.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

兰香听了这打算，硬顶回去。她不想去。理由她自己清楚：她已经想爹娘想得慌，再离远些，疼。更要紧，京城在她心里有旧影。那年她沈家就是在京城垮的，那场旧败的影子还压在那城上。她不肯点头走回那个碎了她骨血的地方。于是她说不，安静，却硬，满府看得出这新媳妇有条线，连丈夫面前也守得住。京城于她不止一座城，是道伤的现场。

</details>

The move is not decided yet, because one of the sisters blocks it in her own way. The second sister kneels outside the room for a long time, refusing to get up, to show she will not go along with whatever is being pushed. It is a quiet protest, a girl using her body to say no when her voice is not enough. The family does not know what to do with a daughter planted on the floor, and the standoff drags. Her knees must hurt, but she stays. The house watches, torn between respect for her will and worry that this will harden into a fight. In a family where girls are meant to obey, a long kneel in the hallway is a loud thing to do. The servants step around her, and the masters pass without a word, because no one wants to be the one to lift a daughter off the floor. A silent girl on her knees is harder to answer than a shouting one, and the house learns that the easy way out is not there.

Help arrives from an unexpected door. The third sister, who is with child, comes back to the house with her husband to lend a hand. A pregnant girl might be expected to stay quiet, but she shows up to stand with the family in the middle of the dispute. Her husband backs her, and the two of them add weight to the side that wants the old man's yes. The second sister's long kneel and the third sister's return turn the matter from one girl's protest into a family question with more than one voice. The old master listens now, because more of his daughters are in the room than just the one on the floor. The house shifts, slowly, toward a decision it did not see coming that morning. The pregnant sister's calm face does more to move the old man than any argument the others could have shouted across the hall. She stands with her husband at her side, and the old master sees that his daughters are no longer the small girls he once told what to do.

In the end Old Master Lin gives the nod. After the kneeling, the return, and the quiet pressure from his daughters, he agrees to let Lin Jinqi take the family to the capital. The word from the head of the house settles the matter, and what was a fight becomes a plan. The old ladies will pack, the sisters will go, and Lanxiang will be pulled along whether her heart is in it or not. The old master does not explain much. He simply decides, the way the head of a great family does, and the rest follow. The capital that Lanxiang fears is now the place they are all headed, and the house begins to turn toward the road. Trunks are opened, lists are drawn, and the quiet that follows a hard decision settles over a house that will soon stand empty. The old master says little more, because in a family like this the head's nod is the only word that ever really counts.

While the family turns toward the capital, Jiugao pulls a stunt of his own. He does not want to be matched with a girl, so to scare off any matchmaker he dresses in women's clothes and walks the streets loud and proud. The point is plain: a man who parades in a gown is a man no proper family will court, and the talk of his marriage dies fast. He would rather be the laugh of the town than the husband of a stranger. The house is shocked, the neighbors stare, and the matchmaking stops. It is a silly, brave move, and it works. Jiugao buys his freedom with a borrowed skirt, and the family, busy with bigger plans, lets him have his strange win. The story of the man in a gown runs through the town by nightfall, and not one family steps forward to match him after that. Jiugao wears the laugh proudly, because a silly name in the street is a small price for a life he still gets to choose.

So episode 28 shows a house at a turning point. Lanxiang met the elders in her own clothes and her own silence, and still won a bowl of soup from the grandmother. Lin Jinqi set the family on the road to the capital, and Lanxiang dug in her heels against the city that broke her. The sisters, one on her knees and one with child, pulled the old master to yes. And Jiugao, dressed as a woman, kept his own life his own. The move is set, the road waits, and the next days will carry this whole household toward the place Lanxiang least wants to be. What she does there, with the old shadow over her head, is the story the next episode will have to tell. The road to the capital is long, and Lanxiang walks it with her old fear packed beside her, ready or not. The house believes it is moving toward power, but she knows it is also moving toward the place where her own blood once fell, and she goes with her eyes open.
