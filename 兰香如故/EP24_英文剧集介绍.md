# 兰香如故 — The Fragrance of Orchids Remains
## Episode 24 · Told Like A Story

Episode 24 opens where episode 23 left off, at the door of the Qingyan House. Lin Jinqi has just walked in a breath too late, missing Lanxiang by the width of a wall as she climbed out the back. He does not leave empty handed. He asks the madam hard questions and pokes through the rooms himself, and in the painter's little corner he finds a fan. On it, half finished, is a painting in a hand he knows better than his own. It is Lanxiang's brush, the same strokes she used on the scrolls back home. The paint is still fresh, which tells him she was here recently and left in a hurry, not broken, not ruined. A man like Lin Jinqi reads a thing like that the way other men read a letter. He slips the fan into his sleeve and steps back into the Yangzhou night with his chest a little looser. She is alive. She got out. That is enough to keep him moving.

第二十四集接着二十三集收尾处开场，就在轻烟楼门前。林锦岐刚走进去，慢了半口气，兰香翻后墙走，与他隔一道墙错过。他没空手出来。他逼问老鸨、自己翻了屋子，在画师那小角落寻到一把扇。扇上半成的一幅画，那笔他比自己的还熟——是兰香的手，和家里临帖时一个样。颜料还湿，说明她刚在这儿、是急慌着走的，没碎、没毁。林锦岐这种人读这个，像旁人读信。他把扇子塞进袖，退回扬州的夜，胸口松了些。她活着，她出来了。这就够他接着动了。

While Lin Jinqi is still turning the city, help for Lanxiang comes from a softer quarter. Han Liang, the decent young man who was nearly dragged into Zheng Manyi's staged scandal, has been looking for her too, and he is not alone. Furong Xiu, a friend with a good heart and a sharper eye, rides with him. The two of them pick up her trail on the southern road and find her at last, thin, wary, and running on nothing but will. When Lanxiang sees familiar faces, the wall she has been holding up all this time finally shakes. They do not ask for the long story. They wrap her in a cloak, put her on a horse, and bring her home, back to the safe walls she was taken from. For the first time in what feels like a year, Lanxiang sleeps without one eye open, because the people around her now are the ones who would rather take a blade than hand her back.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

林锦岐还在城里转，兰香的救兵却从软处来。韩梁——那个险些被郑蔓宜做局拖进去的厚道后生——也在找她，而且不是一个人。芙蓉秀，一个心好、眼更利的朋友，与他同骑。俩人沿着南路拾她的线，终于找着她：瘦、警、全凭一股劲在跑。兰香见着熟脸，一直撑着的墙这回晃了。他们没问长篇。拿斗篷裹了她，扶上马，带回了家——带回她被掳走前那方安稳的墙。兰香像是过了一年头回合眼睡，因为身边这拨人，宁愿挨刀也不肯把她送回去。

</details>

Safe at home, Lanxiang's first thought is not for herself. She remembers Lü Jí'ér, the sharp-tongued girl from the old courtyard who risked her own neck to walk Lanxiang out of the Qingyan House. Leaving her behind feels like a debt that will not sit still. So Lanxiang does what the Lin household does best in a crisis. She asks, and the people who love her answer. Maids and friends pool what silver they have, a little from here, a little from there, because buying a life back is cheaper than mourning one. They mean to redeem Lü Jí'ér, to walk into that painted house with coin in hand and walk her out the front door, proper. It is a small, stubborn act of loyalty in a story full of people who only ever take. Lanxiang has learned that the ones who give are the ones worth living for. She has seen enough taking in her own life to know the difference the moment it shows up.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

回了安稳处，兰香头一个想的不是自己。她记着吕蕺儿——旧院里那个尖牙利齿的姑娘，拿自己脖子去冒险把兰香引出轻烟楼。把她撂下，像一笔静不下的债。于是兰香做了林府遇急最拿手的事：她开口，爱她的人应。丫鬟和朋友凑银子，这儿一点、那儿一点，因为买一条命回来，比哭一条命便宜。他们要去赎吕蕺儿，拿银子堂堂正正从前门把她领出来。在这满篇只知拿人的故事里，这是个小小的、犟犟的义气。兰香早懂了：肯给的人，才值得活。

</details>

With Lü Jí'ér's rescue in motion, Lanxiang turns her mind to the other score, the one with Zheng Manyi's name on it. She hears that the Yongchang Marquis is to be married, and that the great houses, including the Zheng family, will gather for a wedding opera, the kind of show where everyone wears their best mask. Lanxiang sees the opening. A wedding is a crowd, a crowd is noise, and noise is the best cover for a trap. She plans to use the opera stage to draw Zheng Manyi out and show the court what that family is really made of. It is a bold, almost reckless idea for a maid with no rank, but Lanxiang has never let rank decide what she could do. She lays the plan to Lin Jinqi and the others, and the household, against its better judgment, decides to back her. They have followed this girl into worse corners before, and they are not about to stop now.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

吕蕺儿赎救在动，兰香把心思转到另一笔账——账上写着郑蔓宜的名。她听说永昌侯要办大婚，世家包括郑家都会去听堂会，那种人人戴最好面具的戏。兰香看见缝了。婚宴是人堆，人堆是声，声是陷阱最好的遮。她盘算借着戏台把郑蔓宜引出来，让朝廷看清那一家到底什么货色。对一个没名分的丫鬟，这念头大胆、近乎冒失，可兰香从不让名分替她决定能做什么。她把盘算摆在林锦岐和众人面前，府里明知不智，还是决定撑她。

</details>

The wedding day arrives, and the Yongchang Marquis's house is all lanterns and laughter on the surface. Underneath, it is a different thing. The marquis's enemies have not stayed home, and the opera stage that should be harmless is loaded with a quiet, murderous intent no guest can see from the wine table. Lanxiang knows this, and she walks into it anyway. She times her move for the height of the performance, when every eye is on the singers. Then, from the shadows of the hall, she steps into the light, a maid who was supposedly gone, very much present, very much in command of the room. The crowd gasps. Zheng Manyi, seated among the fine ladies, goes white, because the last person she expected to see standing is the one she paid to ruin. The music stutters to a stop, and for one long breath the whole hall holds its breath with her.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

大婚日到了，永昌侯府面上满灯满笑。底下却是另一样。侯爷的对头没在家待着，那本该无害的戏台，悄悄压着一股没人从酒桌上看出的杀机。兰香知道，还是走了进去。她挑了戏到高潮、人人盯台的时候动手。然后，从堂下阴影里，她走到灯下——一个本该没了影的丫鬟，活生生在、稳稳控着场。满堂抽气。坐在贵女堆里的郑蔓宜脸白了，因为她最没料到会站着的人，正是她花钱要毁的那个。

</details>

Lanxiang does not shout. She simply speaks, calm and clear, about what was done to her, the drug, the staged shame, the bought troupe, and she lets her words land one by one on Zheng Manyi's pride. It is a deliberate poke, and Zheng Manyi, proud and cornered, loses the composure she spent a lifetime building. She spits out wild words, loud enough to carry, words that accuse, boast, and threaten in the same breath. What Zheng Manyi does not know is that a door away, behind a thin panel, stands the Crown Prince himself, and every reckless syllable she just loosed falls into his ear. He hears it all, the confession without a confession, the vanity that names itself out loud. The prince on the other side does not move, but his mind is already turning, filing each reckless word away. A door is a thin thing, and on this day it keeps no secret.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

兰香没喊。她只平静清楚地说，对她做了什么：迷药、做局的羞、买通的戏班，一字一字砸在郑蔓宜的傲上。这是存心戳，郑蔓宜又傲又被逼，把一辈子垒的镇定丢了。她吐出狂言，响得传得出去，又告又夸又威胁，一口气里全有。郑蔓宜不知道的是，一门之隔、薄板后头，站着太子本人，她松出去的每句没把门的话，全落进他耳里。他听得一清二楚——不供的供、自己报名的狂。门是薄物，这天它守不住任何秘密。

</details>

The fallout is swift because the witness is the highest in the land. The Crown Prince, having heard Zheng Manyi's own mouth condemn her, does not let the moment pass. Orders are given, and Zheng Manyi is taken not to a family scolding but to the Ministry of Justice, the court that judges the great and the small alike. She is to confess there, in writing and in person, to what she did and said. For a girl who thought her family name made her untouchable, the walk to the Ministry is a long one, and the door that closes behind her is made of stone, not privilege. The Zheng house, so sure of itself a day before, finds its favorite daughter hauled before the law she always believed bent around her. The silver she once spent to ruin a maid now cannot buy her a way out. Pride, it turns out, is the most expensive thing a person can wear.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

余波来得快，因为见证的是天下最尊。太子既亲耳听了郑蔓宜自己这张嘴定自己，就不让这一刻过去。令下，郑蔓宜不是被家法训，而是被送进刑部——辨贵辨贱同一把尺的衙门。她要在那儿、写并当面，供出她做了什么、说了什么。对一个素信家名能免她于难的姑娘，走去刑部那段长，合上的门是石头的，不是特权。前一天还稳如泰山的郑家，发现最疼的闺女被拽到她一向以为绕着她转的王法前。傲，原来是人身最贵的穿戴。

</details>

The wedding is not done with its surprises. The Crown Prince, perhaps to show the court he fears no one, or perhaps because the old art runs in his blood, steps onto the opera stage himself to perform. The hall, still ringing from the scandal, falls quiet again as the heir to the throne takes a singer's role. Then it happens. A hidden weapon, flung from somewhere in the dark of the rafters, strikes him mid song, and the Crown Prince drops where he stands. Screams tear the music apart. Guards flood the stage, the marquis's house erupts into chaos, and the careful trap Lanxiang set for a jealous girl is suddenly swallowed by a much larger attempt on a life. The man meant to be the future of the realm lies on the boards, and no one yet knows if he will rise. Lin Jinqi, still in the crowd, has already started running toward the stage.

婚宴的意外还没完。太子或许要朝廷看他谁也不怕，或许旧艺在血里，竟亲自登台唱戏。堂里还响着方才丑闻的余音，等储君亲自扮了戏子，又静下来。就在这时，一样暗器从梁上暗处飞来，正打在他唱到半途的身上，太子当场倒下。尖叫把丝竹撕碎。护卫涌上台，侯府炸成一片乱，兰香为那妒忌姑娘布的精巧陷阱，一转眼被一场更大的弑命图吞了。那个本该是江山未来的人，躺在台板上，没人知道他起不起得来。

</details>

In the scramble that follows, Lin Jinqi is exactly where he always is when trouble breaks, at the center of it with a sword in his hand. He heard the thud of the weapon and was moving before the scream finished. He clears the stage, pulls the would be assassins into the open, and shields the fallen prince until the marquis's own men take the line. For Lin Jinqi, this is the knot of the whole season tightening at once. The woman he loves is safe beside him. The girl who wronged her is headed for judgment. And the realm's heir has just been struck down at a wedding he helped guard. He does not waste time on feeling. He counts the living, marks the dead, and starts the long work of finding out whose hand threw that dart, because a prince on the floor means the whole country is one bad morning away from war.

乱作一团里，林锦岐正落在他每逢祸起必在的位置——中间，手里一把剑。他听见暗器那声闷响，喊没完已经动了。他清了台，把那几个行刺的逼出暗处，护住倒地的殿下，直到侯爷自己的人接了线。对林锦岐，这是整季的结一下收紧：他爱的女人在身侧安稳；负她的姑娘正去受审；而江山的储君，在他护着的婚宴上刚被人放倒。他没空感触，点活人、记死人，动手查那支镖是谁的手扔的——因为储君躺地上，等于举国离一场坏早晨的仗，只差一步。

</details>

So episode 24 is a day of masks and blades. Lin Jinqi read a fan and knew his maid was alive. Han Liang and Furong Xiu brought her home, and the household scraped together silver to buy back the friend who freed her. Lanxiang turned a wedding opera into a trap, stepped into the light, and watched a proud girl talk herself into the Ministry of Justice, not knowing a prince heard every word. Then the stage became a battlefield, and the Crown Prince fell to a hidden dart. Episode 25 will carry the prince off that floor and show what the rescue costs the people who loved him, and the maid who was almost his ruin. But that is next time's telling. For now, remember this. At a great house's finest hour, the thin door between a secret and a throne is the most dangerous wall in the room.
