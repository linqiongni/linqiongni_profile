Episode 23 opens with Lin Jinqi holding a dangerous secret. He now knows Uncle Liu is the deposed Crown Prince, the lost heir the whole court has been hunting for years. Most men in his place would turn the prince in for a reward, or run the other way. Lin Jinqi does neither. He looks at the worn older man and makes a choice that could cost him his head. He will help the prince disappear. The plan is simple on the surface and bloody underneath. He needs a dead body that roughly matches Uncle Liu's build, something the searchers will accept as proof the prince is gone. Once the court believes the deposed Crown Prince is dead, the net over the city can lift, and the real man can breathe. Lin Jinqi sets his trusted men to find that body, and he starts laying the ground for a funeral that nobody will question.
🇨🇳 中文翻译

第二十三集开头，林锦岐兜着一个要命的秘密。他如今知道刘叔就是废太子，朝廷找了多年的那位走失储君。换作旁人，多半拿他去领赏，或扭头就躲。林锦岐两样都不干。他看着那风霜满面的老人，做出一个可能掉脑袋的选择：帮太子消失。这法子表面简单、底下见血。他得找一具身量差不离的死尸，让搜捕的人信那就是太子，死了。朝廷一旦认定废太子已死，罩全城的网就能撤，真人便能喘气。林锦岐派信得过的人去找那具尸，又动手铺一场没人会疑的丧事。

The substitute corpse is found within a day, a nameless drifter pulled from the river, close enough in size to pass a careless eye. Lin Jinqi's people dress it in the prince's old clothes and stage a death scene by the water, complete with signs of a struggle and a body the ducks have already begun to mark. It is ugly work, but it is the kind of ugly that keeps a man alive. Word is let slip to the right ears that the deposed Crown Prince has been found dead. The court, weary of the hunt, takes the bait and closes the file. Meanwhile, the real Uncle Liu is dressed as a fisherman and walked to a waiting boat under cover of night. He shakes Lin Jinqi's hand once, a soldier's grip, and steps into the dark. The sea will hide him where the throne never could. Lin Jinqi watches the lantern fade and turns back to his own trouble.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

顶包的尸一天内就找着了——河里捞起的无名漂客，身量与刘叔相近，马虎眼过得去。林锦岐的人给他套上太子旧衣，在水边布了处死状，连挣扎的痕迹、鸭群啄过的印子都齐了。活儿脏，可正是这种脏，能保一个人活命。风声有意漏给该听的人：废太子已经找到了，死的。朝廷猎得乏了，一口吞了饵，把这案子销了。与此同时，真刘叔换上渔夫衣裳，趁夜被领上一艘候着的船。他握了握林锦岐的手——兵卒那一种攥法——一步踏进夜色。海会藏他，龙椅从来藏不住。林锦岐看着灯影淡去，转回自己的麻烦。

His own trouble is Lanxiang. With the prince squared away, Lin Jinqi throws himself into the second thread, the one that pulled his maid out of the city. He presses the people he trusts and the ones he does not, and the name that keeps surfacing is Zheng Baichuan. This is Zheng Manyi's family, the same house that smashed the orchid and paid for the staged scandal. It seems the jealousy ran in the blood. Zheng Baichuan's men took Lanxiang off the street and shipped her south, and the trail ends at a city by the water called Yangzhou. Lin Jinqi feels the cold settle in his chest. Yangzhou is far, and the kind of place that swallows a girl without a name. He does not waste a day. He starts packing for a journey south, and he tells the house only half the reason why. He trusts Jiugao to keep the household steady in his absence, because a house left unwatched is a house that turns on itself.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

他的麻烦是兰香。太子安顿好，林锦岐把力气扑到第二道线——那道把丫鬟拽出城的线。他压该压的人、也问不该问的人，浮上来的名字是郑百川。这正是郑蔓宜家的人，摔兰、买局的那一家。看来这妒忌是血脉里带的。郑百川的人当街掳了兰香，往南运，线尾落在一座水边城，叫扬州。林锦岐胸口冷意落定。扬州远，又是那种吞没没名儿姑娘的地方。他一天没耽搁，打点南行，对府里只说了一半缘由。

The half he tells is a story the court will accept. The capital is tense, the sitting Crown Prince is pinned down in Jinling and the roads are thick with suspicion, so Lin Jinqi announces that he is going to Yangzhou to scout a sea route, a way the prince might use to leave the country if things go bad. It is a real task, and an important one, which is what makes it the perfect cover. The truth he keeps to himself is simpler and closer to the bone. He is going to Yangzhou to find Lanxiang. No one in the mansion needs to know that the great Lin Jinqi is riding south for the sake of a maid. He rides out at first light with Jiugao and a few quiet men, and the city gates close behind them like a held breath. The road south is long, and he means to use every mile of it, asking after his maid at each stop along the way.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

他说的那一半，是朝廷能接受的由头。京城紧绷，在位的太子被困金陵，道上满是疑心，于是林锦岐宣称，他去扬州探一条海路——万一事情坏透，太子可借这道走脱。这是个真差事、要紧差事，正因如此，才是最好的幌子。他藏起的真相更简单、更贴骨：他去扬州，是为了找兰香。府里没人需要知道，堂堂林锦岐是为个丫鬟往南骑。他天不亮就出骑，带了九皋和几个闷声的人，城门在身后合上，像屏住的一口气。

Far to the south, in Yangzhou, Lanxiang has already learned what fear tastes like in a new city. Zheng Baichuan's men sold her the moment they arrived, and the buyer was the Qingyan House, a painted pleasure house where girls are traded like porcelain. Most new girls break or vanish within a season. Lanxiang does not. She discovers quickly that the house values one thing above looks, and that is a skill that draws a paying crowd. She can paint. She learned it in the Lin mansion, copying scrolls to pass the quiet hours, and now she puts that skill to use. She paints fans and screens for the guests, and the house keeps her because a girl who brings in silver with a brush is worth more alive than ruined. For the moment, the brush is her shield, and she hides behind it, watching and waiting. She does not smile much, but she is alive, and alive is the only win that counts in a house like this.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

远在南方扬州，兰香已经尝到了在陌生城里害怕的滋味。郑百川的人一到就把她卖了，买主是轻烟楼——一座把姑娘当瓷器般买卖的脂粉行院。多数新来的姑娘，一季内要么碎要么没。兰香没有。她很快摸清，这楼里最值钱的不是脸，是能拉来花钱客的本事。她会画。在林府时她临帖打发静时，学的就是这手，如今派上用场。她给客人画扇面、画屏风，楼里留着她，因为一个拿笔能挣银子的姑娘，活着比毁了值钱。眼下，这笔就是她的盾，她缩在后面，看着、等着。

Then comes a face she did not expect. Among the women of the Qingyan House is Lü Jí'ér, a girl who once served in the same Lin courtyard as Lanxiang. They were never close, and Lü Jí'ér was the jealous, sharp-tongued type who made life harder for everyone. But hardship has a way of softening old edges. When Lü Jí'ér hears how Lanxiang ended up sold here, something in her shifts. She remembers a time Lanxiang covered for her, or spoke up when no one else would, a small debt of kindness from the old house. In the Qingyan House, with no family and no law to lean on, that old debt suddenly matters. Lü Jí'ér comes to Lanxiang one night and says, in a low voice, that she will help her get out. She knows the back ways and the guards' habits, and she is willing to risk her own neck.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

接着出现一张她没想到的脸。轻烟楼的姑娘里，有个吕蕺儿，早先在林府同一处院子当差，和兰香同过事。俩人向来不亲，吕蕺儿是那种尖酸爱妒、专给人添堵的性子。可苦日子有办法把旧棱角磨软。吕蕺儿听兰香怎会落到卖到这里，心里某处动了。她记起旧府里，兰香替她遮过一次、或旁人都不开口时她开了口——那是老府里一笔小人情。在轻烟楼，举目无亲、无王法可恃，那笔旧人情忽然重了。吕蕺儿一夜走到兰香跟前，低声说，她帮她出去。她认得后头的暗道、也摸得守卫的脾性，肯拿自己的脖子去冒险。

The escape is planned in whispers over two nights. Lü Jí'ér maps the route: a kitchen door that is left unlocked for the late deliveries, a wall low enough to climb if you go at the hour the watch changes, a boatman who asks no questions if the silver is right. Lanxiang gathers what little she has, which is mostly the clothes on her back and a scrap of charcoal she kept for sketching. On the third night they move. Lü Jí'ér distracts the guard with a spilled tray and a loud complaint, and Lanxiang slips through the door and over the wall. She hits the ground on the other side with her heart in her throat, free for the first time since Yangzhou. She mouths a thank you she is not sure Lü Jí'ér can hear, and she runs, because running is the only prayer a girl in her place is allowed.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

出逃用两夜低语定下。吕蕺儿画出路线：晚送菜那道厨房门不落锁；换岗那刻那面墙矮得翻得过去；船夫只要银子对，不问来路。兰香收拢身上少得可怜的东西——多半是身上的衣，和一块留着打草稿的炭。第三夜动手。吕蕺儿打翻托盘、扯着嗓子抱怨，引开守卫；兰香从门里溜出，翻过墙。她落地那一面，心提到嗓子眼，自到扬州头回得了自由。她道了声未必吕蕺儿听见的谢，拔腿就跑，因为跑，是她这种姑娘被允的唯一的祷。

Freedom, again, is a thin thing. Lanxiang has not gone two streets before she hears the shouts behind her. The Qingyan House noticed its painter is missing faster than anyone hoped, and the men sent after her are paid to be quick. They know the city better than she does, and they are closing. She ducks into a market, then a temple yard, then an alley that turns out to be a dead end, and every time she thinks she has lost them, a voice or a lantern finds her again. This is the part of the story where a lesser girl would be caught. Lanxiang is not lesser. She keeps moving on instinct, using the small tricks of a person who has been hunted before, and she stays one turn ahead of the men who want to drag her back to that painted house and lock the door. A kind stranger slides her a bun, a doorway hides her for a breath, and these small mercies are the only company she has on the run.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

自由，又一次，薄得很。兰香没跑出两条街，身后就起了喊声。轻烟楼发现它的画师不见，比谁盼的都快，派来追的人，拿银子买的就是快。他们比她熟这座城，咬得紧。她钻进市集，又钻庙院，又钻进一条竟是死巷的胡同，每次以为甩掉，总有一声喊或一盏灯又把她揪出来。这要是换了个孬姑娘，早被拿住了。兰香不是孬的。她凭本能一路挪，用被猎过的人才有的小伎俩，始终比要拖她回那脂粉楼、落锁的人快半个弯。

At the very moment Lanxiang is fleeing those shouts, Lin Jinqi is walking through the front gate of the Qingyan House. He arrived in Yangzhou hard on the heels of the trail, asked the right questions, and followed the name to this door. He does not know she just climbed the back wall. He walks in like a man who owns the street, asks the madam a few flat questions, and learns that yes, a painting girl was here, and no, she is not here now. The madam lies by half, saying the girl was sold on, but Lin Jinqi reads the flicker in her eyes and knows the truth is fresher than that. He is standing in the very house where Lanxiang was held, a breath too late, while outside the woman he came for is running for her life through the same city's dark. The two of them are in the same place at the same time and miss by the width of a wall.

就在兰香甩那些喊声的同一刻，林锦岐正迈过轻烟楼的前门。他紧咬着线到扬州，问对了人，顺着名字摸到这扇门。他不知她刚翻了后墙。他像街是他家开的那样走进去，平问了老鸨几句，得知：是，有个画画的姑娘在过，不，现在不在了。老鸨半真半假，说姑娘早转卖了，可林锦岐读得出她眼里的那点闪，知道真相比那要新。他就站在兰香被关的那座楼里，慢了半口气，而外头他要找的那个女人，正跑着命穿过同一座城的夜。俩人在同一处、同一时，隔一道墙，错过了。

So episode 23 is a story of two near misses and one quiet rescue. Lin Jinqi faked a death to free a prince and rode south under the cover of duty, while Lanxiang turned a brush into a shield and found an old rival willing to risk everything for a kindness remembered. She climbed a wall and ran, and he walked through the door she had just left. The pursuers are close, the city is strange, and the man who loves her in his stiff way is nearer than she knows but not near enough. Episode 24 will close that gap, and show what happens when Lin Jinqi's search and Lanxiang's flight finally cross. But that is next time's telling. For now, remember this. In a city of strangers, the smallest debts of kindness are the only rope you can hold, and the people who save you are often the ones you least expected.
