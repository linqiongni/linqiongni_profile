# 兰香如故 — The Fragrance of Orchids Remains
## Episode 22 · Told Like A Story

Episode 22 opens with a visitor at the Lin mansion. Zheng Manyi comes to call, and she is not there for a small talk. Senior Lady Lin, who has been watching this girl for some time, decides to give her a hand. She makes sure Lin Jinqi is somewhere nearby, and then she finds an excuse to walk away, leaving the two young people alone in the same room. It is the kind of setup a mother makes when she wants something to grow between two people. Lin Jinqi is polite but quiet. Zheng Manyi is bright and eager, chatting about this and that, but he keeps his distance. Still, the old lady is not blind. She sees the way Zheng Manyi looks at her son, and she likes what she sees. What she does not notice is another thing sitting in that room, a small potted orchid on the windowsill that Lin Jinqi once gave to Lanxiang, his maid. That little plant means more to him than anyone guesses, and before this day ends, it will become the spark of something ugly.

第二十二集开头，林府来了位客人。郑蔓宜上门，可不是来闲聊的。林母盯着这姑娘有些日子了，存了心思要推一把。她先把林锦岐支到附近，自己找了个由头走开，把两个年轻人单独留在屋里。当娘的做这种安排，心里打的什么算盘，明眼人都懂。林锦岐客气是客气，话不多。郑蔓宜又鲜活又热络，东拉西扯地聊，可他始终隔着一层。老夫人不瞎，她看得出郑蔓宜看儿子的眼神，也中意。她没留意的是屋里另一样东西——窗台上那盆兰花草，是林锦岐送丫鬟兰香的。那小草在他心里的分量，比谁想的都重，而这一天还没完，它就要变成一场龌龊事的引子。

</details>

Zheng Manyi has sharp eyes, and she notices the orchid right away. She sees how Lin Jinqi glances at it, how he told the servant to keep it in good light, how a man who is cold to most things softens near that one small pot. She asks around without seeming to, and she learns the truth. The orchid was a gift from Lanxiang, the maid who can read and write, the one Lin Jinqi trusts more than anyone in the house. Something cold turns in Zheng Manyi's chest. She is a proper young lady from a good family, and the idea that a lowly maid could hold a piece of Lin Jinqi's heart makes her angry. She decides she will not let that stand. In her mind, the orchid is not just a plant. It is a mark Lanxiang left on him, and marks like that have to be erased. So she begins to plan, quietly, the way a person plans when they smile to your face and sharpen a knife behind their back.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

郑蔓宜眼尖，一下就盯上了那盆兰。她看出林锦岐总拿眼去瞟它，还特意嘱咐下人把它摆在有光的地方；一个对多数事都冷着的人，偏在那一小盆前软下来。她不动声色地打听，问出了底：这兰是兰香送的，那个识文断字的丫鬟，林锦岐最信不过的人。郑蔓宜胸口一阵发凉。她是正经好人家的小姐，想到一个低贱丫鬟竟在林锦岐心里占着位置，就咽不下这口气。她打定主意，绝不让这事儿就这么留着。在她眼里，那兰不是草，是兰香留在他身上的印子，这种印子得抹掉。于是她悄悄盘算起，就像那种对着你笑、背后磨刀的人。

</details>

Her plan takes her out of the mansion and into the city. She hears that the orchid, or one just like it, has passed through the Rongbao Pavilion, the family's shop that deals in paintings and old things. Zheng Manyi goes there in person and asks for it by description. The shopkeeper, seeing a paying customer, sells her the orchid for one hundred taels, a sum that would feed a poor family for a year. She pays without blinking. Back at home, behind closed doors, she lifts the pot and dashes it against the floor. The clay breaks, the leaves scatter, the soil spills across the tiles. She stands there looking at the ruin and feels a small, cold satisfaction. To her, she has cut a thread between Lin Jinqi and the maid. What she does not know is that breaking a plant does not break the bond. It only makes the person who broke it look cruel, and cruelty has a way of coming back around.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

她的盘算把她带出了府，进了城。她听说那盆兰，或是一盆一样的，曾经过荣宝阁——林家那间卖字画老物的铺子。郑蔓宜亲自去，照着样子要那盆。掌柜见是肯掏钱的客，便以一百两卖给了她——那可是穷人家一年的嚼谷。她眼皮都不眨就付了。回到家，关上门，她端起花盆往地上一摔。陶碎了，叶散了，土洒了一地砖。她站在那堆残骸前，心里浮起一丝又冷又小的痛快。她觉得自己剪断了林锦岐和那丫鬟之间的一根线。她不知道的是，摔了草断不了情，只会显出摔草的人有多狠；而狠，迟早绕回来。

</details>

But smashing a flower is not enough for her. Zheng Manyi wants Lanxiang herself out of the picture, and she is willing to pay for it. She finds a traveling troupe of actors who perform at banquets and fairs, and she slips the troupe master a fat purse of silver. Her idea is dirty and simple. She will have Lanxiang drugged, then place her in a compromising position with Han Liang, a decent young man who has been kind to the maid. If word spreads that the two were found together in a private, shameful state, Lanxiang's name is ruined, and so is any chance she has near Lin Jinqi. The troupe master takes the money and nods. He mixes a sleeping drug into Lanxiang's drink one evening when she is sent on an errand near their camp. The maid feels her limbs go heavy, her eyes blur, and then the world goes dark. She never sees the hands that catch her.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

可摔一朵花，她还嫌不够。郑蔓宜要的是兰香这个人从局里消失，而且她舍得花钱。她找着个赶场子唱堂会的戏班，往班主手里塞了鼓囊囊一包银子。她的念头又脏又简单：把兰香迷晕，再把她和韩梁搁在一处，做出不堪的样子。韩梁是个厚道后生，待兰香向来好。要是风声传开，说这俩私底下被人撞见那等光景，兰香的名声就臭了，靠林锦岐边上翻身的想头也一并没了。班主接过钱，点了头。有一晚兰香被支去戏班附近办差，班主把迷药下进她喝的水里。丫鬟只觉手脚发沉、眼前发糊，接着天就黑了。抓她的人，她一个没看清。

</details>

When Lanxiang wakes, she is in a dim room with Han Liang, both of them groggy and arranged to look guilty. She understands in a flash what is being done to her. Panic would be the normal response, but Lanxiang has survived too much to fall apart now. She thinks fast. The room is old and a little haunted looking, with white cloths hanging and a draft that moans through the cracks. She grabs one of those cloths, wraps it around herself, and smears ash on her face. Then she lets out a long, low wail, the kind of sound old houses are said to make when a wronged spirit walks. The people sent to discover the scandal burst in, and instead of a cheap love story, they meet what looks like a shrieking ghost. They scream and bolt. In the chaos Lanxiang slips out the back, her heart pounding, but free. She may be a maid, but she is quicker witted than the ladies who plot against her.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

兰香醒来，是在一间昏暗屋里，挨着也昏沉沉的韩梁，两人被摆成了难堪的模样。她一瞬间就明白了这是要害她。换别人早慌了，可兰香熬过的太多，这会儿垮不了。她脑子转得快。那屋子旧，透着股像闹鬼的味，白布挂着，缝里风呜呜地灌。她扯过一块白布裹身上，又抓灰抹了脸，拖长声发出低低的嚎——老房子冤魂游走的那种动静。本来奉命来"撞破"丑事的人冲进来，没见着脏事，倒撞见个嘶嚎的鬼。他们嗷一嗓子，撒腿就跑。乱中兰香从后头溜了，心怦怦跳，人是自由的。她虽是个丫鬟，脑子比算计她的那些小姐快得多。

</details>

Freedom does not last long. Lanxiang has barely caught her breath when a second danger finds her. Men loyal to the Duke, the powerful lord who has been pulling strings from the shadows, grab her off the street. This is not Zheng Manyi's work. It is bigger and colder. The Duke wants something from Lin Jinqi, and a hold on his trusted maid is a fine lever. They bind her and carry her away before she can cry out. So within a single episode, Lanxiang goes from a setup meant to shame her, to a ghost trick that saved her, to a kidnapping that may break her. The streets she knows so well turn strange and dangerous. She is alone, far from the Lin mansion, with no idea that Lin Jinqi has already noticed she is gone, and that the man who loves her in his own stiff way has started to move.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

自由没撑多久。兰香刚喘口气，第二重祸就找上门。国公的手下——那位一直躲在暗处扯线的权贵——在大街上把她掳了走。这可不是郑蔓宜的手笔，更大、更冷。国公想从林锦岐身上捞点什么，捏住他信得过的丫鬟，正是一根好杠杆。他们捆了她，没等她喊出声就抬走了。于是短短一集里，兰香从一场要把她搞臭的局，到一出让她脱身的装鬼，再到一场可能毁了她的绑架。她熟得不能再熟的街巷，一下变得又生又险。她孤身在外，离林府老远，哪知道林锦岐已经发现她不见了，而那个用自己闷葫芦的方式爱着她的男人，已经动了起来。

</details>

Back at the mansion, Lin Jinqi realizes Lanxiang is missing. He does not shout or gather a crowd. He goes quiet, the way he does before a storm, and he starts to trace her last steps. The trail leads him to the troupe of actors, the same one that took Zheng Manyi's silver. A few plain questions and a little pressure are enough. The troupe master, scared now, confesses the bribe and the drug and the staged scene. Lin Jinqi listens with a face that gives nothing away, but inside he is cold with fury. He knows who paid for this. He also knows the troupe master is small game. The real hand is Zheng Manyi, and behind her, maybe others. He files that anger away for later. Right now his only thought is Lanxiang. She was taken from this troupe once, and then taken again by someone else. He has to find her before the someone else decides what she is worth.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

回到府里，林锦岐察觉兰香不见了。他没嚷没闹，也没兴师动众，只是像风暴前那样，先静下来，然后顺着她最后的脚印去摸。线头把他引到那班唱戏的——就是收了郑蔓宜银子的那拨。几句平话、一点威压，够了。班主这会儿怕了，招了银子、迷药、那场做局。林锦岐听着，脸上什么不露，心里却冷得冒火。他清楚是谁掏的钱。他也清楚班主只是小角色。真正的手是郑蔓宜，而她背后，或许还有别人。这火他先压着，留到日后。眼下他只想着兰香。她从这班手里被带走一次，又被另一拨人带走一次。他得赶在另一拨人替她估出价之前，把她找到。

</details>

Following the second trail, Lin Jinqi ends up at a rough inn on the city's edge, and there he meets a man who calls himself Uncle Liu. Uncle Liu is older, worn, and careful with his words, but there is a bearing about him that does not fit a common traveler. Lin Jinqi is good at reading people, and he reads this one as a man who has lived above his station and lost it. They talk, and slowly the truth comes out. Uncle Liu is no ordinary wanderer. He is the deposed Crown Prince, the former heir the court has been hunting for years. The Duke's men are not only after Lanxiang. They are part of a wider net the court has thrown to catch the lost prince. Lin Jinqi hears this and his mind clicks into place. The kidnapping of his maid and the hunt for a prince are threads of the same rope, and that rope is tightening around the whole city.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

顺着第二道线，林锦岐摸到城边一家破旅馆，在那儿遇见个自称刘叔的男人。刘叔年纪大些，风霜满面，话也收着，可那股气度，不像个寻常脚夫。林锦岐最会看人，一眼便看出这是个曾居高位于、如今失了势的人。两人聊开，真相慢慢浮出。刘叔不是普通漂泊客，他是废太子——朝廷找了多年的那位前储君。国公的人不光是冲兰香来的，他们也是朝堂撒下的大网里的一缕，要捞那位走失的殿下。林锦岐听罢，脑子里咔哒一声对上了：丫鬟被绑、太子被猎，是同一根绳上的两股，而那根绳，正要把整座城勒紧。

</details>

Uncle Liu, the deposed Crown Prince, speaks plainly once he sees Lin Jinqi can be trusted. He tells him the court's soldiers are sweeping the city, asking after a man of his description, offering rewards for any tip. He is hiding in plain sight, moving from inn to inn, because the Duke's men and the court's men are two different masters with the same target. Lin Jinqi listens and weighs his options. He is a soldier and a loyal subject, but he is also a man who hates to see the innocent pulled into games of thrones. Lanxiang was taken because of this prince's shadow, and that makes the prince's safety his business too. He tells Uncle Liu to stay put and stay quiet, and he promises to come back with a plan. For the first time, Lin Jinqi sees the shape of the season's real danger. It is not a jealous girl with a flower pot. It is the throne, and everyone near it is in its path.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

刘叔——那位废太子——见林锦岐靠得住，也就直说了。他讲，朝廷兵丁正在城里大搜，拿着他的模样问人，通风报信的有赏。他借着寻常人的皮藏身，从这家客栈挪到那家，因为国公的人、朝廷的人，是两拨主子、同一个目标。林锦岐听着，掂量手里的牌。他是武将、是忠臣，可也是个见不得无辜人被权斗拖下水的人。兰香因这位殿下的影子被掳，那殿下的安危，也就成了他的事。他让刘叔待着别动、嘴紧，答应回头带法子来。林锦岐头回看清这一季真正的险长什么样：不是个拿花盆的妒忌姑娘，是那把龙椅，而挨着它的人，全在它的道上。

</details>

So episode 22 turns on a single soft plant and a single sharp girl. Zheng Manyi came to win a man and left having smashed an orchid and paid for a maid's ruin, not knowing the maid would slip her net like a ghost. Lin Jinqi came home to an empty space where his trusted girl should be, and he followed the thread to a troupe, a bribe, and a prince in hiding. The orchid is broken on a floor, but the bond it stood for is not. The scandal was staged, but the danger around Lanxiang is real, and it wears the Duke's livery. Episode 23 will pick up where the hunt leaves off, and show what Lin Jinqi does with a deposed prince in his care and a kidnapped maid on his conscience. But that is next time's telling. For now, remember this. In the Lin estate, the small things, a flower, a glance, a bribe, are never small. They are the first moves of a much larger game.

于是第二十二集，落在一盆软草、一个利落姑娘身上。郑蔓宜来赢男人，临走摔了兰、买下丫鬟的身败名裂，却不知那丫鬟像鬼一样从她网里滑走了。林锦岐回家，见本该在的贴心丫头空了位，顺着线摸到戏班、银子、一个藏身的太子。兰碎在地上，可它代表的情没碎。丑事是做局的，可罩着兰香的真险还在，而且披着国公的皮。第二十三集接着猎踪往下讲，看林锦岐拿一个藏身的废太子、一个良心上悬着的被掳丫鬟，怎么摆布。但那是下回的讲法。此刻你记一句：在林府，那些小东西——一朵花、一瞥、一笔银子——从来都不小，它们是一盘更大棋的第一步。
