# 兰香如故 — The Fragrance of Orchids Remains
## Episode 30 · Told Like A Story

Let me tell you about the thirtieth episode, because this is the one where the boat that stole the bride north sets her down in the city she feared, and where the girl who once read a household's lies turns her wit to a friend's bond and a game of balls upon a lawn. The capital receives them without ceremony: Lin Jinqi, the retired-leaning Jing'an Marquis, takes the family to the Shen family's old residence — the house in the city that once belonged to Lanxiang's buried line, now empty and waiting, a roof with a history the bride does not name aloud. The season lingers on the threshold of that house, because to walk into the Shen home is to walk into the ghost of the family the capital erased, and Lanxiang, who would not come for a throne, comes now for a companion she owes. The city that turned her blood away now holds the woman she means to free, and the episode, having carried her across the water in her sleep, sets her on its stones awake and angry.

The first thing Lanxiang does in the capital is ask for Lü Jier, and the answer comes quick and cold: the former maid is in the hands of Zhao Xingtang, Lin Jinqi's former wife, who holds the girl's contract and means to keep it. The season marks the bitter symmetry — the woman once married to the marquis now holds the bond of the woman married to him, and the two wives of one man meet, at last, over a third woman's freedom. Lanxiang does not storm the door; she dresses for the part of a marquis's lady and calls upon Zhao Xingtang among the capital's ladies, and there, in a room of silk and perfume, she argues her cause with a calm that disarms the circle. She meets Zhao's taunts with soft replies and her claims with clear ones, and the gathered wives, expecting a country maid, find instead a woman who reads a room as easily as she once read a fever. The bride who would not bow at her own wedding bowed, by wit alone, to no one.

兰香在京城头一件事，是问吕蕺儿；答来得快而冷：那旧婢在赵星棠——林锦岐的元配——手里，赵握着那姑娘的契、意欲留着。这季记那苦的对称：那个曾嫁这侯的妇，如今握着嫁了他的妇所握的那个女的自由的契；而一男的两妻，终在一第三女的自由上相遇。兰香不撞门；她扮作侯夫人的样，去京中妇人间见赵星棠；在那里，丝与香的一室里，她以一种解了满圈的甲的静，论她的由。她以软答接赵的讥、以清 claim 接赵的索；而聚着的众妻，本预期一个村丫鬟，却见一个读屋如读热一般的妇。那个不肯在自己婚上拜的新妇，凭机，没向谁弯。

Zhao Xingtang names her price: thirty thousand taels of silver for Lü Jier's contract and Azhi's beside it, a sum meant to be impossible and thus to keep the girls. But Lanxiang, who learned the ledgers of the Rongbao Pavilion and the law that saved a widow, answers with a merchant's mind rather than a beggar's plea. She shows Zhao how the ransom, if taken clumsily, would drag the former wife into a dispute with her own family and the court — how a tidy sum paid through the right hands would spare her a scandal the capital loves to chew. The season watches a girl who was property turn the tables on a woman who still thinks her property: Lanxiang's counsel is sounder than Zhao's greed, and the former wife, cornered by her own arithmetic, finds the demand beginning to chafe the hand that made it. What starts as a ransom becomes, under Lanxiang's hand, a negotiation — and a negotiation is a thing a clever woman can win.

The blow that wins the contracts is struck not with words but with a club, upon a lawn where the capital's ladies pass the idle hour at a gentle game of chuiwan, a ball struck through hoops with a slender stick. Zhao Xingtang, certain of her rank over this upstart bride, challenges her to the game for sport — and Lanxiang, who learned a steady eye in harder schools, answers with a single flawless stroke that sends the ball true through the hoop at the first attempt. The gathered ladies, who came to watch a maid be humbled, instead watch a marquis's wife bend the game to her will, and Zhao Xingtang, stung by the public grace of it, honors the wager she did not mean to lose. The contracts change hands across the grass, and the season marks the strange victory: the girl who was once a ball knocked about the great house now drives one home by her own aim, and the friend she sailed north to free is, at last, free.

赢下契的那一击，不是以言、是以杆，在一片草坪上——京中妇人在那以一根细杆把球击过环的闲戏（chuiwan）里，过她们的懒时。赵星棠，恃她阶压这新来的 bride，以这戏约为赌——而兰香，在更硬的学堂里练出的稳眼，以一记无瑕的击应了，第一试便把球真穿过环。聚着的妇，本来看一个丫鬟被辱，却看一个侯夫人把戏弯到她意下；赵星棠，被那当众的雅刺到，认了她本不想输的赌。契在草上易了手；这季记那奇的胜：那个曾如球般被在大宅里敲的姑娘，如今凭自己的准，把一只击回家；而她航行北来要放的友，终于，自由了。

Freed, Lü Jier declares the life she means to live: she will go to Quanzhou and register herself as a woman-headed household, a household with no man's name above the door, and make her own way by the sea she has learned to trust. It is a road no bondservant was meant to walk, and Lanxiang, who understands the cost of a name, outfits her for it. She pawns her own jewelry and has the silver beaten into thin gold leaves, which she sews into the lining of Lü Jier's clothes so the girl may carry a fortune hidden and safe to the south. The season notes the quiet echo: the bride who was given away twice now gives, freely, the means for another woman to belong to no one. And Lin Jinqi, watching, retrieves the pawned jewelry in secret and restores it to his wife, so that the gift she made from her own hand comes back to her hand, unknown to her — a husband's way of letting his wife's mercy stand while mending what it cost.

With Lü Jier bound for the sea, Lanxiang turns her mind to a different bond — the one she owes the man who carried her north. She takes up the pen under a name that is not hers: Shen Rugù, a borrowed sign she writes upon the scripts she sells to a troupe of players in the capital. The season, which has watched her read ledgers and laws, watches her now read an audience, for the play she writes — a tale the troupe calls "Yu Xian Ji" — is built from the same keen sense of what a room will feel that carried her through the poison years. She does not write as the marquis's wife or the Shen heir; she writes as a hidden hand, and the pen name keeps her safe the way the drug kept her quiet, by hiding the woman behind the work. The capital's stages, hungry for a new story, take the script without knowing whose hand shaped it, and the girl who once bent generals now bends a crowd with a printed page.

吕蕺儿向海去，兰香把心转向另一道契——她欠那把她带北的男的那道。她提起笔，用一个不是她的名：沈如故，一个借的 sign，写在她卖给京中一戏班的本子上。这季，曾看她读账与律，如今看她读一个看客；因她写的戏——戏班唤作《羽仙记》——是用那同一种"一间屋会感什么"的敏造的，正是那敏载她过了投毒的年。她不写作出侯夫人、也不写作出沈家继承人；她以一只隐的手写，而那笔名护她，如那药曾护她静，把妇人藏到活后。京的台，饿着新故事，不知谁的手塑了它，便接了这脚本；而那个曾弯过将军的姑娘，如今以一页印的，弯一众。

The play does not merely please; it takes the city. "Yu Xian Ji" runs from troupe to troupe, and the name Shen Rugù, unknown to the faces in the pit, becomes a word on every patron's lips, the author of the season's talk. In three months the scripts and the share of the take bring Lanxiang five hundred taels of silver — exactly the sum, the season does not fail to note, that she means to lay at Lin Jinqi's feet. She repays the marquis who drugged her and carried her north by returning, in clean coin, the value of the road he forced upon her, and the repayment is its own quiet verdict: the bride who would not be taken to the capital has, by her own wit, earned the right to stand in it. The girl who arrived angry on a boat leaves this episode holding a playhouse's purse and a husband's debt discharged, and the city that feared her name never knew the name that wrote its favorite tale.

So the episode turns on the strange freedom of a hidden hand. In the capital that erased her family, Lanxiang moves as Xu Lanxiang the bride, as Shen Rugù the playwright, and as the unseen friend of a woman who will sail free — three names, none of them the one the court would kill her for, and each a door she walks through by her own cleverness. She freed Lü Jier not by plea but by a merchant's sense and a true stroke at a game; she repaid Lin Jinqi not by submission but by a pen that earned what he spent. The season, which carried her here in her sleep, now watches her own the city awake: the girl who refused the capital's wall has built, inside it, a life no edict commanded and no master can claim, and the old residence of the Shen line holds, tonight, a woman who belongs to no one but herself.

So we close the thirtieth episode the way a play closes an act — with the contracts signed, the ball struck home, and the author hidden behind a name the crowd cheers. Lanxiang came to the capital unwilling and woke upon its river; she found Lü Jier in Zhao Xingtang's hand and won her with a merchant's sense and a single shot; she sent the freed girl to Quanzhou with gold sewn in her clothes and repaid her husband with five hundred taels earned by the pen of Shen Rugù. Episode thirty-one will carry the reckoning one step further, where Lin Jinqi comes to the rescue of the very woman he helped free, and the marquis who drugged his bride learns what his bride, unbidden, will do for the friend he delivered. For now, remember only this: in the capital a girl the court would erase writes its favorite tale under a borrowed name, and the friend she owed she frees with a steady hand at a game — proving that the bonds a city keeps are no match for the wit of a woman who has read every lock it owns.
