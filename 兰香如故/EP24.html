Let me tell you about episode twenty-four, because this is the episode where all the season's threads — the maid and the master, the ducal bride and the hidden princes — are finally pulled into one room, and the room happens, with a cruel sort of neatness, to be a wedding. It opens with Lin Jinqi searching the Qingyan House, the courtesan den in Yangzhou where Lanxiang was last seen. He moves through the painted halls with the cold, methodical patience of a man who governs by knowing, questioning the keepers, reading the spaces the girl would have read, and refusing to leave until he has the shape of what happened to her. The Qingyan House, long used to hiding its prizes behind lacquer and laughter, finds itself searched by a master who has spent a whole season learning exactly how clever girls disappear, and who will not be lied to about this one, not when he has crossed a province to stand in its doorway.

让我跟你讲讲第二十四集，因为这一集，是这季所有的线——丫鬟与主子、国公之女与藏起的太子——终被收进一个屋，而那屋恰是一场婚，带着一种残酷的齐整。它始于林锦岐搜轻烟楼，那兰香最后现身的扬州烟花处。他带着一个以知而治的男人的冷而细的耐，穿行那些敷了色的厅，问守者、读那姑娘会读的空隙，不弄清她身上发生了什么，不肯离。惯于把宝藏在漆与笑后头的轻烟楼，发现搜它的是个熬过一整季、恰恰学了聪明姑娘怎样消失、且不会在此事上被诓的主子——尤其当他跨了一省、立在它门口。

And then, in the narrow passage of that house, the season delivers its small, hard-won mercy: Lin Jinqi and the fleeing Lanxiang pass each other in the dark, two people moving the same hallway in opposite directions, neither quite catching the other's face in the dim. He does not stop her — she is a shadow slipping toward the door he has just entered, and he is a man looking the wrong way — but as she passes, his eye falls on the fan in her hand, and on the painting marked upon its folded surface. He knows her hand; he knows the stroke the way he knows the hand that signed his accounts. By that small mark on a folded fan he confirms what he crossed a province to learn: that she is alive and whole, that the cage she was sold into has been sprung, and that the girl he came for is, at this very moment, walking free. The confirmation is wordless, a thing passed between them in the dark, and it is enough to steady him.

接着，在楼里窄道中，这季递来它小的、得来不易的仁：林锦岐与逃着的兰香，在暗里擦身而过，两人同一条廊、反向走，谁也没在昏里真看清谁的脸。他没拦她——她是个溜向他刚进那门的影，而他是个看错了向的人——但擦过时节，他眼落在她手里的扇、与扇上折面所画的画上。他认她的手；认那笔，如认签他账的那只手。凭折扇上那一点痕，他证出了他跨一省来知的：她活且全，那把她卖进去的笼已被弹开，而他为她来的姑娘，此刻正走着自由。这证无声，是暗里递过的一物，而它，足以稳住他。

Lanxiang, once clear of the house, is found by Han Liang and by Furong Xiu — the woman of the opera troupe, who proves to be a good deal friendlier than the troupe that drugged her and staged the bed. The two of them bring her safely home, and there, among the people who have been quietly worrying the thread of her disappearance, a plan takes shape that aims not at revenge but at rescue. The group pools what silver it can scrape together to redeem Lü Jier, the former Lin maid still held in the Qingyan House, because a girl who helped another walk out means the other now walks back in for her. It is a quiet, practical solidarity, the kind this season has taught its survivors — not grand, but real, the gathering of coins and courage to buy one more life out of the painted cage, and the first true sign that Lanxiang, now free, means to free whom she can, even at cost to herself.

兰香一脱了楼，被韩梁与芙蓉秀寻着——那戏班的女子，比曾下药、摆了床的那班，显得可亲得多。两人把她平安送回家。在那儿，在一直揪着她那根失踪线的人中间，成形的不是复仇、是救的计划。众人凑出能刮出的银，去赎吕蕺儿，那仍困在轻烟楼的前林家丫鬟；因一个助别个走出的姑娘，意即别个今要为她走回去。这是一种静而务实的同心，这季教给幸存者的那一种——不宏大，却真，是聚币与勇，去买又一个命出那画笼；也是头一个真征兆：自由的兰香，意欲放她能放的人，即便自损。

With herself safe at last, Lanxiang turns her quick mind to the ducal bride who engineered the trap, and she chooses the stage the great house least expects: the Yongchang Marquis's wedding, where opera will be performed and the whole city's standing will gather to watch the match. She plans to use the marriage celebration as the setting for a trap of her own — not a poison in a cup this time, but a scandal turned and aimed, a way to draw Zheng Manyi's hand into the light where the right eyes can see it plainly. The season, which handed this girl barrel-poison and ghost-faces by turns, now hands her a wedding stage, and she receives it the way she has received every weapon put in her path: by calculating the angles in the dark, and by trusting the hard lesson she has earned — that a great house's pride is the one thing more fragile than its walls, and the one thing most easily cracked.

自身终安，兰香把机敏的心思转到那布了套的国公之女身上，挑了大宅最不料的一处台：永昌侯的婚，那里要唱戏，全城的门第会聚来看这门亲。她打算借这合卺之庆，作她自己一套的场——这一回不是杯里毒，而是翻过来、瞄准的丑闻，一种把郑蔓宜的手，引到该看的眼能明白见的明处之法。这季，既轮番递过这姑娘桶中毒与鬼脸，今递她一场婚台；而她接它，如接路上每件兵器：在暗里算准角度，且信她挣来的硬课——大宅的骄，是比它的墙更脆、也最易裂的那一物。

On the wedding day the marquis's residence is draped and bright, but beneath the lanterns a murderous intent is hidden — the same powers that wanted Lanxiang silenced have not stopped wanting it, and the celebration is rigged with a danger the happy guests cannot see. Into this Lanxiang walks and makes herself known, stepping into the open where Zheng Manyi can see her, alive and unruined, the maid the ducal bride believed she had broken beyond repair. She provokes the bride with a calm that is itself a weapon, drawing her out with a few measured words, and Zheng Manyi — certain of her own standing and undone by the sight of the girl she could not keep down — loses her composure in exactly the way the plan was built to invite, the way a trapped animal is drawn by the stillness of the hunter into the open where it can be seen.

婚日，侯府张灯结彩，可灯下藏着杀机——那要兰香噤声的力，没停过要它；这庆被缀了喜客看不见的险。兰香走进去，自报身份，走到郑蔓宜能看见的明处：活着的、没被毁的，那个国公之女以为已被她折得救不回的丫鬟。她以一份本身就是兵器的静，去激那新娘，以几句拿捏的话，把人引出来；而郑蔓宜——自负门第、又被这压不下的姑娘之景击溃——失了态，失得恰是这计要引的态，如阱中兽被猎者的静引出，到那能被看见的明处。

The trap springs exactly as Lanxiang meant it to, with a clean, merciless precision. Zheng Manyi's wild, unguarded words — her boasts and her threats, her open admission of what she did and meant to do to the maid — are spoken within a room divided only by a thin door, and on the other side of that door stands the Crown Prince, listening without a sound. He hears it all: the scheme against the maid, the contempt for the household, the ambition that reached higher than any bride should dare. The words, meant for a private ear, become a public confession, and the prince, who holds the ear of the throne itself, renders judgment on the spot: Zheng Manyi is sentenced to go to the Ministry of Justice and confess her crimes in full. The ducal daughter's pride, which began this episode smashing an orchid, ends it marched toward a cell, and the great house behind her finds its chosen bride turned to ash in a single careless hour.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

套弹得恰如兰香所愿，干净而无情。郑蔓宜那狂而无防的话——她的夸、她的胁、她对自己对丫鬟所行与所图之公然认——说在一间只隔一道薄门的屋里；门那侧，立着听的太子。他听尽：对丫鬟的算、对这府的轻、那越过了任何新娘该有的野心的。这些本为私耳的话，成了公供；而握着御耳本身的太子，就地下判：郑蔓宜被判赴刑部，全数认罪。这国公之女的骄，本集始于摔一盆兰，终于是被押向一间牢；而她身后的大宅，发现自己择的新娘，在漫不经心的一时之间，化作了灰。

But the wedding is not done with its surprises, and the season is not yet finished tightening its knots. The Crown Prince himself, in a gesture that turns the celebration strange and solemn both, steps onto the opera stage to sing — perhaps to mark the day, perhaps to show a captured heir's grace before the city that will soon crown him. The gathered crowd turns its eyes upward, expecting a performance, and for a moment the music rises. Then, from the dark of the rafters or the edge of the stage, a hidden weapon flies swift and silent, and the prince is struck and falls. The festivity collapses into alarm and shouting; the man who heard the confession and sentenced the bride is himself struck down before the very faces he had been performing for, and the season, which had seemed at last to be resolving its knots, yanks the string taut once more and leaves the house, and the uneasy throne, gasping at the edge of a new and sudden peril.

但这场婚还有它的惊，而这季还没把它的结束紧完。太子本人，以一个把庆转怪也转肃的姿，踏上台唱起戏——或许为记这日，或许为在即将拥他登基的城前，显一个被擒继承人的雅。聚来的人仰了眼，待着一场演；有一刻乐起了。接着，从梁上或台边的暗里，一记暗器飞出，迅而无声，太子中而倒。喜散作惊与喊；那听了供、判了新娘的人，自己在他方才献艺的那些脸前被击倒；而这季，似在解它的结，却把那绳猛地绷紧，留这府、留那不安的御座，在一场新的、骤来的险之边缘，喘着。

It is worth marking how neatly this episode gathers the year's medicine into a single room, only to show that no room is safe. A wedding, of all things — the season's oldest symbol of setting things right — becomes the place where a bride is unmade, a prince is struck, and a maid, having crossed a province in another's thoughts, stands finally free in the same air as the man who came for her. The season has spent many episodes teaching this household that peace is a thing poured by a smiling hand, and episode twenty-four proves the lesson once more: the cup offered at a wedding can hold poison just as readily as the cup offered in a sickroom. The Lin estate, which thought the war behind it, discovers that the war merely changed its clothes and walked in with the musicians. Nothing in this household's hard-won calm is allowed to last past the lighting of the lamps, and the season, with a smile that promises more, means to keep it exactly that way.

值得记一记：这一集多齐整地把这年的药收进一个屋，却只为了显出没有屋是安全的。一场婚，偏是——这季最旧的"了结"之象——成了新娘被毁、太子被击之处；而一个丫鬟，在另一个人的思里跨过一省，终立在与那为她而来的人同一口气里、自由着。这季耗了许多集教这府：太平是笑着的手倒出的物；而二十四集，再证这课一回：婚上奉的杯，与病房里奉的杯一样，盛得了毒。林府，以为战已在身后，却发觉那战不过换了衣、随乐师走了进来。

So we close episode twenty-four the way a performance closes — mid-note, with the music cut and the stage in disorder. Lin Jinqi searched the painted house and knew her safe by a single stroke on a fan; Han Liang and Furong Xiu carried her home and the group pooled silver for Lü Jier; Lanxiang set her trap at a wedding and drew the ducal bride into the prince's ear; Zheng Manyi was sentenced to confess; and the Crown Prince, singing on the stage, fell to a hidden weapon. Episode twenty-five, when it comes, will carry the fallen prince back toward the capital and will show what a master will claim when he has just, unknowingly, saved a throne. But that is next week's tell, and the lanterns of the Lin estate, unaware that the empire has just brushed past their gate, burn on. The song is left unfinished, the knot is left half-tied, and the season, satisfied for the moment with the mischief it has made, draws back into the dark to watch what the morning will untangle.
