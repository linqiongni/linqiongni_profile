Episode 27 is the wedding day, and it starts in a quiet, sad way. Lanxiang gets ready to leave her parents' house for good. She kneels in front of her father and mother and bows low, the way a daughter does when she is about to marry and move into another family. Her eyes fill with tears, but she does not make a sound. She has lived under this roof for years, and now she has to go. Her mother holds her hand a little too long, and her father looks away so she will not see him cry. There is no big scene and no loud crying, just a girl who does not want to leave and parents who cannot stop her. She hugs them once, then turns and walks out the door. From this day on she belongs to the Lin family, at least in name. She keeps her head down on the way to the sedan, because if she looks back she knows she will not be able to keep walking. The house behind her is the only home she has known, and the door closes like a soft end to the life she had before today.

第二十七集是大婚的日子，开头安静又心酸。兰香收拾好，要离开爹娘家了。她跪在父母面前重重磕头，那是女儿要出嫁、进别家人门时该行的礼。眼里含泪，可她没出声。她在这屋檐下住了好些年，如今得走了。娘拉她的手拉得久了些，爹别过脸去，是不想让她看见自己掉泪。没有什么大场面，也没人嚎，就是一个舍不得走的姑娘，和拦不住她的爹娘。她抱了爹娘一下，转身出门。从这天起，她名义上就是林家的人了。上花轿一路她低着头，因为只要回头，她知道自己就走不动了。身后的屋是她唯一熟的家，门轻轻一关，像给她从前那段日子画了个软软的句号。

The wedding sedan moves slowly through the streets, carried by the Lin family's men. Inside, Lanxiang sits still with the red veil over her head. She cannot see the road, but she can feel every step. The sound of the drums and the music should feel happy, yet to her it feels like a long walk she did not choose. She thinks about the letter she once read, the one that told her the Lin family had broken off her own family's engagement years ago. That old wound sits in her chest the whole way. She does not smile, and she does not lift the veil to peek. She just sits and lets the bearers carry her, like a package being delivered to a house that once threw her family away. The city goes by outside, but she is not really in it. Her hands rest in her lap, still, and she counts the steps in her head so the ride will feel shorter than it is.

花轿被林府的人慢慢抬着走。兰香坐在里头，红盖头蒙着头，一动不动。路看不见，可每一步她都感觉得到。外头鼓乐该是喜庆的，可她只觉得像走一条自己没选的长路。她想起早年读过的那封信——信上说林家多年前退了她自家那门亲。那道旧伤，一路都压在胸口。她不笑，也没掀盖头偷看，就坐着，由人抬着走，像个被送到一户曾经甩过她家的包裹。外头街市过去，她的心却不在里头。手搁在膝上，静静的，她在心里数着步子，好让这趟路显得比实际短些。

Then something unexpected happens on the road. The procession meets another crowd coming the other way, and this one is loud with cheer and gongs. It is Han Liang, a young man from the neighborhood, and he is riding home in triumph. He has just taken the top place in the provincial exam, the title everyone calls the top graduate, and his family is parading him through town. Lanxiang hears the noise from inside the sedan and asks what it is. When she learns it is Han Liang, a small, real smile touches her face for the first time that day. He was always a good sort, and his win feels like a piece of normal life breaking through the strange day. The two processions pass side by side for a moment, his joy against her silence, and then they part. She holds that small bit of cheer in her mind all the way to the Lin gate, like a warm stone kept through a cold walk.

When the sedan finally reaches the Lin residence, the real trouble begins. Lanxiang is helped out and led into the main hall where the wedding rite will happen. The hall is full of Lin family members and guests, all waiting to see the bow. Lin Jinqi stands across from her in his red robe, calm and ready. But when the moment comes to bow to heaven and earth, and then to each other, Lanxiang will not bend. She just stands there. The music keeps playing, the guests go quiet, and still she does not move. Everyone can see she is not willing. This is not a shy bride but a girl who was brought here by force, and she means for the whole room to know it. The master of the rite clears his throat, the drums falter, and a long, awkward pause opens in the middle of what should have been the happiest minute of the day.

The bride's maid, the old woman hired to keep the rite moving, panics a little. She steps behind Lanxiang and presses down hard on her back and shoulders, forcing her to bend at the waist so the bow can be finished. Lanxiang fights it for a second, then lets herself be pushed, because fighting in front of everyone would only make a worse scene. The bow is done, the guests breathe out, but nobody in that hall mistakes it for a happy match. They all saw the hand on her back, and they all know the bride did not choose this. The rite is complete on paper, yet the room is heavy with the truth that no one says out loud. The bride's maid steps back, flustered, and the music limps on, as if everyone is pretending the pause never happened and the girl bent of her own will. A few old guests lower their eyes, because they have seen forced matches before, and they know how this one will sit in the house for years to come. The rite is written down as done, yet every person there has just watched a wedding that the bride did not agree to, and that memory will not wash out with the wine.

Later, in the bridal chamber, the last part of the old custom comes. Lin Jinqi lifts the red veil from her face. The room has people in it, family and servants who came to watch the moment. The air is thick. Then, before anyone can speak a kind word, Lanxiang raises her hand and slaps Lin Jinqi across the face. Hard. The sound lands in the quiet room like a stone. Nobody moves. She does not cry or scream. She just stands there, her face cold, and says the words her own mother spoke years ago, when the Lin family broke off the Shen family's engagement: you have gone too far. She says them now, to his face, in front of his whole house. The slap is loud, the words are louder, and the small crowd that came to smile leaves instead with something they will never forget. For a breath the only sound is the veil cloth settling back onto her shoulders, and the red mark on his face says plainer than any word that this marriage started with a wound. What should have been a tender moment became the honestest one in the whole house.

后来进了洞房，旧规矩最后一步来了。林锦岐挑起她脸上的红盖头。屋里有人，本家和外人都来看这一刻。空气沉。然后，谁还没来得及说句好话，兰香抬手，狠狠一巴掌扇在林锦岐脸上。响声在静屋里像块石头落地。没人动。她没哭也没喊，就那么站着，脸冷冷的，说出当年她娘的话——那年林家退了沈家的亲，她娘就说：欺人太甚。如今她当着他的面，当着他满屋的人，把这句话还了回去。巴掌响，话更响，那拨本要来笑的人，走的时候带走的，是这辈子忘不了的东西。

Lin Jinqi does not hit back, and he does not even look angry. He lifts a hand to his cheek, feels the sting, and gives a small, tired smile. He takes it, because he knows he earned it. He forced this marriage, he dragged a girl who did not want him into his house, and a slap is the softest thing she could have done. So he says nothing to shame her, and he does not order anyone to drag her away. He just lets the mark sit on his face as proof of what he did. The guests who saw it will talk, but he does not care. In his own way, he thinks he deserved worse, and he is willing to carry it. The room clears out slowly, and he stays where he is, one hand still on his cheek, letting the silence say the part he will not say himself.

Senior Lady Lin is in the room too, and she does not cover for her son. When the moment settles, she speaks plainly to the people near her. She says this was his doing. He forced the match, he ignored the girl's wishes, and what happened today is the fruit of his own choice. She does not scold Lanxiang, and she does not call the slap disrespect. She simply tells the truth, that her son brought this on himself. For a mother in a great house to say that, in front of others, is rare. But the old lady is honest in a way the house is not used to. The words hang there, and nobody argues, because everybody in the room knows she is right. A mother who blames her own son in front of guests is a rare thing in a family like this, and the old lady knows it. She would rather speak the truth than let the house pretend the bride was willing, because a lie told on a wedding day tends to grow teeth later.

Night falls, and the house expects the two to share the marriage bed. But Lanxiang will not. She takes the small warm side room, the heated chamber meant for resting, and lays herself down there alone. She leaves the big bed empty. The servants whisper, but no one makes her move. She has made her line clear: she is here because she was made to be, not because she gives herself to him. The warm room is hers, and she guards that small bit of space like a wall. No one is allowed past that door tonight, not even the husband the house gave her. She sleeps with her back to the door, and for the first time since the sedan she lets herself be still, as if the lock on the inside mattered more than the ring on her finger. The lamp burns low, the house goes quiet, and she finally closes her eyes on a day that took everything she did not want to give.

Lin Jinqi hears she is in the warm room and will not come to the bed. So he does something the servants did not expect. He walks in, picks her up where she sleeps, and carries her back to the main bed himself. She wakes and resists, but he does not use force the way others might. He just moves her, then steps back. He lays her on the bed, covers her, and then he turns and goes to sleep in the warm side room himself. He gives her the big bed and takes the small one. The house is surprised, but he does not explain. He simply trades places and lets her have the comfort he thinks she should have on this hard night. In the morning the servants will find him there, and they will understand, without a word, the kind of man who takes the slap and then gives up his own bed.

So episode 27 ends not with a happy couple but with two people in two rooms. Lanxiang got through the day she feared, said the words her mother once said, and kept a piece of herself behind a closed door. Lin Jinqi took the slap and the cold bed without complaint, paying for a choice he made on his own. The Lin family got the bride they forced, but not the peace they hoped for. The wedding is done, the name is set, yet the gap between them is wide and plain. What comes next will be about whether a forced match can ever become something real, or whether the slap was only the first honest thing between them. The door to the warm room stays shut, and on the other side, the house waits to see what these two will do next. A wedding that opened with a slap is a wedding everyone will watch, and the Lin family has just learned that the girl they forced inside is not the quiet thing they thought she was.
