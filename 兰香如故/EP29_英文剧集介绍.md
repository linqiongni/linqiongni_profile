# 兰香如故 — The Fragrance of Orchids Remains
## Episode 29 · Told Like A Story

Episode 29 starts with the return visit, the custom where a new bride goes back to her own parents' house a few days after the wedding. Lanxiang makes the trip, and Lin Jinqi goes with her. On the way, he brings up the capital plan again, the one she already said no to. He tells her the whole family is getting ready to move, and he wants her to go along without more fight. The road to her parents' home should be a soft, happy errand, but he turns it into another push. She listens, says nothing sweet, and the old wall between them shows up again on what should have been a gentle day. The man she was forced to marry is still trying to move her where she does not want to go. She thought the return visit would be a small peace, a few hours with her mother and father away from the Lin house. Instead it becomes another field where he presses his will, and she learns again that this husband does not take a no as the final word, only as a pause before the next push.

Lanxiang's answer is the same as before. She does not want the capital. The reasons sit close to her chest. She has just left her parents' roof, and the thought of going farther away from them makes her chest tight. On top of that, the capital is where the Shen family, her true blood, fell and broke years ago, and the memory still stings like a fresh cut. She will not walk back into that city happy. So she says no again, plain and flat, and Lin Jinqi hears it. He does not shout, but he does not drop it either. He simply stores the refusal and waits, because he has learned that with this girl a straight no is rarely the end of the talk. He has the patience of a man who believes he is right, and she has the stubbornness of a girl who knows what the city cost her family. Neither gives, and the road to her parents' gate carries both of them in a silence that is its own kind of fight, with the capital waiting at the end of it.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

兰香的答复和从前一样。她不要京城。理由压在胸口。她刚离了爹娘的屋檐，再往远走，胸口就发紧。更要紧，京城是她沈家真骨血多年前垮掉的地方，那记忆还像新伤疼。她不肯欢欢喜喜走回那城。于是她又说了不，平平板板，林锦岐听着。他没吼，也没放下，只是把这句拒收着、等着，因为他早知道，对这姑娘，一句直白的"不"很少是话的尽头。

</details>

Then a person from the past walks in and changes everything. A Zhi, the maid who once served Lü Jí'ér, shows up where Lanxiang can see her. A Zhi was not supposed to be there, and her face says she has come with a message that could not wait. She looks thin and worried, the kind of worried that comes from running a long way to tell someone something they need to hear. Lanxiang is surprised to see her, because A Zhi belongs to another house and another girl, and a maid does not just appear on a road for no reason. The meeting is quiet but loaded, and right away Lanxiang knows this is not a casual visit. A Zhi keeps her voice low and her eyes on the ground, the way a servant does who knows she is out of place. But the urgency in her holds the room, and Lanxiang feels the hair on her arms rise, because a messenger with that look never brings good weather, only the kind of news that changes where a person is headed.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

接着，一个旧人走进来，把一切变了。阿枳，那个从前伺候吕蕺儿的丫鬟，在兰香能看见的地方出现了。阿枳本不该在那儿，她那张脸写着：她是带着等不得的话来的。她瘦了，神情慌，是那种跑了长路、只为说句要紧话的慌。兰香见她，意外，因为阿枳是别家、别个姑娘的人，丫鬟不会没缘由在路上冒出来。这见面安静，却沉，兰香立刻知道这不是闲串门。

</details>

The message A Zhi carries is about Lü Jí'ér. She tells Lanxiang that Lü Jí'ér is in the capital, and not by her own choice. Her bond, the paper that says who owns her, sits in the hands of Zhao Xingtang, Lin Jinqi's wife, the woman who holds the power in that city. So Lü Jí'ér is stuck there, a servant under a hard mistress, with no way out that she can find alone. A Zhi has come to beg Lanxiang for help, because she knows Lanxiang is the one person clever and brave enough to do something. The words land heavy. The capital was the place Lanxiang feared, and now it is the place where her old friend is trapped. Zhao Xingtang keeps the paper tight, and Lü Jí'ér has no family left to buy it back or pull her free. A Zhi has walked far to find Lanxiang, and the plain truth is that without help her mistress will stay a slave in that city until someone with wit and nerve steps in to break the hold.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

阿枳带的话是关于吕蕺儿。她告诉兰香，吕蕺儿在京城，且不是自己愿意的。她的身契——那张写着归谁所有的纸——攥在赵星棠手里。赵星棠是林锦岐的妻，是在那城里掌着权的人。所以吕蕺儿困在那儿，是硬主母手下的奴，凭自己找不出路。阿枳是来求兰香搭手的，因为她知道满府就兰香又聪明又敢，能办点事。这话沉。京城本是兰香怕的地方，如今成了她旧友被困的地方。

</details>

This is the crack in Lanxiang's wall. She has been saying no to the capital for herself, but Lü Jí'ér is different. The two girls grew up close, and leaving a friend in Zhao Xingtang's grip sits wrong with her. A Zhi's plea turns the city from a place she feared into a place she might have to enter after all. She does not say yes out loud yet, but something in her shifts. The refusal that was solid starts to soften at the edge. Lin Jinqi watches this change without a word, because he knows the news has done the work his pushing could not. For the first time, the capital looks less like a prison and more like a errand she cannot refuse. The fear does not leave, but it gets crowded out by something warmer, the pull of a friend in trouble. Lanxiang has always been the one who moves when others are stuck, and Lü Jí'ér's name in the capital is the one string that could ever drag her toward that gate, no matter how much the old shadow there frightens her.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

这道缝裂在兰香墙上。她为自己拒京城，可吕蕺儿不同。两个姑娘一处长大，把朋友撂在赵星棠手心里，她不落忍。阿枳这一求，把那城从她怕的地方，变成了她或许终究得进的地方。她还没开口说好，可心里某处动了。那堵得实的拒，边角软了。林锦岐不言不语看着这变化，因为他知道这消息办成了他催不来的事。头一回，京城不像牢，倒像桩她推不掉的差。

</details>

Lin Jinqi sees the opening and takes it, but not in the way anyone expects. Instead of arguing more, he quietly decides to end the debate by ending her choice. That night, or soon after, he slips something into what she drinks, a drug meant to put her under without a fight. He does not tell her, because he knows she would never agree to be carried to the capital like a parcel. The move is cold, but in his mind it is the only way to get her there before she digs in deeper. He would rather she wake up past the point of turning back than listen to another flat no. The cup goes down, and the plan is set in secret. He watches her lift it and swallow, and his face does not change. Whatever he feels about tricking his own wife, he hides it well. To him the end clears the means, and a girl who will not be reasoned with must be carried, even if the carrying starts with a lie in a cup and a friend's name used as the hook.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

林锦岐看准了空子，却用了没人料到的法子。他不再争，而是悄悄决定：结束争，就结束她的选择。那夜，或不久后，他往她喝的东西里下了药，为叫她不挣扎就昏过去。他没告诉她，因为他知道她绝不肯像包裹一样被抬去京城。这手冷，可他心里，这是把她弄去、赶在她更犟之前唯一的法子。他宁可她醒来已过不能回头，也不愿再听一句平板的"不"。那杯下了肚，计就暗中定了。

</details>

Lanxiang drinks without suspicion. She has no reason to think the man at her side would drug her, even a man she does not trust. The stuff works slow at first, a little warmth, a little blur at the edge of her sight, then a pull down she cannot fight. She tries to stand, tries to ask what is wrong, but her legs will not hold and her words come out thick. By the time she understands something is wrong, it is already too late to call for help. The last thing she registers is Lin Jinqi's face, calm, watching, and then the dark closes in. She is out, and the house goes quiet around her. No one else in the room seems to notice what he did, or if they do, they say nothing, because orders in this house run from the top. Lanxiang slips under, trusting the wrong man one last time, and the Lin residence keeps its walls around a secret she will not wake to learn for hours yet, on a deck she has never stood on.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

兰香喝得毫无防备。她没理由疑身边这男人会下药，哪怕一个她不信任的。药先慢：一点热，视线边角发糊，然后一股压不下去的沉。她想站、想问怎么了，腿却撑不住，话也黏。等她明白出事，叫人已晚。她最后认出的，是林锦岐那张脸，镇定，看着她，然后黑幕合拢。她昏了，屋在她四周静下。

</details>

When she opens her eyes again, nothing is where it should be. The room rocks in a way a house never does, and the sound is wrong, water against wood, a low creak that means one thing. She is on a boat. Not in her bed, not in the Lin residence, but on a river vessel moving somewhere she did not choose. She sits up fast and the sway hits her, and the truth lands at once. She has been taken. While she slept, Lin Jinqi put her on this ship and pointed it at the capital. The drug did its job, and the road she refused is now the water under her feet. She feels for the deck with bare feet and finds only moving planks and cold air. The drug has worn thin enough to wake her but not enough to clear her head, and for a confused moment she thinks she is dreaming, until the rock of the hull tells her the dream is real and she is already gone from everything she knows.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

她再睁眼，处处不该。屋子晃，房子从不这么晃；声也不对，是水拍木、低低的嘎吱，只意味着一件事。她在船上。不在自己床上，不在林府，在一条河船上，去她没选的地方。她猛坐起，晃劲撞来，真相立刻落到头上：她被带走了。她睡着时，林锦岐把她弄上这船，船头指向京城。药成了事，她拒的路，如今成了脚下这水。

</details>

She rushes to the deck and finds Lin Jinqi there, calm as ever, watching the river. He does not pretend he did not do it. He tells her, plain, that they are on the way to the capital, and that he could not let her stay behind. She is furious, but the fury has nowhere to go, because the boat is mid-river and there is no stepping off. He says Lü Jí'ér is there too, and that this is the only way she could have gone to help. The words are true, and that makes them harder to throw back. She stands at the rail, the wind in her face, a prisoner of a plan she did not agree to but cannot undo. The river runs wide and fast, and there is no shore close enough to reach by swimming, no cart to flag, no way back but the one he chose. She grips the rail until her knuckles pale, not because it helps, but because she needs something solid while the life she refused carries her forward into the city she dreads.

So episode 29 ends on the water, with Lanxiang taken to the very city she feared, for the very friend she could not abandon. Lin Jinqi won the argument the only way he knows how, by removing her say in it. The drug, the boat, the calm face at the rail, all of it says the same thing: he will move her where he thinks she must go, willing or not. But the capital ahead holds Lü Jí'ér, and Zhao Xingtang, and the old Shen shadow, and the next episodes will show whether a bride carried by force can still turn the tables once she reaches the shore. The water will land them in the capital before she is ready, and the city that broke her blood will open its gates to a bride who came by force. But Lin Jinqi forgets one thing, that a girl carried to a fight is still a girl who can fight, and the shore is where her real move begins, with Lü Jí'ér waiting at the other end.
