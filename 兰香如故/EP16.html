Let me tell you about episode sixteen, because this is the episode where the house learns that some doors are best left unopened, and that the secret behind one of them, had it swung wide, would have brought the Lin name down in the street. It begins in a cave on the estate's wild edge, where the third daughter, meaning only to escape the heat, comes upon her second sister and Du Zheng locked in a shame that should never have been spoken aloud. The girl is clever before she is frightened: instead of crying out and dragging the whole residence into the mud, she makes a small, deliberate noise — a cough, a footstep, the kind of sound that says I am here and I have eyes — and the two scramble apart, the moment broken, the sin uncompleted. She tells no one. She simply walks away, and in walking away she buys the house one more day of standing, one more morning before the whispers start. It is a small mercy, but in a great family a small mercy is sometimes the only kind that holds.

让我跟你讲讲第十六集，因为这一集，是这府学了有些门最好别开；而门后那桩秘，若大开，会叫林家的名在街上塌。它始于府外野处的山洞，三小姐本只为避热，却撞见二姐与杜正缠在见不得人的羞里。姑娘在怕之前先聪明：她不出声喊、不把全府拖进泥，只弄出一点刻意响动——一声嗽、一步脚——像在说"我在此、我有眼"；两人慌散，那刻碎了、那罪未成。她不告人，只走开，这一走，为府多买一日站立，在流言起前多一个清晨。这是小仁，但大宅里，小仁有时是唯一种撑得住的仁。

Now, here is the quiet part of the tale, the part that happens inside a girl's chest where no one can watch. The third daughter keeps what she saw to herself through the long afternoon, and the keeping is its own kind of labor. She is fond of her second sister, and she knows that to name the man in that cave is to name a ruin — for the sister, for the family, for the marriage the old master has already begun to plan. So she weighs the mercy: tell the brother and break the sister, or hold the silence and leave the house's throat open to the next who might step into that cave. She chooses, in the end, the harder path — she will tell Lin Jinqi, but she will tell him alone, and she will ask him to spare the second daughter the knowing for as long as the walls can stand without it. It is the first time she decides not only to act, but to protect, and the house, without knowing it, has found a second steward in a week.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

这里头静的部，是发生在姑娘胸口、无人能看处。三小姐把所见自藏过整下午，而藏本身是一种劳。她疼二姐，也知点出洞中那男人，便是点出一场毁——毁了姐、毁了家、毁了老太爷已起意的婚。于是她秤那仁：告兄、碎了姐；或缄口、留府喉对着下一个踏进洞的人开着。终她选了更难的路——她要告林锦岐，但只私下，且求他在墙还撑得住时，替二姐瞒下这知。这是她头回不只是定下动手，而是定下护人；而府，不知觉间，一周内得了第二个管家。

Before she can reach him, however, the world outside the estate delivers its own news. Nankè returns from the river road with a report that lands like a stone in still water: the wokou remnant the master cleared some weeks back was never the whole snake, only the tail of it. The head, the true hand behind the smuggling, is the Dai family — merchants who for years moved forbidden cotton and worse under a loyal name, and who now have the Yongchang Marquis himself in their net. The old general, riding unawares into what he thought was a routine muster, is the one caught, the bait in a trap laid long before this season turned. Nankè's words and the third daughter's paper are two halves of a single treason, and when Lin Jinqi reads both, he goes pale not with fear but with the cold clarity of a man who at last sees the whole board. He does not wait for morning light to move.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

但她未及寻他，府外先递来自己的消息。南客自江路回，报如石落静水：主子前些时洗清的那支倭寇残党，从不是整条蛇，只是尾。蛇头、那私运的后台，是戴家——一姓多年以忠名行私、运禁棉更运更糟，如今把永昌侯本人也网了进去。老将不察，以例常点操为名踏进早布好的阱，反成了饵。南客的话与三小姐的纸，是同一桩叛的两半；林锦岐两下一读，白了脸，不是怕，是一个终于看清整盘棋的人那种冷清。他不等天明便动。

Lin Jinqi writes a single line to his mother and wakes Jiugao and the stable in the dark, for the house's cleared name is only as safe as the old general who cleared it, and a general in a trap is a name dragged through mud. He rides out before the second daughter's wing stirs, taking with him the men he trusts to a man, and he leaves the residence not to war but to the marquis's side. At the gate he pauses, and the pause is for Lanxiang; he tells her only that he goes out and that no soul inside is to know where, and in that plain instruction she hears the whole of his trust — the maid who reads is the maid he leaves the house to. He presses her hand once, as he did at the river, and is gone into the night. Outside the walls, he and the Yongchang Marquis turn the Dai family's net inside out along the road where the wokou once ran, and the snake's head, for a while, is cut.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

林锦岐给母亲留一行字，趁夜唤起九皋与马厩，因这府洗清的名，只稳到替它洗清的那老将稳，而老将落阱，便是名落泥。他趁二小姐院未动便出骑，带所信之人，离府不是去战，是去侯身边。门口他停了步，那一步留给兰香：他只说，他出府，府里无人可知去向；而这句平白的话里，她听尽了他的信——识字的丫鬟，是他留府交托的那个。他按了她手一下，如江边那样，已没入夜色。墙外，他与永昌侯沿倭寇曾跑的路，把戴家的网翻了里朝外，蛇头，暂且斩了。

Inside the sealed estate, meanwhile, the other crisis shows its face. The fourth daughter, who has worn a faint rash since the river victory, wakes feverish, and the old bite on her arm where the summer herbs were gathered has swollen angry and hot, the skin drawn tight and bright as a struck bell. The physician the house keeps comes, frowns, and names it erysipelas — a fire in the blood that, in a crowded residence, walks from one body to the next like a rumor no wall can stop. It is, he says, the look of an illness that spreads, and the word epidemic passes his lips like a stone dropped in a well. Within the day the fourth daughter's fever climbs, and the household, which thought the war was only outside its gates, discovers the war has a second front, and this one is laid out on a child's flushed cheek and a mother's first, small fright.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

封起的府内，另一桩危机露了面。四小姐，江胜后一直带些淡疹，这早发热醒转，采药那处被咬的臂上肿得红热，皮绷紧发亮如被击的钟。府里常请的大夫来，蹙眉，断为丹毒——血里一把火，在拥挤的宅里，如墙挡不住的流言般从一身高到另一身。他说，这是会传的病模样，"疫"字出口如石落井。不日四小姐热升；这府本以为战只在门外，方知战有第二线，而这一线，摊在一个孩烧红的颊、与母亲头一遭小小的慌。

The magistrate, hearing of a spreading sickness in a great house, sends his men to seal the Lin gates, lest the city catch what the court cannot even name. Lin Jinqi, already beyond the wall on his night errand, is spared the lock; but inside, the Senior Lady Lin does the only thing a mother of a great house can. She draws every soul into the Zhichun Residence and sets watchers at the door, turning the household into a jar with the sickness sealed inside it — and the ones she loves closest to the flame she cannot reach through the glass. The residence becomes a world of its own, small and afraid, where medicine is measured by the drop and news comes only from the gate. And in this jar, where every hand is accounted for and none may leave, the clever girls of the house find themselves pressed together with the very people they have been watching, and the watching grows sharp.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

县官闻大宅有传病，派人封了林府门，怕这城染上官也命名不出的东西。林锦岐既已因夜差在墙外，免了这锁；墙内，林母做了大宅之母唯一能做的——把众人尽数收进知春馆、门口设人守，将这座府，变成一只把病封在里的罐，而她最爱的，隔了玻璃，是她伸手不及的近火的那些。府成一方自有的小世界，怕着，药按滴量，消息只从门来。就在这罐里，人人被点清、无人可出，府里聪明的姑娘们，与她们一向盯着的人挤在一处，而那盯着，愈发尖了。

It is in this sealed jar that Du Cuique makes her first true move. She comes to the Senior Lady offering to tend the fourth daughter, speaking of a mother who knew herbs and a girl raised to read a fever, and the old lady, weary and short of hands, nearly assents. Lanxiang, passing with the water, sees the calculation under the sweetness: Du Cuique does not want to heal the child, she wants the room where the household's weakness is laid bare, the ear of the dying, the confidence of the frightened, the door a guest has no right to hold. She says nothing loud. She lets a hairpin slip from her sleeve to the floor at the Senior Lady's feet, and when the old lady bends, Lanxiang murmurs only that the fourth daughter asked for her grandmother, and would fret to be tended by any hand but family's. The assent dies unspoken, and for the first time the two clever girls look at each other without a screen between them.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

就在这只封起的罐里，杜翠雀下了她头一着真棋。她来见林母，请去照四小姐，说母亲识药、自己自幼会看热——而老夫人乏了、手短，几乎应了。兰香端水过，看见甜下的算：杜翠雀不是要医孩，她要的是那间摊着府之弱的屋、将死者的耳、受怕者的信、一个客本无权握的门。她不出声。她让一支簪从袖滑落、掉在林母脚边；老夫人俯身时，兰香只低语：四小姐寻祖母，非家人之手不肯安。那应允未出口便死了；两聪明姑娘头回无屏相对。

The refusal, gentle as it is, lands like a slap wrapped in silk. Du Cuique smiles, bows, and withdraws, but the wariness in her eyes is plain — she knows she has been seen, and she knows the hand that saw her was not the old lady's but the maid's. From that moment the balance inside the jar shifts by a hair: Lanxiang has named herself as a rival without ever speaking a word of accusation, and Du Cuique, who meant to rule the inner house through the sickroom, now understands that the quiet girl with the water is the one wall she cannot climb. The season, watching, files this small victory where it keeps the others, for it is in such moments — a pin dropped, a grandmother named, a smile that does not reach the eyes — that the real war of the Lin estate is won or lost, far from any banner or blade.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

那拒，轻如裹绸的掌。杜翠雀笑、福身、退下，但眼里戒备分明——她知自己被看穿，也知看穿她的不是老夫人，是那丫鬟。自那一刻，罐里天平偏了一发：兰香未发一言指控，已把自己立作对手；而杜翠雀，本要借病房掌内宅，此刻明白，那端水静女，是她爬不过的墙。这季把这场小胜，收进收着别处的那一处；因正是在这样的时刻——簪一落、祖母一名、笑不及眼——林府真正的战，分了胜负，远在旗帜与刀刃之外。

So we close episode sixteen the way a fever closes a door — by sealing everyone in with the thing they fear. The third daughter gave the name and learned to protect; the master rode out and cut the snake's head; the old lady drew the household into one room and met, across it, the girl who means to rule it. And Lanxiang, blocking the poisoner with a dropped pin and a whispered name, stands now at the center the season always meant her to reach. Episode seventeen, when it comes, will follow the water to its source and prove that the sickness was never a curse at all, but a hand — and the house will learn whose hand it was that held the ladle. But that is next week's tell. For now, remember only this: in the Lin estate, a sealed gate keeps the danger in as surely as out, and the clever girl who reads the fever is the same one who reads the lie, and the lie, this week, has begun to stir.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

于是我们如热封一门那样收束第十六集——把众人同他们所怕的物，封在一处。三小姐交了名、学了护；主子出骑、斩了蛇头；老夫人将府收进一屋、隔着屋，迎面那要掌它的姑娘。而兰香，以落簪挡下投毒人、以低名点破，如今立在季从不改要她到的中心。第十七集，来的时候，会沿水追到源，证这病从不是咒，而是一只手——而这府将知执勺的是谁的手。但那是下周的讲法。此刻你只记一句：在林府，封起的门，把险关在内的程度，不亚于关在外，而那读热的聪明姑娘，正是读谎的那个，而谎，这周，已起了动静。
