Let me tell you about episode sixteen, because this is the episode where the house learns that a secret kept is a secret loaded, and that the two crises the season planted — the treason in the hills and the sickness in the walls — bloom on the same morning. The third daughter wakes with the folded paper still against her thigh, and for one long hour she sits with the choice Lanxiang laid before her: give the name to her brother and break her sister's heart, or hide it and leave the house's throat open. She is, after last week, no child. She chooses the harder mercy — she will tell Lin Jinqi, but she will tell him alone, and she will ask him to spare the second daughter the knowing as long as the house can stand without it. It is the first time she decides not only to act, but to protect, and the season, watching, marks the girl as its own, the way a smith marks a blade he means to keep and never sell. The mark is small, but the season has been pressing it into her since the cave, and iron does not lie about what it means to keep, or about who it means to keep her for.

让我跟你讲讲第十六集，因为这一集，是这府学了：藏着不说的秘，是上了膛的秘；而这季埋下的两桩危机——山里的叛、墙里的病——在同一日清晨同时开了花。三小姐醒来，折纸还压在大腿上，有整整一个时辰，她坐着，面对兰香摆给她的那个选择：把名字交给兄长、碎了二姐的心，还是藏了、留着府的喉开着。她，过了上周，已不是孩子。她选了更难的仁——她要告知林锦岐，但只私下告知，且求他在府还撑得住时，替二姐瞒下这知。这是她头回不只是"定下动手"，而是"定下护人"；而这季，看着，把姑娘认作了自己人，如铁匠认下他要留、永不肯卖的刃。

She does not reach him first. Before her sandal touches the outer step, Nankè returns from the river road with a report that lands like a stone in still water: the wokou remnant the master cleared was never the whole snake — its head is the Dai family, merchants who for years moved forbidden cotton and worse under a loyal name, and the Yongchang Marquis, riding unawares into their net on the pretense of a routine muster, is now the one in the trap. The third daughter's paper and Nankè's words are two halves of one treason, and Lin Jinqi, reading both, goes pale not with fear but with the cold clarity of a man who sees the board at last. He does not wait for morning. He bids Jiugao wake the stable, writes a single line to his mother, and prepares to ride — not to war, but to the marquis's side, because the house's cleared name is only as safe as the old general who cleared it, and a general in a trap is a name in the mud.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

她没能先寻到他。脚还没踏出外阶，南客已从江路回来，报如石落静水：主子洗清的那支倭寇残党，从不是整条蛇——蛇头是戴家，一姓多年以忠名行私、运禁棉更运更糟的东西；而永昌侯，以例常点操为名不察踏进他们的网，如今反成了被困的那个。三小姐的纸与南客的话，是同一桩叛的两半；林锦岐两下一读，白了脸，不是怕，是一个终于看清棋面的人那种冷清。他不等天明。他命九皋唤起马厩，给母亲留一行字，备马欲行——不是去战，是去侯身边，因这府洗清的名，只稳到替它洗清的那老将稳，而老将落阱，便是名落泥。

At the gate he pauses, and the pause is for Lanxiang. He tells her only that he goes out and that no one in the residence is to know where, and in that plain instruction she hears the whole of his trust: the maid who reads is the maid he leaves the house to. He presses her hand once, the way he did at the river, and is gone into the dark before the second daughter's wing stirs. And it is then, as the household shakes itself awake, that the other crisis shows its face — the fourth daughter, who has worn a faint rash since the river victory, wakes feverish, and the bite on her arm where the summer herbs were gathered has swollen angry and hot, the skin drawn tight and bright as a struck bell. The sickness the season promised arrives not with a bang but with a child's flushed cheek and a mother's first, small fright.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

门口他停了步，那一步是留给兰香的。他只说：他出府，府里无人可知去向；而这句平白的话里，她听尽了他的信：识字的丫鬟，是他留府交托的那个。他按了她手一下，如江边那样，趁二小姐院里未动，已没入夜色。而就在此时，这府揉眼醒转，另一桩危机露了面——四小姐，江胜后一直带些淡疹，这早发热醒转，采药那处被咬的臂上肿得红热，皮绷紧发亮如被击的钟。这季许下的病，不是轰然，而是一个孩烧红的颊、与母亲头一遭小小的慌。

The physician the house keeps comes, frowns, and names it erysipelas — a fire in the blood that, in a crowded residence, walks from one body to the next like a rumor no wall can stop. Within the day the fourth daughter's fever climbs; Yuchan, who shared the summer herb, breaks out in the same angry marks; and word of a spreading sickness reaches the magistrate's ear, who sends men to seal the Lin gates lest the city catch what the court cannot name. Lin Jinqi, already beyond the wall on the night errand, is spared the lock; inside, the Senior Lady Lin does the only thing a mother of a great house can — she draws every soul into the Zhichun Residence and sets watchers at the door, turning the household into a jar with the sickness sealed inside it, and the ones she loves closest to the flame she cannot reach through the glass.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

府里常请的大夫来，蹙眉，断为丹毒——血里一把火，在拥挤的宅里，如墙挡不住的流言般从一身高到另一身。不日四小姐热升；同采夏草的玉蝉，也发了同样的红痕；而"病在传"的风声入了县官耳，他派人封了林府门，怕这城染上官也命名不出的东西。林锦岐既已因夜差在墙外，免了这锁；墙内，林母做了大宅之母唯一能做的——把众人尽数收进知春馆、门口设人守，将这座府，变成一只把病封在里的罐，而她最爱的，隔了玻璃，是她伸手不及的近火的那些。

It is in this sealed jar that Du Cuique makes her first true move. She comes to the Senior Lady offering to tend the fourth daughter, speaking of a mother who knew herbs and a girl raised to read a fever, and the old lady, weary and short of hands, nearly assents. Lanxiang, passing with the water, sees the calculation under the sweetness: Du Cuique does not want to heal the child, she wants the room where the household's weakness is laid bare, the ear of the dying, the confidence of the frightened, the door a guest has no right to hold. She says nothing loud. She lets a hairpin slip from her sleeve to the floor at the Senior Lady's feet, and when the old lady bends, Lanxiang murmurs only that the fourth daughter asked for her grandmother, and would fret to be tended by any hand but family's. The assent dies unspoken; Du Cuique smiles, and for the first time the two clever girls look at each other without a screen between them.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

就在这只封起的罐里，杜翠雀下了她头一着真棋。她来见林母，请去照四小姐，说母亲识药、自己自幼会看热——而老夫人乏了、手短，几乎应了。兰香端水过，看见甜下的算：杜翠雀不是要医孩，她要的是那间摊着府之弱的屋、将死者的耳、受怕者的信、一个客本无权握的门。她不出声。她让一支簪从袖滑落、掉在林母脚边；老夫人俯身时，兰香只低语：四小姐寻祖母，非家人之手不肯安。那应允未出口便死了；杜翠雀笑了，两聪明姑娘头回无屏相对。

Lanxiang climbs to Yuchan's pallet and finds the seasoned maid burning, but clear-eyed and watching. Yuchan, who has been the master's hand and the household's spine, whispers what the fever cannot hide: that the rash came not from the hills but from the bath, the shared hot water drawn the night the sickness began, the same barrel the fourth daughter and the second master's concubine, Lady Zhou, were given to wash with. Lanxiang files it — not yet a certainty, only a shape, a question with the smell of poison about it, a thread the season laid last week and left for her to pull. And in the filing she sees Du Cuique whole: not merely ambitious, not merely jealous, but a woman who would poison a child to hold a room. The war the season moved to its lowest floor is no longer a metaphor. It is in the water, and the water is in the house, and the house is sealed.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

兰香爬到玉蝉榻边，见这老练的丫鬟烧着，却眼清、看着。玉蝉，曾为主子的手、府的脊，低语出热掩不住的：疹不从山上来，从浴来——病起那夜共汲的热水，那同一桶，四小姐与二爷的妾周姨娘都被给来洗。兰香记下了——还不是确，只是个形，一个带着毒气的问题，一根这季上周铺下、留她来拽的线。而在记时，把杜翠雀看了个全：不只是野心、不只是妒，是一个会毒孩以掌一屋的妇人。这季移到最低那层的战，不再是个喻。它在水里，水在府里，府封着。

Outside, Lin Jinqi finds the Yongchang Marquis not trapped but baiting a trap of his own, and the two old soldiers turn the Dai family's net inside out along the river road where the wokou once ran. He sends word back by Nankè — brief, coded, meant for Lanxiang's eyes: the head is cut, the marquis lives, hold the house. And hold it she does. Through the sealed days she becomes the hand the Senior Lady trusts without naming, moving water and medicine and quiet between the watchers, and the old lady, who once saw a maid, begins to see a steward. The treason in the hills is answered; the sickness in the walls is only beginning, and the girl who reads the lie is the same girl now set to read the fever, the same girl the season has been steering, episode by episode, toward the center of the room.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

墙外，林锦岐寻见永昌侯，不是被困，是自分着阱；两老卒沿倭寇曾跑的江路，把戴家的网翻了里朝外。他托南客带回话——短、密，只给兰香的眼睛：蛇头已斩，侯活，稳府。而她稳住了。封起的日里，她成了林母不点名却信的那只手，在守人间递水、药、与静；而老夫人，曾见一丫鬟，渐见一管家。山里的叛有了应；墙里的病，才刚起，而那读谎的姑娘，正是如今被派驻来读热的那个，正是这季一集集、把 kennel 往屋中心引的那个姑娘。

So we close episode sixteen the way a fever closes a door — by sealing everyone in with the thing they fear. The third daughter gave the name and learned to protect; the master rode out and cut the snake's head; the old lady drew the household into one room and met, across it, the girl who means to rule it. And Lanxiang, tending the sick and blocking the poisoner with a dropped pin, stands now at the center the season always meant her to reach. Episode seventeen, when it comes, will follow the water to its source, and the house will learn whether the sickness was a curse or a hand, and whose hand it was that held the ladle. But that is next week's tell. For now, remember only this: in the Lin estate, a sealed gate keeps the danger in as surely as out — and the clever girl who reads the fever is the same one who reads the lie, and the lie, this week, has a name. The house has learned to fear the paper and the water in the same breath, and the girl who carried both to the light now stands at the center the season always meant her to reach, the place where a steward is made and a war is named.
