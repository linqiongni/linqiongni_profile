Episode 16 opens with something that should never have been seen. The third daughter of the Lin family is walking near a cave on the edge of the estate, just trying to get away from the heat, when she hears a sound and looks inside. There she finds her second sister and Du Zheng, one of the household guards, together in a way they should not be. The girl is smart before she is scared. She does not shout or run for help, because that would drag the whole family into shame. Instead she makes a small noise on purpose, a cough, a step on the stones, enough to say I am here, and I can see you. The two pull apart fast, and whatever was about to happen does not happen. She tells no one. She simply turns and walks away. By walking away, she buys the family one more day before the whispers can start. It is a small thing, but in a big household, a small thing like this can be the only thing that keeps everyone standing.

第十六集开头是件不该看见的事。林家三小姐在府外野地的山洞边溜达，本来只想躲躲热，听见动静往里一瞧，正撞见二姐和家丁杜正在一起，干着不该干的事。这姑娘怕归怕，先机灵起来了：她没喊人、没去告状，因为那一闹，全家都得跟着丢人。她就故意弄出点动静，咳一声、踩块石头，意思是"我在这儿，我看见了"。俩人赶紧分开，那事没办成。她谁也没告诉，扭头就走。这一走，给家里多换来一天清静，流言还没起。事不大，可大宅门里，往往就靠这点小事撑着。

After that, the harder part begins, which is keeping the secret. The third daughter carries what she saw through the rest of the day, and holding it in is its own kind of work. She cares about her second sister. She also knows that if she names the man in that cave, she names a ruin, ruin for her sister, ruin for the family, and ruin for the marriage the old master has already started planning. So she thinks it over. Should she tell her brother and break her sister? Or stay quiet and leave the door open for the next person who might wander into that cave? In the end she picks the harder road. She will tell Lin Jinqi, but only him, and only in private. She will ask him to spare their second sister the truth for as long as the walls can hold without it. This is the first time she decides not just to act, but to protect. Without knowing it, the household has found itself a second steady hand in the span of a single week.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

之后更难的是把秘密压住。三小姐把看见的这事憋了整整一下午，而憋着本身就是一种累。她心疼二姐，也知道只要点出洞里那个男人，就等于点出一场祸：毁了姐、毁了家、也毁了老太爷已经起意的亲事。于是她琢磨：告了兄长、碎了姐姐？还是闭嘴、留着门给下一个可能踏进洞的人？最后她选了更难那条，告诉林锦岐，但只私下、只告诉他一个。她会求他在墙还撑得住的时候，替二姐瞒下这真相。这是她头一回不只是决定动手，而是决定护人。府里不知不觉，一周内又多了一只稳得住的手。

Before she can reach him, news arrives from outside the walls. Nankè comes back from the river road with a report that lands like a stone in still water. The wokou remnants the master cleared a few weeks ago were never the whole problem, only the tail of the snake. The real head, the hand behind the smuggling, is the Dai family. For years they moved forbidden cotton and worse under a name everyone trusted, and now they have pulled the Yongchang Marquis himself into their net. The old general rode out thinking it was just a normal muster, and walked straight into a trap set long before this season turned. He is the one caught. Nankè's words and the third daughter's note are two halves of the same treason. When Lin Jinqi reads both, his face goes white, not from fear, but from the cold clear moment of a man who finally sees the whole board. He does not wait for morning to move.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

她还没找到兄长，府外先递来消息。南客从江路回来，带来的消息像石头砸进静水那么重：主子前些时候清掉的倭寇残党，根本不是全部，只是蛇尾。真正的蛇头、私运的后台，是戴家。多年他们借人人信得过的名头运禁棉、干更糟的事，如今把永昌侯本人也网了进去。老将以为只是例行点操，踏进早布好的阱，反成了饵。南客的话和三小姐的纸条，是同一桩叛逆的两半。林锦岐两下一读，脸白了，不是怕，是一个终于看清整盘棋的人，那一刻的冷清。他没等天亮就动身。

Lin Jinqi leaves a single line for his mother and wakes Jiugao and the stable hands in the dark. The family's cleared name is only as safe as the old general who cleared it, and a general in a trap means the name gets dragged through mud. He rides out before the second daughter's wing stirs, taking only the men he trusts completely. He is not riding to war. He is riding to stand at the marquis's side. At the gate he stops, and that stop is for Lanxiang. He tells her only that he is leaving, and that no one inside is to know where. In those plain words she hears all of his trust. The maid who can read is the maid he leaves the house to. He squeezes her hand once, the way he did by the river, and then he is gone into the night. Outside the walls, he and the Yongchang Marquis turn the Dai family's trap inside out along the old wokou road, and for a while, the snake's head is cut off.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

林锦岐给母亲留了一行字，趁夜唤起九皋和马夫。这府洗清的名声，只稳到替它洗清的那位老将稳；老将落阱，名声就得拖进泥。他趁二小姐院里还没动静就出骑，只带信得过的自己人。他不是去打仗，是去侯爷身边站着。门口他停了步，这一停是留给兰香的：他只说，他要出府，府里谁也不能知道去向。这句平白的话里，她听出了他全部的信。识字的丫鬟，是他留府托付的那个。他像江边那样按了她手一下，没入夜色。墙外，他和永昌侯沿倭寇旧路把戴家的阱翻了个里朝外，蛇头暂且被斩了。

Back inside the sealed estate, a different crisis shows its face. The fourth daughter has had a faint rash since the river victory. Now she wakes up burning with fever. The old bite on her arm, where the summer herbs were gathered, has swollen red and hot, the skin tight and bright. The family physician comes, frowns, and calls it erysipelas, a fire in the blood. In a crowded house, he says, it moves from one person to the next like a rumor no wall can stop. The word epidemic leaves his mouth and drops into the room like a stone in a well. By the end of the day the fourth daughter's fever climbs higher. The household, which thought the war was only outside its gates, finds out the war has a second front, and this one is laid out on a child's flushed cheek, and in a mother's first small fright.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

封起的府里，另一场危机露了脸。四小姐从江上那场胜仗后一直有点淡疹，这回发热醒转，采药那处咬的口子肿得红烫，皮绷紧发亮。府里常请的大夫来，皱眉，断为丹毒，血里一把火。他说，在挤的宅子里，这病像墙挡不住的流言，从一人身爬到另一人身。"疫"字出口，像石落井。不日四小姐热更凶。这府本以为战只在门外，这下才知战有第二线，而这一线，摊在孩子烧红的脸颊，和母亲头一回那点小的慌。

When the magistrate hears that a sickness is spreading in a great house, he sends men to seal the Lin gates, so the city does not catch what the court cannot even name. Lin Jinqi is already past the wall on his night errand, so he misses the lock. Inside, Senior Lady Lin does the only thing a mother of a great house can do. She gathers every soul into the Zhichun Residence and posts watchers at the door. The household becomes a jar with the sickness sealed inside it, and the people she loves most are the ones she cannot reach through the glass. The residence turns into a small world of its own, afraid and close, where medicine is measured drop by drop and news comes only from the gate. In this jar, where every hand is counted and no one may leave, the clever girls of the house end up pressed together with the very people they have been watching, and the watching gets sharper.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

县官听说大宅里病在传，派人封了林府门，怕这城染上官也喊不出名的东西。林锦岐既已因夜差在墙外，躲过了这锁。墙内，林母做了大宅之母唯一能做的：把所有人收进知春馆，门口派人守。这府成了只把病封在里的罐，而她最疼的，隔着玻璃，是她伸手够不到的近火那些人。府变成一方自有的小天地，怕着、挤着，药按滴量，消息只从门缝来。就在这罐里，人人被点清、谁也出不去，府里聪明的姑娘们，和她们一直盯着的人挤到一处，那盯着，更尖了。

It is in this sealed jar that Du Cuique makes her first real move. She comes to Senior Lady Lin and offers to nurse the fourth daughter, talking about a mother who knew herbs and a girl raised to read a fever. The old lady is tired and short of hands, and almost says yes. Lanxiang walks past with a basin of water and sees the calculation under the sweetness. Du Cuique does not want to heal the child. She wants the room where the household's weakness is laid bare, the ear of the dying, the confidence of the frightened, a door no guest should be allowed to hold. Lanxiang says nothing out loud. She lets a hairpin slip from her sleeve to the floor at the old lady's feet. When Senior Lady Lin bends down, Lanxiang murmurs only that the fourth daughter asked for her grandmother, and would fuss to be tended by any hand but family's.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

就在这只封起的罐里，杜翠雀下了头一着真棋。她来见林母，说要照看四小姐，提自己母亲识药、自己从小会看热。老夫人乏了、手不够，差点应了。兰香端水走过，看见甜话底下的算计。杜翠雀不是要医孩子，她要的是那间摊着府里软弱的屋，将死人的耳、受怕人的信、一个客本不该握的门。兰香没出声，让一支簪从袖里滑落，掉在林母脚边。老夫人弯腰时，兰香只低声说：四小姐找祖母，不是自家人之手不肯安。

The yes dies before it is spoken. For the first time, the two clever girls look at each other with no screen between them. Du Cuique smiles, bows, and steps back, but the wariness in her eyes is clear. She knows she has been seen, and she knows the hand that saw her was not the old lady's but the maid's. From that moment the balance inside the jar shifts by a hair. Lanxiang has named herself a rival without ever saying a word of blame. And Du Cuique, who meant to rule the inner house through the sickroom, now understands that the quiet girl with the water is the one wall she cannot climb. The season notes this small win and files it with the others, because it is in moments like these, a pin dropped, a grandmother named, a smile that does not reach the eyes, that the real war of the Lin estate is won or lost, far from any flag or blade.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

那句应允没出口就咽了回去。两聪明姑娘头回没了屏风，面对面。杜翠雀笑笑、福身、退下，可眼里的戒备明明白白。她知道自己被看穿，也知道看穿她的不是老夫人，是那丫鬟。自那一刻，罐里天平偏了一丝。兰香没说一句责怪，已把自己立成对手；而杜翠雀本想借病房掌内宅，这下明白，那端水静女，是她爬不过的墙。这季把这场小胜收进收着别处的那一格，因为正是在这种时候，簪一落、祖母一名、笑不及眼，林府真正的仗，分了胜负，远在旗与刀之外。

So episode 16 ends the way a fever ends a door, by locking everyone in with the thing they fear. The third daughter gave up the name and learned to protect. The master rode out and cut the snake's head. The old lady pulled the household into one room and met, across it, the girl who means to rule it. And Lanxiang, blocking the poisoner with a dropped pin and a whispered name, now stands at the center the season always meant her to reach. Episode 17, when it comes, will follow the water to its source and show that the sickness was never a curse at all, but a hand, and the house will learn whose hand held the ladle. But that is next time's story. For now, remember just this. In the Lin estate, a sealed gate keeps danger in as surely as it keeps it out, and the clever girl who reads the fever is the same one who reads the lie. This week, the lie has started to stir.

于是第十六集，像热封一门那样收住，把众人同他们怕的东西，锁在一处。三小姐交了名、学了护；主子出骑、斩了蛇头；老夫人把府收进一屋，隔着屋，迎面那要掌它的姑娘。而兰香，用落簪挡下投毒的、用低名声点破，如今立在季一直要她到的中心。第十七集来的时候，会顺水追到源，说明这病从不是咒，是一只手，而这府会知道执勺的是谁。但那是下回的事。此刻你只记一句：在林府，封起的门，把险关在内的程度，不亚于关在外；而那读热的聪明姑娘，正是读谎的那个。这周，谎已经起了动静。
