Episode five is where the mask starts to crack. Up to now the marriage between Lin Jinqi and Zhao Xingtang has been a thing of paper — an alliance of interests, a union in name only, two strangers bound by their families and nothing else. Episode five is the first time we see the paper tear, and the first time Jialan is forced to choose, however quietly, whose side the unseen girl is really on. It is also where the house first teaches her that mercy, here, is a kind of treason. The household has dressed its cruelties in ceremony for so long that the children who grow up in it learn to call mercy weakness before they learn to call it by its name.
🇨🇳 中文翻译

第五集，是那张面具开始裂缝的一集。至此，林锦岐与赵星棠的婚姻，始终只是一纸空文——利益的结盟，名分的凑合，两个被家族绑在一起、除此之外毫无瓜葛的陌生人。第五集，是我们头一回看见那张纸被撕开，也是嘉兰头一回被迫去选——无论多么悄无声息——这个隐身的姑娘，究竟站在谁一边。也是在这一集，宅子头一回教她明白：在这里，怜悯，多少算是一种叛逆。

Lin Jinqi moves first. He has spent his years at the border gathering proof of what Zhao Xingtang has been doing behind the smiling face — the usury, the private lending, the money that flows where it should not, the second set of books she believes no one can read. He lays it before his mother, the Senior Lady Lin, not to shame his wife but to arm his family. The Senior Lady, who never wanted this match to begin with, now holds the proof in her hands and decides that the time has come to cut her daughter-in-law down to size — before the usury poisons the family's name as well as its purse. He does not show her everything — only enough to make the point that the wife he married for alliance has become a liability the family can no longer afford to ignore. The Senior Lady receives the proof the way a general receives reinforcements — quietly, and with a plan already forming.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

林锦岐先动了手。他在边关的岁月里，暗中攒下了赵星棠在那张笑脸背后所作所为的证据——是印子钱，是私放借贷，是那笔本不该流动的银钱，是她以为无人能识的第二本账。他把证据呈到母亲林大太太面前，不为辱妻，只为给自家备一把刀。大太太本就不乐见这门亲事，如今证据握在手里，便决意，是时候把儿媳削一削了——免得这印子钱，既污了家财，也污了家门。

The blow falls where Zhao Xingtang least expects it: not on her, but on the one person she trusts. The Senior Lady, unwilling to let husband and wife tear each other apart in public, marries off Qingxiang — Zhao Xingtang's closest confidante and the keeper of half her secrets — to an old widower in the capital, Yang the Second Steward. Zhao Xingtang protests; she and Qingxiang are like sisters, bound by years of shared scheming. But Qingxiang, having seen the evidence with her own eyes, agrees without a moment's hesitation. She walks out of the inner rooms and out of Zhao Xingtang's protection in the same breath — and the lady of the house is left standing with one fewer hand, and no one left who knows where all the bodies are buried. Zhao Xingtang watches her closest confidante walk out the gate and understands, with a cold clarity, that in this house loyalty is a garment worn only until it is no longer convenient. It is a lesson Zhao Xingtang will, in time, be forced to teach others as harshly as it has been taught to her.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

这一击，落在赵星棠最想不到的地方：不是打在她身上，而是打在她最信得过的人身上。大太太不愿让夫妻二人在人前撕破脸，便把青葙——赵星棠最贴心的亲信、替她守着半壁秘密的人——许给了京城里一位老鳏夫，杨二管家。赵星棠反对；她与青葙情同姐妹，多少年是一起算计过来的。可青葙见了证据，毫不犹豫便应下了。她一步跨出内院，也一步跨出了赵星棠的庇护，这位主母，便少了一只手，也再无人知晓那些见不得光的勾当埋在何处。

And the house does not stop there. In the same chapter of cruelty, the illiterate Zijin — another of Zhao Xingtang's attendants — is beaten in public for a fault she cannot even read well enough to defend herself against. It is a small thing, a slap heard across a courtyard, but Jialan files it away: in this place, not knowing how to read is itself a crime the powerful can punish you for, and the powerless have no words with which to plead. The lesson lands quietly, beside the louder one of Qingxiang's marriage, and Jialan stores them both. She will remember Zijin's face long after the marks fade, because it is the face she herself might wear, on any day the wrong person decides she is useless.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

而这宅子的狠，并不止于此。在同一段酷烈里，不识字的紫堇——赵星棠的另一个随侍——因一桩她连读懂去分辩都做不到的过错，当众挨了打。这是件小事，是一记隔着院子都能听见的耳光，可嘉兰把它记下了：在这里，不识字本身，便是掌权者可以罚你的罪，而无力者，连替自己申辩的字句都没有。这教训静静落下，挨着青葙出嫁那记更响的教诲，嘉兰将两样，都收进了心里。

Cornered and stripped of her ally, Zhao Xingtang strikes back the only way she knows — cruelly, and at someone small. She fixes on Lü Jí'ér, whose ambition has made her an easy mark, and accuses her of theft. It is a lie. But in this house a lie from the lady's lips is law, and Zhao Xingtang orders the girl beaten — beaten until she lies still, until the blood comes, until the breath is nearly gone. The courtyard, which earlier heard only a slap, now hears the sound of a life being methodically beaten out of a girl who wanted nothing more than to be noticed. Jialan, hidden in the colonnade, counts the blows and understands that Lü Jí'ér is not being punished for theft at all, but for the crime of being seen wanting too much. In this house, desire itself is a punishable offense, and the rod falls fastest on those who forget to hide it.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

被逼到墙角、失了臂助的赵星棠，用她唯一熟稔的方式还击——狠毒，且拿小人物开刀。她盯上了吕蕺儿，那个因野心而好拿捏的姑娘，诬她偷窃。这是一桩假案。可在这座宅子里，主母嘴里的一句假话便是王法，赵星棠命人将这丫头打死——打到不动，打到见血，打到一口气几乎要断。方才还只听见一记耳光的院子，此刻听见的是一条性命，正被有条不紊地从那个只想被人看见的姑娘身上，活活打出去。

And here Jialan breaks her own rule. The girl who has trained herself to see nothing, to be nobody, cannot watch a life be beaten out beside her. She smashes a vase, drops to her knees, and begs — not for mercy, but with a warning, spoken loud enough for the watchers to hear: that a master who beats a slave to death is herself punished by the law, that the statutes fix a year of prison for a life taken so. The words land like cold water on a hot brazier. The beating stops. Jialan has just done the one thing she swore she would not — she has made herself seen, to pull another girl back from the brink. She has bought Lü Jí'ér's life with her own anonymity, and she knows, even as the courtyard falls silent, that the debt will be called in.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

而在这里，嘉兰破了自己立下的规矩。那个训诫自己“什么也不看、什么也不是”的姑娘，看不得一条性命在身侧被活活打死。她砸碎一只花瓶，扑通跪下，求的不是饶，是一句足以让旁观者听清的警醒：主家把奴婢活活打死，自己也要依律问罪，那律条里，白纸黑字写着打死要抵一年监禁。这话像一盆冷水浇在灼热的炭火上。打，停了。嘉兰刚刚做了她发誓不做的事——她让自己被看见，只为把另一个姑娘，从鬼门关前拽回来。

Zhao Xingtang, stopped but not repentant, sells off Lü Jí'ér's entire family — mother, father, the whole household — as chattel, a kill-the-chicken-to-scare-the-monkeys warning to every pair of eyes in the compound. Xu Wanquan, watching from the margins, reads the spectacle for exactly what it is and resolves to spend what little silver he has to pass a message to his daughter, to tell her the knife that fell on Lü Jí'ér was aimed at every girl who might ever refuse. The lesson is not lost on Jialan. She has saved a life; she has not saved the girl's family, nor quieted the house. Xu Wanquan's silver buys a whisper down the servant lines; Jialan receives it and understands that her father, too, now reads the house's grammar of fear.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

赵星棠，被拦下了却并不悔，索性将吕蕺儿一家——父母、满门——尽数发卖为奴，杀鸡儆猴，给这院子里每一双眼睛都立个警告。许万全在边上看得分明，一眼认出这场戏文是冲着谁，便打定主意，要耗尽手里那点银钱，递句话给女儿，告诉她那落在吕蕺儿身上的刀，本就是冲着每一个敢说不的姑娘去的。嘉兰没漏过这教训。她救下了一条命；却没能救下那姑娘的一家，也没能平息这座宅子。

Deprived now of both her confidante and her financial cunning, Zhao Xingtang turns to the one person she has kept apart from the house — her cousin and secret lover, Qian Shaoze. The two meet in private and set a secret signal between them: a pair of mandarin-duck seals, one half his, one half hers, to be matched when they reconcile the accounts he now keeps for her. It is a small, intimate conspiracy, and it binds them tighter even as the rest of Zhao Xingtang's world comes loose. The usury will continue; only the hands that hold the books have changed. Jialan, who has learned to read the rooms she serves, senses the new current before she can name it, and notes that Zhao Xingtang's smiles have found a new, private source.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

如今既失了亲信、又短了理财的心思，赵星棠转向那个她一直摒在宅门之外的人——她的表兄兼私通之人，钱少泽。两人私下相会，定下彼此之间的暗号：一对鸳鸯印，他执一半，她执一半，待他替她理账对账之时，以印相合为凭。这是一场小小的、暧昧的密谋，却把两人绑得更紧，纵使赵星棠周遭的一切正一寸寸松脱。印子钱还要接着放；只是握账的那双手，换了人。

The Senior Lady, sensing weakness, moves to reclaim the housekeeping power Zhao Xingtang has held — citing her lack of an heir and her mounting errors. But Zhao Xingtang, cornered, fights back with the only coin she has left: she promotes Lanxiang and Xiaolian to second-class maids, gifts them silk, and bends, visibly, to the Lin family's will. It is a calculated surrender, and it works. The Senior Lady, given the appearance of victory, backs off a step. Yet neither Lanxiang nor Xiaolian feels a flicker of joy. They have been lifted out of the stove room and into the eye of the storm. Promotion here is not rescue; it is relocation — from a stove where one can be invisible to a chamber where one is always, by definition, observed.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

林大太太见缝插针，要收回赵星棠手中的理家之权——拿她无子、又连出差错为由。可赵星棠被逼到墙角，只用最后一张牌还击：她将兰香与小莲擢为二等丫鬟，赐下绸缎，并明面上，向林家的意思低头。这是一场算计过的退让，且奏了效。大太太得了胜的表象，便暂退一步。然而兰香与小莲，没有半分喜色。她们是从灶房被提了出来，提进了风暴的眼。

Episode five closes on the shape of things to come. Lin Jinqi has armed his mother; Zhao Xingtang has been humbled and made dangerous; Qingxiang is gone; and Jialan, the girl who swore to stay invisible, has stepped into the light to save a rival she owes nothing to. The ledger, the beating, the broken vase, the sold-off family, the mandarin-duck seals — none of it is an accident. The house is tightening, and the unseen girl is, slowly and against her will, becoming someone the house cannot ignore. The vase is broken, and someone will have to account for it; Jialan has already decided the account will be hers to pay.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

第五集收在万事将变的轮廓里。林锦岐给母亲递了刀；赵星棠受了挫、也变得更危险；青葙走了；而那个发誓隐身的嘉兰，为了救一个与她无恩无怨的对手，踏进了光里。那本账，那场打，那只碎掉的花瓶，那被发卖的一家，那对鸳鸯印——没有一样是偶然。宅子正在收紧，而那个隐身的姑娘，正违背着自己的本意，一点点，成了这座宅子无法忽视的人。
