Episode five is where the mask starts to crack. Up to now the marriage between Lin Jinqi and Zhao Xingtang has been a thing of paper — an alliance of interests, a union in name only, two strangers bound by their families and nothing else. Episode five is the first time we see the paper tear, and the first time Jialan is forced to choose whose side the unseen girl is really on.
🇨🇳 中文翻译

第五集，是那张面具开始裂缝的一集。至此，林锦岐与赵星棠的婚姻，始终只是一纸空文——利益的结盟，名分的凑合，两个被家族绑在一起、除此之外毫无瓜葛的陌生人。第五集，是我们头一回看见那张纸被撕开，也是嘉兰头一回被迫去选：这个隐身的姑娘，究竟站在谁一边。

Lin Jinqi moves first. He has spent his years at the border gathering proof of what Zhao Xingtang has been doing behind the smiling face — the usury, the private lending, the money that flows where it should not. He lays it before his mother, the Senior Lady Lin, not to shame his wife but to arm his family. The Senior Lady, who never wanted this match to begin with, now holds the proof in her hands and decides the time has come to cut her daughter-in-law down to size.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

林锦岐先动了手。他在边关的岁月里，暗中攒下了赵星棠在那张笑脸背后所作所为的证据——是印子钱，是私放借贷，是那笔本不该流动的银钱。他把证据呈到母亲林大太太面前，不为辱妻，只为给自家备一把刀。大太太本就不乐见这门亲事，如今证据握在手里，便决意，是时候把儿媳削一削了。

The blow falls where Zhao Xingtang least expects it: not on her, but on the one person she trusts. The Senior Lady, unwilling to let husband and wife tear each other apart in public, marries off Qingxiang — Zhao Xingtang's closest confidante and the keeper of half her secrets — to an old widower in the capital, Yang the Second Steward. Zhao Xingtang protests; she and Qingxiang are like sisters. But Qingxiang, having seen the evidence, agrees without a moment's hesitation. She walks out of the inner rooms and out of Zhao Xingtang's protection in the same breath, and the lady of the house is left standing with one fewer hand.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

这一击，落在赵星棠最想不到的地方：不是打在她身上，而是打在她最信得过的人身上。大太太不愿让夫妻二人在人前撕破脸，便把青葙——赵星棠最贴心的亲信、替她守着半壁秘密的人——许给了京城里一位老鳏夫，杨二管家。赵星棠反对；她与青葙情同姐妹。可青葙见了证据，毫不犹豫便应下了。她一步跨出内院，也一步跨出了赵星棠的庇护，这位主母，便少了一只手。

Cornered and stripped of her ally, Zhao Xingtang strikes back the only way she knows — cruelly, and at someone small. She fixes on Lü Jí'ér, whose ambition has made her an easy mark, and accuses her of theft. It is a lie. But in this house a lie from the lady's lips is law, and Zhao Xingtang orders the girl beaten — beaten until she lies still, until the blood comes, until the breath is nearly gone.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

被逼到墙角、失了臂助的赵星棠，用她唯一熟稔的方式还击——狠毒，且拿小人物开刀。她盯上了吕蕺儿，那个因野心而好拿捏的姑娘，诬她偷窃。这是一桩假案。可在这座宅子里，主母嘴里的一句假话便是王法，赵星棠命人将这丫头打死——打到不动，打到见血，打到一口气几乎要断。

And here Jialan breaks her own rule. The girl who has trained herself to see nothing, to be nobody, cannot watch a life be beaten out beside her. She smashes a vase, drops to her knees, and begs — not for mercy, but with a warning, spoken loud enough for the watchers to hear: a master who beats a slave to death is herself punished by the law. The words land like cold water. The beating stops. Jialan has just done the one thing she swore she would not — she has made herself seen, to pull another girl back from the brink.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

而在这里，嘉兰破了自己立下的规矩。那个训诫自己“什么也不看、什么也不是”的姑娘，看不得一条性命在身侧被活活打死。她砸碎一只花瓶，扑通跪下，求的不是饶，是一句足以让旁观者听清的警醒：主家把奴婢活活打死，自己也要依律问罪。这话像一盆冷水浇下。打，停了。嘉兰刚刚做了她发誓不做的事——她让自己被看见，只为把另一个姑娘，从鬼门关前拽回来。

Episode five closes on the shape of things to come. Lin Jinqi has armed his mother; Zhao Xingtang has been humbled and made dangerous; Qingxiang is gone; and Jialan, the girl who swore to stay invisible, has stepped into the light to save a rival she owes nothing to. The ledger, the beating, the broken vase — none of it is an accident. The house is tightening, and the unseen girl is, slowly and against her will, becoming someone the house cannot ignore.

🇨🇳 中文翻译

第五集收在万事将变的轮廓里。林锦岐给母亲递了刀；赵星棠受了挫、也变得更危险；青葙走了；而那个发誓隐身的嘉兰，为了救一个与她无恩无怨的对手，踏进了光里。那本账，那场打，那只碎掉的花瓶——没有一样是偶然。宅子正在收紧，而那个隐身的姑娘，正违背着自己的本意，一点点，成了这座宅子无法忽视的人。
