# 兰香如故 — The Fragrance of Orchids Remains
## Episode 34 · Told Like A Story

Let me tell you about episode thirty-four, because this is the episode where the quiet Jinling years are broken by a drumbeat from the border, and the retired warrior who thought his marching days were done is called, one last time, to the road. Word comes down the river that the northern frontier has flared again — the same old enemies pressing at the passes, the same shortage of grain that has broken armies since the first wall was built. The capital, short of commanders it can trust, sends word to Jinling, and the word is for Lin Jinqi: the Jing'an Marquis is needed, not to lead the charge, but to do the unglamorous thing that wins wars — to see the grain through. He reads the summons without surprise, the way a man reads a letter he always knew would arrive, and Lanxiang, watching, knows the answer before he speaks it.

让我跟你讲讲第三十四集，因为这一集，安宁的金陵年，被边关的一阵鼓声打碎，而那以为行伍已终的退隐战者，最后一次，被召上了路。消息顺江下来：北境又起了烽——还是那些老敌在关前压，还是那自第一道墙起就断过军的粮缺。京城缺它信得过的将，便送话到金陵，话是给林锦岐的：靖安侯被需，不是去领冲，而是去做那赢战的、不风光的事——把粮送到底。他读那召，无惊，如一个人读一封他早知会到的信；兰香看着，在他开口前已知道了答案。

</details>

There is a moment, before he agrees, when the two of them stand in the courtyard the way they stood at every threshold the season set before them — weighing the cost. He is no longer the man who rode out to cut a snake's head; the old wounds make themselves known on damp mornings, and the fiftieth year is closer than he admits. But the border is starving, and a starving border is a door flung open to every enemy beyond it, including the pirates of the southern sea who have, he suspects, been feeding the unrest. He tells her he will go, and he says it plainly, without the grandeur of a younger man's oath, only the calm of one who knows the grain is the wall that holds the realm together. Lanxiang does not argue, because she married the man who answers such calls, and because she knows the argument would change nothing and cost them both the truth.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

在他应下前，有一刻，二人立在院里，如他们立在每道这季摆面前的门槛那样——掂着代价。他已不是那骑马去斩蛇头的男人；旧伤在潮的晨里自报，而五十之岁比他肯认的更近。可边在饿，饿的边是朝每个境外之敌大开的门，包括那南边海的贼——他疑他们一直在喂这乱。他告诉她他要去，说得平白，无少年誓言的壮，只有一个知"粮是撑起江山的墙"的人的静。兰香不劝，因她嫁的正是应这种召的男人，也因她知劝改不了什么、只让二人同失那真。

</details>

So the marquis becomes again a commander of wagons, and the sight is a strange one on the northern road — an old man at the head of a long train of grain carts, escorted by a thin guard of veterans who served under him in other wars. They move slow, because grain is heavy and the road is long and the season is turning cold, and each evening he walks the line of carts to be sure the sacks are dry and the drivers fed. Lanxiang, left in Jinling, returns to the page with a new urgency, as if the brush can hasten his safe return, and the play scripts flow from her faster than before, as though the telling of a true tale is the only charm she has against the distance. In the quiet of the courtyard she reads her own lines aloud to an empty chair, and the empty chair, she pretends, listens the way he would.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

于是侯又成了粮车的将，而这景在北路上怪得很——一个老者，立于长长一列粮车前，护的是一薄层曾在他别场战里效命的旧卒。他们走得慢，因粮重、路长、季正转冷，每夜他走一遍车队，确袋是干的、驭手吃饱。留在金陵的兰香，带着一种新的急回了页，似笔能催他早归，戏本比从前流得更疾，如讲一个真故是她对抗那远仅有的符。院里静时，她对一张空椅念自己的行，而那空椅，她假装，如他那样听着。

</details>

It is Du Cuique's remnants who strike, and they strike, as such enemies do, where the guard is thinnest. The pirate chief's scattered crew, driven from the sea by the imperial patrols, has taken to the inland roads and joined itself to the border unrest, and they know exactly whose grain train this is. They come at the train in the grey hour before dawn, from the wooded slope above the pass, a rush of dark figures and raised blades, meaning to seize the carts and leave the old commander dead in the dirt. Lin Jinqi, who has slept in his armor since the first night out, is already awake, and he does not reach for glory — he reaches for the wagons, shouting the drivers into motion and the guard into a wall, because the grain is the only thing that matters, and the man who dies for it dies for the realm.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

出手的是杜翠雀的残部，而他们如这类敌那样，挑守最薄处下手。那匪首的散卒，被官巡从海上赶出，已上了内陆的路、并入了边乱，且他们清楚这是谁的粮队。他们在破晓前的灰时，从关上林坡冲下，一片暗影与举刀，意欲夺车、把老将留在泥里死。林锦岐自头夜起便甲中眠，早已醒着，而他伸手不是取功——是取车，喝驭手动、喝守卫成墙，因粮是唯一要紧的物，而为它死的人，是为江山死。

</details>

The fight is not a duel but a holding. Outnumbered and on broken ground, Lin Jinqi throws his thin line across the road and refuses to break, taking the weight of the ambush onto himself so the carts can keep moving behind him. A blade finds his side; another glances his shoulder; the old wounds, never fully healed, split open and remind him that the body keeps its accounts. Still he holds, because a commander of grain is a commander of time, and every moment the train buys is a moment the frontier eats instead of starving. The veterans, shamed and rallied by his standing, close around him, and between the old man's stubbornness and the guard's loyalty, the ambush is spent without taking the train — but it takes its price in the marquis, who goes to his knees only after the last cart is clear.  A blade finds his side, but the line holds, because a man who will not break is harder to move than a man who will not fall.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

这仗不是决斗，是撑。寡不敌众、地又碎，林锦岐把他的薄线横在路心、不肯断，把伏击的力引到自己身上，好让车队在他背后继续走。一把刀入了胁；另一把擦了肩；那从未全愈的旧伤裂开，提醒他身是记账的。他仍撑，因管粮的将也是管时的将，车队每买下的一刻，便是边境用来吃、而非饿的一刻。旧卒们被他立着的样子既羞且奋，围拢上来；靠老者的拗与卫的忠，伏击耗尽了、没夺下车——却在那侯身上取了它的价：他直到最后一辆车道过，才跪下。

</details>

They carry him back on one of the very carts he saved, and the grain arrives, and the frontier holds, and the price of both is paid in the marquis's blood. The physicians at the border camp say what physicians always say about old wounds reopened — that he is fortunate to be alive, that he must rest, that the road home will be slow. He hears none of it as defeat. He asks first whether the grain reached the storehouses, and when they tell him it did, he closes his eyes the way a man closes a ledger he has balanced at last. Lin Jinqi, who once cut treasons and held households together by sheer will, has held a grain train against pirates and a border against hunger, and the cost of that holding is written now in a body that will not forget it. It is, he supposes with the dry humor of the dying, a fitting end for a soldier — not a charge, but a delivery, and the delivery is the harder of the two.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

他们把他抬回他自己救下的其中一辆车上，粮到了，边稳了，二者的价都以侯的血付了。边营的医，说医对"旧伤重裂"总说的那套——他命大、须养、归路会慢。他没一句听作败。他先问粮进了仓没有，他们说是，他便闭了眼，如一个人合上一本终于平了的账。林锦岐，曾斩叛、凭一股志把一座座家撑住，如今以一列车拒了海盗、以一边拒了饥，而这撑的代价，如今写进了一副不会忘的身子里。

</details>

The journey home is the longest road of all, because a wounded man cannot be hurried and a winter road does not care. They move by easy stages, the old commander propped in a cart among the sacks that are now his companions, and the veterans who guarded the grain take turns guarding the guard. Lin Jinqi, in the dull pain of the moving, has time to reckon the shape of his life — the treasons cut, the households held, the grain delivered — and he reckons it not with pride but with a soldier's plain accounting, the way one totals a campaign that is nearly done. He thinks often of Jinling, of the courtyard and the lamp and the woman who reads her pages aloud to an empty chair, and the thought of her is the one thing that makes the slow road bearable, the one warmth that reaches him through the bandages.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

回家的路是最长的，因伤的人催不得、冬的路不理会。他们缓缓而行，老将垫在车里，与他如今作伴的麻袋之间；护粮的旧卒，轮着护这护的人。林锦岐在移动的钝痛里，有时间算他一生的形——斩的叛、撑的家、送到的粮——而他不算以傲，只以兵的平账，如人结一场将完的役。他常想金陵，想那院、那灯、那对空椅念稿的女子；而想她，是那慢路唯一可忍的事，是透过绷带能及他的唯一暖。

</details>

Lanxiang meets the news the way she has met every blow the season dealt her — with a stillness that looks like calm and is, in truth, the gathering of strength. The letter says he is alive but grievously wounded, that he travels home slowly, that the southern sea has at last washed up on the northern road. She does not weep where the neighbors can see; she goes to the kitchen and makes the bitter broth the old campaigners swear by, and she sets it by the door as if he might walk in needing it that very night. The quiet Jinling life, she understands now, was never a wall but a truce, and the truce has been broken by the same woman who poisoned a child for a room — Du Cuique's shadow, it seems, reaches farther than either of them reckoned, from the sea to the grain to the man Lanxiang loves.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

兰香接这消息，如她接这季砸下的每一下——以一副看似静、实是聚力的静。信说他还活、却重伤，正缓缓归，说那南边的海，终在北路上了岸。她不在邻人看得见处泣；她去厨房煮老卒们信的苦汤，把它搁在门边，似他今夜便会走进来需它。这安宁的金陵日子，她如今懂，从不是墙，只是一次休战，而这休战，被同一个为争一屋毒了孩的女人破了——杜翠雀的影，看来比他们任一人估的更远，从海到粮、到兰香爱的那个男人。

</details>

So we close episode thirty-four the way a border camp closes a day — with the wounded tended and the watch set and the knowing that the respite is borrowed. The marquis has held the grain and paid for it in blood; the playwright keeps her vigil in Jinling; the pirate's remnant has proven it can strike far from the shore. Episode thirty-five, when it comes, will bring the old commander home to a fire and a wife, and will ask the hard question of what a body owed to too many wars is finally worth. But that is next week's tell. For now, remember only this: the grain reached the wall, and the man who saw it there went to his knees only after the last cart passed, and the woman who waits by the door has already begun, without knowing it, to count the cost of the silence after the war.
