# 兰香如故 — The Fragrance of Orchids Remains
## Episode 18 · Told Like A Story

Episode 18 blows the roof off the whole pretence. Up to now Du Zheng has worn the face of the loyal guard who once saved Lin Jinqi's life, the grateful brother of a house guest. That face comes off. He makes his move with a blade, not a bow, and he comes for Lin Jinqi with everything he has. The man who was supposed to protect the master tries to kill him in his own court. Lin Jinqi is not caught sleeping. He meets the strike and the two men go at it hard, because Du Zheng has nothing left to lose and Lin Jinqi has a house to keep standing. The fight spills across the yard while servants scatter and the old lady's rooms go still. For a few ugly minutes it looks like the saved man might be the one buried. But Du Zheng's desperation is also his weakness. He fights to end a life, and Lin Jinqi only has to outlast him. The blade that was meant for the master finds only air and the yard.

While the blades clash outside, the poison Lanxiang sent home does its quiet work inside. Du Cuique is already sick from the tea brewed in her own bath water, the same fever she dealt to the others. She lies in her rooms, weak, the sweet smile gone, and for the first time the house does not hurry to comfort her. The third daughter watches from the door and feels no softness. This is the woman who put death in the family's water, who killed her own maid to close the story. Now the story is closing on her. The old lady is told at last, plainly, what the tea was and where it came from. Senior Lady Lin listens, and the tired kindness in her eyes hardens. A guest who poisons the well is no guest. She gives the word to let the girl sicken, and to watch the door. Du Cuique, who thought she would rule the Lin estate, is now its prisoner in a fevered bed.

外头刀碰刀，兰香送回去的那毒在墙里悄悄发作。杜翠雀已经因那壶用自己澡水煮的茶病倒——正是她给别人的那场热。她躺屋里，弱，甜笑没了，满府头回不急着哄她。三小姐在门口看，心里不软。就是这女人，往自家水里搁死，杀自己丫鬟封口。如今故事收在她身上。老夫人终于被明明白白告知那茶是什么、从哪来。林母听着，眼里乏了的善转硬。毒井的客不是客。她发话，让那姑娘病着，盯住门。杜翠雀本想掌林府，这下成了自己热病床上的囚。

The reckoning needs names said out loud, and the third daughter is the one who says them. She steps into the open, in front of the family, and tells what she saw by the cave. She speaks of her second sister and Du Zheng together, the way they should not have been, the day she walked near and coughed on purpose so they would part. She says it plain, no tears, no drama, because the house needs the truth more than it needs her shame. The room goes quiet. The second daughter's face goes white. Du Zheng, still fighting in the yard, is named at last as the man in the cave, the man who used a daughter of the house to climb. The third daughter does not enjoy it. She simply refuses to carry the secret one more day. By saying it where everyone can hear, she takes the weapon out of Du Zheng's hand and puts it in her brother's.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

这笔账得有人大声点出名，而点名的是三小姐。她走到明处，当着全家，说出山洞边看见的。她说二姐和杜正在一处，本不该的那般，那天她走近、故意咳一声让他们分开。她说得平白，不哭不闹，因为府要的是真相，不是她的脸面。屋里静了。二小姐脸白。院里还打着的那杜正，终于被点成洞里那个人、借着府里姑娘往上爬的那个。三小姐不觉得痛快，她只是不肯再背这秘密一天。当着众人说出来，她把刀从杜正手里夺下，交到了兄长手上。

</details>

Then Lanxiang lays the second charge, and she lays it with proof. She brings the cloth, the water, the token of the house still warm in her hand, and she tells the room how the sickness was never a sickness. She walks them through the bath house, the bucket, the maid who came and went. She names Lingdang, the dead girl, and says who silenced her and why. She shows that three sick women and one murdered maid all point at the same cup, the same guest. Senior Lady Lin hears it and the last doubt dies. This is not a rumor from a jealous tongue. It is a chain of facts, link by link, held up in a quiet girl's hand. Du Cuique, pale on her sickbed, cannot gainsay a single one of them. The poisoner is caught by her own poison, and the proof is the fever burning in her own skin.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

接着兰香递第二状，而且带着证。她拿来布、水，手里还攥着温的内宅令牌，告诉屋里这病从不是病。她带他们过一遍澡房、水桶、那个进进出出的丫鬟。她点出铃铛——那死去的姑娘——说谁灭的口、为什么。她摆明三个病女、一个被杀的丫鬟，都指同一只杯、同一个客。林母听着，最后一点疑死了。这不是妒嘴的闲言，是一环环事实，攥在这静女手里。病床上惨白的杜翠雀，一句也驳不倒。投毒的被自己的毒拿住，证就是她自己皮上烧着的那场热。

</details>

Cornered and burning, Du Cuique does the one honest thing she has done. She admits it. Not all of it, not the part that would hang her brother, but the part that explains the hate. She says she never wanted the fever to take the whole house. She wanted Lanxiang. The quiet maid who reads, who listens, who turned into the one wall she could not climb, that was the target. Kill the maid and the inner house opens like a door with no lock. So she aimed the water at Lanxiang and let the others fall in the way. The room hears this and understands the shape of it. Du Cuique did not want a household. She wanted a corpse and a key. Lanxiang stands and takes the confession without flinching. She has known for days that the poison was personal. Now the poisoner has said so herself, in front of the old lady, with the fever as her witness.

<details><summary>🇨🇳 中文翻译</summary>

被逼到角里、烧着，杜翠雀干了她头一桩老实事：认了。没全认，没认会绞死她哥那截，只认了能说明恨的那截。她说她从没想让全府都热倒，她要的是兰香。那个识字的、肯听的、成了她爬不过的墙的静丫鬟，才是目标。除了这丫鬟，内宅就像没锁的门开了。所以她把水对准兰香，别人顺带倒。屋里听着，懂了那形状：杜翠雀不要一个家，她要一具尸、一把钥匙。兰香站着，听这供词不眨眼。她早知道那毒是冲人来的。这下投毒的自己说了，当着老夫人，拿那场热作见证。

</details>

Just when it seems the sick will not rise, Senior Lady Lin does what only the head of the house can. She steps forward with the antidote. All this time she has kept the cure folded in her sleeve, waiting for the proof before she spent it, because a cure given too early only feeds the poisoner's hand. Now she tips it into the cups of Yuchan, the fourth daughter, and Concubine Zhou, and the fever breaks. The three women who drank death from the bath water drink life from the old lady's hand. Even Du Cuique is given a dose, not from mercy alone but because a live prisoner tells more than a dead one. The house lets out the breath it has held for days. The children are saved, the concubine is saved, and the liar is saved only to be judged. Senior Lady Lin has not just run the house. She has, at the last moment, kept it alive.

眼看病倒的起不来，林母做了只有当家能做的：她拿着解药上前。这一路她把药叠在袖里，等证到了才肯用，因为解药给早了只是喂了投毒人的手。这会儿她把药倒进玉蝉、四小姐、周姨娘的杯里，热退了。三个从澡水喝下死的人，从老夫人手里喝下生。连杜翠雀也喂了一剂——不只因怜，更因活囚比死囚说得清。府把憋了几天的那口气吐出来。孩子救了，姨娘救了，说谎的救下只为审。林母不只要管家，末了那刻，是她撑住了这府的命。

</details>

Out in the yard the fight finds its end. The Yongchang Marquis, the old general Lin Jinqi rode out to save, arrives in time to see Du Zheng's blade come down, and he answers it himself. The marquis is not a man to waste a strike. He drives in and badly wounds Du Zheng, the man who would have killed two houses for one grudge. Du Zheng drops, done. The guards take him, and the Du brother and sister, the guest and the guard, are bound for the same prison. The cave, the bath water, the road, all of it traces back to these two, and now they are chained. The Yongchang Marquis stands over them and says little, because the bodies and the bonds speak for him. The Lin estate's worst enemy is carried out the gate in iron. For the first time in many episodes, the walls of the house hold no traitor inside.

With the Du siblings gone and the sick on the mend, the Lin estate does the oldest thing a household does after terror. It eats. Senior Lady Lin orders a feast, not a grand one, a real one, with the good dishes and the doors open and the lamps lit longer than the curfew. The family sits together without watching the shadows. The second daughter, shamed but alive, keeps her eyes down. The third daughter, who carried the secret and then threw it down, sits a little taller. Lin Jinqi comes in from the yard, still dust on his sleeves, and takes his place. Lanxiang is there too, no longer just a maid at the wall but the girl who held the house through its worst week. They pass the wine and they laugh, small and tired, because the kind of relief that follows a poison is its own quiet joy. The food tastes like safety.

The night turns soft. Lanxiang, who rarely drinks, takes a cup too many and goes warm and loose at the edges, the way a careful person does when the fear finally lets go. She laughs at something small and does not mind who hears. Lin Jinqi watches her from across the room, and when she sets her cup down half full, he picks it up and drinks what she left. No one remarks on it. It is a small thing, a shared cup, but in a house that just learned how poison travels, it says everything. He does not speak to her about it. He simply rises when she does, and he follows her down the lamp-lit path as she walks back, keeping his distance but keeping her in sight. She does not look back, and he does not call. He just walks behind her, quiet, all the way, the way a man does when the person ahead of him is the only one he trusts to lead.
